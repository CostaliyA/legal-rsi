## How the work was done

1. **Inventory and full read** of all seven source documents (term sheet, credit memorandum, appraisal summary, title commitment, Phase I ESA excerpt, org chart/operating-agreement excerpts, and the construction budget/draw-schedule workbook — including its four sheets).
2. **Source-to-issue log** (Memo Appendix A) mapping each proposition, figure, date and obligation to its location, so every position in the draft is traceable.
3. **Independent recomputation** of every total, ratio, threshold, date interval and cross-reference rather than accepting the sources' arithmetic (Memo Appendix B, B-1 to B-7). Several published figures do not survive the check.
4. **Drafting** from the verified log: Term Sheet economics preserved; Credit Memorandum conditions implemented; every conflict given a stated position in the instrument and a flagged resolution in the memo.

## Principal findings (Memo §3 and §4)

**Closing-critical:**
- The **$3,400,000 Triangle Commercial Bank deed of trust** has no source of funds and no line in the Sources and Uses; the payoff amount is unverified.
- The Borrower's own budget shows the **$8,200,000 land cost funded by mezzanine proceeds** with $0 equity — contradicting the term sheet, the credit memorandum and the org chart, and undercutting the LTC and HVCRE analyses.
- **Two of the four NFA conditions** (Soil Management Plan; Deed Notice, due 29 Nov 2024) appear in no bank document; the 0.38-acre residential restriction remains unresolved (our recomputation: as-completed LTV would be **70.68%** if the 14 units are lost).
- **Mechanics' lien relation-back** under N.C.G.S. §44A-10 from 2024 demolition/remediation work can prime the Deed of Trust; the title company's own suggested affidavit cannot truthfully be given.
- **Combined senior + mezzanine coverage is 0.93x–1.00x** — below break-even at the appraiser's own NOI.

**Material:**
- The term sheet's own **65% LTV cap is breached** by the approved loan (65.25%; $180,000 over) while the credit memorandum tests 70%.
- The **draw schedule does not foot**: draws 2–8 exhaust 100% of the GMP despite a stated 10% holdback; the "retainage release" is 9.42%, not 10%; the workbook's columns sum to $38.2m and $48.5m against $47.5m of draws. Exhibit C is a rebuilt, footing schedule.
- **Interest reserve**: the workbook's "adequate" conclusion assumes 3.50% SOFR; the correct shortfall at the current 4.35% is **≈$362,000** (the credit memorandum's $565,000 overstates it by ≈$200,000).
- The **partial repayment guaranty** is stated as $11,875,000 aggregate in the term sheet but "$11,875,000 per guarantor" ($23.75m) in the credit memorandum.
- Sponsor track record is **356 units** (appraisal) vs **580 units** (credit memorandum); the land acquisition is described four different ways; the **$14.2m borrower net worth is unsupported**; lender address, parcel acreage (2.09/1.38 vs 1.89/1.58), frontage (500.5 ft vs 420 ft), flood panels, UST history and remediation quantities all conflict across documents.
- The operating agreement's **75% supermajority** gives the mezzanine lender's principal (28% member) an effective veto over the senior pledge and the SPE/independent-manager amendments.

## Drafting decisions worth noting

- The loan is drafted as a **fully committed, non-revolving, nine-draw facility** — the term sheet's "single advance facility" language is internally inconsistent with its own draw schedule.
- LTC/LTV compliance is enforced through a **borrowing base limitation** and a permanent commitment reduction, because the credit memorandum's equity escrow alone does not arithmetically restore compliance.
- No nonrecourse exculpation was drafted (the completion and carry guaranties are unlimited and an exculpation clause would contradict them).
- Provisions beyond the executed term sheet are tabulated in Memo §6 so they can be papered in a term-sheet amendment before final documents issue.
- Four items are deliberately left to be conformed at closing and are cross-referenced in the draft's opening note: the Exhibit A legal description (to the survey), the Required Equity Contribution and Refinancing Payoff on the Closing Statement, the recorded Note amount, and Exhibit C.

## Caveats

The record contains only an appraisal **executive summary**, an ESA **excerpt** and operating-agreement **excerpts**; the full reports, GMP contract, Phase II report, NFA letter, mezzanine documents, survey, payoff letter and financial statements were not available and are listed as required closing deliverables (Memo §7). All recomputations are arithmetic checks on the sources, not independent title, survey, environmental or valuation opinions.
