## RECITALS

A. Borrower is the owner in fee simple of two contiguous parcels of real property located at 601 and 615 Foster Street, Durham, Durham County, North Carolina 27701, identified on the records of the Durham County Tax Administration as Parcel Identification Numbers 0821-04-72-3341 and 0821-04-72-4102, containing in the aggregate approximately 3.47 acres (the "**Land**").

B. Borrower has requested that Lender make available to Borrower a construction-to-mini-permanent loan in the principal amount of Forty-Seven Million Five Hundred Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($47,500,000) to finance a portion of the costs of constructing, on the Land, a six-story Class A mixed-use development consisting of 212 residential units (142 one-bedroom, 56 two-bedroom and 14 three-bedroom units), approximately 18,400 square feet of ground-floor retail space in seven retail bays, and a four-level structured parking garage containing 276 parking spaces, together with related amenities and site improvements (collectively, the "**Project**").

C. The Project is to be constructed by Graystone Construction Group Inc., a North Carolina corporation (NC General Contractor License No. 78442) (the "**Contractor**"), pursuant to a guaranteed maximum price construction contract in the guaranteed maximum price amount of $34,750,000 (as amended, supplemented and assigned from time to time in accordance with this Agreement, the "**GMP Contract**"), and has been designed by Meridian Design Collective PC, a North Carolina professional corporation (the "**Architect**").

D. Borrower has advised Lender that Brightleaf Mezzanine Capital LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company (NC Secretary of State File No. 2103988) (the "**Mezzanine Lender**"), has made or will make a mezzanine loan to Borrower in the original principal amount of $9,200,000 (the "**Mezzanine Loan**"), secured by a pledge of one hundred percent (100%) of the membership interests in Borrower, which Mezzanine Loan is to be governed by the Intercreditor Agreement described herein.

E. The Land was formerly improved with tobacco warehouse structures that were demolished in August 2024, and petroleum contamination associated with a former underground storage tank located in the northeast corner of Parcel 0821-04-72-4102 has been investigated pursuant to a Phase II Environmental Site Assessment dated August 2024 and remediated in September 2024 under the oversight of the North Carolina Department of Environmental Quality, Inactive Hazardous Sites Branch, which issued a conditional No Further Action determination letter dated September 30, 2024 for Site ID IHSB-24-1203 / Incident No. NONUST-24-0847.

F. The Loan is being made on the basis of, among other things, the Appraisal, the Phase I ESA, the NFA Letter, the Title Commitment and the other Due Diligence Items described in this Agreement, and is subject to the conditions precedent set forth in **Article 9** and in the other Loan Documents.

G. Each Guarantor has agreed to execute and deliver the Completion Guaranty, the Carry Guaranty, the Partial Repayment Guaranty and the Environmental Indemnity, in each case as more particularly described in **Article 13**.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the other Loan Documents, Lender agrees to make Advances to Borrower from time to time during the Construction Period (and, in the case of the Retainage release and the final portion of the Developer Fee, during the Mini-Perm Period) in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed at any time outstanding the Borrowing Base Availability, and in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed the Commitment. ## Section 2.2 Purpose

The proceeds of the Loan shall be used solely (a) to pay or reimburse the hard costs, soft costs and financing costs of the Project set forth in the Approved Budget to the extent allocated to the Loan in the Approved Budget, (b) to fund the Interest Reserve, (c) to pay the Origination Fee to the extent permitted by **Section 5.1**, and (d) to pay such other costs of the Project as Lender may approve in writing. # ARTICLE 3 — INTEREST

## Section 3.1 Construction Rate

During the Construction Period, interest shall accrue on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan at the Construction Rate, computed daily on the basis of actual days elapsed over a 360-day year. As of the Effective Date, and assuming a Daily Simple SOFR rate of 4.35%, the Construction Rate would be 7.60% per annum.

## Section 3.2 Mini-Perm Rate

During the Mini-Perm Period, interest shall accrue on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan at the Mini-Perm Rate, computed daily on the basis of actual days elapsed over a 360-day year. As of the Effective Date, and assuming a Daily Simple SOFR rate of 4.35%, the Mini-Perm Rate would be 7.85% per annum.

## Section 3.3 SOFR Floor

Notwithstanding any change in Daily Simple SOFR, Daily Simple SOFR shall in no event be less than the SOFR Floor of one percent (1.00%) per annum for purposes of determining the Construction Rate or the Mini-Perm Rate. Accordingly, the Applicable Rate shall in no event be less than 4.25% per annum during the Construction Period or 4.50% per annum during the Mini-Perm Period.

## Section 3.4 Default Rate

From and after the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default (and after the occurrence of an Event of Default described in **Section 24.5**, automatically and without notice), all outstanding amounts of principal, and to the extent permitted by applicable law all accrued interest, fees and other Obligations, shall bear interest at the Default Rate, computed daily, payable on demand. The application of the Default Rate is not a penalty but a charge for the additional risk and expense of carrying an overdue obligation, and it is agreed to be just compensation therefor.

## Section 3.5 Computation; Notices

Interest shall be computed on the daily outstanding principal balance of the Loan. Lender shall, upon request, deliver to Borrower a statement of the amount of interest and fees due for any period; such statement shall be conclusive absent manifest error. Lender shall not be required to deliver any notice of a change in, or determination of, Daily Simple SOFR or the Applicable Rate.

## Section 3.6 Benchmark Replacement

(a) **Triggers.** If, at any time, Lender determines that (i) Daily Simple SOFR has been permanently or indefinitely discontinued or is no longer representative, (ii) the regulatory supervisor for the administrator of SOFR or the Relevant Governmental Body has made a public statement that SOFR is no longer representative or is to be discontinued, or (iii) the administrator of SOFR, its supervisory authority or a bankruptcy or insolvency official has declared SOFR to be unlawful or unworkable, then Lender and Borrower shall endeavor in good faith to establish a Benchmark Replacement consistent with the Alternative Reference Rates Committee's recommended best practices, including an appropriate term rate or a daily adjusted version of SOFR, a spread adjustment (which shall equal or be consistent with the applicable ARRC spread adjustment for the relevant tenor), and conforming changes to this Agreement and the Note.

(b) **Unilateral determination.** If, after thirty (30) days, Lender and Borrower have not agreed to a Benchmark Replacement, Lender may designate a Benchmark Replacement (including a spread adjustment, if any, and conforming changes) that is consistent with market practice for comparable commercial real estate construction facilities and is not adverse to Borrower in any material respect, and any such designation shall be binding on Borrower; *provided* that no such designation shall increase the Applicable Rate above the rate in effect on the date of such designation unless required to reflect the difference between SOFR and the successor rate plus the spread adjustment.

(c) **No compensation.** Borrower shall not be entitled to any payment or compensation solely as a result of any Benchmark Replacement, and the SOFR Floor shall continue to apply to any Benchmark Replacement (with the floor adjusted, if applicable, in a manner consistent with the ARRC recommendation for the successor rate).

## Section 3.7 Interest Rate Limitation

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement, the Note or any other Loan Document, in no event shall the amount of interest or any other charge constituting interest under applicable law (including 12 U.S.C. §85 or Chapter 24 of the North Carolina General Statutes) exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law (the "**Maximum Rate**"). If any such amount would exceed the Maximum Rate, the rate of interest shall be automatically reduced to the Maximum Rate, and any amount previously collected in excess of the Maximum Rate shall be, at Lender's option, applied to reduce the principal balance of the Loan or refunded to Borrower. To the extent permitted by law, all interest at any time contracted for, charged or received shall be amortized, prorated, allocated and spread over the full period of the Loan in accordance with 12 C.F.R. §160.5 (or any analogous law).

## Section 3.8 Inability to Determine Rate

If Lender determines that (a) adequate and reasonable means do not exist for ascertaining Daily Simple SOFR, or (b) Daily Simple SOFR is not available for any reason and no Benchmark Replacement has been established under **Section 3.6**, Lender shall promptly notify Borrower, and until such notice is withdrawn, the Applicable Rate shall be determined using the last available Daily Simple SOFR (but not less than the SOFR Floor), and Lender shall not be obligated to make Advances during such period if the determination of the Applicable Rate is impractical in Lender's reasonable judgment.
