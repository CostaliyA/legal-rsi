# ARTICLE 4 — PAYMENTS

## Section 4.1 Payment of Interest

Interest on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall be payable monthly in arrears on the first (1st) Business Day of each calendar month, commencing on the first (1st) Business Day of the first full calendar month following the Closing Date. During the Construction Period, and for so long as sufficient funds remain in the Interest Reserve Account, each monthly payment of interest shall be funded by Lender from the Interest Reserve Account in accordance with **Article 8**. From and after the date on which the Interest Reserve is exhausted, and throughout the Mini-Perm Period, all interest shall be paid by Borrower from its own funds (subject to the Carry Guaranty).

## Section 4.2 Payment of Principal

The outstanding principal balance of the Loan, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, fees, costs and other Obligations, shall be due and payable in full on the Maturity Date, without notice, presentment, demand or protest, all of which are waived by Borrower. The Loan is interest-only; no scheduled amortization shall apply, except that the outstanding principal balance may be reduced by optional prepayments permitted under **Article 7** and by mandatory prepayments required under **Section 7.2**.

## Section 4.3 Manner of Payment

All payments shall be made in immediately available funds, in lawful currency of the United States, without setoff, counterclaim, deduction or withholding of any kind, to the account designated by Lender in writing (or by wire transfer in accordance with Lender's written instructions). Any payment received after 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a Business Day shall be deemed received on the next Business Day. If any payment is due on a day that is not a Business Day, such payment shall be due on the next succeeding Business Day, and interest shall continue to accrue during such extension.

## Section 4.4 No Setoff; Absolute Obligation

Borrower's obligation to pay all amounts due under the Loan Documents is absolute and unconditional. Borrower waives any right of setoff, counterclaim, defense or recoupment with respect to any payment due, and shall not withhold or delay any payment by reason of any dispute with Lender, the Contractor, the Architect, the Mezzanine Lender or any other Person.

## Section 4.5 Application of Payments

All payments received by Lender shall be applied in the following order of priority: (a) to reimburse Lender for any costs, expenses and indemnities (including reasonable attorneys' fees) incurred in connection with the Loan; (b) to late charges and fees; (c) to accrued and unpaid interest (including interest at the Default Rate); (d) to the outstanding principal balance of the Loan; and (e) to any other Obligations. Notwithstanding the foregoing, during the continuance of an Event of Default, Lender may apply payments in any order it determines in its discretion.

## Section 4.6 Payments Received from Third Parties

Lender may, but shall not be obligated to, accept payments from any Guarantor or other Person on account of the Obligations, and any such payment shall be applied as provided in **Section 4.5** without releasing Borrower or any Guarantor from liability.

# ARTICLE 5 — FEES

## Section 5.1 Origination Fee

Borrower shall pay to Lender at Closing a non-refundable origination fee equal to one percent (1.00%) of the original principal amount of the Loan, being $475,000 (the "**Origination Fee**"). The Origination Fee may, at Borrower's election and to the extent reflected in the Approved Budget (line item 4.02), be funded from the proceeds of the Loan as part of the Closing Advance.

## Section 5.2 Exit Fee

Borrower shall pay to Lender a non-refundable exit fee (the "**Exit Fee**") determined as follows: (a) if the Loan is repaid in full at any time during the Construction Period, an amount equal to one-half of one percent (0.50%) of the outstanding principal balance of the Loan immediately prior to such repayment; (b) if the Loan is repaid in full during the first six (6) months of the Mini-Perm Period, an amount equal to one-quarter of one percent (0.25%) of the outstanding principal balance of the Loan immediately prior to such repayment; and (c) no Exit Fee shall be payable if the Loan is repaid in full after the first six (6) months of the Mini-Perm Period or after the Mini-Perm Maturity Date. For the avoidance of doubt, the Exit Fee shall not be payable in connection with a refinancing of the Loan by Lender or any Affiliate of Lender.

## Section 5.3 Unused Line Fee

Borrower shall pay to Lender a fee equal to one-quarter of one percent (0.25%) per annum on the average daily undrawn amount of the Commitment, computed on the basis of actual days elapsed over a 360-day year, payable quarterly in arrears commencing with the first Business Day of the first full calendar quarter following the Closing Date and continuing through the earlier of (a) the date on which the Loan is fully advanced and (b) the Maturity Date. For purposes of this Section, amounts held in the Interest Reserve Account and the Equity Escrow Account shall be deemed drawn, and the undrawn amount of the Commitment shall exclude the amount of the Commitment permanently reduced under **Section 2.4(c)**.

## Section 5.4 Extension Fee

If Borrower validly exercises the Extension Option, Borrower shall pay to Lender, on or before the Construction Maturity Date, a non-refundable extension fee equal to one-quarter of one percent (0.25%) of the outstanding principal balance of the Loan as of the date of such payment.

## Section 5.5 Late Charge

If any installment of principal, interest, a fee or any other amount (other than an amount that is subject to a permitted grace period under **Section 24.1**) is not paid in full within five (5) days after its due date, Borrower shall pay to Lender, upon demand, a late charge equal to five percent (5.00%) of the overdue amount. Borrower acknowledges that such late charge represents a fair and reasonable estimate of the costs and administrative burdens Lender will incur as a result of late payment and does not constitute a penalty; *provided* that such charge shall be subject to **Section 3.7**.

## Section 5.6 Other Fees and Charges

Borrower shall pay (a) all fees and charges of the Disbursement Agent, the construction inspector, the appraiser, the environmental consultant and any other third-party consultant engaged by Lender in connection with the Loan, (b) all recording, filing, endorsement and title charges, and (c) all administrative, servicing, wire-transfer, processing and similar fees customarily charged by Lender for administration of construction facilities. Lender's schedule of such fees as in effect at Closing is attached as **Schedule 5.6**.

## Section 5.7 Fees Non-Refundable

The Origination Fee, the Exit Fee, the Unused Line Fee, the Extension Fee and all other fees paid under this Article shall be non-refundable when paid, except as expressly provided in this Agreement or as required by applicable law.

# ARTICLE 6 — MATURITY AND EXTENSION

## Section 6.1 Maturity; Transition to Mini-Perm

(a) **Construction Period.** The entire outstanding principal balance of the Loan, together with all accrued interest and other Obligations, shall be due and payable on the Construction Maturity Date, unless Borrower satisfies the conditions of **Section 6.1(b)** and the Loan converts to the Mini-Perm Period.

(b) **Conversion Conditions.** The Loan shall convert to the Mini-Perm Period if, on or before the Construction Maturity Date, each of the following conditions is satisfied (or waived by Lender in writing): (i) no Default or Event of Default exists; (ii) Substantial Completion has been achieved and a certificate of occupancy has been issued for all residential units, retail bays and the parking garage; (iii) the Vapor Mitigation System has been installed, verified and accepted by NCDEQ, and all other conditions of the NFA Letter then due have been satisfied; (iv) the Interest Reserve is not then subject to an unremedied Interest Reserve Shortfall; (v) Lender has received evidence of the required insurance, a current Rent Roll (to the extent any leases are in place) and a then-current budget for the Mini-Perm Period; (vi) the Required Equity Contribution has been fully deposited and applied; and (vii) no Construction Delay Default has occurred and is continuing.

(c) **Extension of Construction Maturity Date.** If Substantial Completion has not occurred by July 1, 2027, Lender may, in its sole discretion, extend the Construction Maturity Date to the Outside Completion Date; *provided* that any such extension shall not constitute a waiver of any Construction Delay Default and shall be subject to Borrower's payment of a fee of 0.15% of the outstanding principal balance. If the Construction Maturity Date is so extended, the Mini-Perm Maturity Date and the Extended Maturity Date shall each be extended by a like period.

## Section 6.2 Extension Option

Borrower shall have one (1) option (the "**Extension Option**") to extend the Mini-Perm Maturity Date from August 28, 2028 to the Extended Maturity Date, subject to the satisfaction of each of the conditions set forth in **Exhibit F** (together with such other customary conditions as Lender may reasonably establish) and the payment of the Extension Fee. The Extension Option is personal to Borrower and may not be exercised following the occurrence of an Event of Default or after March 1, 2028.

## Section 6.3 Relations to Mini-Perm; No Further Extension

The Mini-Perm Period is not a new or additional loan and does not constitute a novation or refinancing of the Loan. Borrower acknowledges that Lender's agreement to the Mini-Perm Period is conditioned upon the representations and covenants in this Agreement. Borrower shall have no right to any further extension of the Maturity Date beyond the Extended Maturity Date, and Lender shall have no obligation to refinance the Loan at or after the Maturity Date.

## Section 6.4 Take-Out Financing Cooperation

Borrower shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain a permanent take-out refinancing or sale of the Property. Not later than one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the then-current Maturity Date, Borrower shall deliver to Lender a written plan (including term sheets, lender identification, or marketing materials) reasonably satisfactory to Lender demonstrating Borrower's progress toward repayment of the Loan on or before the Maturity Date. Failure to deliver such a plan shall not itself constitute an Event of Default but shall be a factor considered by Lender in evaluating any request for an extension.

# ARTICLE 7 — PREPAYMENT

## Section 7.1 Optional Prepayment

Borrower may prepay the Loan in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty, other than payment of the Exit Fee to the extent payable under **Section 5.2**, accrued interest to the date of prepayment, and all other Obligations then due. Borrower shall give Lender not less than five (5) Business Days' prior written notice of any optional prepayment.

## Section 7.2 Mandatory Prepayments

Borrower shall prepay the Loan, without premium or penalty (other than the Exit Fee to the extent payable), in the following circumstances: (a) upon receipt of any Net Proceeds of casualty insurance or condemnation that Lender determines not to make available for restoration under **Section 20.6**, in an amount equal to such Net Proceeds; (b) upon any sale, transfer or other disposition of the Property or any material portion thereof or interest therein permitted under this Agreement (including any permitted transfer requiring Lender's consent that is conditioned on a prepayment), in the amount of the Net Proceeds thereof; (c) upon the incurrence of any Indebtedness not constituting Permitted Indebtedness, in the amount of the net proceeds thereof; (d) upon any DSCR cure required under **Section 16.3**; and (e) upon any determination by Lender that Borrower has received a capital contribution in excess of the amounts contemplated by the Approved Budget, to the extent required to maintain compliance with **Section 16.1** or **Section 16.2**.

## Section 7.3 Reduction of Commitment

Any prepayment of the Loan shall permanently reduce the Commitment by the amount so prepaid, and no portion of any amount repaid may be reborrowed. Upon Lender's demand, Borrower shall execute such confirmations of the reduction of the Commitment as Lender may reasonably request.

## Section 7.4 Prepayment of Other Debt

Borrower shall not prepay, defease, redeem or otherwise satisfy the Mezzanine Loan or any other Indebtedness (other than Permitted Indebtedness described in **Section 1.1**, clause (c) or (d)) without Lender's prior written consent.

# ARTICLE 8 — INTEREST RESERVE

## Section 8.1 Funding of Interest Reserve

At Closing, Lender shall fund from the proceeds of the Closing Advance an interest reserve in the amount of $4,850,000 (the "**Interest Reserve**"), to be held in a segregated sub-account of the Loan (the "**Interest Reserve Account**") established with Lender or the Disbursement Agent and subject to a Pledged Account Agreement. The Interest Reserve is part of the Loan and bears interest at the Applicable Rate; interest accruing on the Interest Reserve itself shall be paid from the Interest Reserve Account.

## Section 8.2 Application of Interest Reserve

Amounts in the Interest Reserve Account shall be disbursed solely to pay interest accruing on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan during the Construction Period. Lender is authorized to withdraw from the Interest Reserve Account on each interest payment date the amount of interest then due, without further request or authorization from Borrower. No amounts in the Interest Reserve Account shall be available for any other purpose, and Borrower shall have no right to withdraw or direct the application of such amounts.

## Section 8.3 Interest Reserve Shortfall; Replenishment

(a) Borrower acknowledges that, based on the draw timing reflected in the Draw Schedule and a Daily Simple SOFR rate of 4.35% per annum (the rate in effect on the date of the Credit Memorandum), the projected aggregate interest accruing during the thirty (30) month Construction Period is approximately $5,211,500, which exceeds the Interest Reserve by approximately $361,500. Borrower further acknowledges that the draw schedule analysis delivered with the Approved Budget projected aggregate interest of $4,628,675 and a surplus of $221,325 only on the assumption of a Daily Simple SOFR rate of 3.50% per annum, which is below the rate in effect on the date of this Agreement.

(b) If at any time the balance of the Interest Reserve Account is or is projected to be insufficient to pay interest through the Construction Maturity Date (an "**Interest Reserve Shortfall**"), Borrower shall, within five (5) Business Days after written notice from Lender, deposit into the Interest Reserve Account, in immediately available funds and from sources other than the proceeds of the Loan or the Mezzanine Loan, an amount sufficient to eliminate the Interest Reserve Shortfall. Amounts so deposited shall be applied as provided in **Section 8.2** and shall not reduce the Commitment or be credited against any other obligation of Borrower.

(c) At Lender's election, any unremedied Interest Reserve Shortfall may be funded by Lender (without any obligation to do so) and the amount so funded shall be added to the outstanding principal balance of the Loan, shall bear interest at the Default Rate until reimbursed, and shall be secured by the Loan Documents.

## Section 8.4 Adequacy Testing

Lender shall test the adequacy of the Interest Reserve at each Draw Request and not less often than quarterly, by comparing (a) the balance of the Interest Reserve Account with (b) the projected interest accruing through the earlier of (i) the next anticipated Draw date and (ii) the date ninety (90) days after the date of the test, based on the then-current Daily Simple SOFR, the Applicable Rate and the projected outstanding principal balance. If the test is not satisfied, Lender may condition any pending Advance on Borrower's deposit of additional funds into the Interest Reserve Account under **Section 8.3(b)**.

## Section 8.5 Monthly Reconciliation

Within fifteen (15) days after the end of each calendar month during the Construction Period, Borrower shall deliver to Lender a reconciliation of the Interest Reserve Account showing (a) the opening balance, (b) interest charged during the month, (c) any deposits, (d) the closing balance, and (e) Borrower's projection of interest accruing through the Construction Maturity Date, together with the SOFR assumptions used.

## Section 8.6 Rate Protection

If at any time (a) Daily Simple SOFR exceeds 5.00% per annum, (b) Lender reasonably determines that the Interest Reserve will be exhausted more than one hundred eighty (180) days before the Construction Maturity Date, or (c) the DSCR on a pro forma basis would be less than 1.25x, Lender may require Borrower, within thirty (30) days, to obtain and maintain an interest rate cap agreement on terms (including notional amount, strike rate, term and counterparty rating) reasonably satisfactory to Lender, at Borrower's sole cost, and to collaterally assign such cap to Lender. Borrower's failure to do so after demand shall constitute an Event of Default under **Section 24.3**.

## Section 8.7 Interest Reserve Is Not a Funded Reserve

The Interest Reserve is a funded accounting reserve within the Loan and is not a trust fund, escrow or deposit for the benefit of Borrower. Amounts in the Interest Reserve Account remain the property of Lender until disbursed, and Borrower has no interest therein except the right to have such amounts applied as provided in this Article.
