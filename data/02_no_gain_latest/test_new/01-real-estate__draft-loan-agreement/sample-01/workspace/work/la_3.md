# ARTICLE 19 — ENVIRONMENTAL COVENANTS

## Section 19.1 Compliance with Environmental Laws

Borrower shall, and shall cause all contractors, subcontractors, tenants and occupants to, comply with all Environmental Laws and with all permits, licenses and approvals relating to the environmental condition of the Property. Borrower shall not cause or permit any Release of Hazardous Materials at, on, under or from the Property, and shall not use the Property for any use that would cause Lender to be deemed an "owner or operator" under CERCLA or any similar state law.

## Section 19.2 NFA Letter Compliance

Borrower shall comply with each condition of the NFA Letter and shall, in particular:

(a) **Condition 1 — Vapor Mitigation System.** Cause the Vapor Mitigation System to be designed by a professional engineer licensed in North Carolina, incorporated into the Plans and Specifications, installed beneath any enclosed structure constructed over the Affected Area in accordance with the NCDEQ Division of Waste Management guidelines for vapor intrusion mitigation, and made capable of conversion from passive to active depressurization. Borrower shall deliver as-built drawings and a system performance verification report to NCDEQ within ninety (90) days after completion of the building foundation in the Affected Area, and shall deliver copies of all such submissions and any NCDEQ response to Lender within five (5) Business Days after submission or receipt. Borrower shall maintain the Vapor Mitigation System in good working order for so long as the NFA Letter remains in effect and shall not decommission, modify or impair the system without Lender's prior written consent.

(b) **Condition 2 — Non-Residential Land Use Restriction.** Not construct, use, lease, occupy or permit the occupancy of any residential dwelling unit, or any other "residential use" within the meaning of the NFA Letter, within the Affected Area, unless and until Borrower has obtained from NCDEQ a written modification of the NFA Letter expressly permitting such use, in form and substance satisfactory to Lender, and has delivered a copy thereof to Lender together with an updated appraisal and such other information as Lender may require. Borrower acknowledges that Lender's underwriting and the Appraisal do not reflect the loss of any residential units, and that if the NFA Letter is not modified, Borrower must (i) redesign the Affected Area units (or the affected portion of the building) as non-residential space and deliver revised Plans and Specifications and revised income projections, and (ii) demonstrate to Lender's satisfaction that the Financial Covenants are satisfied on the revised program.

(c) **Condition 3 — Soil Management Plan.** Prior to any excavation, grading or other subsurface disturbance in the Affected Area, prepare and submit to NCDEQ for approval, and obtain NCDEQ's approval of, the Soil Management Plan addressing soil screening, stockpile management, dust control, worker health and safety, personal protective equipment, disposal of impacted soil and confirmation sampling. Borrower shall incorporate the Soil Management Plan into the construction documents, deliver a copy to the Contractor and all relevant subcontractors, provide training to all site personnel, and deliver a copy of the approved Soil Management Plan to Lender.

(d) **Condition 4 — Deed Notice.** Cause the Deed Notice to be prepared, executed and recorded in the Durham County Registry of Deeds in accordance with the NFA Letter, and deliver to Lender a certified copy of the recorded Deed Notice with the recording stamp. Borrower acknowledges that the NFA Letter required the Deed Notice to be recorded within sixty (60) days after September 30, 2024 (i.e., on or before November 29, 2024), that the Phase I ESA dated October 28, 2024 does not reflect that the Deed Notice had been recorded, and that the Deed Notice is not reflected in the Title Commitment. Borrower shall deliver evidence of the recorded Deed Notice not later than five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date, or, if not previously recorded, shall cause it to be recorded on the Closing Date concurrently with the recording of the Deed of Trust, and shall deliver to Lender a written statement from NCDEQ or environmental counsel confirming that the late recording does not impair the NFA determination.

## Section 19.3 Environmental Reporting

Borrower shall deliver to Lender (a) copies of all submissions to and correspondence with NCDEQ relating to the Property, within five (5) Business Days after submission or receipt; (b) evidence of the annual inspection and certification of the Vapor Mitigation System within thirty (30) days after completion; (c) copies of all indoor air quality or sub-slab soil gas monitoring results, within ten (10) days after receipt; and (d) an annual environmental compliance certificate, signed by the Managing Member, certifying compliance with this Article and the NFA Letter.

## Section 19.4 No Further Remediation; Additional Investigation

If any Governmental Authority requires additional investigation, remediation, monitoring or reporting with respect to the Property, or if Lender reasonably determines that any Hazardous Materials condition at the Property requires investigation or remediation, Borrower shall, at its sole cost, promptly undertake and diligently complete such work in accordance with applicable Environmental Laws and shall keep Lender fully informed. Borrower shall not enter into any consent decree, administrative order or settlement relating to the environmental condition of the Property without Lender's prior written consent.

## Section 19.5 Notice of Releases

Borrower shall notify Lender in writing within twenty-four (24) hours after Borrower obtains knowledge of (a) any Release or threatened Release of Hazardous Materials at, on, under or from the Property that requires reporting under any Environmental Law, (b) any inspection, investigation, notice of violation, order or information request from any Governmental Authority relating to the environmental condition of the Property, or (c) any claim by any third party relating to Hazardous Materials or Environmental Laws.

## Section 19.6 Lender's Environmental Rights

Lender may, at Borrower's expense (but not more than once per year in the absence of an Event of Default or a reasonable basis for concern), obtain a Phase I ESA update or other environmental assessment of the Property. Borrower shall cooperate with any such assessment and shall provide access to the Property and to all environmental records. Borrower shall not communicate with NCDEQ regarding the NFA Letter, the Affected Area or the Vapor Mitigation System without prior notice to Lender (except in an emergency), and shall permit Lender's counsel to participate in any such communications.

## Section 19.7 Environmental Indemnity; Survival

The Environmental Indemnity shall be in addition to, and not in limitation of, the covenants in this Article, and shall survive repayment of the Loan and any transfer of the Property by foreclosure, deed in lieu of foreclosure or otherwise. The obligations of Borrower and the Guarantors under this Article and the Environmental Indemnity shall not be limited by any exculpatory or nonrecourse provision of any Loan Document.

## Section 19.8 Pollution Legal Liability Insurance

Lender may require, and Borrower shall obtain within sixty (60) days after Lender's written request, a pollution legal liability insurance policy (or environmental remediation insurance) with coverage, limits, deductible, term and insurer rating reasonably satisfactory to Lender, naming Lender as additional insured and loss payee, and shall collaterally assign such policy to Lender. Lender's failure to require such insurance at Closing shall not constitute a waiver of its right to require it thereafter.

## Section 19.9 Environmental Default

Any failure to comply with this Article, the NFA Letter, the Soil Management Plan or the Deed Notice shall constitute an Event of Default under **Section 24.13** and a Recourse Trigger under **Section 13.4(e)**.

## Section 19.10 Lender Not an Operator

Nothing in this Agreement, and no inspection, approval, consent or advance by Lender, shall be deemed to make Lender an "owner or operator," "arranger," "transporter" or "participant in management" of the Property or the Project for purposes of CERCLA, any Environmental Law or any other law. Borrower shall include in all leases and construction contracts such provisions as are reasonably necessary to preserve Lender's secured creditor exemption.
