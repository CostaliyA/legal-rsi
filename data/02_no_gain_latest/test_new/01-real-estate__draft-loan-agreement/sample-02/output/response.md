## Method

I inventoried and read the seven source documents in full (executed term sheet, credit memorandum, appraisal summary, preliminary title commitment, Phase I ESA excerpt, borrower organizational chart/operating-agreement excerpts, and the construction budget/draw-schedule workbook), then:

1. **Built a source-to-issue log** (Memorandum Appendix A, 30 propositions) recording where each material fact, figure, date and obligation appears and flagging every cross-document conflict.
2. **Recomputed every figure independently** rather than adopting stated numbers — every ratio, total, acreage, draw amount and date interval (Memorandum § 8, 35 rows). Several reported figures do not reconcile.
3. **Drafted the instrument from the verified record**, using defined terms and self-executing mechanisms rather than recitals, and left genuine business decisions in bracketed form (Draft Agreement Exhibit H, 15 items).
4. **Validated both files** (ZIP/XML/relationship validation clean; rendered to PDF to check pagination, table fit and orphan pages).

## Principal findings

**Critical**

- **The Land has five incompatible histories.** The recorded deed (Book 7598/443 and /445, June 12, 2023) shows a purchase from Foster Street Properties LLC; the operating agreement says Ms. Vasquez-Torres contributed it at formation in 2019; the credit memorandum says it was acquired through capital calls from all three members; the Phase I ESA says 2022; the appraisal says early 2024. The borrower's own budget says it was funded with **$8,200,000 of mezzanine proceeds** — which, if true, leaves only $1,000,000 of mezzanine availability and an outstanding balance already above $9.2 million on a 12.5% PIK basis.
- **The loan breaches two covenants at closing, and the approved cure does not work.** LTC 83.77% (max 80%); as-completed LTV 65.25% (max 65% — a breach the credit memorandum does not report because it measures against the 70% policy limit). The $2,140,000 escrow condition cannot change LTC while the commitment stays at $47,500,000. **Recommended fix: reduce the Commitment Amount to $45,360,000** and fund $2,140,000 of verified equity — sources then equal uses and both breaches cure (LTC 80.00%, LTV 62.31%).
- **$3,400,000 prior deed of trust is unfunded.** The Triangle Commercial Bank deed of trust is unreleased and appears in neither the uses nor the sources of the $56,700,000 budget.
- **Deed Notice due November 29, 2024 is not evidenced**, and the NCDEQ non-residential use restriction is in direct tension with the 14 units said to sit above the affected area (loss of those units → DSCR 1.21x, as-stabilized LTV 67.56%, a second breach).

**High** — draw schedule that disburses 100% of the GMP and then "releases" retainage never withheld (10% = $3,475,000, not $3,275,000; the workbook's Draw 9 foots $1,000,000 off); interest reserve characterized as both adequate (+$221,325) and short (−$365,830 / −$565,000); competing 100% membership-interest pledges; a 75% member-approval threshold that gives the mezzanine lender's affiliate a veto over the take-out and over the SPE amendments; a DSCR test set at a date nine months before projected stabilization; and unbudgeted costs (mezzanine PIK, payoff, fees, association assessments).

The memorandum also records twelve medium/low items — a 7.78-foot boundary discrepancy between the two parcels, conflicting parcel areas and site orientation, unverified Architectural Review Committee approval, an omitted $34,500 association assessment in NOI, mechanic's-lien relation-back over pre-closing site work, an underinsured builder's-risk limit, a grantor-net-worth Event of Default that cross-references a Borrower covenant, and HVCRE classification that flips on whether contributed land is counted at cost or appraised value.

## Structure of the draft agreement

Articles I–XV: definitions; the loan (commitment with a conforming-adjustment mechanism, SOFR, interest reserve, fees, prepayment, extension option); closing conditions; disbursement mechanics and retainage; representations; affirmative and negative covenants; financial covenants and reporting; insurance/casualty; environmental (NFA compliance, use restriction, vapor mitigation, soil management plan, deed notice); single-purpose entity and separateness; collateral, pledges and intercreditor terms; guaranties and recourse carve-outs; default and remedies (including North Carolina power-of-sale and deficiency provisions); miscellaneous.

Schedules 1.1, 5.2 and 8.11; Exhibits A (legal description as scheduled, with the discontinuity identified), B (Permitted Exceptions conformed to what title can insure), C (budget in two panels — as proposed and as conformed), D (a draw schedule that foots, with a reconciliation to the term sheet), E (interest-reserve sensitivity), F (NFA compliance schedule), G (form of draw request), H (schedule of open items).
