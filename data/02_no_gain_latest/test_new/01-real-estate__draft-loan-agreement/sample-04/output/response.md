## 2. `cover-memorandum.docx` (~21,600 words, 59 pages)

A privileged report to the Lender (and a copy to the title company) setting out 60 numbered issues across the seven source documents, each presented under **Record / Analysis / Open / Recommendation** so that fact, assumption, legal characterisation and recommendation are kept distinct.

**Contents:** scope and method; source inventory; executive summary with a 16-item risk-prioritised register; a 45-metric **recomputation table** verifying or challenging every stated figure; detailed issue analysis in eight groups (valuation and covenants; title/survey/site; environmental; structure/SPE/intercreditor; construction and disbursement; commercial terms and insurance; legal/documentation); a 60-row issue-to-draft cross-reference; a 24-item diligence and information-request table; assumptions, limitations and reliance; and a sequenced next-steps timetable to a February 28, 2025 closing.

**Principal findings:**

- The loan **fails two of its own covenants at closing**: LTC 83.77% v. 80% ($2,140,000 over) and as-completed LTV 65.25% v. 65% ($180,000 over); the Credit Memorandum's 70% policy column masks the second.
- The proposed $2,140,000 equity escrow **does not cure LTC** — the numerator and denominator are unchanged. The cure is a reduced commitment ($45,360,000) or added costs plus genuine equity ($4,080,000, producing 78.15%).
- The **$3,400,000 Triangle Commercial Bank deed of trust is funded nowhere** in sources and uses, yet the title policy requires its release.
- **Combined senior and mezzanine coverage is 0.9996x** (0.93x with PIK accretion) — never computed in the record.
- The Borrower's own budget says the land was **funded by mezzanine loan proceeds**, contradicting the org chart and the Credit Memorandum and undermining the LTC, LTV and HVCRE analyses.
- The NFA Letter has **four** conditions (the Term Sheet describes one); the **deed notice was due November 29, 2024** and is not evidenced in the title commitment; the Appraisal wrongly states it was recorded.
- Site geometry is described three incompatible ways (Foster Street north v. west; 420 ft v. 500.50 ft frontage; acreage split), and the 0.38-acre restricted area cannot be located without the outstanding survey.
- Draw schedule does not foot ($48,500,000 v. $47,500,000; Draw 9 row foots to $4,275,000 v. $3,275,000); $200,000 of retainage would never be released; construction maturity (Aug. 28, 2027) precedes projected completion (Aug. 31, 2027).
- Plus: partial-guaranty quantum conflict ($11,875,000 v. $23,750,000), an infeasible $12,000,000 Borrower net-worth covenant, a missing guarantor net-worth covenant and a bad cross-reference, CC&R assessments omitted from NOI, three different interest-reserve conclusions, a mechanic's-lien relation-back exposure, architectural-review approval absent, and an apparently unfunded ~$475,000 NC deed-of-trust excise tax.
