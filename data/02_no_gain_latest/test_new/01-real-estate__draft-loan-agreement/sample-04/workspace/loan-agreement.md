# ARTICLE 2 — THE LOAN; COMMITMENT; PURPOSE

## 2.1 The Commitment; Single Commitment, Multiple Advances.

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Lender agrees to make Advances to the Borrower from time to time during the Construction Period in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed the Commitment. The Loan is a single committed facility with multiple Advances, and is not a revolving facility. No amount repaid or prepaid may be reborrowed. Any portion of the Commitment that has not been advanced on or before the last day for Advances under Section 2.4 is automatically cancelled.

::: {custom-style="DraftNote"}
**Drafting Note 1 (Memo Issues A-2 and E-1).** The Term Sheet (§3.1) describes the Loan as "a single advance facility, with loan proceeds to be disbursed in accordance with the approved draw schedule", which is internally contradictory: a draw-scheduled construction loan is a multiple-advance facility under a single commitment. Section 2.1 adopts the substance (one commitment, staged advances) and corrects the label. In addition, the Term Sheet and the Credit Memorandum both fail to reconcile the Commitment with the 80% LTC covenant (see Section 2.6 and Drafting Note 2).
:::

## 2.2 Purpose.

The proceeds of the Loan shall be used solely to fund Project Costs in accordance with the Approved Budget and the Draw Schedule, and for no other purpose. No proceeds of the Loan shall be used (i) to fund any cost that is not an approved line item of the Approved Budget, (ii) to fund any cost overrun, (iii) to repay the Mezzanine Loan, (iv) to make any distribution to any member of the Borrower or any Affiliate, other than payment of the Developer Fee strictly in accordance with Section 6.5(c), or (v) for any purpose that would violate Regulation U or Regulation X of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.

## 3.3 SOFR Conventions.

::: {custom-style="SubPara"}
(a) "**Daily Simple SOFR**" means, for any day, SOFR for such day, determined by the Lender in accordance with the convention described in this Section 3.3. Interest shall be calculated on the basis of a 360-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

(b) SOFR shall be determined by the Lender on each U.S. Government Securities Business Day, using a **five (5) U.S. Government Securities Business Day lookback** (without observation shift) from the day for which interest is being determined, as recommended by the Alternative Reference Rates Committee. The Lender's determination of SOFR and of the applicable rate of interest shall be conclusive and binding on the Borrower absent manifest error.

(c) **SOFR Floor.** If Daily Simple SOFR is less than **1.00%** on any day, SOFR shall be deemed to be 1.00% for purposes of determining the rate of interest for that day. The parties acknowledge that the SOFR Floor results in a minimum all-in rate of 4.25% during the Construction Period and 4.50% during the Mini-Perm Period.

(d) **Benchmark Replacement.** If the Lender determines that (i) SOFR has been or will be permanently or indefinitely discontinued or is no longer representative, (ii) the administrator of SOFR or its regulator has made a public statement to that effect, or (iii) the Lender is advised by counsel that it may not lawfully use SOFR, then the Lender may replace SOFR with a successor benchmark rate and make conforming changes (including a spread adjustment) consistent with the Alternative Reference Rates Committee's recommended best practices and the then-prevailing market convention, in each case after not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the Borrower. Any such replacement shall be a "hardwired" replacement and shall not require the Borrower's consent, provided that the successor rate and adjustment are consistent with the ARRC recommended fallback language and are applied consistently by the Lender to comparable commercial real estate loans.
