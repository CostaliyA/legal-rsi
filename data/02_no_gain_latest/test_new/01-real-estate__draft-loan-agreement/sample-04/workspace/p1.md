# ARTICLE 2 — THE LOAN; COMMITMENT; PURPOSE

## 2.1 The Commitment; Single Commitment, Multiple Advances.

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Lender agrees to make Advances to the Borrower from time to time during the Construction Period in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed the Commitment. The Loan is a single committed facility with multiple Advances, and is not a revolving facility. No amount repaid or prepaid may be reborrowed. Any portion of the Commitment that has not been advanced on or before the last day for Advances under Section 2.4 is automatically cancelled.

::: {custom-style="DraftNote"}
**Drafting Note 1 (Memo Issues A-2 and E-1).** The Term Sheet (§3.1) describes the Loan as "a single advance facility, with loan proceeds to be disbursed in accordance with the approved draw schedule", which is internally contradictory: a draw-scheduled construction loan is a multiple-advance facility under a single commitment. Section 2.1 adopts the substance (one commitment, staged advances) and corrects the label. In addition, the Term Sheet and the Credit Memorandum both fail to reconcile the Commitment with the 80% LTC covenant (see Section 2.6 and Drafting Note 2).
:::

## 2.2 Purpose.

The proceeds of the Loan shall be used solely to fund Project Costs in accordance with the Approved Budget and the Draw Schedule, and for no other purpose. No proceeds of the Loan shall be used (i) to fund any cost that is not an approved line item of the Approved Budget, (ii) to fund any cost overrun, (iii) to repay the Mezzanine Loan, (iv) to make any distribution to any member of the Borrower or any Affiliate, other than payment of the Developer Fee strictly in accordance with Section 6.5(c), or (v) for any purpose that would violate Regulation U or Regulation X of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.

## 2.3 Note; Interest and Principal.

The Loan shall be evidenced by the Note and shall bear interest and be payable as provided in Article 3. The Loan is interest-only during the Construction Period and the Mini-Perm Period; no scheduled principal amortisation is required, and the outstanding principal balance, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, fees and other amounts, is due and payable in full on the Maturity Date unless earlier accelerated.

## 2.4 Advance Period.

No Advance shall be made after the earlier of (i) the Construction Maturity Date and (ii) the date on which the Commitment is fully advanced or cancelled; provided that the Final Advance (Retainage release) may be made after Substantial Completion in accordance with Section 5.9, and any Advance in respect of an approved Change Order may be made during the Mini-Perm Period if, and only if, the Lender has approved such Change Order and sufficient undisbursed Commitment remains available.

## 2.5 Conversion to Mini-Perm Period.

If Substantial Completion occurs on or before the Construction Maturity Date, the Loan shall convert to the Mini-Perm Period as of the first day of the calendar month following the month in which Substantial Completion occurs, and the rate of interest shall change accordingly on that date. If Substantial Completion has not occurred on or before the Construction Maturity Date, (i) the Loan shall nonetheless convert to the Mini-Perm Period and the rate of interest applicable to the Mini-Perm Period shall apply from the day following the Construction Maturity Date, (ii) the failure to achieve Substantial Completion by the Construction Maturity Date shall constitute an Event of Default under Section 16.1(f) unless waived in writing by the Lender, and (iii) the Lender's obligation to make further Advances shall terminate except as expressly provided in Section 5.9.

## 2.6 Loan-to-Cost and Loan-to-Value Compliance at Closing; Reduction of the Commitment.

::: {custom-style="SubPara"}
(a) **LTC Condition.** It is a condition to the Lender's obligation to close that the Loan-to-Cost Ratio, determined as of the Closing Date on the basis of Total Project Costs and the Commitment (after giving effect to any reduction under this Section 2.6), shall not exceed **eighty percent (80%)**.

(b) **LTV Condition.** It is a condition to the Lender's obligation to close that (i) the Loan-to-Value Ratio determined by dividing the Commitment by the As-Completed Value shall not exceed **sixty-five percent (65%)**, and (ii) the Loan-to-Value Ratio determined by dividing the Commitment by the As-Stabilized Value shall not exceed **sixty-five percent (65%)**.

(c) **Mandatory Reduction or Cure.** If either the LTC Condition or the LTV Condition is not satisfied on the Closing Date, the Commitment shall be permanently reduced, without penalty or fee, to the largest amount that satisfies each such condition, being (i) in the case of the LTC Condition, the product of Total Project Costs multiplied by 80%, and (ii) in the case of the LTV Condition, the product of the As-Completed Value multiplied by 65%; and the Approved Budget, the Draw Schedule and each Guaranty denominated as a percentage of the Loan Amount shall be conformed accordingly. As an alternative to such reduction, the Borrower may satisfy the applicable condition by funding additional equity in an amount sufficient to cause Total Project Costs (in the case of the LTC Condition, after giving effect to the inclusion of the Existing Mortgage Payoff Amount and all pre-closing costs properly treated as Project Costs) to bear the required ratio to the Commitment. The Borrower shall deliver to the Lender, not later than five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date, a Compliance Certificate setting out the computations required by this Section 2.6.

(d) **Conformed Amounts.** The parties acknowledge that, on the figures contained in the source documents, (i) LTC calculated as $47,500,000 ÷ $56,700,000 equals **83.77%**, which exceeds the 80% maximum, and the maximum Commitment at 80% LTC would be **$45,360,000**; and (ii) LTV calculated as $47,500,000 ÷ $72,800,000 equals **65.25%**, which exceeds the 65% maximum, and the maximum Commitment at 65% LTV would be **$47,320,000**. The binding constraint on those figures is the LTC Condition, and the conforming Commitment would be **$45,360,000**. The Loan Amount stated on the cover page and in Section 1.1(xx) is therefore subject to reduction in accordance with this Section 2.6.
