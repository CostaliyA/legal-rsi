# ARTICLE 3 — INTEREST, FEES, YIELD PROTECTION AND PAYMENTS

## 3.1 Interest Rate — Construction Period.

From the Closing Date through the last day of the Construction Period, the outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall bear interest at a rate per annum equal to Daily Simple SOFR plus **3.25%** (the "**Construction Rate**").

## 3.2 Interest Rate — Mini-Perm Period.

From the first day of the Mini-Perm Period through the Maturity Date, the outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall bear interest at a rate per annum equal to Daily Simple SOFR plus **3.50%** (the "**Mini-Perm Rate**").

## 3.3 SOFR Conventions.

::: {custom-style="SubPara"}
(a) "**Daily Simple SOFR**" means, for any day, SOFR for such day, determined by the Lender in accordance with the convention described in this Section 3.3. Interest shall be calculated on the basis of a 360-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

(b) SOFR shall be determined by the Lender on each U.S. Government Securities Business Day, using a **five (5) U.S. Government Securities Business Day lookback** (without observation shift) from the day for which interest is being determined, as recommended by the Alternative Reference Rates Committee. The Lender's determination of SOFR and of the applicable rate of interest shall be conclusive and binding on the Borrower absent manifest error.

(c) **SOFR Floor.** If Daily Simple SOFR is less than **1.00%** on any day, SOFR shall be deemed to be 1.00% for purposes of determining the rate of interest for that day. The parties acknowledge that the SOFR Floor results in a minimum all-in rate of 4.25% during the Construction Period and 4.50% during the Mini-Perm Period.

(d) **Benchmark Replacement.** If the Lender determines that (i) SOFR has been or will be permanently or indefinitely discontinued or is no longer representative, (ii) the administrator of SOFR or its regulator has made a public statement to that effect, or (iii) the Lender is advised by counsel that it may not lawfully use SOFR, then the Lender may replace SOFR with a successor benchmark rate and make conforming changes (including a spread adjustment) consistent with the Alternative Reference Rates Committee's recommended best practices and the then-prevailing market convention, in each case after not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the Borrower. Any such replacement shall be a "hardwired" replacement and shall not require the Borrower's consent, provided that the successor rate and adjustment are consistent with the ARRC recommended fallback language and are applied consistently by the Lender to comparable commercial real estate loans.
:::

## 3.4 Default Rate.

Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of any Event of Default, all outstanding amounts under the Loan Documents shall bear interest at the Default Rate, payable on demand, in lieu of the rate otherwise applicable. Interest at the Default Rate shall accrue from the date of the Event of Default until the earlier of the date on which the Event of Default is cured or waived in writing or the date on which all amounts are paid in full. The application of the Default Rate is not a remedy in lieu of any other remedy and does not constitute a waiver of any Event of Default.

## 3.6 Voluntary Prepayment; Exit Fee Calculation.

The Borrower may prepay the Loan in whole but not in part upon not less than ten (10) Business Days' prior irrevocable written notice, without premium or penalty other than the Exit Fee payable under Section 3.5(b) and the reimbursement of the Lender's costs under Section 18.2. No portion of the Loan may be reborrowed after prepayment. Any prepayment during the Construction Period shall be applied first to the Exit Fee, then to accrued interest, then to principal on the outstanding Direct Advances (as defined in Section 7.1), and shall not affect the Lender's right to cancel the Commitment.

## 3.7 Payments.

All payments of principal, interest, fees and other amounts shall be made in immediately available funds, without setoff, counterclaim, deduction or withholding, to the account designated by the Lender in writing, not later than 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time on the due date. Interest shall be payable monthly in arrears on the first Business Day of each calendar month (each a "**Payment Date**"), commencing on the first Business Day of the calendar month following the Closing Date, and on the Maturity Date. During the Construction Period, interest shall be paid from the Interest Reserve Account in accordance with Article 7 until the Interest Reserve is exhausted; thereafter, and at all times during the Mini-Perm Period, interest shall be paid by the Borrower from its own funds.

## 3.8 Application of Payments.

All payments received shall be applied in the following order: (i) to the Lender's costs and expenses reimbursable under Section 18.2; (ii) to the Late Charge; (iii) to accrued and unpaid interest (including interest at the Default Rate); (iv) to fees; and (v) to principal. After acceleration, payments shall be applied in such order as the Lender determines in its discretion.

## 3.9 Late Charge.

If any amount (other than principal due at maturity or on acceleration) is not paid within five (5) days after its due date, the Borrower shall pay a late charge equal to five percent (5%) of the overdue amount (the "**Late Charge**"), which is agreed to be a reasonable estimate of the Lender's administrative costs and is in addition to interest at the Default Rate.

## 3.10 Computations; Determination of Rate.

All computations of interest, fees and other amounts shall be made by the Lender on the basis of a 360-day year and the actual number of days elapsed. Each determination by the Lender of an interest rate, a fee or an amount owing shall be conclusive and binding on the Borrower absent manifest error, and the Lender shall deliver to the Borrower, on request, a certificate setting out the computation.

## 3.11 Usury Savings.

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in any Loan Document, in no event shall the aggregate of all amounts that constitute interest under applicable law exceed the maximum lawful rate permitted under North Carolina law. If any amount in excess of that maximum is paid or charged, (i) it shall be applied to reduce principal, and (ii) if principal has been repaid in full, it shall be refunded to the Borrower. To the extent permitted by law, all amounts that could be characterised as interest shall be amortised, prorated, allocated and spread over the full stated term of the Loan so that the effective rate of interest does not exceed the lawful maximum.

## 3.12 Evidence of Indebtedness; Loss of Note.

The Lender's books and records shall constitute prima facie evidence of the principal amount of the Loan outstanding and of all amounts owing. If the Note is lost, mutilated or destroyed, the Borrower shall execute and deliver a replacement note on the Lender's customary terms (without any requirement for a bond or indemnity other than the Lender's standard form of indemnity).

The parties acknowledge that the Draw Schedule as delivered states the Retainage release amount as **$[3,275,000]**, which is $[200,000] less than ten percent (10%) of the guaranteed maximum price; the Draw Schedule is to be conformed to the Approved Budget and to this Section 5.9 [*see Drafting Note 7 and Schedule 4*].
