**Drafting Note 10 (Memo Issues A-3 and A-4).** Two problems arise from the Term Sheet's financial covenants. (a) **Borrower net worth is arithmetically unachievable.** Section 6.4 of the Term Sheet requires the Borrower to maintain net worth of $12,000,000, while Section 20 requires the Borrower to hold no assets other than the Property and Project assets and to incur no liabilities other than the Loan, the Mezzanine Loan and trade payables. (b) **The Term Sheet contains no guarantor net-worth covenant.** Section 15 of the Term Sheet treats a breach of "the minimum threshold set forth in Section 6.4" as an Event of Default, but Section 6.4 addresses the *Borrower's* net worth, not the Guarantors'. Section 11.1(g) cures the gap; the Term Sheet cross-reference should be conformed.
