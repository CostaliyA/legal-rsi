# Principal findings flagged to the client

- **The loan is not supported by the collateral's income.** On the file's own stabilized best case (NOI $622,772), DSCR is 0.55x on post-interest-only amortizing debt service ($1,124,239) and 0.72x on interest-only debt service ($862,875), against a 1.25x covenant; debt yield is 4.22%. The covenant fails at its first test as a matter of arithmetic.
- **The appraisal does not reconcile to itself.** Stated NOI implies capitalisation rates of 1.86% (as-is) and 2.52% (as-stabilized) against concluded values of $18.2M/$23.5M and a stated market range of 5.75%–6.50%.
- **Execution authority gap.** The Operating Agreement requires 75% Member approval for financing over $1,000,000; the Managing Member holds 72%, so GDG's signature alone does not bind the borrower.
- **Single-purpose entity provisions are absent** and must be added by an Operating Agreement Amendment signed by all three Members as a closing condition.
- **Anson Lane strip** was conveyed by quitclaim deed; insured on the face of the commitment but with the title officer reserving additional premium/endorsement limits — affirmative coverage must be negotiated.
- **SCDHEC land use restriction** on Lot 7 (no residential use; vapor barrier maintenance and inspection) is implemented as bespoke covenants, draw conditions and an event of default.
- **Tenant estoppels/SNDAs** are closing conditions, but the Lowcountry Provisions lease contains no estoppel, SNDA or assignment provision, so the condition depends on tenant cooperation.
- **Sources and uses do not balance**: the $12.5M initial advance plus $6.2M equity equals purchase price plus renovation budget exactly, leaving closing costs and the $150,000 TI/LC reserve unfunded.
- **The draw schedule ignores the 10% retainage** required by the Term Sheet and overruns the budget by $5,000.
- **Yield maintenance** was drafted to the Term Sheet's "greater of" structure (1% floor plus the Treasury-spread differential), departing from the formulation in the markup notes, which omits the floor; the 50 basis point treatment remains for client confirmation.
