**Note on dates.** Every period in the instrument is measured from the Closing Date: the Maturity Date (April 15, 2030), the end of the Interest-Only Period (April 15, 2027), the Yield Maintenance Period (through April 15, 2029), the Lockout Period (through April 15, 2027) and the Substantial Completion Deadline (June 15, 2026) are all defined by reference to the Closing Date and then stated as specific dates. If closing is delayed (the Term Sheet permits closing through May 15, 2025), each stated date must be conformed. The Term Sheet itself defines the Maturity Date as April 15, 2030 "based on the anticipated closing date," which does not address the consequence of delay; the Draft Mortgage defines the Maturity Date as the fifth anniversary of the Closing Date and states the expected date, which resolves the ambiguity in the lender's favor.
