ARTICLE 2 — THE LOAN, THE NOTE AND PAYMENT TERMS
2.1 The Note; Principal Amount. Borrower has executed and delivered the Note
in the original principal amount of Fourteen Million Seven Hundred Fifty Thousand
and No/100 Dollars ($14,750,000.00), representing the Initial Advance and the
Renovation Holdback. The outstanding principal balance of the Note, together with
all accrued and unpaid interest and all other Secured Obligations, shall be due and
payable on the Maturity Date.

2.2 Interest Rate. Interest shall accrue on the outstanding principal balance of
the Loan at a floating rate per annum equal to Daily SOFR plus two hundred


                                          Page 9 of 65
           Mortgage and Security Agreement — CHB / Palmetto Gateway Partners LLC — DRAFT 03/27/2025



eighty-five (285) basis points (2.85%), provided that, for purposes of determining
the applicable rate, Daily SOFR shall not be less than the SOFR Floor. Interest
shall be computed on the basis of the actual number of days elapsed over a year of
three hundred sixty (360) days.

2.3 Payments. Accrued interest on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan
shall be due and payable monthly in arrears on the fifteenth (15th) day of each
calendar month (each, a “Payment Date”), commencing on May 15, 2025, with
the final payment due on the Maturity Date. All payments shall be made in lawful
money of the United States, in immediately available funds, to Lender at its
address for payment set forth in Section 17.6 or to such other account as Lender
may designate in writing, without setoff, deduction, counterclaim or withholding of
any kind.

2.4 Interest-Only Period; Amortization. From the Closing Date through and
including April 15, 2027 (the “Interest-Only Period”), each monthly payment
shall consist solely of accrued interest on the outstanding principal balance of the
Loan. Commencing with the Payment Date occurring on May 15, 2027, and
continuing on each Payment Date thereafter through and including the Maturity
Date, each monthly payment shall consist of principal and interest in the amount
necessary to fully amortize the then outstanding principal balance of the Loan over
a twenty-five (25) year amortization schedule at the interest rate then in effect
(after giving effect to the SOFR Floor), with the entire remaining unpaid principal
balance, all accrued and unpaid interest and all other Secured Obligations due and
payable on the Maturity Date. Borrower acknowledges that the Loan is a balloon
loan and that the scheduled monthly payments after the Interest-Only Period will
not fully amortize the Loan over its term.

2.5 Default Rate. Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of
Default, the outstanding principal balance of the Loan and, to the extent permitted
by applicable law, all other Secured Obligations, shall bear interest at the Default
Rate, accruing from the date of the Event of Default until such Event of Default is
cured or the Loan is repaid in full, whichever first occurs. The application of the
Default Rate is not a waiver of any Event of Default nor a waiver of Lender’s right
to accelerate the Loan, and interest at the Default Rate shall be a Secured
Obligation.

2.6 Late Charge. If any monthly installment of principal and/or interest is not
received by Lender within ten (10) calendar days after the applicable Payment
Date, Borrower shall pay to Lender, on demand, a late charge equal to five percent
(5%) of the overdue installment. Borrower acknowledges that the late charge is
intended to compensate Lender for the additional administrative expense of
processing late payments and is not a penalty, and that the late charge is payable
whether or not an Event of Default has occurred and whether or not Lender has
given notice. The late charge is in addition to, and not in lieu of, interest at the
Default Rate.

2.7 Maximum Lawful Rate; Savings Clause. Notwithstanding anything to the
contrary in this Mortgage or the other Loan Documents, in no event shall the total
