
# ARTICLE 8 — RENOVATION AND CONSTRUCTION

**8.1 The Renovation Holdback.** Lender shall hold the Renovation Holdback and shall disburse the same to Borrower from time to time in accordance with this Article 8 for the purpose of paying Renovation Costs (as defined in Section 8.2). Lender shall have no obligation to disburse any portion of the Renovation Holdback unless and until each applicable condition in Section 8.5 has been satisfied. The Renovation Holdback is a portion of the Loan and bears interest from the Closing Date regardless of whether it has been disbursed, in accordance with the Note.

**8.2 Renovation Budget; Renovation Costs; Reallocations.** "**Renovation Costs**" means all hard and soft costs of completing the Renovation in accordance with the Plans, the GC Contract and the Renovation Budget, including the cost of construction, labor, materials, tenant improvement allowances, architects', engineers' and consultants' fees, permit and inspection fees, construction management fees, builder's risk and other insurance attributable to the Renovation, and the contingency set forth in the Renovation Budget. Borrower shall cause the Renovation to be completed in accordance with the Renovation Budget attached as **Exhibit D**. Borrower may reallocate amounts among line items of the Renovation Budget without Lender's consent, provided that (a) no reallocation increases the total Renovation Budget, (b) no reallocation increases the amount allocated to any line item by more than ten percent (10%) of such line item, (c) no reallocation decreases the contingent or soft-cost line items below the amounts shown on **Exhibit D**, and (d) Borrower delivers to Lender written notice of the reallocation within ten (10) business days. Any reallocation not satisfying the foregoing shall require Lender's prior written consent.

**8.3 Borrower Equity; Cost Overruns.** Borrower shall fund, from its own funds and not from Loan proceeds, the portion of the Renovation Costs not funded by the Renovation Holdback, being not less than Nine Hundred Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($900,000) (the "**Renovation Equity**"), together with all costs of the Renovation in excess of the Renovation Budget and all costs of completing the Renovation not funded by the Renovation Holdback. Borrower shall not be entitled to any reimbursement of the Renovation Equity from Loan proceeds. If at any time Lender reasonably determines that the undisbursed portion of the Renovation Holdback is insufficient to pay the remaining Renovation Costs, Borrower shall, within fifteen (15) days after Lender's written demand, deposit with Lender an amount equal to the deficiency, which amount shall be disbursed by Lender in accordance with this Article 8. Borrower shall fund its share of Renovation Costs such that at no time shall the aggregate amount disbursed from the Renovation Holdback exceed seventy-one and forty-three hundredths percent (71.43%) of the aggregate Renovation Costs incurred and certified to Lender as of such date (being the ratio of the Renovation Holdback, $2,250,000, to the Renovation Budget, $3,150,000).

**8.4 Draw Request Procedure.**

(a) Borrower may request a disbursement from the Renovation Holdback no more frequently than once per calendar month, by delivering to Lender a written draw request not less than ten (10) Business Days prior to the requested disbursement date.

(b) Each draw request shall be substantially in the form attached as **Exhibit E**, shall be signed by Borrower and certified by Borrower's architect of record, and shall include: (i) an AIA G702/G703 Application and Certificate for Payment detailing the work completed, the amounts previously paid, the amount currently requested and the percentage of completion; (ii) a schedule of the line items of the Renovation Budget to which the requested disbursement is to be applied and the remaining balance of each such line item; (iii) conditional lien waivers for the current draw and unconditional lien waivers for all prior draws from the General Contractor and each subcontractor performing work in an amount exceeding Twenty-Five Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($25,000.00); (iv) a title date-down endorsement from the title insurer confirming that no intervening mechanics', materialmen's or other liens or encumbrances have been recorded against the Property since the date of this Mortgage or the most recent date-down endorsement, as applicable; (v) evidence of Lender's satisfactory inspection of the work in progress in accordance with Section 8.9; (vi) invoices or other evidence of the costs for which reimbursement is requested, to the extent reasonably requested by Lender; (vii) a certification that Borrower has funded its portion of Renovation Costs in accordance with Section 8.3; and (viii) such other information as Lender may reasonably request.

(c) Lender shall, within ten (10) Business Days after receipt of a complete and conforming draw request and satisfaction of the conditions in Section 8.5, disburse the requested amount, less the retainage required by Section 8.6, directly to Borrower or, at Lender's election, to the General Contractor, subcontractors, materialmen or other Persons entitled to payment, by joint check or otherwise.

**8.5 Conditions to Each Disbursement.** Lender's obligation to disburse any portion of the Renovation Holdback is subject to satisfaction of each of the following conditions, each in form and substance satisfactory to Lender:

(a) no Event of Default or event that with notice or lapse of time, or both, would constitute an Event of Default shall have occurred and be continuing;

(b) all representations and warranties in Article 5 shall be true and correct in all material respects;

(c) the Renovation shall be proceeding in accordance with the Plans and the Renovation Budget, and there shall exist no material defect, deficiency or departure from the Plans;

(d) each condition set forth in Section 8.4 shall have been satisfied and all documents required thereunder shall have been delivered to Lender;

(e) Borrower shall have funded the Renovation Equity in accordance with Section 8.3;

(f) no mechanics' or materialmen's lien shall have been filed against the Property and remain undischarged or unbonded, and the title insurer shall have issued the date-down endorsement required by Section 8.4(b)(iv);

(g) all building permits, Board of Architectural Review approvals (to the extent required for the work in question) and other governmental approvals required for the work shall have been obtained;

(h) if the work in question involves any penetration of, alteration to, or work within ten (10) feet of the ground-floor slab of Bay 1 on Lot 7, Borrower shall have delivered to Lender (i) evidence of written notice to SCDHEC as required by the LUR, (ii) a vapor barrier protection and restoration plan prepared by a licensed environmental professional reasonably acceptable to Lender, and (iii) a certification from such professional that the vapor barrier has been inspected and, following the work, restored in accordance with the LUR; and

(i) Lender shall have received evidence that the undisbursed Renovation Holdback, together with Borrower's remaining unfunded Renovation Equity commitment, is sufficient to complete the Renovation in accordance with the Renovation Budget.

**8.6 Retainage.** Lender shall retain ten percent (10%) of each disbursement from the Renovation Holdback (the "**Retainage**") until Substantial Completion has been achieved in accordance with Section 8.11. Retainage shall be released in accordance with Section 8.15.

**8.7 Plans and Specifications; Permits; Board of Architectural Review.** The Renovation shall be performed in accordance with the Plans approved by Lender. Borrower shall not amend, modify or supplement the Plans in any manner that constitutes a Major Change (as defined in Section 8.8) without Lender's prior written consent. Borrower shall obtain and maintain in full force and effect all permits, licenses and approvals necessary for the Renovation, including any approval of the City of Charleston Board of Architectural Review applicable to the façade restoration and to the decorative iron balcony described in Section 5.14, and shall deliver copies thereof to Lender upon request.

**8.8 GC Contract; Change Orders.** Borrower has delivered to Lender a complete copy of the GC Contract, including all schedules, exhibits and amendments. Borrower shall not amend, modify, supplement, terminate or accept the assignment of the GC Contract, and shall not approve or enter into any change order, amendment or supplemental agreement under the GC Contract, without Lender's prior written consent if such action (a) increases the contract sum of the GC Contract or the Renovation Budget, (b) reduces the scope of the Renovation, (c) affects the structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, elevator or façade systems, (d) affects the ground-floor slab of Bay 1 on Lot 7 or the vapor barrier beneath such slab, or (e) would reasonably be expected to delay Substantial Completion beyond the Substantial Completion Deadline (each, a "**Major Change**"). Borrower hereby assigns to Lender, as part of the Collateral, all of Borrower's right, title and interest in, to and under the GC Contract and the Plans, and shall cause the General Contractor and Borrower's architect to execute and deliver to Lender a consent to such assignment and collateral assignment in form reasonably satisfactory to Lender.

**8.9 Inspections.** Lender, and any third-party inspector retained by Lender (at Borrower's expense), may inspect the Property and the work in progress at any reasonable time upon reasonable prior notice, and Borrower shall provide access and such information as Lender or its inspector may request. Borrower shall cause the work to be corrected promptly if any inspection discloses defective work or a departure from the Plans. Borrower acknowledges that no inspection by Lender shall constitute a waiver of any default, an approval of any work, or an assumption of responsibility for the adequacy, legality or quality of the Renovation, and that Lender has no duty to inspect.

**8.10 Mechanic's Liens; Lien Waivers; Title Date-Downs.** Borrower shall pay when due all costs of the Renovation and shall keep the Property free from mechanics', materialmen's and other liens. If any such lien is filed, Borrower shall, within thirty (30) days after notice thereof, cause the lien to be discharged of record or bonded over in a manner reasonably satisfactory to Lender and the title insurer. Borrower shall obtain unconditional lien waivers from the General Contractor and all subcontractors as a condition to each disbursement, and shall obtain a final title date-down endorsement and final lien waivers as a condition to the release of Retainage. Borrower shall not permit any stop notice, notice of nonpayment or similar claim to remain undischarged in a manner that could impair the Property or Lender's lien.

**8.11 Substantial Completion; Substantial Completion Deadline.** Borrower shall achieve Substantial Completion of the Renovation on or before June 15, 2026, time being of the essence. Borrower shall notify Lender in writing not less than fifteen (15) days prior to the anticipated date of Substantial Completion, and shall deliver to Lender (a) a certificate of Substantial Completion from Borrower's architect, (b) a copy of the certificate of occupancy (or equivalent) issued by the City of Charleston for the renovated portions of the Property, (c) a certification that the Renovation has been completed in accordance with the Plans and within the Renovation Budget, and (d) evidence that all costs of the Renovation have been paid or will be paid from the final disbursement. Failure to achieve Substantial Completion on or before the Substantial Completion Deadline shall constitute an Event of Default in accordance with Section 14.1(h) and shall entitle Lender to exercise its rights under the Completion Guaranty.

**8.12 Failure to Complete; Deemed Completion.** If Borrower fails to achieve Substantial Completion by the Substantial Completion Deadline, then, in addition to Lender's other remedies, Lender may (but shall not be obligated to) assume responsibility for completing the Renovation, and all amounts expended by Lender in doing so, together with interest at the Default Rate, shall be Secured Obligations. Lender's exercise of any right under this Section 8.12 shall not release Borrower or Guarantor from any obligation under the Loan Documents or the Completion Guaranty.

**8.13 Coordination with the LUR and Vapor Barrier.** Borrower shall cause the General Contractor, all subcontractors, Borrower's architect and all other Persons performing work at the Property to be informed in writing of the existence and location of the vapor barrier beneath the ground-floor slab of Bay 1 on Lot 7, of the restrictions imposed by the LUR, and of the requirements of Section 11.3, prior to the commencement of any work at the Property. Borrower shall not permit any penetration, removal, alteration or compromise of the vapor barrier, and shall not permit any work affecting the Bay 1 slab, without (a) prior written notice to SCDHEC in accordance with the LUR, (b) Lender's prior written consent, and (c) supervision and post-work inspection and certification by a licensed environmental professional reasonably acceptable to Lender, all at Borrower's expense.

**8.14 No Obligation to Complete; No Lender Liability.** Lender shall have no obligation to complete the Renovation, to advance any amount in excess of the Renovation Holdback, or to pay any cost of the Renovation in excess of the amounts available in the Renovation Holdback and Borrower's Renovation Equity. Lender's approval of the Plans, the Renovation Budget, the GC Contract or any draw request, and any inspection by Lender or its consultant, shall not constitute a representation or warranty by Lender as to the adequacy, sufficiency, quality, legality or feasibility of the Renovation or the Plans, and Lender shall have no liability to Borrower or any other Person with respect thereto. Borrower acknowledges that Borrower, and not Lender, is the owner of the Property and the party responsible for the Renovation.

**8.15 Retainage Release; Final Disbursement.** Upon Lender's receipt of satisfactory evidence of Substantial Completion, final unconditional lien waivers from the General Contractor and all subcontractors and materialmen, a final title date-down endorsement, the certificate of occupancy, and confirmation that the vapor barrier and all LUR requirements have been satisfied, Lender shall release the Retainage and make the final disbursement from the Renovation Holdback. Lender may withhold the Retainage or any portion thereof until completion of all punch list items reasonably acceptable to Lender.

**8.16 Completion Guaranty.** The obligations of Borrower under this Article 8 are guaranteed by Guarantor pursuant to the Completion Guaranty, which guarantees completion of the Renovation within the Renovation Budget and on or before the Substantial Completion Deadline. Borrower's failure to achieve Substantial Completion by the Substantial Completion Deadline shall constitute a failure under the Completion Guaranty and an Event of Default under this Mortgage.

**8.17 No Waiver.** Acceptance by Borrower of any disbursement, or Lender's approval of any draw request, inspection report or plan, shall not constitute a waiver of any condition, covenant or Event of Default, or an approval of any work not conforming to the Plans.

# ARTICLE 9 — INSURANCE

**9.1 Property Insurance.** Borrower shall maintain at all times during the term of the Loan, at its sole expense, comprehensive all-risk (special form) property insurance covering the Property and all improvements, fixtures and betterments thereon, on a replacement cost basis without deduction for depreciation, with an agreed amount endorsement (waiver of coinsurance) and, if the Property is in a special flood hazard area, flood coverage as required by Section 9.3. Such insurance shall include coverage for fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, explosion, riot and civil commotion, vandalism, malicious mischief, sprinkler leakage, water damage, and such other perils as Lender may reasonably require, and shall include ordinance or law coverage, debris removal, and business income/rental value coverage in an amount not less than twelve (12) months of gross rents and operating expenses.

**9.2 Commercial General Liability.** Borrower shall maintain commercial general liability insurance written on an occurrence basis, with minimum limits of Two Million and No/100 Dollars ($2,000,000.00) per occurrence and Five Million and No/100 Dollars ($5,000,000.00) in the aggregate, including coverage for premises liability, operations liability, products and completed operations, personal and advertising injury, and contractual liability (including the liability assumed under this Mortgage), naming Lender as an additional insured. Borrower shall also maintain automobile liability and employers' liability insurance in amounts reasonably satisfactory to Lender.

**9.3 Flood Insurance.** Borrower shall maintain flood insurance with respect to the improvements on the Property in an amount not less than Five Million and No/100 Dollars ($5,000,000.00), naming Lender as mortgagee and loss payee. Borrower acknowledges that the Property is located in FEMA Flood Zone X (an area of minimal flood hazard), that flood insurance is not required by federal regulation for properties in Zone X, and that Lender nevertheless requires such flood insurance as a condition of the Loan. Borrower shall deliver to Lender evidence of the flood zone determination and shall comply with all applicable requirements of the Flood Disaster Protection Act of 1973, as amended, and the regulations thereunder.

**9.4 Builder's Risk Insurance.** During the period from the Closing Date through Substantial Completion, Borrower shall maintain builder's risk insurance written on a completed value, all-risk basis in an amount not less than Three Million One Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($3,150,000.00), covering the Property and the Renovation against all risks of physical loss or damage during the course of construction, including coverage for materials in transit and stored off-site, and naming Lender as mortgagee and loss payee. Such coverage may be provided as part of the General Contractor's policy, provided that Lender's interests are protected by an appropriate endorsement and Lender approves the form.

**9.5 Other Insurance.** Borrower shall maintain (a) workers' compensation insurance in the amounts required by the laws of the State of South Carolina, covering all of Borrower's employees and, to the extent not covered by the General Contractor's or a subcontractor's policy, all persons engaged in the Renovation; (b) umbrella/excess liability insurance with limits of not less than Five Million and No/100 Dollars ($5,000,000.00) per occurrence and in the aggregate, providing excess coverage over commercial general liability, automobile liability and employers' liability; and (c) such other insurance, in such amounts and covering such risks, as Lender may reasonably require from time to time.

**9.6 Carrier Ratings; Policy Terms.** All insurance required under this Article 9 shall be issued by insurers licensed or authorized to do business in the State of South Carolina, having an A.M. Best rating of not less than A-:VII, and shall be written on forms reasonably acceptable to Lender. Each policy shall provide that it may not be canceled, non-renewed or materially modified without not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Lender (ten (10) days' notice for nonpayment of premium), and shall contain a waiver of subrogation against Lender to the extent available and a mortgagee/loss payee clause in favor of Lender (or, for liability policies, an additional insured endorsement) on forms acceptable to Lender.

**9.7 Evidence of Insurance.** Borrower shall deliver to Lender, at or prior to the Closing Date and thereafter at least ten (10) days prior to each policy renewal, certificates of insurance and, upon Lender's request, copies of policies and endorsements, naming Lender as mortgagee, loss payee and/or additional insured as applicable. If Lender does not receive evidence of renewal coverage at least ten (10) days prior to expiration, Lender may, but shall not be obligated to, procure such coverage and the cost thereof shall be a Secured Obligation.

**9.8 Application of Proceeds.** All insurance proceeds shall be held and applied in accordance with Article 15 and Section 14.7. Borrower shall not settle or compromise any claim under any policy in excess of Two Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($250,000.00) without Lender's prior written consent. Lender shall be entitled to participate in any adjustment of any material claim.

**9.9 Lender's Right to Insure.** If Borrower fails to obtain or maintain any insurance required by this Article 9, Lender may (but shall not be obligated to) obtain such insurance and pay the premiums therefor, and the amount so paid, together with interest at the Default Rate, shall be a Secured Obligation payable on demand and secured by this Mortgage.

**9.10 No Representation as to Adequacy.** Lender's approval of any insurance policy, carrier or limit shall not constitute a representation that such insurance is adequate to protect Borrower's or Lender's interests, and Lender shall have no liability for the adequacy or sufficiency of any coverage.

# ARTICLE 10 — RESERVES AND ESCROWS

**10.1 Tax and Insurance Escrow.** Borrower shall make monthly deposits with Lender, on each Payment Date, in an amount equal to one-twelfth (1/12) of the estimated annual Impositions and casualty and liability insurance premiums for the Property (for all three tax parcels described on **Exhibit A**), as reasonably determined by Lender, and shall deposit with Lender at the Closing Date any positive escrow balance or amount reasonably required by Lender to establish the escrow (the "**Tax and Insurance Escrow**"). Lender shall apply the Tax and Insurance Escrow toward the payment of Impositions and insurance premiums as they become due and payable. Lender may adjust the monthly escrow amount periodically to reflect actual or anticipated tax assessments and insurance premiums and shall give Borrower notice of any such adjustment; Borrower shall pay the adjusted amount commencing with the first Payment Date occurring after such notice. If the amount on deposit is insufficient to pay any Imposition or premium when due, Borrower shall pay the deficiency to Lender within fifteen (15) days after demand. Lender shall have no obligation to ascertain whether any Imposition has been paid, and shall have no liability for any penalty or interest arising out of the payment obligations of Borrower.

**10.2 Capital Replacement Reserve.** Borrower shall deposit with Lender, on each Payment Date, the amount of Six Hundred Forty-Five and 83/100 Dollars ($645.83), being Twenty-Five Cents ($0.25) per square foot per year multiplied by 31,000 square feet of gross building area, divided by twelve (12) months (the "**Capital Replacement Reserve**"). Funds in the Capital Replacement Reserve shall be disbursed to Borrower upon Lender's approval of qualifying capital expenditures, which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed and shall be subject to Lender's reasonable review and approval procedures, including receipt of invoices, paid receipts and lien waivers for the completed work.

**10.3 Tenant Improvement and Leasing Commission Reserve.** Borrower shall deposit with Lender at the Closing Date the sum of One Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($150,000.00) into a tenant improvement and leasing commission reserve account (the "**TI/LC Reserve**"). Disbursements from the TI/LC Reserve shall be subject to Lender's prior written approval, shall be made upon Lender's receipt of documentation reasonably acceptable to Lender, and shall be used exclusively for tenant improvement costs and leasing commissions incurred in connection with the leasing of vacant space at the Property. Upon any draw-down from the TI/LC Reserve, Borrower shall, within thirty (30) days after Lender's written demand, replenish the TI/LC Reserve to the full amount of One Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($150,000.00) from Borrower's own funds.

**10.4 Renovation Holdback.** The Renovation Holdback shall be held and disbursed in accordance with Article 8 and is not subject to this Article 10, except that Lender shall account for the Renovation Holdback in the same manner as the other reserves and escrows for record-keeping purposes.

**10.5 General Reserve Provisions.** All reserves and escrows shall be held by Lender in accounts maintained by Lender or by a depository institution acceptable to Lender, and may be commingled with other funds of Lender (including funds held for other borrowers). No interest shall be payable to Borrower on any reserve or escrow except as required by applicable law, and any interest actually earned shall be credited to the applicable account and applied as provided in this Article 10. All reserves and escrows shall be Collateral for the Secured Obligations and, at Lender's election upon an Event of Default, may be applied to the Secured Obligations in such order as Lender determines. Lender shall have no fiduciary obligation to Borrower with respect to any reserve or escrow, and any relationship created hereby is that of debtor and creditor. Lender may require additional reserves as a condition to any consent, waiver or amendment requested by Borrower.

**10.6 Failure to Deposit.** If Borrower fails to make any deposit required by this Article 10, Lender may, but shall not be obligated to, fund such deposit and the amount so funded shall be a Secured Obligation bearing interest at the Default Rate; provided that a failure to make any deposit required by this Article 10 that continues for five (5) Business Days after written notice from Lender shall constitute an Event of Default.

**10.7 Return of Reserves.** Upon the payment and performance in full of all Secured Obligations and the termination of Lender's commitment to make advances, Lender shall return to Borrower any balance remaining in the reserves and escrows, less any amounts applied or required to be applied to the Secured Obligations.

**10.8 Application to Secured Obligations.** At Lender's election upon the occurrence of an Event of Default, Lender may apply any or all amounts held in the reserves and escrows to the Secured Obligations, whether or not then due.

# ARTICLE 11 — ENVIRONMENTAL MATTERS AND THE LAND USE RESTRICTION

**11.1 Environmental Covenants.** Borrower shall, at its sole cost and expense: (a) comply, and cause all tenants, occupants, contractors and agents at the Property to comply, with all Environmental Laws and with the LUR; (b) not cause or permit any release of Hazardous Materials at, on, under or from the Property that requires reporting, investigation, remediation or the imposition of any institutional control under any Environmental Law; (c) not permit any Person to conduct any activity at the Property that would reasonably be expected to result in the reopening of SCDHEC Site ID SC-29403-0772 or the impairment of the No Further Action determination dated August 12, 2021; (d) maintain all institutional controls required by the LUR in full force and effect and in good working order; and (e) promptly deliver to Lender copies of all notices, orders, correspondence and reports received from or delivered to SCDHEC or any other environmental authority relating to the Property.

**11.2 Compliance with the Land Use Restriction.** Without limiting Section 11.1, Borrower shall (a) comply with, and cause all tenants and occupants of the Property to comply with, each covenant, condition and restriction contained in the LUR; (b) not use, and not permit any tenant or occupant to use, the ground floor of Lot 7 (Tax Map No. 460-12-04-007, comprising Bay 1 at 520 King Street) for residential purposes, overnight occupancy or any use prohibited by the LUR; (c) maintain, repair and keep in good condition and working order the vapor barrier system installed beneath the concrete floor slab of Bay 1 on Lot 7; (d) not penetrate, remove, alter, compromise or permit the penetration, removal, alteration or compromise of such vapor barrier without prior written approval of SCDHEC and Lender's prior written consent; (e) cause any renovation, construction, demolition or alteration activity affecting the floor slab of Bay 1 to include inspection and, if necessary, repair or replacement of the vapor barrier, with prior written notification to SCDHEC; (f) cause periodic inspections of the vapor barrier to be conducted by a licensed environmental professional not less frequently than annually during the term of the Loan and at any other time required by the LUR or requested by SCDHEC; (g) deliver to Lender, within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar year, an officer's certificate substantially in the form attached as **Exhibit F** certifying compliance with the LUR and attaching the most recent inspection report; (h) not modify, amend, terminate, release or seek the modification, amendment, termination or release of the LUR without Lender's prior written consent; and (i) not permit the Property or any portion thereof to be used in a manner that would cause SCDHEC to reopen SCDHEC Site ID SC-29403-0772.

**11.3 Notice Prior to Work Affecting Bay 1.** Borrower shall notify Lender and SCDHEC in writing not less than fifteen (15) days prior to the commencement of any work that will penetrate, disturb or come within ten (10) feet of the ground-floor slab of Bay 1 on Lot 7, and shall furnish Lender with the vapor barrier protection plan, the identity and qualifications of the environmental professional engaged to supervise the work, and the post-work certification described in Section 8.5(h).

**11.4 Environmental Indemnity; Recourse.** Borrower and Guarantor have executed and delivered the Environmental Indemnity Agreement, which indemnifies Lender against all losses, liabilities, damages, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from or relating to the presence, release or threatened release of Hazardous Materials at, on, under or from the Property, and from any violation of the LUR or any Environmental Law. The obligations under the Environmental Indemnity Agreement are full recourse obligations of Borrower and Guarantor, are not limited by Section 16.2 or by the value of the Collateral, and shall survive the repayment of the Loan, foreclosure, any conveyance in lieu of foreclosure, and the release of this Mortgage.

**11.5 Lender's Right to Cure Environmental Matters.** If Borrower fails to comply with this Article 11, or if Lender reasonably determines that immediate action is necessary to protect human health, the environment or the Collateral, Lender may, after such notice to Borrower as is reasonable under the circumstances (or without notice in an emergency), take such action as Lender deems necessary, including retaining environmental consultants, conducting investigations, implementing or maintaining institutional controls, communicating with SCDHEC, and performing remediation, and all costs and expenses so incurred, together with interest at the Default Rate, shall be Secured Obligations.

**11.6 No Reliance; No Representation by Lender.** Lender's acceptance of the Phase I ESA and any other environmental report shall not constitute a representation, warranty or acknowledgment by Lender as to the environmental condition of the Property, and Borrower acknowledges that Borrower has conducted its own investigation of the environmental condition of the Property and is not relying on Lender with respect thereto.

**11.7 Survival.** The obligations of Borrower under this Article 11 shall survive the repayment of the Loan, foreclosure, any conveyance in lieu of foreclosure and the release of this Mortgage, to the extent of any liability arising from conditions existing or acts occurring prior to such release.

**11.8 Event of Default.** Without limiting Section 14.1, the failure of Borrower to comply with Section 11.1 or Section 11.2, or the occurrence of any violation, modification or termination of the LUR, shall constitute an Event of Default (subject only to the cure period provided in Section 14.2(b), where applicable).
