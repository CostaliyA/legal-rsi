
# ARTICLE 12 — FINANCIAL COVENANTS AND REPORTING

**12.1 Minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio.**

(a) Borrower shall maintain a DSCR of not less than 1.25 to 1.00 (the "**DSCR Covenant**"), tested as of the last day of each calendar quarter, commencing with the calendar quarter ending June 30, 2027 (being the first full calendar quarter ending after the twenty-fourth (24th) month following the Closing Date), and continuing quarterly thereafter. (b) Borrower shall deliver to Lender, concurrently with each quarterly test, a compliance certificate setting forth in reasonable detail the calculation of Net Operating Income, Annual Debt Service and DSCR, together with supporting operating statements and rent rolls.

(c) A failure to maintain the DSCR Covenant as of the end of any two (2) consecutive calendar quarters shall constitute an Event of Default in accordance with Section 14.1(i), subject to Borrower's right to cure in accordance with Section 12.6.

(d) Lender and Borrower acknowledge that the DSCR Covenant is measured against annual debt service calculated on a twenty-five (25) year amortization schedule and that the DSCR Covenant is not measured during the Interest-Only Period.

**12.2 Net Worth and Liquidity Covenants.**

# ARTICLE 13 — PREPAYMENT

**13.1 Lockout Period.** The Loan may not be prepaid, in whole or in part, during the Lockout Period. No voluntary prepayment of principal shall be permitted during the Lockout Period. Any tender of payment in violation of this Section 13.1 is prohibited, and Lender may refuse to accept such tender. If Lender elects, in its sole discretion, to accept any such tender, the Yield Maintenance Premium shall be payable as though the prepayment had been made on the first day of the Yield Maintenance Period, in addition to all other amounts payable under the Loan Documents, and such acceptance shall not constitute a waiver of any Event of Default.

**13.2 Yield Maintenance Period.** From the expiration of the Lockout Period through the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, the Loan may be prepaid in whole, but not in part, upon not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Lender, and upon simultaneous payment of a yield maintenance premium (the "**Yield Maintenance Premium**") equal to the greater of:

(a) one percent (1.00%) of the outstanding principal balance of the Loan as of the date of prepayment; and

(b) the present value, discounted at the Treasury Rate, of the excess, if any, of (i) the interest that would have accrued on the principal amount being prepaid at the interest rate in effect under the Note on the date of prepayment (after giving effect to the SOFR Floor) for the period from the date of prepayment through the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, over (ii) the interest that would accrue on such principal amount at a rate per annum equal to the Treasury Rate plus fifty (50) basis points (0.50%) for such period.

The present value described in clause (b) shall be calculated using monthly discounting, with each month's interest differential discounted from the last day of the applicable month to the date of prepayment, and shall in no event be less than zero. If the date of prepayment is not the first day of a month, the interest differential for the month in which the prepayment occurs shall be prorated on the basis of the actual number of days remaining in such month. The Yield Maintenance Premium shall be determined by Lender in good faith, and Lender's written calculation, absent manifest error, shall be conclusive and binding on Borrower.

**13.3 Open Prepayment Period.** During the final twelve (12) months of the term of the Loan (being from and after April 15, 2029 through the Maturity Date), the Loan may be prepaid in whole or in part, at any time and from time to time, without payment of any prepayment premium, penalty or yield maintenance amount, upon not less than fifteen (15) days' prior written notice to Lender.

**13.4 Notice; Conditions.** Each notice of prepayment shall be irrevocable and shall specify the proposed prepayment date and amount. No prepayment shall be effective unless and until (a) all accrued and unpaid interest, all late charges, all prepayment premiums or yield maintenance amounts due and all other Secured Obligations owing on the prepayment date are paid in full, and (b) Lender has received the notice required by this Article 13. Any partial prepayment permitted under Section 13.3 shall be applied to the outstanding principal balance in inverse order of maturity and shall not reduce or postpone any scheduled payment of principal or interest, and the regularly scheduled payments shall continue in effect.

**13.5 Involuntary Prepayments.** Any prepayment of the Loan resulting from the application of insurance proceeds or condemnation awards under Article 15, or from Lender's application of any reserve, escrow or other Collateral, shall be applied at par without any prepayment premium, penalty or yield maintenance amount.

**13.6 Acceleration Following an Event of Default.** The acceleration of the maturity of the Loan following an Event of Default is not a voluntary prepayment; however, the Yield Maintenance Premium that would have been payable under Section 13.2 had Borrower prepaid the Loan in full on the date of acceleration shall be due and payable as part of the Secured Obligations, together with interest at the Default Rate and all other amounts owing under the Loan Documents.

**13.7 No Other Prepayments.** Except as expressly provided in this Article 13 and in Article 15, Borrower shall not prepay the Loan in whole or in part, and no prepayment shall be made from the proceeds of any subordinate financing.
