Any bona fide third-party offer to lease Bay 2 must first be offered to Harmon & Wren Design Studio LLC on the same material terms, with a fifteen (15) business day acceptance period. The ROFR survives renewal of the Bay 3 lease. If the Bay 3 lease is not renewed, the ROFR terminates.

3. **Renewal options.** Lowcountry Provisions Inc.: one five-year renewal at fair market value with a floor equal to the then-current rent (nine months' notice; notice deadline March 31, 2027). Harmon & Wren Design Studio LLC: one three-year renewal at 95% of fair market value (six months' notice; notice deadline December 31, 2025). Tidemark Consulting Group LLC: two three-year renewals at fair market value (twelve months' notice).

4. **Tenant early termination right (Harmon & Wren).** The Bay 3 lease contained an early termination right effective June 30, 2025 upon payment of three months' rent, exercisable only upon six months' prior written notice. The notice deadline of December 31, 2024 has passed without exercise, and the termination right has therefore lapsed.

5. **Assignment and subletting.** Lowcountry Provisions Inc.: no provision — the lease is silent on assignment and subletting and contains no consent requirement. Harmon & Wren Design Studio LLC: assignment requires Landlord's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld; subletting of up to 50% of the premises permitted with 30 days' notice. Tidemark Consulting Group LLC: assignment requires Landlord's prior written consent, which may be withheld in Landlord's sole discretion; subletting with consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

6. **Estoppel and SNDA obligations.** Lowcountry Provisions Inc.: lease contains no estoppel certificate obligation and no subordination, non-disturbance or attornment provisions. Harmon & Wren Design Studio LLC: estoppel certificate within 15 business days of request; lease subordinate to any mortgage provided a commercially reasonable non-disturbance agreement is delivered to tenant. Tidemark Consulting Group LLC: estoppel certificate within 10 business days of request; lease subordinate to any mortgage and tenant agrees to execute an SNDA upon request.
