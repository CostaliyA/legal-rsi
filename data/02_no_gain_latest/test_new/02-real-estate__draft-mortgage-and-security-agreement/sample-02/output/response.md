## What was done

**Source inventory and traceability.** All eight source documents were read in full: executed Term Sheet, title commitment, Phase I ESA executive summary, redacted operating agreement summary, rent roll and lease abstracts, renovation budget and draw schedule, appraisal summary, and the March 18, 2025 markup notes. Appendix 1 of the memorandum is a 35-entry source-to-issue log mapping each material proposition to its source and to the provision of the mortgage that implements it.

**Independent recomputation.** Every figure was recomputed rather than adopted (40-row verification schedule, Part V of the memorandum). Findings that changed the deliverable:

- **DSCR covenant is unachievable at any occupancy.** Recomputed 25-year debt service is $1,124,239/yr (the workbook shows $1,122,000). Against the borrower's own best-case NOI of $622,772, DSCR is 0.554x — not 1.25x. Achieving 1.25x requires NOI of $1,405,299 ($45.33/SF) versus market rents of $19–$28/SF; even at 100% occupancy with zero operating expenses DSCR would be 0.672x.
- **Appraisal does not reconcile internally.** Its own NOI capitalized at its own selected cap rates yields ~$5.42M (as-is) and ~$10.03M (stabilized), not $18.2M and $23.5M (implied cap rates 1.86% and 2.50%). The loan is sized at 81.04% LTV on that value.
- **Sources and uses leave at least $352,075 unfunded** at closing ($147,500 origination fee + $54,575 SC recording fee + $150,000 TI/LC reserve), before title, survey, legal and appraisal costs.
- **Occupancy is 56.13%, not 68%**; the appraisal's 21,400 SF and $19.40/SF weighted rent both derive from that error (correct figure $23.86/SF). Escalated in-place rent at closing is ~$422,966, not $415,100.
- **Draw schedule exceeds budget by $5,000** and ignores the 10% retainage (90% ceiling $2,025,000 against $2,235,000 of scheduled draws by month 13).

**Drafting.** The mortgage contains purpose-built provisions for the SCDHEC land use restriction (Article 12: residential-use prohibition, vapor barrier maintenance and inspection, SCDHEC notification, Event of Default, springing carve-out, draw conditions for Bay 1 slab work), an absolute and present assignment of leases and rents with the collateral-assignment vocabulary removed (Article 3), a single-purpose entity/separateness/independent-manager regime binding on all three Members (Article 14 and 23), a deterministic yield maintenance formula per the Term Sheet (with the 1.00% floor, and step-by-step computation methodology), judicial foreclosure with no power of sale (Article 17), non-recourse carve-outs (Article 18), obligatory future advances for the renovation holdback (§2.5), and title/encroachment/Anson Lane provisions (§§4.4–4.5) with exhibits drawn from the record.

**Judgment preserved rather than papered over.** The single placeholder is the surveyed metes and bounds for Exhibit A, expressly marked and flagged (no description was invented). Diligence gaps are disclosed in the instrument (limit of Borrower's environmental representation to disclosed matters; no representation that the Phase II waiver confirms condition) and escalated in the memorandum (18 source conflicts in a table; 32 open items with owners).

## Headline issues flagged for the client

Priority register in Part I; detailed analysis in Part IV (49 issues, A-1 to K-2). The three that bear on whether the loan should close as structured: the DSCR structure, the appraisal's internal inconsistency, and the closing-date funding shortfall. Also material: the Lowcountry Provisions lease has no assignment, estoppel or SNDA clause although estoppels and SNDAs are conditions precedent; execution of the loan is itself a 75% "Major Decision" under the Operating Agreement that GDG's 72% cannot approve alone; the Term Sheet's "transfers among existing members" carve-out could permit a change of control of the 72% managing member; the interest-shortfall guaranty expires twelve months before the first DSCR test; and the Anson Lane strip is insured but was conveyed by quitclaim only.
