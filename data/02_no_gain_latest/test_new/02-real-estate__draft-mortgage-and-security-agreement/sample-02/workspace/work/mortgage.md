## ARTICLE 3 — ABSOLUTE ASSIGNMENT OF LEASES AND RENTS

### 3.1 Absolute and Present Assignment

For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Borrower hereby unconditionally, absolutely and presently grants, transfers and assigns to Lender all of Borrower's right, title and interest in, to and under all Leases, and all Rents, issues, profits and other sums now or hereafter payable thereunder or in connection with the use or occupancy of the Property, together with all rights and remedies of the landlord under the Leases, all security deposits and other tenant deposits, all guaranties and other credit support for tenant obligations, and all claims and causes of action arising under or in connection with the Leases, in each case without reservation of any kind. This assignment is an absolute and present assignment and not a collateral assignment given as additional security only. Borrower and Lender intend that this Article 3 constitute an absolute assignment of Rents within the meaning of the Uniform Assignment of Rents Act as enacted in the State of South Carolina (S.C. Code Ann. § 29-3-100 *et seq.*), and that Lender's right to receive the Rents be perfected upon the recording of this Mortgage in the RMC Office. The assignment set forth in this Article 3 is not a substitute for, and does not limit, the lien of this Mortgage on the Leases and Rents; Lender's rights under this Article 3 are in addition to its rights under the other provisions of this Mortgage and applicable law.

### 3.2 License to Collect Prior to Default

Notwithstanding the absolute and present nature of the assignment set forth in Section 3.1, Lender grants to Borrower a revocable license to collect, receive, use and apply the Rents in the ordinary course of the operation of the Property, subject to the terms of the Loan Documents, prior to the occurrence of an Event of Default. Such license shall be automatically revoked, without notice, upon the occurrence of an Event of Default and the giving of the notice described in Section 3.3, and may be revoked by Lender at any time upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default. Borrower shall hold the Rents, or a portion thereof sufficient to discharge all current Obligations, in trust for the benefit of Lender as its interests may appear, and shall apply the Rents first to the payment of operating expenses of the Property and the Obligations as required by the Loan Documents. Borrower's failure to apply Rents as required by this Section 3.2 shall constitute a misapplication of Rents for purposes of Section 18.2(b).

### 3.3 Enforcement; Notice to Tenants

At any time after the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, Lender may, in its sole discretion and without further notice to Borrower (except as expressly required by this Section 3.3 or by applicable law), (a) notify any or all tenants under the Leases, in writing, that the Leases and Rents have been absolutely assigned to Lender and that the Rents are to be paid directly to Lender or as Lender directs, (b) demand, collect, sue for and receive the Rents directly, and (c) enforce the Leases and exercise all rights and remedies of the landlord thereunder. Borrower irrevocably authorizes and directs each tenant under the Leases to pay Rents directly to Lender upon receipt of written notice from Lender, and Borrower agrees that any tenant making such payment shall be fully protected and discharged from any further obligation to Borrower with respect to the amounts so paid. Lender's enforcement of the assignment of Rents shall not require the appointment of a receiver, the entry of any court order or the taking of any other action, provided that Lender may apply for the appointment of a receiver in accordance with Section 17.2.

### 3.4 Security Deposits

Borrower shall maintain the security deposits under the Leases in accordance with the Leases and applicable law and shall not apply any security deposit except as expressly permitted by the applicable Lease. On the Closing Date and at any time thereafter upon Lender's request, Borrower shall deliver to Lender a schedule of all security deposits held under the Leases, and, at Lender's election following the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, shall deposit or transfer all security deposits to an account controlled by Lender or as Lender directs.

### 5.8 Prepayment — Yield Maintenance Period

During the Yield Maintenance Period, Borrower may prepay the Loan in whole (but not in part) at any time, upon not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Lender, which notice shall be irrevocable, provided that Borrower pays to Lender, simultaneously with such prepayment, (a) the entire outstanding principal balance of the Loan, (b) all accrued and unpaid interest through the date of prepayment, (c) all other amounts due under the Loan Documents, and (d) the Yield Maintenance Premium. The "**Yield Maintenance Premium**" shall be the greater of:

(i) one percent (1.00%) of the outstanding principal balance of the Loan as of the date of prepayment; and

(ii) the present value of the excess, if any, of (A) the interest that would have accrued on the amount prepaid at the Note Rate during the period from the date of prepayment through the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, over (B) the interest that would have accrued on such amount during such period at a rate per annum equal to the Treasury Rate plus fifty (50) basis points, discounted to present value at the Treasury Rate as of the date of prepayment.

In no event shall the Yield Maintenance Premium be less than the amount determined under clause (i) above. No partial prepayment of the Loan shall be permitted during the Yield Maintenance Period.

### 5.9 Computation of Yield Maintenance Premium

The Yield Maintenance Premium shall be determined in accordance with the following methodology, which the parties intend to be mathematically determinative and objectively calculable as of the prepayment date:

(a) The amount prepaid shall be allocated to the scheduled monthly payment periods remaining from the prepayment date through the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period (the last such period ending on April 15, 2029). The number of full and partial monthly periods remaining shall be determined by counting, on an actual-day basis, the period from the prepayment date to April 15, 2029.

(b) "**Treasury Rate**" means the yield to maturity, determined as of the second (2nd) Business Day preceding the prepayment date, of the United States Treasury security having a maturity date closest to, but not exceeding, the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, as reported in Federal Reserve Statistical Release H.15 — Selected Interest Rates (or any successor publication) under the caption "Treasury Constant Maturities." If no published maturity date exactly matches the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, the Treasury Rate shall be determined by linear interpolation between the yields reported for the two published maturities nearest to such date, applied on an actual-day basis. If the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period precedes the shortest published maturity, the shortest published maturity shall be used.

(c) The interest differential for each remaining monthly period shall equal the product of (i) the amount prepaid, (ii) the difference (expressed as a per annum rate, and not less than zero) between the Note Rate and the sum of the Treasury Rate plus fifty (50) basis points, and (iii) a fraction, the numerator of which is the number of days in such monthly period and the denominator of which is three hundred sixty (360).

(d) The present value of each such interest differential shall be computed by discounting such amount from the last day of the applicable monthly period to the prepayment date using a discount rate equal to the Treasury Rate, applied on an actual/360 basis with monthly compounding.

(e) If Federal Reserve Statistical Release H.15 ceases to be published, the Treasury Rate shall be determined by reference to the successor publication or source designated by the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System or, absent such designation, by reference to the yield on the applicable United States Treasury security as reported by a nationally recognized financial information service selected by Lender in good faith.

(f) Lender shall deliver to Borrower, within five (5) Business Days after receipt of Borrower's prepayment notice, a statement setting forth in reasonable detail Lender's computation of the Yield Maintenance Premium. Absent manifest error, Lender's computation shall be conclusive and binding on Borrower.
