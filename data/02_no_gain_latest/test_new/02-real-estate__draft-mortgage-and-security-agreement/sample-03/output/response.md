## Principal findings in the memorandum

Three findings are material and are put to the Bank for decision rather than resolved by drafting:

1. **The 1.25x DSCR covenant cannot be met.** Recomputed from the Bank's own rent roll and pro forma: stabilized best-case NOI $622,772 against annual debt service of $1,124,082 (0.55x) and interest at the floor rate of $862,875 (0.72x). Achieving 1.25x would require NOI of $1,078,594 (interest-only) or $1,405,103 (amortizing). The covenant as drafted from Term Sheet §7.1 is a scheduled default at the first test date.
2. **The appraisal's income approach does not support its conclusions.** The reported NOI implies capitalization rates of approximately 1.87% (as-is) and 2.50% (as-stabilized) against the stated 6.25% and 5.85%, so the 62.77% as-stabilized loan-to-value ratio is unsupported.
3. **Sources and uses do not balance.** Total project cost of $20,950,000 equals loan plus equity exactly, leaving no source for $147,500 of origination fee, $54,575 of documentary stamps, the $150,000 TI/LC reserve, or title, survey, appraisal, environmental and legal costs — $352,075 quantified.

Also flagged: the payment guaranty covering 14 months against a 24-month interest-only period (a ten-month unguaranteed gap); the absence of any independent manager, separateness covenant or bankruptcy consent in the operating agreement; the absence of any indirect-transfer restriction in either the Term Sheet or the operating agreement; the Lowcountry lease having no assignment or consent provision; the quitclaim Anson Lane parcel insured without affirmative coverage; the survey sitting exactly at the 90-day recertification limit; two conflicting FEMA panel citations; and a $5,000 overrun in the draw schedule.

## Departures from the markup notes (recorded, not silently made)

- **Yield maintenance:** prior counsel's proposed formula omitted the Term Sheet's 1% floor. The draft restores the "greater of" structure required by Term Sheet §4.2 and relocates the 50 basis points to the Treasury leg as the Term Sheet does. Both choices are flagged for the Bank, since they change the premium.
- **Net worth/liquidity:** imposed on Borrower (Term Sheet §7.2) rather than Guarantor (markup notes ¶6), because a $1,000,000 test on a $28,000,000 guarantor is not a credit test.
- **DSCR test date:** adopted the Term Sheet's quarter-ending basis (first Test Period ending June 30, 2027, certified within 45 days) rather than the April 15, 2027 date used in the rent roll and markup notes.
- **Assignment of rents:** incorporated in the Mortgage by election under Term Sheet §5.2 rather than executed separately, to satisfy Title Requirement 5 and reduce recording cost.

Fifteen provisions that are additions or tightenings relative to the Term Sheet are listed in Part IV of the memorandum for the Bank's written approval; each is drafted to be deletable.
