**THIS MORTGAGE AND SECURITY AGREEMENT** (this "**Mortgage**") is made and entered into as of the 15th day of April, 2025 (the "**Closing Date**"), by and between **PALMETTO GATEWAY PARTNERS LLC**, a South Carolina limited liability company, having its principal office at 88 Broad Street, Suite 200, Charleston, South Carolina 29401, South Carolina Secretary of State Filing No. 2024-0011287, Federal Employer Identification No. 84-6213750 (together with its successors and permitted assigns, "**Borrower**"), and **CALVERLEY HERITAGE BANK**, a Georgia state-chartered commercial bank, insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, having its principal office at 412 Whitaker Street, Savannah, Georgia 31401, Federal Employer Identification No. 58-2947136 (together with its successors and assigns, "**Lender**").

**RECITALS**

**A.** Lender has agreed to make a loan to Borrower in the original principal amount of Fourteen Million Seven Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($14,750,000.00) (the "**Loan**"), the proceeds of which will be used to finance, in part, Borrower's acquisition and renovation of the improved real property commonly known as 520–534 King Street, Charleston, South Carolina 29403, as more particularly described on **Exhibit A** attached hereto and incorporated herein (the "**Land**").

**B.** The Loan is evidenced by that certain Promissory Note of even date herewith made by Borrower payable to the order of Lender in the original principal amount of the Loan (the "**Note**"), and is further evidenced, secured and guaranteed by the Loan Documents described in **Section 1.1**.

**C.** The Loan is being made on the terms and conditions set forth in that certain Term Sheet — Commercial Real Estate Loan dated April 1, 2025, executed by Lender and Borrower and acknowledged by Neil Ashford Graydon, relating to Deal Reference No. CHB CRE Loan No. 2025-CHS-0047 (the "**Term Sheet**"). The Loan is structured in two components: (i) the Initial Advance of Twelve Million Five Hundred Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($12,500,000.00), funded at closing; and (ii) the Renovation Holdback of Two Million Two Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($2,250,000.00), retained by Lender and disbursed to Borrower from time to time on a draw basis in accordance with **Article XI** for costs of the renovation of the Improvements.

**D.** The Loan is structured as a non-recourse obligation of Borrower, subject to the limited recourse and carve-out exceptions set forth in the Note and in the Guaranty, and is guaranteed by Neil Ashford Graydon, an individual residing at 14 Church Street, Charleston, South Carolina 29401 ("**Guarantor**"), pursuant to the Guaranty.

**E.** As a condition to making the Loan, Lender requires that the Loan be secured by, among other things, a first-priority mortgage lien on the Land and the Improvements, a first-priority security interest in the personal property, fixtures and other collateral described herein, and an absolute and present assignment of all leases, rents, issues and profits of the Property, in each case as more particularly set forth below.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the Loan, the sum of Ten and No/100 Dollars ($10.00) and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Borrower hereby mortgages, grants, bargains, sells, conveys, assigns, transfers, pledges, hypothecates and sets over to Lender, and grants to Lender a continuing general lien upon and security interest in, all of Borrower's right, title and interest, whether now owned or hereafter acquired, in and to the following property, rights and interests (collectively, the "**Mortgaged Property**"), to have and to hold the same unto Lender, its successors and assigns, forever, as security for the payment and performance of the Obligations:

**(a) The Real Property.** The Land, together with all and singular the tenements, hereditaments, easements, rights-of-way, appurtenances, privileges, royalties, reversions, remainders and improvements now or hereafter situated thereon or appertaining thereto, including without limitation all three (3) tax parcels comprising the Land, identified as Charleston County Tax Map Nos. **460-12-04-007**, **460-12-04-008** and **460-12-04-009**, and including that certain strip of land formerly known as Anson Lane, vacated by City of Charleston Ordinance No. 2019-084 and conveyed to Borrower's predecessor in title by quitclaim deed recorded in Deed Book 1128 at Page 443 in the RMC Office (collectively with the Land, the "**Real Property**");

**(b) The Improvements and Fixtures.** All buildings, structures, improvements, fixtures and building systems now or hereafter located on, affixed to or used in connection with the Real Property, including without limitation the mixed-use commercial building comprising approximately 31,000 square feet of gross building area, and all HVAC equipment and systems, the passenger elevator, plumbing, electrical systems, fire protection and life-safety systems, lighting, roofing, windows, the decorative iron balcony on the King Street elevation, and all other machinery, apparatus, equipment, fittings and articles of fixtures, together with all replacements, additions, accessions, substitutions and proceeds thereof (the "**Improvements**");

**(c) Tangible Personal Property.** All furniture, furnishings, trade fixtures, equipment, machinery, tools, supplies, inventory, building materials, construction materials, plans, specifications, drawings and other tangible personal property of every kind and description now or hereafter owned by Borrower and used or useful in connection with the ownership, operation, renovation, leasing, maintenance or repair of the Real Property;

**(d) Intangible Personal Property.** All accounts, accounts receivable, general intangibles, chattel paper, instruments, documents, payment intangibles, software, deposit accounts, letter-of-credit rights, investment property, commercial tort claims, permits, licenses, approvals, entitlements, development rights, density rights, zoning rights, governmental authorizations, trade names, trademarks, service marks, goodwill, warranties, guaranties, indemnities and causes of action relating to the Real Property or the Improvements or to the construction, renovation, operation, maintenance, leasing or use thereof, including without limitation all of Borrower's right, title and interest in, to and under the GC Contract and all warranties and guaranties thereunder;

**(e) Leases, Rents and Profits.** All Leases, subleases, licenses and occupancy agreements now or hereafter affecting the Real Property or any part thereof, together with all rents, security deposits, minimum rents, additional rents, percentage rents, common area maintenance charges, tax and insurance reimbursements, escalation payments, parking revenues, proceeds of rent-loss insurance, and all other issues, profits, revenues, income, receipts and sums of every kind now or hereafter payable under or on account of the use or occupancy of the Real Property, and all rights and remedies of Borrower under the Leases, all as more particularly set forth in **Article IV**;

**(f) Accounts, Reserves and Proceeds.** All Reserve Accounts, escrows, deposits, trust funds, reserve funds and other accounts now or hereafter maintained with Lender (or with any other Person for the benefit of Lender) in connection with the Loan, all sums on deposit therein, and all proceeds of the conversion, voluntary or involuntary, of any of the foregoing into cash or liquidated claims, including without limitation all insurance proceeds (including premium refunds and unearned premiums), condemnation awards, purchase prices, damages, awards and settlements arising out of any damage to, destruction of, taking of, or conveyance of the Real Property or any part thereof, and all rights of Borrower to receive the same (which proceeds Borrower hereby absolutely assigns to Lender); and

**(g) All Accessions and Proceeds.** All additions, accessions, substitutions, replacements, renewals, products, offspring, profits, rents, issues, and all cash and noncash proceeds (as defined in the South Carolina Uniform Commercial Code, S.C. Code Ann. § 36-9-101 *et seq.* (the "**UCC**")) of any and all of the foregoing.

**TO HAVE AND TO HOLD** the Mortgaged Property unto Lender, its successors and assigns, forever, for the purposes and on the terms and conditions set forth in this Mortgage.
