## ARTICLE IX — NEGATIVE COVENANTS

### Section 9.1 No Subordinate Financing

Borrower shall not incur, create, assume, guarantee, permit to exist or suffer any lien, mortgage, deed of trust, security interest, pledge, hypothecation, encumbrance or other charge upon the Property or any portion thereof or any interest therein, or any indebtedness secured thereby, or any mezzanine loan, preferred equity interest, participating interest or other financing arrangement that may affect Lender's collateral position, without Lender's prior written consent in each instance. Borrower shall not permit any financing statement naming Borrower as debtor to be on file in any filing office covering any portion of the Mortgaged Property, other than those filed by or for the benefit of Lender.

### Section 9.2 Transfer Restrictions

(a) **Property Transfers.** Borrower shall not sell, convey, transfer, assign, exchange, lease (other than Leases permitted under **Section 4.8**), option, pledge, encumber, partition or otherwise dispose of the Property or any portion thereof or any interest therein, whether voluntarily, involuntarily or by operation of law, without Lender's prior written consent.

(b) **Direct Transfers of Membership Interests.** Borrower shall not permit any Transfer of any direct membership interest in Borrower, and no Member shall Transfer any membership interest in Borrower, if such Transfer, individually or together with all other Transfers occurring during the term of the Loan, would result in (i) any Person or group of Persons acquiring more than ten percent (10%) of the membership interests in Borrower in any single transaction or series of related transactions, or (ii) Transfers aggregating more than twenty-five percent (25%) of the membership interests in Borrower, in each case without Lender's prior written consent. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Transfers among the existing Members (that is, among Graydon Development Group LLC, Tidewater Opportunity Fund II LP and Harborline Capital Advisors LLC) shall not require Lender's consent, provided that (A) no such Transfer results in a change in Control of Borrower, (B) the transferee is an existing Member, (C) Borrower gives Lender at least ten (10) Business Days' prior written notice, and (D) Graydon Development Group LLC continues to hold not less than fifty-one percent (51%) of the membership interests in Borrower and to serve as Managing Member.

(c) **Indirect Transfers and Change of Control.** Borrower acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section are intended to prevent any change in the identity or Control of the Persons who ultimately own and control Borrower, and not merely changes in the record ownership of membership interests. Accordingly, and without limiting clauses (a) and (b), the following shall each constitute a Transfer requiring Lender's prior written consent: (i) any Transfer of any direct or indirect ownership interest in any Member, or in any Person that directly or indirectly Controls any Member, including any transfer of the membership interest in Graydon Development Group LLC held by Guarantor; (ii) any merger, consolidation, conversion, division, reorganization or dissolution of any Member; (iii) any change in the general partner of Tidewater Opportunity Fund II LP, or any transfer by Tidewater Opportunity Fund II LP of its membership interest in Borrower to any "successor fund" or other Person pursuant to Section 4.2(c) of the Operating Agreement; (iv) any change in the manager, managing member or Control of Harborline Capital Advisors LLC; and (v) the admission of any new Member. Notwithstanding any provision of the Operating Agreement (including any provision designating a transfer as a "Permitted Transfer"), no Transfer of a membership interest in Borrower shall be effective for purposes of the Loan Documents unless made in compliance with this Section.

(d) **Permitted Estate Planning Transfers.** Notwithstanding clauses (b) and (c), Guarantor may transfer his membership interest in Graydon Development Group LLC to a revocable trust established by Guarantor for estate planning purposes, and may transfer such interest upon his death to his estate or heirs, provided that (i) Guarantor (or his estate or heirs) retains voting and management control of Graydon Development Group LLC or its successor, (ii) Graydon Development Group LLC continues to hold 72% of the membership interests in Borrower and to serve as Managing Member, (iii) Borrower gives Lender at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice (or, in the case of a transfer upon death, notice within thirty (30) days thereafter), and (iv) no Event of Default has occurred and is continuing.

(e) **Consequences.** Any Transfer in violation of this Section shall be void ab initio, shall constitute an immediate Event of Default without notice or opportunity to cure in accordance with **Section 14.3(c)**, and shall entitle Lender to exercise all remedies available under **Article XV** and applicable law, including acceleration, imposition of the Default Rate and foreclosure.

### Section 9.3 Restrictions on Organizational Documents and SPE Provisions

Borrower shall not amend, restate, modify, waive, repeal or terminate any provision of its Operating Agreement or certificate of formation relating to its single-purpose status, the Independent Manager, the separateness covenants, the restriction on bankruptcy filings, dissolution, merger or consolidation, the admission of Members, or the management of Borrower, without Lender's prior written consent in each instance. Borrower shall deliver to Lender a copy of any permitted amendment within five (5) Business Days after its execution.

### Section 9.4 Change of Use; Alterations; Demolition

Borrower shall not (a) change the use of the Property from mixed-use retail and office use, (b) use or permit the use of the ground floor of Lot 7 (Bay 1) for residential purposes or for any use prohibited by the LUR, (c) demolish, raze, structurally alter or materially modify the Improvements other than in connection with the Renovation in accordance with the approved plans and **Article XII**, or (d) permit any use of the Property that is not in compliance with applicable zoning, the LUR, or any recorded covenant, condition or restriction affecting the Property.

### Section 9.5 No Additional Indebtedness

Borrower shall not incur, assume, guarantee or permit to exist any indebtedness for borrowed money, or any capitalized lease obligation, other than (a) the Obligations, (b) trade payables and equipment leases incurred in the ordinary course of business and not exceeding $100,000 in the aggregate outstanding at any time, and (c) as otherwise approved in writing by Lender.

### Section 9.6 No Distributions in Default

Borrower shall not make any distribution, dividend, return of capital or other payment to any Member if an Event of Default has occurred and is continuing, or if such distribution would cause Borrower to fail to satisfy the net worth or liquidity covenants set forth in **Section 11.2**.

### Section 9.7 No Merger, Dissolution or Change of Business Structure

Borrower shall not merge or consolidate with, or acquire all or substantially all of the assets or equity of, any other Person; shall not divide or convert into another form of entity; and shall not dissolve, liquidate or wind up its affairs, in each case without Lender's prior written consent and, where applicable, the consent of the Independent Manager and the unanimous consent of the Members.

### Section 9.8 No Consent to Liens on Leases or Rents

Borrower shall not further assign, pledge or encumber the Leases or the Rents, or grant any Person other than Lender any interest therein, or direct or permit any Tenant to pay Rents to any Person other than Borrower (prior to an Event of Default) or Lender (after an Event of Default).

## ARTICLE X — INSURANCE

### Section 10.1 Property Insurance

Borrower shall maintain at all times during the term of the Loan comprehensive all-risk (special form) property insurance covering the Property and all Improvements, on a replacement cost basis, with an agreed amount endorsement (waiver of coinsurance), without deduction for depreciation, and with ordinance or law coverage (including coverage for increased cost of construction and demolition), covering fire, windstorm, hail, explosion, riot, civil commotion, vandalism, malicious mischief, sprinkler leakage, water damage, and such other perils as Lender may reasonably require. The policy shall name Lender as mortgagee and loss payee pursuant to a standard mortgagee clause in form satisfactory to Lender, shall provide that Lender's interest is not impaired by any act or neglect of Borrower, and shall contain a deductible not exceeding $50,000 without Lender's prior written consent.

### Section 10.2 Commercial General Liability

Borrower shall maintain commercial general liability insurance on an occurrence basis, with limits of not less than Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000) per occurrence and Five Million Dollars ($5,000,000) in the aggregate, covering premises liability, operations liability, products and completed operations, personal and advertising injury, and contractual liability (including the indemnities in the Loan Documents). Lender shall be named as an additional insured, and the policy shall be primary and non-contributory with any insurance maintained by Lender.

### Section 10.3 Flood Insurance

Borrower shall maintain flood insurance covering the Property in an amount not less than Five Million Dollars ($5,000,000), naming Lender as mortgagee and loss payee. Borrower acknowledges that the Property is located in FEMA Flood Zone X (area of minimal flood hazard) and that federal law does not require flood insurance for property in Zone X, and that the flood insurance required by this Section is a contractual requirement of Lender and not a federally mandated requirement. To the extent the coverage required by this Section exceeds the maximum limit of coverage available for commercial property under the National Flood Insurance Program (currently $500,000 per building), Borrower shall obtain the excess coverage from a private flood insurer acceptable to Lender, and shall maintain the maximum available NFIP coverage (or such lesser amount as Lender may approve) together with such excess coverage. Borrower shall deliver to Lender evidence of the FEMA flood zone determination and any letter of map amendment upon request.

### Section 10.4 Builder's Risk Insurance

From the Closing Date through Substantial Completion of the Renovation, Borrower shall maintain builder's risk insurance on a completed-value, all-risk (special form) basis in an amount not less than the full Renovation Budget of Three Million One Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($3,150,000.00), covering the Improvements and the Renovation against all risks of physical loss or damage during the course of construction, including coverage for materials stored at the site and in transit, scaffolding (including the façade scaffolding), and soft costs and delayed-completion coverage reasonably acceptable to Lender. Lender shall be named as mortgagee and loss payee on such policy, and such coverage shall include the interest of the General Contractor and all subcontractors as their interests may appear.

### Section 10.5 Other Insurance

Borrower shall maintain: (a) workers' compensation insurance in the amounts required by the laws of the State of South Carolina, covering all employees of Borrower and, to the extent not covered under the General Contractor's policies, all Persons engaged in the Renovation; (b) employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per accident; (c) umbrella or excess liability insurance with limits of not less than Five Million Dollars ($5,000,000), providing excess coverage over the commercial general liability, automobile liability and employer's liability policies; (d) automobile liability insurance covering all owned, hired and non-owned vehicles used in connection with the Property or the Renovation, with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per accident; and (e) such other insurance coverages, in such amounts and with such carriers, as Lender may reasonably require from time to time, including, if Lender reasonably determines that the Property is located in or adjacent to a special flood hazard area after any map revision, such additional flood coverage as Lender may require.

### Section 10.6 Carrier Ratings; Policy Requirements

All insurance policies required under this **Article X** shall be issued by insurers licensed or authorized to do business in the State of South Carolina and having an A.M. Best rating of not less than "A-:VII," and shall be in form and substance reasonably acceptable to Lender. Each policy shall contain a provision requiring not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Lender of cancellation, non-renewal, material modification or reduction in coverage (and not less than ten (10) days' prior written notice of cancellation for nonpayment of premium), shall not be cancellable without such notice, and, in the case of property insurance, shall contain a standard mortgagee clause. Borrower shall deliver to Lender certificates of insurance and, upon request, certified copies of the policies, prior to the Closing Date and thereafter not less than fifteen (15) days prior to each expiration, together with evidence of payment of premiums. Borrower shall not permit any policy to be cancelled, reduced or allowed to lapse.

### Section 10.7 Application of Insurance Proceeds

Lender shall have the right, at its election, to apply any insurance proceeds received by Lender to the restoration of the Improvements or to the payment of the Obligations (in such order of application as Lender may determine), provided that Lender shall make proceeds available to Borrower for the restoration of the Improvements if (a) no Event of Default has occurred and is continuing, (b) the loss is fully covered by insurance (other than the deductible), (c) Lender reasonably determines that the Improvements can be restored to their pre-casualty condition, utility and value within the earlier of twelve (12) months after the casualty or the Substantial Completion Deadline, and (d) Borrower demonstrates to Lender's reasonable satisfaction that sufficient funds (including insurance proceeds, the remaining Renovation Holdback and Borrower's own funds) are available to complete the restoration. Any proceeds made available for restoration shall be disbursed in accordance with the draw procedures of **Section 12.4**, applied mutatis mutandis. Any proceeds not made available for restoration shall be applied to the Obligations in accordance with **Section 13.4** and without payment of any Prepayment Premium. Borrower irrevocably appoints Lender as its attorney-in-fact to endorse and collect any insurance proceeds payable in respect of the Property.

### Section 10.8 Condemnation

Borrower shall promptly deliver to Lender a copy of any notice of condemnation, eminent domain, taking or threatened taking affecting the Property. All condemnation awards and other compensation for any taking, whether permanent or temporary, are hereby assigned to Lender and shall be paid to Lender, and Borrower irrevocably appoints Lender as its attorney-in-fact to collect the same. Lender shall have the right, at its election, to apply any such award or compensation to the restoration of the Property (on the conditions set forth in **Section 10.7**, applied mutatis mutandis) or to the payment of the Obligations, in each case without payment of any Prepayment Premium. If the entire Property is taken, or if the taking would, in Lender's reasonable judgment, render the Property unsuitable for its current use, Lender may declare the Obligations immediately due and payable.

### Section 10.9 Failure to Maintain Insurance

If Borrower fails to maintain any insurance required by this **Article X**, Lender may, at its election, obtain such insurance at Borrower's expense (which amount shall be a Protective Advance), and Borrower shall reimburse Lender upon demand. Any such failure shall constitute an Event of Default in accordance with **Section 14.1(g)**.

## ARTICLE XI — FINANCIAL COVENANTS AND REPORTING

### Section 11.1 Debt Service Coverage Ratio

(a) Borrower shall maintain, as of the last day of each Test Period, a DSCR of not less than 1.25x.

(b) DSCR testing shall commence with the Test Period ending June 30, 2027, being the first full fiscal quarter ending after the twenty-fourth (24th) month following the Closing Date, and shall continue on a quarterly basis thereafter for each Test Period. Borrower shall deliver to Lender, within forty-five (45) days after the last day of each Test Period, a compliance certificate certified by Borrower and, if requested by Lender, verified by an independent certified public accountant acceptable to Lender, setting forth in reasonable detail Lender's and Borrower's calculation of Net Operating Income, Debt Service and DSCR for such Test Period.

(c) If the DSCR is less than 1.25x for any two (2) consecutive Test Periods, and such failure is not cured in accordance with **Section 14.3(d)**, an Event of Default shall exist in accordance with **Section 14.1(i)**.

(d) Borrower shall not, during any period in which the DSCR is less than 1.10x, make any distribution to any Member other than tax distributions required by the Operating Agreement and permitted by **Section 9.6**.

### Section 11.2 Net Worth and Liquidity

Borrower shall maintain at all times during the term of the Loan: (a) a net worth of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00); and (b) liquid assets (defined as cash, cash equivalents, marketable securities and unencumbered deposits held at federally insured depository institutions) of not less than Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000.00). Compliance shall be tested annually, concurrently with the delivery of the annual financial statements and the annual compliance certificate required by **Section 11.3**, and shall be evidenced by such certificate. For purposes of this Section, net worth shall be determined in accordance with GAAP, excluding from assets any value attributable to the Property in excess of its then-outstanding appraised value and excluding any asset that is not readily marketable or that is pledged to any other Person.

### Section 11.3 Financial Reporting

Borrower shall deliver, or cause to be delivered, to Lender:

(a) **Annual Financial Statements.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year of Borrower, audited financial statements of Borrower prepared in accordance with GAAP by an independent certified public accounting firm acceptable to Lender, together with an unqualified opinion and all notes and supporting schedules;

(b) **Guarantor Financial Information.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, the annual personal financial statement of Guarantor, in form and detail satisfactory to Lender, certified as true and correct by Guarantor, together with such supporting tax returns and verification as Lender may reasonably request;

(c) **Quarterly Rent Rolls.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, a rent roll for the Property, certified by Borrower as true, correct and complete, in the form of **Exhibit D** with such additional information as Lender may reasonably request (including lease expirations, security deposits and delinquencies);

(d) **Annual Property Operating Statements.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year of Borrower, annual operating statements for the Property, including a detailed income and expense report and a comparison of actual results to the approved annual budget, certified by Borrower;

(e) **Quarterly Operating Statements.** Within forty-five (45) days after the end of each calendar quarter, unaudited quarterly operating statements for the Property;

(f) **Leases.** Copies of all Leases, lease amendments, guaranties, side letters, termination agreements and estoppel certificates executed by Borrower with respect to the Property, within ten (10) Business Days after execution;

(g) **Compliance Certificate.** Concurrently with the delivery of the items described in clauses (a) and (c), a certificate of a manager of Borrower certifying compliance with **Sections 11.1** and **11.2** and stating whether any Event of Default or event that, with notice or lapse of time or both, would constitute an Event of Default has occurred;

(h) **Budgets.** Within thirty (30) days prior to the beginning of each fiscal year, the proposed annual operating budget for the Property for such fiscal year, and within thirty (30) days after Lender's request, any revised budget; and

(i) **Other Information.** Promptly upon Lender's request, such other financial and operating information regarding Borrower, Guarantor and the Property as Lender may reasonably require.

### Section 11.4 Accounting Standards; Audit Rights

All financial statements delivered under this **Article XI** shall be prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied, applied on a basis consistent with prior periods, except that interim statements may be subject to normal year-end adjustments and may lack footnotes. Lender and its representatives shall have the right, upon reasonable prior notice and at Borrower's expense not more than once per fiscal year (and at any time after an Event of Default), to audit and verify the books and records of Borrower relating to the Property and to discuss the affairs of Borrower with its officers, managers and accountants.

### Section 11.5 Failure to Deliver Reports

If Borrower fails to deliver any item required under this **Article XI** within the applicable period, Lender may deliver written notice thereof, and if such failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after such notice (or, in the case of audited financial statements, thirty (30) days after such notice), such failure shall constitute an Event of Default in accordance with **Section 14.1(b)**.

### Section 11.6 Lease-Up Reporting

During any period in which the Property is not at least ninety percent (90%) occupied, Borrower shall deliver to Lender, concurrently with each quarterly rent roll, a written leasing status report describing marketing efforts, prospective tenants, executed letters of intent, and the status of the right of first refusal with respect to Bay 2 held by Harmon & Wren Design Studio LLC.

## ARTICLE XII — ESCROWS, RESERVES AND RENOVATION DISBURSEMENTS

### Section 12.1 Tax and Insurance Escrow

(a) Borrower shall make monthly deposits with Lender, on each Payment Date, in an amount equal to one-twelfth (1/12) of the estimated annual real property taxes and casualty and liability insurance premiums for the Property, covering all three (3) tax parcels (Charleston County Tax Map Nos. 460-12-04-007, 460-12-04-008 and 460-12-04-009), plus one-twelfth (1/12) of any special assessments. Such deposits shall be held by Lender in escrow in an account designated as the "**Tax and Insurance Escrow Account**" and applied by Lender toward the payment of real property taxes and insurance premiums as they become due and payable.

(b) Lender shall have the right to adjust the monthly escrow amount periodically (and not less frequently than annually) based upon actual or anticipated tax assessments and insurance premiums, and Borrower shall pay any resulting deficiency within thirty (30) days after Lender's written demand.

(c) Upon an Event of Default, Lender may apply any sums in the Tax and Insurance Escrow Account to the Obligations in such order as Lender may determine.

### Section 12.2 Capital Replacement Reserve

(a) Borrower shall make monthly deposits with Lender, on each Payment Date, in the amount of Six Hundred Forty-Five and 83/100 Dollars ($645.83) into an account designated as the "**Capital Replacement Reserve Account**," calculated as $0.25 per square foot per year multiplied by 31,000 square feet of gross building area ($7,750.00 per year), divided by twelve (12) months.

(b) Funds in the Capital Replacement Reserve Account shall be disbursed to Borrower upon Lender's approval of qualifying capital expenditures (being non-recurring replacements and capital repairs of the Improvements, including building systems, roof, parking areas and structural components, but excluding tenant improvements and leasing commissions), subject to Lender's reasonable review and approval procedures and the delivery of such invoices, lien waivers and other documentation as Lender may reasonably require. Lender may require that any disbursement be made on a reimbursable basis.

(c) Lender may, at its election, apply any sums in the Capital Replacement Reserve Account to the Obligations upon an Event of Default or to any repair or replacement of the Property that Lender reasonably determines is necessary to protect its security.

### Section 12.3 Tenant Improvement and Leasing Commission Reserve

(a) Borrower shall deposit at closing the sum of One Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($150,000.00) into an account designated as the "**Tenant Improvement and Leasing Commission Reserve Account**."

(b) Upon any draw from such account, Borrower shall replenish the account to the full amount of $150,000.00 from Borrower's own funds within thirty (30) days after Lender's written demand.

(c) Disbursements from the account shall be subject to Lender's prior written approval and shall be used exclusively for tenant improvement costs and leasing commissions incurred in connection with the leasing of vacant space at the Property, and shall be supported by such invoices, executed leases, lien waivers and other documentation as Lender may reasonably require.

### Section 12.4 Renovation Holdback

(a) **Amount and Purpose.** The Renovation Holdback of Two Million Two Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($2,250,000.00) shall be held by Lender and disbursed to Borrower on a draw basis solely for payment of the costs of the Renovation in accordance with the Renovation Budget attached as **Exhibit C**.

(b) **Draw Frequency and Notice.** Draws shall be made no more frequently than once per calendar month. Borrower shall submit each draw request to Lender not less than ten (10) Business Days prior to the requested disbursement date.

(c) **Draw Request Requirements.** Each draw request shall include: (i) an AIA G702/G703 Application and Certificate for Payment, certified by Borrower's architect of record, detailing the work completed, amounts previously paid and the amount currently requested; (ii) conditional lien waivers from the General Contractor and from each subcontractor and supplier performing work or supplying materials in an amount exceeding Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($25,000) with respect to the current draw, and unconditional lien waivers from all such Persons with respect to all prior draws; (iii) a title date-down endorsement from the Title Insurer confirming that no intervening mechanic's, materialman's or other liens or encumbrances have been filed against the Property since the date of recording of this Mortgage or the most recent date-down endorsement; (iv) a certification by Borrower's architect that the work for which payment is requested has been completed in accordance with the approved plans and specifications; (v) evidence of Lender's satisfactory inspection of the work in progress, which inspection may be conducted by a third-party inspector retained by Lender at Borrower's expense; and (vi) such other documentation as Lender may reasonably require.

(d) **Retainage.** Lender shall retain ten percent (10%) retainage from each disbursement from the Renovation Holdback until Substantial Completion has been achieved in accordance with **Section 12.7**. Retainage shall be released upon Lender's receipt of (i) satisfactory evidence of Substantial Completion, (ii) final unconditional lien waivers from the General Contractor and all subcontractors and suppliers, (iii) a final title date-down endorsement, and (iv) an as-built survey if required by Lender.

(e) **Conditions to Each Disbursement.** Lender shall have no obligation to disburse any portion of the Renovation Holdback (i) if an Event of Default has occurred and is continuing, (ii) if any condition precedent to the Initial Advance under **Section 12.1** of the Term Sheet has ceased to be satisfied, (iii) if the cost of completing the remaining Renovation exceeds the sum of the undisbursed Renovation Holdback plus Borrower's committed and available equity, (iv) if Borrower has failed to fund its own equity contribution when required, or (v) if the work is not being performed in a good and workmanlike manner in accordance with the approved plans and specifications and applicable law.

(f) **Borrower's Funding Obligation.** Borrower shall be solely responsible for all Renovation costs in excess of the Renovation Holdback, including without limitation the portion of the GC Contract sum of $2,850,000.00 that exceeds the Renovation Holdback, all soft costs and contingency in excess of the amounts allocated to the Renovation Holdback in **Exhibit C**, and all cost overruns, change orders, design changes and code-compliance costs. Borrower's equity contribution for the Renovation is $900,000.00, as reflected on **Exhibit C**. Lender shall have no obligation to increase the Loan or the Renovation Holdback.

(g) **Change Orders and Budget Revisions.** No change order, amendment or modification to the GC Contract, and no revision to the Renovation Budget, that (i) individually or in the aggregate increases the total cost of the Renovation, (ii) reduces or reallocates any line item of the Renovation Budget by more than ten percent (10%), or (iii) involves any work affecting the floor slab of Bay 1 on Lot 7, shall be effective without Lender's prior written written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed as to change orders that do not increase the total cost of the Renovation and that are funded from the contingency line item. Borrower shall deliver copies of all executed change orders to Lender within five (5) Business Days after execution.

(h) **LUR Compliance During Renovation.** Borrower shall (i) cause the General Contractor and all subcontractors performing work in or near Bay 1 to acknowledge in writing the existence and location of the Vapor Barrier and the requirements of the LUR, (ii) notify SCDHEC in writing in advance of any work affecting the Bay 1 floor slab, and (iii) deliver copies of all such acknowledgments and notices to Lender.

(i) **Application of Retainage and Undisbursed Amounts.** Upon an Event of Default, Lender may retain all undisbursed amounts of the Renovation Holdback and, at its election, apply the same to the completion of the Renovation or to the Obligations. No undisbursed amount shall be deemed earned by Borrower.
