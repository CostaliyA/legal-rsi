## ARTICLE XIII — PREPAYMENT

### Section 13.1 General; Relationship to the Note

The provisions of this **Article XIII** restate the prepayment terms applicable to the Loan and shall be read together with the prepayment provisions of the Note. Borrower shall not prepay all or any portion of the principal balance of the Loan except as expressly permitted by this **Article XIII**, and no prepayment shall relieve Borrower of any obligation to pay interest, fees, late charges, costs or other amounts then due. In the event of any inconsistency between this **Article XIII** and the Note as to the amount or calculation of any Prepayment Premium, the Note shall control.

### Section 13.2 Lockout Period

(a) The Loan may not be prepaid, in whole or in part, at any time during the period commencing on the Closing Date and ending on the last day of the twenty-fourth (24th) month following the Closing Date (that is, through April 15, 2027) (the "**Lockout Period**"). During the Lockout Period, no voluntary prepayment of principal shall be permitted, and Borrower shall not be entitled to tender any such prepayment.

(b) Any purported prepayment made during the Lockout Period shall not be accepted by Lender and shall not discharge any Obligation, and any such tender shall constitute an immediate Event of Default. Without limiting the foregoing, and to the extent Lender elects in its sole discretion to accept any amount tendered during the Lockout Period, Borrower shall pay, in addition to all other amounts due, a prepayment premium equal to the Yield Maintenance Premium computed as if the prepayment date were the last day of the Lockout Period, plus one percent (1.00%) of the principal amount so accepted.

(c) Notwithstanding clause (a), involuntary prepayments resulting from the application of casualty insurance proceeds or condemnation awards in accordance with **Sections 10.7** and **10.8**, and prepayments resulting from Lender's acceleration of the Obligations following an Event of Default, may be applied to the Obligations during the Lockout Period without payment of any Prepayment Premium.

### Section 13.3 Yield Maintenance Period

(a) **Permitted Prepayment.** During the period commencing on the day immediately following the last day of the Lockout Period and ending on the last day of the forty-eighth (48th) month following the Closing Date (that is, from April 16, 2027 through April 15, 2029) (the "**Yield Maintenance Period**"), Borrower may prepay the Loan in whole (but not in part) upon (i) not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Lender (which notice shall be irrevocable), and (ii) payment, on the date of prepayment, of the Yield Maintenance Premium, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, all other amounts then due and all costs and expenses (including any breakage, hedging or funding costs) incurred by Lender as a result of such prepayment.

(b) **Yield Maintenance Premium.** The "**Yield Maintenance Premium**" payable in connection with a prepayment during the Yield Maintenance Period shall equal the greater of:

> (i) one percent (1.00%) of the outstanding principal balance of the Loan as of the date of prepayment; and
>
> (ii) the present value of the excess, if any, of (A) the aggregate interest that would have accrued on the principal amount being prepaid at the Applicable Rate for the period from the date of prepayment through the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, assuming that such principal amount remained outstanding through the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period and that interest is payable monthly, over (B) the aggregate interest that would accrue on such principal amount for the same period at a rate per annum equal to the Treasury Rate plus fifty (50) basis points (0.50%), in each case discounted to present value as of the date of prepayment at a discount rate equal to the Treasury Rate, on a monthly basis, using a 30/360 day count convention.

In no event shall the amount determined under clause (ii) above be less than zero. The Yield Maintenance Premium shall be due and payable on the date of prepayment, and shall be in addition to all other amounts due under the Loan Documents. Partial prepayments shall not be permitted during the Yield Maintenance Period.

(c) **Treasury Rate.** "**Treasury Rate**" means the yield to maturity, as of the second (2nd) Business Day preceding the date of prepayment, of the United States Treasury security reported in Federal Reserve Statistical Release H.15 — Selected Interest Rates (or any successor publication) under the heading "Treasury Constant Maturities" having a maturity date closest to the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period; provided that if no published Treasury Constant Maturity has a maturity date within ninety (90) days of the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, the Treasury Rate shall be determined by linear interpolation between the yields of the two (2) published Treasury Constant Maturities having maturity dates nearest to (one above and one below) the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period.

(d) **Determination.** Lender shall deliver to Borrower, concurrently with or promptly after any demand for the Yield Maintenance Premium, a statement setting forth Lender's calculation of the Treasury Rate and the Yield Maintenance Premium in reasonable detail. Absent manifest error, Lender's calculation shall be conclusive and binding on Borrower. Borrower's obligation to pay the Yield Maintenance Premium shall not be affected by any change in the Applicable Rate, by any hedging transaction entered into by Lender, or by the source of the funds used to make the prepayment.

(e) **Characterization.** The parties acknowledge that the Yield Maintenance Premium is intended to compensate Lender for the loss of its bargained-for yield upon an early prepayment, is a reasonable estimate of Lender's probable loss, is not a penalty, and is a material inducement to Lender to make the Loan at the rate and on the terms set forth in the Loan Documents.

### Section 13.4 Open Prepayment Period

During the period commencing on the day immediately following the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period and ending on the Maturity Date (that is, from April 16, 2029 through April 15, 2030) (the "**Open Prepayment Period**"), Borrower may prepay the Loan in whole or in part, at any time and from time to time, without payment of any prepayment premium, penalty or yield maintenance amount, upon not less than fifteen (15) days' prior written notice to Lender.

### Section 13.5 Involuntary Prepayments

Any prepayment resulting from the application of casualty insurance proceeds or condemnation awards in accordance with **Sections 10.7** and **10.8**, and any payment resulting from Lender's acceleration of the Obligations, shall be applied to the Obligations at par without payment of any Prepayment Premium, and no notice period shall apply. Any amounts so applied shall be applied first to costs and expenses, then to accrued and unpaid interest, any late charges and Prepayment Premiums due, and then to principal.

### Section 13.6 No Reborrowing; No Defeasance

## ARTICLE XIV — EVENTS OF DEFAULT; DEFAULT RATE; LATE CHARGES

### Section 14.1 Events of Default

Each of the following shall constitute an "**Event of Default**" under this Mortgage and the other Loan Documents:

(a) **Payment Default.** Failure by Borrower to pay when due any installment of principal, interest, or any other amount payable under the Note or this Mortgage, including any deposit required to be made into any Reserve Account, subject to the cure period set forth in **Section 14.3(a)**;

(b) **Covenant Default.** Failure by Borrower to observe or perform any covenant, agreement, obligation, term or condition contained in this Mortgage or any other Loan Document (other than those specifically addressed in another clause of this Section), continuing beyond the applicable cure period set forth in **Section 14.3(b)**;

(c) **Representation Default.** Any representation or warranty made by Borrower or Guarantor in this Mortgage, in any other Loan Document, or in any certificate, financial statement or report furnished to Lender, proves to have been materially false, misleading or inaccurate in any material respect when made or deemed made;

(d) **Insolvency.** The filing by or against Borrower or Guarantor of a petition in bankruptcy or for reorganization or the appointment of a receiver, trustee or custodian for any substantial part of the property or assets of Borrower or Guarantor, or any assignment for the benefit of creditors, or the commencement of any similar insolvency proceeding, in each case without dismissal within sixty (60) days (and, in the case of a voluntary filing or assignment, without any cure period);

(e) **Cross-Default.** A default by Borrower or Guarantor under any other indebtedness for borrowed money in an amount exceeding Two Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($250,000.00), individually or in the aggregate, which default is not cured within any applicable cure or grace period, or the occurrence of any event that permits the acceleration of any such indebtedness;

(f) **Cross-Default under Loan Documents.** A default by Borrower or Guarantor under the Note, the Guaranty, the Environmental Indemnity or any other Loan Document (after giving effect to any applicable notice and cure periods thereunder);

(g) **Insurance.** Failure by Borrower to maintain any insurance required under **Article X** or under any other Loan Document;

(h) **Completion.** Failure by Borrower to achieve Substantial Completion of the Renovation by the Substantial Completion Deadline in accordance with **Section 12.7**;

(i) **DSCR.** The DSCR falling below 1.25x for two (2) consecutive Test Periods following the commencement of DSCR testing in accordance with **Section 11.1**, without cure in accordance with **Section 14.3(d)**;

(j) **SPE Default.** Breach of any covenant set forth in **Article VIII**, or any failure of Borrower to maintain its status as a Single-Purpose Entity, or any amendment to the Operating Agreement or certificate of formation that impairs the SPE Provisions without Lender's consent;

(k) **Transfer Default.** Violation of the transfer restrictions set forth in **Section 9.2**;

(l) **Environmental Default.** Breach of any covenant set forth in **Section 7.1**, or the occurrence of any of the following with respect to the Property: (i) any violation of, or failure to comply with, the LUR; (ii) any penetration, removal, breach, compromise or disturbance of the Vapor Barrier without SCDHEC's prior written approval and Lender's prior written consent; (iii) any modification, termination, release or amendment of the LUR without Lender's prior written consent; (iv) any reopening by SCDHEC of Site ID No. SC-29403-0772 as a result of any act or omission of Borrower, any Tenant, the General Contractor or any of their respective agents or contractors; (v) the presence or release of any Hazardous Materials at, on, under or migrating from the Property in violation of Environmental Laws; or (vi) any failure to deliver the inspection report required by **Section 7.1(a)**;

(m) **Material Adverse Change.** A material adverse change in the financial condition, business, assets or operations of Borrower or Guarantor that, in Lender's reasonable judgment, materially impairs the ability of Borrower to perform its obligations under the Loan Documents or of Guarantor to perform its obligations under the Guaranty;

(n) **Judgments.** Entry of one or more final, non-appealable judgments against Borrower or Guarantor in an aggregate amount exceeding $250,000.00 that remains unsatisfied, unbonded or unstayed for thirty (30) days;

(o) **Loss of Priority.** This Mortgage ceases to constitute a valid first-priority lien on the Mortgaged Property, subject only to the Permitted Exceptions, or the Title Policy is cancelled, terminated or materially modified without Lender's consent;

(p) **Change of Use.** Any use of the Property in violation of **Section 9.4**; and

(q) **Other.** The occurrence of any other event or condition that constitutes an Event of Default as defined in the Note or in any other Loan Document.

### Section 14.2 Notice

Lender shall give Borrower written notice of any Event of Default described in **Section 14.1(b)** or **14.1(i)** for which a notice and cure period is provided in **Section 14.3**; provided that no notice shall be required, and no cure period shall apply, with respect to any Event of Default described in **Sections 14.1(a)** (following the expiration of the applicable cure period), **14.1(d)**, **14.1(j)**, **14.1(k)**, **14.1(l)**, or in the case of any Event of Default arising from a voluntary bankruptcy filing, any assignment for the benefit of creditors, or any transfer in violation of **Section 9.2**.

### Section 14.3 Cure Periods

The following cure periods shall apply to the Events of Default described in **Section 14.1**:

(a) **Payment Defaults.** Five (5) Business Days after the date on which payment was due.

(b) **Non-Monetary Defaults.** For any Event of Default described in **Section 14.1(b)** (other than SPE defaults, transfer restriction defaults, insurance defaults, environmental defaults and completion defaults, which are addressed in clauses (c), (f) and (g) below), thirty (30) calendar days after written notice from Lender to Borrower of such default; provided, however, that if the default is of a nature that it cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) calendar days, and Borrower has commenced and is diligently pursuing the cure thereof, the cure period shall be extended for an additional period not to exceed sixty (60) calendar days (for a total cure period not to exceed ninety (90) calendar days).

(c) **SPE and Transfer Defaults.** No cure period. Any breach of the covenants set forth in **Article VIII** or **Section 9.2** shall constitute an immediate Event of Default without notice or opportunity to cure.

(d) **DSCR Defaults.** No cure period, other than that the DSCR must remain below 1.25x for two (2) consecutive Test Periods before an Event of Default arises, as provided in **Section 11.1(c)**.

(e) **Completion Defaults.** Lender may, in its sole discretion and upon Borrower's written request delivered not less than thirty (30) days prior to the Substantial Completion Deadline, extend the Substantial Completion Deadline by up to ninety (90) days if Lender determines that (i) the delay is attributable solely to force majeure events, (ii) Borrower has funded all Renovation costs required to be funded by Borrower, and (iii) no other Event of Default has occurred and is continuing.

(f) **Insurance Defaults and Environmental Defaults.** No cure period, except that where the failure to maintain insurance results solely from the cancellation or non-renewal of a policy by the insurer, Borrower shall have ten (10) Business Days after written notice from Lender to obtain replacement coverage (which cure shall not be available more than once during the term of the Loan).

(g) **Other Defaults.** With respect to any Event of Default for which no cure period is expressly provided in this Section, no cure period shall apply.

### Section 14.4 Default Rate

Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, the outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall bear interest at the Default Rate, being the Applicable Rate plus five percent (5.00%) per annum, accruing from the date of the Event of Default until such Event of Default is cured or the Loan is repaid in full, whichever first occurs, in each case as more particularly provided in the Note. Interest at the Default Rate shall be payable on demand and shall be secured by this Mortgage.

### Section 14.5 Late Charge

A late charge equal to five percent (5%) of any monthly installment of principal and/or interest shall be assessed on any payment not received by Lender within ten (10) calendar days after the applicable due date. The late charge is intended to compensate Lender for the additional administrative expense of processing late payments, is agreed by the parties to be a reasonable estimate of such expense, and shall not be deemed a penalty. The assessment of a late charge shall not cure any default, shall not preclude the imposition of the Default Rate, and shall not limit any other remedy of Lender.

### Section 14.6 Protective Advances

If Borrower fails to perform any covenant or obligation under the Loan Documents, or if Lender reasonably determines that it is necessary to protect the Mortgaged Property or its lien thereon, Lender may (but shall not be obligated to) make any payment, advance or expenditure on Borrower's behalf, including the payment of taxes, assessments, insurance premiums, liens, repairs, property preservation costs, and legal fees.
