**One further observation.** The Loan as documented is a value-add, non-recourse, interest-only facility on a 56% occupied building with a lease-up covenant that the building cannot satisfy and a guaranty that expires before the risk period ends. If the Bank's loan committee approved this on the strength of the 62.77% as-stabilized loan-to-value ratio, it should be told that the ratio is not supported by the appraisal's own income analysis (D.2) and that the covenant intended to monitor the risk cannot be met (D.1). Those two points should be put in writing to Marcus before the documents are signed, and I would recommend they be raised at the same time the Term Sheet amendments contemplated in D.1 and G.1 are discussed. The file should record that they were raised.
