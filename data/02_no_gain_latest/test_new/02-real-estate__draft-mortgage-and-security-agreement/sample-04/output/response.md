# Deliverables — CHB CRE Loan No. 2025-CHS-0047

Two deliverables have been produced for the Calverley Heritage Bank / Palmetto Gateway Partners LLC loan (520–534 King Street, Charleston, SC):

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `mortgage-and-security-agreement.docx` | Long-form South Carolina Mortgage and Security Agreement with integrated absolute assignment of leases and rents (14 Articles, 5 Exhibits, ~22,200 words / 61 pages) |
| `drafting-issues-memorandum.docx` | Privileged drafting issues memorandum with risk-prioritized register, 31 numbered issues, figure reconciliation and source-to-issue schedules (~14,500 words / 35 pages) |

## Approach

1. **Inventory and full read** of all eight source documents, recording which is controlling (executed term sheet) and which are summaries requiring qualification (redacted operating agreement summary; Phase I executive summary).
2. **Independent recomputation** of every figure in the record — rent roll, budgets, loan metrics, reserves, fees, amortization and DSCR — rather than adopting quoted figures. Results are in Appendix A of the memorandum (39 line items, stated vs. recomputed).
3. **Drafting from the verified record**, with every economic and covenant term traced to the term sheet and each title/environmental term traced to the title commitment and Phase I ESA (Appendix B map).
4. **Validation** of both .docx files against ECMA-376 and PDF render checks (tables, signature blocks, cross-references; no dangling section references, no stray markup).

## Principal findings recorded in the memorandum

- **DSCR covenant is unattainable.** On every income figure in the file (in-place $320,100; as-is $332,000–$345,000; stabilized $582,000–$592,000) DSCR is 0.28x–0.55x on amortizing debt service against a 1.25x covenant; the supportable loan at 1.25x is ~$6.16M against a $14.75M loan.
- **Occupancy misstated** at 68% in the term sheet and appraisal; correct figure is 56.13% (17,400 SF of 31,000 SF), and the 21,400 SF error also produced the appraisal's $19.40/SF blended rent (correct: $23.86).
- **Borrower is not an SPE** — no independent manager, separateness covenants, or lender consents — and the amendment required by Term Sheet §10.1(g) has not been delivered. The draft conditions its SPE representation on closing delivery rather than asserting a representation known to be untrue.
- **Title issues:** vesting in Hampstead Village Holdings LLC (deed must record first); Anson Lane strip conveyed by quitclaim and described in the term sheet without the recorded plat reference (Plat Book 64, Page 91); no Permitted Exceptions ever approved; future advance endorsement essential for the $2,250,000 holdback.
- **Yield maintenance** drafted to the term sheet's greater-of structure with all four open variables defined; the partner's suggested formula and its zero floor depart from the term sheet and are flagged.
- **Estoppels/SNDAs** are conditions precedent, but the Lowcountry Provisions lease has no estoppel, SNDA or assignment clause.
- **Sources and uses gap** of ~$441,825 (origination fee, mortgage tax, $150,000 TI/LC reserve, $89,750 leasing commissions) with no funding source.
- **SCDHEC land use restriction** on Lot 7 (residential prohibition and vapor barrier) drafted as Article VIII covenants with an immediate default, per the title company's special instruction and the Phase I's recommendation.
- **Arithmetic errors** in the draw schedule ($5,000 over-draw; $50,000 shifted between HVAC and elevator lines; no 10% retainage) and in the appraisal's as-is NOI.

## Assumptions and limitations

The ALTA/NSPS survey, the full Phase I report, the full operating agreement and the Construction Contract were not in the file; Exhibit A therefore carries the title commitment's description with the survey metes and bounds incorporated by reference. The documentary stamp tax rate (0.37% / $54,575) and the South Carolina Uniform Assignment of Rents Act codification are flagged in the memorandum as requiring verification. Execution, notarial and recording blanks are left in the instrument, as is customary, and are identified as closing items.
