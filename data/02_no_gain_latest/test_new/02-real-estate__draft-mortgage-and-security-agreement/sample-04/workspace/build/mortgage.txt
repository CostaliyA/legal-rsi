[A]RECITALS
[P]**A. The Loan.** Lender has agreed to make a loan to Borrower in the original principal amount of Fourteen Million Seven Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($14,750,000.00) (the "**Loan**"), to be advanced in two components: (i) an initial advance at closing in the amount of Twelve Million Five Hundred Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($12,500,000.00) (the "**Initial Advance**"), and (ii) a renovation holdback in the amount of Two Million Two Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($2,250,000.00) (the "**Renovation Holdback**"), to be advanced on a draw basis in accordance with Article IX. The Loan is evidenced by a promissory note of even date herewith in the original principal amount of $14,750,000.00 executed by Borrower and payable to the order of Lender (as amended, restated, extended, renewed, replaced or supplemented from time to time, the "**Note**").
[P]**B. The Property.** Borrower is acquiring fee simple title to the real property commonly known as 520–534 King Street, Charleston, South Carolina 29403, consisting of Lots 7, 8 and 9 of the Hampstead Village Plat, as recorded in Plat Book 41 at Page 218, together with the vacated Anson Lane strip, all in the Office of the Register of Mesne Conveyances for Charleston County, South Carolina (the "**RMC Office**"), and more particularly described on **Exhibit A** attached hereto and incorporated herein by this reference.
[P]**C. Security.** This Mortgage is given to secure the Obligations (as defined in Article I), and Borrower desires to grant to Lender a first-priority mortgage lien on and security interest in the Property, and to absolutely assign to Lender all leases, rents, issues and profits of the Property, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Mortgage.
[P]**D. Related Loan Documents.** The Loan is also evidenced and secured by, and the Obligations are also guaranteed and supported by, the Note, an Assignment of Leases and Rents (to the extent Lender elects to require a separate instrument as contemplated by Article III), an Environmental Indemnity Agreement executed by Borrower and Neil Ashford Graydon, a Guaranty Agreement executed by Neil Ashford Graydon, a UCC-1 Financing Statement naming Borrower as debtor and Lender as secured party to be filed with the South Carolina Secretary of State (together with a fixture filing to be recorded in the RMC Office), and the other documents, instruments and agreements described in Section 1.1 or listed in the closing index for the Loan (collectively, together with this Mortgage, the "**Loan Documents**").
[P]**E. Guarantor.** Neil Ashford Graydon, an individual residing at 14 Church Street, Charleston, South Carolina 29401, is the managing member and sole owner of Graydon Development Group LLC, the managing member of Borrower, and is the "**Guarantor**" under the Guaranty Agreement.
[P]**F. Renovation.** Borrower intends to renovate and improve the Improvements in accordance with the Renovation Budget attached as **Exhibit C** at a total cost of $3,150,000.00, of which $2,250,000.00 is to be funded from the Renovation Holdback and $900,000.00 from Borrower's equity.
[P]**G. Environmental Condition.** The Property is subject to a recorded land use restriction affecting Lot 7 (Tax Map No. 460-12-04-007) imposed by the South Carolina Department of Health and Environmental Control, which restriction runs with the land and must be complied with by Borrower and all successors and assigns, as more particularly provided in Article VIII.
[P]**H. Recording.** This Mortgage is intended to be recorded in the RMC Office and constitutes a mortgage of real property and a security agreement within the meaning of the Uniform Commercial Code as enacted in South Carolina.

[A]ABSOLUTE ASSIGNMENT OF LEASES, RENTS AND PROFITS
[H]**3.1 Absolute and Present Assignment.** For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Borrower hereby unconditionally, presently and absolutely grants, transfers, sells, assigns, conveys and sets over to Lender all of Borrower's right, title and interest, whether now owned or hereafter acquired, in, to and under (a) all Leases and all guaranties, security and other credit support therefor, and (b) all rents, issues, profits, revenues, income, percentage rent, additional rent, expense reimbursements, escalation payments, parking income, security deposits, advance rentals and all other sums now or hereafter payable under or in connection with the Leases or otherwise arising from the use or occupancy of the Property, without reservation and without any limitation. This assignment is an absolute and present assignment and not a collateral assignment given as additional security, and it is intended to be effective immediately upon the execution and delivery of this Mortgage and to be perfected upon recordation hereof in the RMC Office, in accordance with the South Carolina Uniform Assignment of Rents Act, S.C. Code Ann. Title 29, Chapter 3, Article 6, as amended (the "**Assignment of Rents Act**"). Borrower acknowledges that it has received the consideration for this absolute assignment and that the assignment is not conditioned upon the occurrence of an Event of Default.
[H]**3.2 License to Collect Prior to Default.** Notwithstanding Section 3.1, Lender hereby grants to Borrower a revocable license to collect, use and enjoy the rents, issues and profits of the Property for so long as no Event of Default has occurred and is continuing. Such license shall be automatically revoked, without further notice or action, upon the occurrence of an Event of Default. Borrower shall hold all security deposits in trust for the benefit of Lender and the applicable Tenants and shall not apply any security deposit except in accordance with the applicable Lease and applicable law. Borrower shall not waive, discount, prepay, compromise or anticipate any rent or other sum due under any Lease more than one (1) month in advance without Lender's prior written consent.
[H]**3.3 Enforcement Upon Default.** Upon and during the continuance of an Event of Default, Lender may, at its option and in addition to all other remedies, enforce this assignment by giving written notice to any or all Tenants directing them to pay all rents and other sums due under their respective Leases directly to Lender or to its designee, and Borrower irrevocably authorizes and directs each Tenant to make such payments upon receipt of such notice. Borrower acknowledges and agrees that, under the Assignment of Rents Act, Lender may collect the rents and enforce the assignment without the necessity of obtaining a court order, without appointing a receiver, and without any further act, consent or authorization by Borrower, and that each Tenant is authorized to rely conclusively on Lender's written notice. Tenant payments made to Lender in accordance with such notice shall discharge such Tenant's payment obligation to the extent paid. Borrower hereby irrevocably appoints Lender as its attorney-in-fact, coupled with an interest, to demand, collect, receive, receipt for, sue for, compromise and give acquittance for any rents assigned hereby, and to endorse any instrument or check received in payment thereof. The foregoing appointment is coupled with an interest and is not revoked by Borrower's dissolution, insolvency or bankruptcy.
[H]**3.4 Tenant Notices; Estoppel Certificates; SNDAs.** Borrower shall, promptly upon Lender's request (and in any event within five (5) Business Days after request), (a) join with Lender in notifying any Tenant of the existence of this Mortgage and this assignment, and (b) use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain and deliver to Lender tenant estoppel certificates and subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreements ("**SNDAs**") in form and substance reasonably acceptable to Lender. Borrower acknowledges that (i) the Leases identified in **Exhibit D** as lacking express estoppel, SNDA or assignment-consent provisions are so identified, (ii) the Estoppel Certificates and SNDAs from the Tenants in occupancy as of the Closing Date are conditions precedent to the Closing under the Loan Documents and the title commitment, and (iii) Borrower shall not be relieved of such condition by reason of any Tenant's refusal to cooperate, it being agreed that Borrower shall pursue such certificates and agreements diligently and in good faith.

[H]**4.1 Payment of the Indebtedness.** Borrower shall pay the Indebtedness strictly in accordance with the Note and this Mortgage, without setoff, counterclaim, defense or deduction of any kind. Monthly installments of interest and, following the Interest-Only Period, of principal and interest shall be due and payable on the fifteenth (15th) day of each calendar month, commencing May 15, 2025. The entire outstanding principal balance, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts due under the Loan Documents, shall be due and payable in full on the Maturity Date. In the event of any conflict between this Section 4.1 and the Note, the Note shall control.
[H]**4.2 Interest Rate.** The Loan shall bear interest at a floating rate per annum equal to Daily SOFR plus two hundred eighty-five (285) basis points (2.85%), subject to a SOFR floor of three percent (3.00%) per annum; accordingly, the interest rate under the Note shall not be less than five and 85/100 percent (5.85%) per annum. Capitalized terms used in this Section 4.2 and not defined herein have the meanings given in the Note.
[H]**4.3 Lockout Period; No Voluntary Prepayment.** Borrower shall not prepay the Loan, in whole or in part, at any time during the Lockout Period. Any tender of payment made in violation of this Section 4.3 may be refused by Lender, and if Lender elects to accept any such payment, Borrower shall pay, in addition to all other amounts then due, the Yield Maintenance Premium computed in accordance with Section 4.5 as though the Prepayment Date were a date within the Yield Maintenance Period. The foregoing restriction shall not apply to (a) payments of principal and interest in accordance with the Note, (b) application of insurance proceeds or Condemnation awards in accordance with Article XI, which, subject to Section 4.8, shall be applied at par without premium, or (c) payments made with the proceeds of a casualty or Condemnation, which shall likewise be applied at par without premium.
[H]**4.4 Yield Maintenance Period; Whole Prepayment Only.** At any time during the Yield Maintenance Period, Borrower may prepay the Loan in whole, but not in part, upon not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Lender (which notice shall be revocable only with Lender's consent and which shall specify the Prepayment Date), and upon payment on the Prepayment Date of (a) the entire outstanding principal balance of the Loan, (b) all accrued and unpaid interest thereon, (c) the Yield Maintenance Premium, and (d) all other amounts then due under the Loan Documents. No partial prepayment shall be permitted during the Yield Maintenance Period.
[H]**4.5 Yield Maintenance Premium.** The "Yield Maintenance Premium" payable in connection with a prepayment during the Yield Maintenance Period (or an ineligible prepayment under Section 4.3 or Section 4.7) shall equal the greater of:
[1](a) one percent (1.00%) of the principal amount of the Loan outstanding on the Prepayment Date; and
[1](b) the present value, determined as of the Prepayment Date, of the excess, if any, of (i) the aggregate amount of interest that would have accrued on the principal amount prepaid, computed at the rate of interest in effect under the Note on the Prepayment Date, for the period from the Prepayment Date through the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, over (ii) the aggregate amount of interest that would have accrued on such principal amount for the same period, computed at a per annum rate equal to the Treasury Rate plus fifty (50) basis points (0.50%).
[P]For purposes of clause (b), the differential described above shall be computed on a monthly basis over the number of full and partial months remaining in the Yield Maintenance Period (each such month being treated as one-twelfth of a year and each partial month being prorated on the basis of a 30-day month and a 360-day year), and each monthly amount shall be discounted to present value as of the Prepayment Date at a monthly discount rate equal to the Treasury Rate divided by twelve (12), using the formula P = A ÷ (1 + r)^n, where "P" is the present value of each monthly amount, "A" is such monthly amount, "r" is the Treasury Rate divided by twelve (12), and "n" is the number of months from the Prepayment Date to the date on which such amount would have accrued. The Yield Maintenance Premium shall in no event be less than the amount determined under clause (a), and in no event shall any circumstance result in a Yield Maintenance Premium of less than zero.
[P]Borrower acknowledges that the Yield Maintenance Premium is intended to compensate Lender for the loss of the benefit of its bargain resulting from the prepayment of a fixed-spread, floating-rate obligation at a time when interest rates have declined, that such loss is difficult if not impossible to calculate with precision, and that the Yield Maintenance Premium is a reasonable estimate of such loss and constitutes liquidated damages and not a penalty. Borrower waives, to the fullest extent permitted by law, any defense to the enforcement of the Yield Maintenance Premium based on the assertion that it is a penalty, unconscionable, or an unreasonable estimate of Lender's loss. Lender shall provide Borrower with a written calculation of the Yield Maintenance Premium within five (5) Business Days after request, which calculation shall be conclusive absent manifest error.
[H]**4.6 Open Prepayment Period.** At any time during the final twelve (12) months of the term of the Loan (that is, from and after the expiration of the Yield Maintenance Period through the Maturity Date), Borrower may prepay the Loan in whole or in part, at any time and from time to time, without payment of any prepayment premium, penalty or yield maintenance amount, upon not less than fifteen (15) days' prior written notice to Lender specifying the Prepayment Date and the amount to be prepaid. Amounts prepaid in part shall be applied to the outstanding principal balance and shall not reduce or otherwise affect the amount of any scheduled installment of interest or principal thereafter due, except as calculated in accordance with the Note.
[H]**4.7 Ineligible Prepayments.** If Borrower makes any prepayment (a) during the Lockout Period, (b) in part during the Yield Maintenance Period, or (c) otherwise other than in accordance with Sections 4.4 or 4.6, then Borrower shall pay, in addition to the amount prepaid, a prepayment premium equal to the Yield Maintenance Premium determined under Section 4.5 (with the principal amount prepaid being substituted for the principal amount outstanding for purposes of clause (a) thereof), and Lender may, at its option, refuse to accept such payment. Acceptance of any ineligible prepayment shall not constitute a waiver of any Event of Default arising therefrom.
[H]**4.8 Involuntary Prepayments.** Amounts applied to the Indebtedness as a result of a casualty or a Condemnation in accordance with Article XI shall be applied at par, without any prepayment premium or Yield Maintenance Premium, and shall be applied in the order of application set forth in Section 13.8. Lender may, but shall not be obligated to, permit restoration of the Improvements in accordance with Section 11.8.
[H]**4.9 Application of Payments.** All payments received shall be applied in the following order: (a) costs and expenses (including attorneys' fees) incurred by Lender in connection with the enforcement or preservation of its rights; (b) late charges; (c) the Yield Maintenance Premium and any prepayment premium; (d) interest at the Default Rate; (e) interest at the rate otherwise payable under the Note; (f) principal; and (g) all other amounts due under the Loan Documents. Lender may apply payments in any other order it elects in its discretion notwithstanding any designation by Borrower.
[H]**4.10 Late Charge.** If any installment of principal, interest or other amount due under the Loan Documents is not received by Lender within ten (10) calendar days after the date on which it is due, Borrower shall pay to Lender on demand a late charge equal to five percent (5.00%) of the overdue amount. The late charge is intended to compensate Lender for the additional administrative expense of processing delinquent payments and is not a penalty, and it is payable in addition to, and not in lieu of, interest at the Default Rate. The imposition of a late charge shall not cure any payment default, shall not extend any cure period, and shall not constitute a waiver of any Event of Default.
[H]**4.11 Default Rate.** Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, the outstanding principal balance of the Loan and, to the extent permitted by law, all other amounts due under the Loan Documents, shall bear interest at the Default Rate, from the date of the occurrence of such Event of Default until such Event of Default is cured or the Loan is repaid in full, whichever first occurs. Interest at the Default Rate shall be payable on demand, shall itself bear interest at the Default Rate if not paid when due, and shall be secured by this Mortgage.
