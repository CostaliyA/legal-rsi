# What I did

**1. Inventoried and read the full record.** Seven documents: executed Term Sheet (Jan 10, 2025), Senior Loan Committee Credit Memorandum (Jan 22, 2025), Appraisal executive summary, Preliminary Title Commitment (NC-2024-88712), Phase I ESA excerpt, Borrower organizational chart / operating-agreement excerpts, and the construction budget and draw schedule workbook. Every material proposition in the deliverables is tied to a specific document and section.

**2. Recomputed every figure independently** rather than relying on the totals in the sources. This surfaced errors that the record itself does not catch — for example, the 10% retainage is $3,475,000 (not the $3,275,000 scheduled, which is 9.42%); the draw schedule's column components total $48,500,000 against a $47,500,000 commitment; the interest reserve is short by ~$361,500 at the Credit Memorandum's own 4.35% SOFR assumption (~$1,047,000 at +100 bps), with the Credit Memorandum's $565,000 estimate itself overstated by ~$203,000; 212 × $2,245 × 12 = $5,711,280, not $5,712,000; the hard-cost contingency is 3.28%, not the 4.14% stated; and the mezzanine loan's pay-in-kind interest accretes to ~$12.55M, taking combined leverage to ~82.5% of as-completed value.

**3. Drafted the agreement from the verified record, repairing the mechanics.** The agreement implements the Term Sheet and the Credit Memorandum's conditions of approval, and fixes what the sources get wrong: an 80% loan-to-cost borrowing base that caps advances at $45,360,000 and cures both the LTC and the 65% LTV breaches; a corrected net-of-retainage draw schedule reconciled to the commitment; a full set of NFA covenants covering all four conditions (not just the vapour system); single-purpose-entity, independent-manager and Article 8 pledge mechanics; SOFR benchmark-replacement language; and North Carolina foreclosure, fair-value and usury provisions.

**4. Flagged, rather than papered over, what the record cannot resolve.** Twelve italicised *Note to Draft* items in the agreement, each cross-referenced to the numbered issue in the memorandum, plus a §7.2 list of the business decisions the Bank must make before execution.

# The ten closing-critical findings

1. **The Loan breaches the Term Sheet's own 80% LTC covenant** (83.77%; $2,140,000 over) — and the Credit Memorandum's equity-escrow cure does not change the ratio arithmetic unless advances are capped.
2. **It also breaches the 65% as-completed LTV ceiling** by $180,000; the Credit Memorandum tests against a 70% "policy" figure and reports "Pass."
3. **There is no genuine equity.** The sources and uses funds all $56.7M — including the $8.2M land cost already paid in 2023 — with debt, so the mezzanine loan can only be reimbursing the land. The Credit Memorandum's "18.2% equity" and "14.2% HVCRE" figures double-count the land; the real contributed capital is ~2.9%.
4. **The equity escrow is circular** if applied to the developer fee — resolved in the draft by allocating it to hard/soft costs only.
5. **A $3.4M Triangle Commercial Bank deed of trust is unsatisfied, unquantified and unbudgeted**, with no source of funds in the uses.
6. **The NCDEQ non-residential use restriction conflicts with the residential programme**, and the "14 units" the Credit Memorandum quantifies is unsourced (it equals the three-bedroom count; the ESA expressly declines to verify).
7. **The deed notice required by NFA Condition 4 (due Nov 29, 2024) appears unrecorded** and is absent from Schedule B-II — although the Appraisal says it was recorded.
8. **The membership-interest pledge is promised to two lenders at once**, with no Article 8 election or priority mechanism.
9. **The $12,000,000 Borrower net worth covenant is unachievable** — post-closing book equity is ~$0, or $2.14M with the equity deposit.
10. **The 75% supermajority gives the mezzanine lender's affiliate a veto over any refinancing over $1,000,000** — i.e. over the Bank's own exit.

Also flagged: a 70%-vs-65% LTV conflict, a $11.875M-vs-$23.75M guaranty ambiguity, a cross-reference to a guarantor net worth threshold that does not exist, mechanics'-lien relation-back exposure from the 2024 demolition and remediation, parcel acreage and site-orientation conflicts between the ESA and the title record, ARC approval assumed by the Appraiser but not evidenced, a $34,500/yr CC&R assessment missing from NOI, two blank pages' worth of unbudgeted carrying costs, an unsigned credit approval, and the Credit Memorandum's inaccurate description of North Carolina as a judicial foreclosure state.
