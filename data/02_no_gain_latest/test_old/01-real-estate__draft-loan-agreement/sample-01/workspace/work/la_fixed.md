**A.** Borrower is a North Carolina limited liability company formed on March 14, 2019 (North Carolina Secretary of State File No. 1923847) and is the owner of two contiguous parcels of real property located at 601 and 615 Foster Street, Durham, Durham County, North Carolina 27701, identified as Durham County Parcel Identification Nos. 0821-04-72-3341 and 0821-04-72-4102, respectively, aggregating approximately 3.47 acres (the "Property"), together with the improvements to be constructed thereon.

**B.** Borrower has requested that Lender provide a construction-to-mini-permanent loan in the principal amount of $47,500,000 to finance a portion of the costs of constructing a six-story, 212-unit Class A mixed-use residential and retail development, containing approximately 18,400 square feet of ground-floor retail space and a four-level structured parking garage with 276 parking spaces, together with related amenities and site improvements (the "Project"), and to finance related soft costs, an interest reserve and the fees described herein.

**C.** The loan is being made on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in (i) that certain Term Sheet dated January 10, 2025 between Lender and Borrower (the "Term Sheet"), as approved by Lender's Senior Loan Committee on January 22, 2025 (the "Credit Approval"), and (ii) this Agreement.

**D.** Brightleaf Mezzanine Capital LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company (North Carolina Secretary of State File No. 2103988) (the "Mezzanine Lender"), is making a mezzanine loan to Borrower in the original principal amount of $9,200,000 (the "Mezzanine Loan"), secured by a subordinate pledge of 100% of the membership interests in Borrower. Lender's making of the Loan is conditioned upon, among other things, the execution and delivery of an intercreditor agreement among Lender, Mezzanine Lender and Borrower on terms satisfactory to Lender.

**E.** Elena Vasquez-Torres and Reginald K. Osei have agreed to guarantee certain obligations of Borrower as provided in Article IX, and each of the members of Borrower has agreed to pledge its membership interests in Borrower to Lender as additional security for the Obligations.

**F.** As a condition to closing, and as a condition to each Advance, Borrower has agreed to satisfy the conditions, and to make the representations, warranties and covenants, set forth in this Agreement, including the environmental covenants set forth in Sections 5.8 and 6.4 relating to the conditional No Further Action determination issued by the North Carolina Department of Environmental Quality on September 30, 2024.

## ARTICLE II — THE LOAN

**2.1 The Commitment; Maximum Outstanding Amount.**

(a) Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, Lender agrees to make Advances to Borrower from time to time in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed the Commitment.

(b) **Borrowing Base Limitation.** Notwithstanding the Commitment, in no event shall the aggregate outstanding principal balance of the Loan at any time exceed the lesser of (i) the Commitment and (ii) an amount equal to eighty percent (80%) of Total Project Costs (the "LTC Availability"), it being acknowledged that, based on Total Project Costs of $56,700,000 as of the Closing Date and the Equity Deposit of $2,140,000 required by Section 4.8, the LTC Availability would be $45,360,000 and the Loan would accordingly be advanced in the maximum aggregate principal amount of $45,360,000 unless Total Project Costs are increased (whether by Change Orders, Cost Overruns or otherwise) and approved by Lender in accordance with Section 4.7. The amount of the Commitment not advanced as a result of this limitation shall remain available for Advance if, when and to the extent Total Project Costs increase as permitted by this Agreement.

(c) The Loan is a multiple-disbursement facility. Nothing in the Term Sheet's reference to a "single advance facility" shall be construed to require a single disbursement of the Loan proceeds; Advances shall be made in accordance with the Approved Draw Schedule and Article IV.

**2.2 Purpose and Use of Proceeds.** The proceeds of the Loan shall be used solely to (a) pay Eligible Costs included in the Approved Budget, (b) fund the Interest Reserve, (c) pay the Origination Fee, and (d) pay such other costs as are expressly approved in writing by Lender. No proceeds of the Loan shall be used to (i) reimburse or return to any Member or Affiliate any capital contribution or prior expenditure, including any portion of the land acquisition cost reflected in the Approved Budget, except as expressly approved by Lender in writing, (ii) make any Distribution, (iii) pay any portion of the Mezzanine Loan, or (iv) pay any cost that is not an Eligible Cost.

**2.3 The Note.** The Loan shall be evidenced by the Note, which shall be payable to the order of Lender in the principal amount of the Commitment, with interest as provided herein and in the Note. The Note shall be repaid in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and the Note.

**2.4 Interest Rate.** Interest shall accrue on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan at the Applicable Rate for each day, computed on the basis of the actual number of days elapsed over a 360-day year in accordance with the definition of "Daily Simple SOFR."

**2.5 SOFR Floor.** If Daily Simple SOFR is less than 1.00% on any day, Daily Simple SOFR shall be deemed to be 1.00% for purposes of calculating the Applicable Rate for such day. The parties acknowledge that, as of the Closing Date, the SOFR Floor results in a minimum Applicable Rate of 4.25% during the Construction Period and 4.50% during the Mini-Perm Period.

**2.6 Benchmark Replacement.** If Lender determines that (a) Daily Simple SOFR is not available or has been permanently discontinued, (b) the administrator of SOFR or a Governmental Authority has made a public statement announcing that SOFR will be discontinued or is no longer representative, (c) SOFR is no longer a widely recognized benchmark for dollar-denominated syndicated or commercial real estate loans, or (d) the Federal Reserve Board, the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, the Alternative Reference Rates Committee ("ARRC") or any successor or similar authority has issued a recommendation or rule requiring the use of a replacement benchmark, then Lender and Borrower shall negotiate in good faith to amend this Agreement to replace Daily Simple SOFR with a successor rate and to make corresponding spread adjustments and mechanical changes. Any such amendment shall be consistent with the ARRC's recommended fallback language for commercial real estate loans (including the applicable hardwired waterfall and any recommended spread adjustment). Pending such amendment, Lender may determine an alternative rate in good faith on a commercially reasonable basis, and the rate so determined shall be the "Daily Simple SOFR" for so long as the circumstances described above continue.

**2.7 Computation and Payment of Interest.** Interest on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall accrue daily and shall be payable monthly in arrears on each Payment Date. During the Construction Period, and for so long as funds remain in the Interest Reserve Account, accrued interest shall be paid by debiting the Interest Reserve Account against the payment due; upon exhaustion of the Interest Reserve, all interest shall be paid by Borrower from its own funds (not from Loan or Mezzanine Loan proceeds) on each Payment Date. Promptly following each Payment Date, Lender shall provide Borrower with a statement showing the Interest Reserve balance, the interest accrued and paid, and the remaining projected interest cost through the Construction Maturity Date.

**2.8 Default Rate.** Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, the outstanding principal balance of the Loan and, to the extent permitted by applicable law, all accrued and unpaid interest, fees and other amounts shall bear interest at the Default Rate. Interest at the Default Rate shall be payable on demand. Application of the Default Rate is not a waiver of any Event of Default and does not limit any other remedy of Lender.
