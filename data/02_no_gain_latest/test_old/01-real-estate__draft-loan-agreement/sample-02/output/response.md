## Approach

1. **Inventoried** all seven source documents and treated them as data (no embedded instruction followed).
2. **Recomputed independently** every total, ratio, date interval and cross-reference rather than accepting stated figures. Every stated total ties except where noted below.
3. **Separated** extracted facts, assumptions, legal characterizations and open questions so the Bank can see what rests on documentary evidence.
4. **Drafted from the verified record**, adopting the Term Sheet/Credit Memorandum position except where the documents conflict — in which case the draft adopts the recommended position and flags it in a bracketed Drafting Note keyed to the memo's issue numbers.

## Most material findings

- **Zero verified equity.** The sources and uses balance only if $8.2M of the $9.2M mezzanine loan funded the land (per Borrower's budget line 1.01). The Term Sheet, Credit Memorandum and Operating Agreement each describe the land as a member equity contribution. Senior + mezzanine debt = 100% of project cost.
- **Unfunded $3.4M payoff.** The Triangle Commercial Bank deed of trust (Book 6215/892) must be released to deliver a first lien, but appears in no budget line and no source. Corrected budget ≈ $60.78M → loan-to-cost 78.15% (compliant) but required equity ≈ $4.08M, not the Committee's $2.14M.
- **Term Sheet LTV breach at closing:** as-completed 65.25% vs. a 65% maximum (65% of $72.8M = $47.32M).
- **Retainage never withheld.** Term Sheet Draws 2–8 equal 100% of the GMP; the $3,275,000 "retainage release" is exactly the Contingency ($1,440,000) + Loan-funded Developer Fee ($1,835,000), and 10% of the GMP is $3,475,000. Borrower's schedule carries a separate $1,000,000 imbalance. The draft's Schedule 1 restates the schedule and foots exactly to $47,500,000.
- **Interest reserve adequate only below 3.82% SOFR** (recomputed month-by-month; the file contains three contradictory conclusions: "+$221,325 adequate", "−$365,830", "−$565,000").
- **Combined coverage ≈ 1.00x** once the 12.50% mezzanine is included; senior coverage 1.04x at +200 bps.
- **NFA non-residential use restriction** not reconciled to the residential floors above the 0.38-acre affected area; the "14 units" figure is unsupported (proportional overlay ≈ 23 units → DSCR 1.14x). Deed notice required by 11/29/2024 — no evidence of recording, and absent from the Title Commitment, though the appraisal says it was recorded.
- **Parcel geometry conflicts:** Phase I acreages (1.89/1.58 ac) do not tie to the surveyed metes and bounds (2.094/1.379 ac); parcels are north/south, not west/east; frontage is 500.5 ft, not the appraisal's 420 ft.
- **Document defects:** "single advance" vs. nine draws; Events of Default clause keyed to § 6.4 (a Borrower covenant) as a Guarantor test; extension option with no conditions; incomplete permitted-exception list; partial repayment guaranty described as both $11.875M aggregate and "per guarantor".

## Where the draft departs from the source documents

Each departure is marked with a bracketed Drafting Note citing the relevant memo issue: Schedule 1 (draw schedule), Schedule 2 (corrected sources and uses), Schedule 3 (permitted exceptions), Article III (conditions precedent consolidated from Term Sheet § 11 and Credit Memorandum § 10.1), Article XII and Schedule 4 (all four NFA conditions, not just the vapor system), and Article XI (guaranty cap and environmental indemnity parties).
