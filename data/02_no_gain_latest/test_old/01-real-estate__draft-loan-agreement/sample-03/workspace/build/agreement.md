## ARTICLE II — THE LOAN

**Section 2.1 — The Commitment; the Loan.** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Lender agrees to make the Loan to the Borrower in one or more Advances on and after the Closing Date, in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed the Commitment. The Loan is a multiple-advance facility. Amounts repaid or prepaid may not be reborrowed. The Borrower's obligation to repay the Loan is evidenced by the Note.

**Section 2.2 — Purpose.** The proceeds of the Loan shall be used solely to (a) fund the hard and soft costs of the Project in accordance with the Approved Budget and the Approved Draw Schedule, (b) fund the Interest Reserve, (c) pay the Origination Fee to the extent permitted by Section 2.10, and (d) pay such other Project costs as are expressly approved by the Lender in writing. No portion of the Loan proceeds may be used to pay any distribution to any member of the Borrower, to repay any Indebtedness of any member or Affiliate of the Borrower, or to reimburse any member for any capital contribution, except as expressly approved in writing by the Lender.

**Section 2.3 — The Note.** The Loan shall be evidenced by the Note, which shall be payable to the order of the Lender in the principal amount of the Commitment and shall bear interest as provided in this Agreement and the Note. In the event of any conflict between the Note and this Agreement as to the payment of interest or principal, this Agreement controls.

**Section 2.4 — Condition of the Property; Lender Not a Builder.** The Lender is not a party to the Construction Contract or the architect's agreement and has no obligation to supervise, inspect, or inform the Borrower of any aspect of the Work. Any inspection or review performed by the Lender or the Construction Consultant is for the sole benefit of the Lender and does not constitute a warranty or representation as to the adequacy, sufficiency, quality, or compliance of the Work, the Approved Plans and Specifications, or the Approved Budget, nor does it relieve the Borrower or the General Contractor of any obligation.

**Section 2.5 — Interest Rate.** (a) During the Construction Period, the outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall bear interest at the Applicable Rate for the Construction Period. (b) During the Mini-Perm Period (including any Extension Period), the outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall bear interest at the Applicable Rate for the Mini-Perm Period. (c) Interest shall be computed on the basis of actual days elapsed over a three hundred sixty (360)-day year, applied to the actual outstanding principal balance each day, in accordance with Section 2.8. (d) The Alternate Basis for determining interest — *inapplicable; the Loan bears interest on a Daily Simple SOFR basis from the Closing Date.*

**Section 2.6 — SOFR Floor; Negative SOFR.** Daily Simple SOFR shall at no time be less than the SOFR Floor of 1.00% per annum. If Daily Simple SOFR would otherwise be negative, Daily Simple SOFR shall be deemed to be the SOFR Floor. The SOFR Floor applies during both the Construction Period and the Mini-Perm Period and may not be adjusted except through a Benchmark Replacement.

**Section 2.7 — Default Rate.** Upon and during the continuance of an Event of Default, and at the Lender's election from the date of the Event of Default (and, with respect to any Event of Default under Section 11.5, automatically and without notice), all outstanding amounts under the Loan Documents shall bear interest at the Default Rate, being the Applicable Rate plus five percent (5.00%) per annum, and interest at the Default Rate shall be payable on demand and shall be secured by the Loan Documents.

**Section 2.8 — Computation of Interest; Usury Savings.** (a) Interest for each day shall equal the product of (i) the outstanding principal balance on that day, (ii) the Applicable Rate in effect for that day divided by three hundred sixty (360), and (iii) the number of days elapsed. (b) Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in any Loan Document, no provision of any Loan Document shall require the payment or permit the collection of interest in excess of the maximum rate permitted by applicable North Carolina law (the "Maximum Lawful Rate"). If any excess interest is provided for or received, (i) the excess shall be applied first to unpaid principal, then to other Obligations, and then refunded to the Borrower, and (ii) the effective rate of interest shall be automatically reduced to the Maximum Lawful Rate. To the extent permitted by applicable law, all interest at any time contracted for, charged, or received shall be amortized, prorated, allocated, and spread over the full period of the Loan.

**Section 2.9 — Payments.** (a) Accrued interest on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall be payable monthly in arrears on the first Business Day of each calendar month, commencing on the first Business Day of the first full calendar month following the Closing Date. (b) During the Construction Period, interest shall be paid from the Interest Reserve Account until the Interest Reserve is exhausted; thereafter, and during the Mini-Perm Period, interest shall be payable by the Borrower from its own funds (subject to Section 4.8 and the Carry Guaranty). (c) The outstanding principal balance of the Loan, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, fees, costs, and other Obligations, shall be due and payable in full on the Maturity Date. (d) All payments shall be made in immediately available funds to the account designated by the Lender, without setoff, counterclaim, deduction, or withholding, not later than 1:00 p.m. (Eastern Time) on the due date.
