# LOAN AGREEMENT

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
THIS LOAN AGREEMENT (this **"Agreement"**) is made and entered into as of February [●], 2025 (the **"Effective Date"**), by and between **PINNACLE SAVINGS BANK, N.A.**, a nationally chartered savings bank organized under the laws of the United States and regulated by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, with its principal office at 215 South Tryon Street, 18th Floor, Charlotte, North Carolina 28202 (together with its successors and assigns, **"Lender"**), and **HARBORVIEW CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company, with North Carolina Secretary of State File No. 1923847 and its principal office at 400 West Main Street, Suite 310, Durham, North Carolina 27701 (together with its permitted successors and assigns, **"Borrower"**).
:::

## RECITALS

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
A. Borrower is the fee simple owner of two contiguous parcels of real property located at 601 and 615 Foster Street, Durham, Durham County, North Carolina 27701, identified by Durham County Parcel Identification Numbers 0821-04-72-3341 and 0821-04-72-4102, comprising approximately 3.47 acres in the aggregate, together with all improvements, fixtures and appurtenances thereto (collectively, the **"Property"**).

B. Borrower has requested that Lender make available to Borrower a construction-to-mini-permanent loan in the principal amount of Forty-Seven Million Five Hundred Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($47,500,000) to finance a portion of the costs of constructing the Project (as defined below) and to fund the Interest Reserve (as defined below), and Lender is willing to do so on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

C. The Loan is being made on the basis of, and in reliance upon, the Term Sheet dated January 10, 2025 and executed by Lender and Borrower (the **"Term Sheet"**), and the Senior Loan Committee Credit Memorandum dated January 22, 2025 relating to Lender's Loan No. CRE-2025-0342 (the **"Credit Memorandum"**). This Agreement sets forth the definitive terms and conditions of the Loan. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and the Term Sheet, this Agreement shall control as between Lender and Borrower, except that the fees, rates, maturities, financial covenants and other economic terms set forth in the Term Sheet shall not be modified except as expressly provided herein or as approved in writing by Lender's Senior Loan Committee.

D. Concurrently with the closing of the Loan, Brightleaf Mezzanine Capital LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company (North Carolina Secretary of State File No. 2103988) (the **"Mezzanine Lender"**), will make a mezzanine loan to Borrower in the original principal amount of $9,200,000 (the **"Mezzanine Loan"**), which Mezzanine Loan will be secured by a pledge of one hundred percent (100%) of the membership interests in Borrower, subordinated to the Membership Interest Pledge granted to Lender pursuant to this Agreement and the other Loan Documents.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Lender and Borrower agree as follows:
