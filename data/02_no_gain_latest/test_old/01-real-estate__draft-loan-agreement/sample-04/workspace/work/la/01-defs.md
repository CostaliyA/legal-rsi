**"Business Day"** means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in Charlotte, North Carolina are authorized or required by law to close.

**"Capital Event"** means any sale, transfer, refinancing, recapitalization or other liquidity event with respect to the Property or the Project, or any sale or transfer of a direct or indirect equity interest in Borrower.

**"Cash Equivalents"** means (a) marketable direct obligations of the United States or any agency thereof maturing within one year, (b) certificates of deposit or time deposits of any bank having capital and surplus of not less than $500,000,000, maturing within one year, and (c) money market funds investing at least ninety-five percent (95%) of their assets in the foregoing.

## 1.2 Interpretation

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." (b) The word "or" is not exclusive. (c) References to Articles, Sections, Exhibits and Schedules are to this Agreement unless otherwise specified. (d) References to any statute, regulation or agreement include any amendment, successor or replacement thereof. (e) The singular includes the plural and vice versa. (f) Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. (g) "Hereof," "herein," "hereunder" and similar terms refer to this Agreement as a whole. (h) All amounts are stated, and all payments shall be made, in lawful currency of the United States. (i) Any determination, consent, approval, waiver or satisfaction of conditions by Lender may be made in Lender's sole and absolute discretion unless this Agreement expressly provides for a different standard, and shall be made in writing. (j) Whenever a payment is due on a day that is not a Business Day, the payment date shall be extended to the next succeeding Business Day, and interest shall accrue during such extension. (k) Time is of the essence with respect to each obligation of Borrower and each Guarantor under the Loan Documents.

## 1.3 Accounting Terms; Rounding

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
All accounting terms not otherwise defined herein shall be construed in accordance with GAAP. All financial covenants and other financial calculations shall be computed to four (4) decimal places for ratios and to the nearest dollar for amounts, and shall be tested on a consolidated basis for Borrower.
