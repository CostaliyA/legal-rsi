# ARTICLE II — THE LOAN

## 2.1 The Commitment

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the other Loan Documents, and in reliance on the representations, warranties and covenants of Borrower and the Guarantors, Lender agrees to make Advances to Borrower from time to time during the Construction Period in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed Forty-Seven Million Five Hundred Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($47,500,000), in accordance with the Draw Schedule attached as Exhibit C. The Loan is a single-advance, non-revolving construction facility permitting multiple disbursements in accordance with Article IV. No principal amount repaid or prepaid may be re-borrowed. Borrower's obligation to repay the Loan is evidenced by the Note and secured by the Security Documents.
:::

## 2.2 The Note

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
The Loan shall be evidenced by the Note, which shall bear interest and be payable as provided in this Agreement and in the Note. In the event of any conflict between the Note and this Agreement, this Agreement shall control.
:::

## 2.3 Purpose

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
The proceeds of the Loan shall be used solely to (a) pay Hard Costs and Soft Costs set forth in the Approved Project Budget, (b) fund the Interest Reserve, and (c) pay the Origination Fee to the extent permitted by the Approved Project Budget. No proceeds of the Loan shall be used (i) to pay any Distribution, (ii) to repay, reimburse or refinance any capital contribution, member loan or other advance made by any member of Borrower (including any advance used to acquire the Property or to fund the remediation of the Property) except as expressly permitted in Section 7.5(f), (iii) to pay any amount in respect of the Mezzanine Loan, or (iv) for any purpose that would cause the Loan-to-Cost Ratio or the Loan-to-Value Ratio to exceed the limitations in Article VIII.
:::

## 2.4 Interest

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) **Applicable Rate.** Interest on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall accrue at all times at the Applicable Rate.

\(b\) **Payment.** Interest shall be calculated on the basis of a 360-day year and the actual number of days elapsed, and shall be payable monthly in arrears on the first (1st) Business Day of each calendar month, commencing on the first Business Day of the first full calendar month following the Closing Date.

\(c\) **Construction Period Interest; Interest Reserve.** During the Construction Period, and for so long as the Interest Reserve Balance is sufficient to pay interest then due, interest shall be funded by disbursements from the Interest Reserve Account as provided in Section 4.5. When and to the extent the Interest Reserve Balance is insufficient to pay interest when due, Borrower shall pay such interest from its own funds, and the Guarantors shall be obligated to do so pursuant to the Carry Guaranty, in each case without notice, demand or grace period beyond the third (3rd) Business Day following such insufficiency.

\(d\) **Mini-Perm Period Interest.** From and after the Construction Maturity Date, interest shall be payable by Borrower monthly in arrears from Borrower's own funds. No portion of the Interest Reserve may be applied to interest accruing after the Construction Maturity Date.
:::

## 2.5 SOFR; Benchmark Replacement; Conforming Changes

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) **Daily Simple SOFR.** If Lender determines that Daily Simple SOFR cannot be determined in accordance with its definition for any day, the rate for that day shall be the most recently published Daily Simple SOFR, and the next Business Day on which Daily Simple SOFR is determinable shall apply. Lender's determination of the Applicable Rate shall be conclusive absent manifest error.

\(b\) **Benchmark Transition.** If Lender determines in its reasonable discretion that (i) Daily Simple SOFR has been permanently or indefinitely discontinued or is no longer representative, (ii) the administrator of Daily Simple SOFR has made a public statement to that effect, or (iii) the regulatory supervisor of the administrator has made a public statement to that effect, then Lender may designate a replacement benchmark rate, giving due consideration to (A) any replacement rate or successor rate then recommended for the applicable loan type by the Alternative Reference Rates Committee ("**ARRC**") and (B) any evolving or then-prevailing market convention for syndicated and commercial real estate construction loans in the United States for such replacement. Lender shall determine any spread adjustment (including any ARRC-recommended spread adjustment) and any conforming changes to the definition of Business Day, the interest payment dates, the lookback period and the timing and frequency of rate determinations. Lender shall provide Borrower with not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any such designation.

\(c\) **Conforming Changes.** Lender may make conforming changes to the timing and manner of determining the Applicable Rate, the interest payment dates and the lookback period, and such changes shall become effective without further consent of Borrower; provided that no such change shall increase the aggregate amount of interest payable by more than the sum of the applicable spread adjustment plus the replacement benchmark rate would produce.

\(d\) **SOFR Floor.** The SOFR Floor shall apply notwithstanding any discontinuation, replacement or negative rate.
:::

## 2.6 Default Rate

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of any Event of Default, the outstanding principal balance of the Loan and, to the extent permitted by applicable law, all other amounts payable hereunder (including past-due interest) shall bear interest at the Default Rate, compounded as provided in the Note, from the date of such Event of Default until the earlier of (a) the date on which such Event of Default is cured in a manner acceptable to Lender and (b) the date on which the Indebtedness is paid in full. The application of the Default Rate is not a waiver of, and does not cure, any Event of Default, and is in addition to all other remedies available to Lender. In no event shall the Default Rate exceed the Maximum Lawful Rate.
:::

## 2.7 Payments; Application

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
All payments shall be made in immediately available funds to the account designated by Lender in writing, not later than 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time on the due date. Lender shall apply each payment in the following order: (a) costs, expenses and indemnities then due; (b) Fees then due; (c) accrued and unpaid interest (including Default Rate interest); (d) principal (including any mandatory curtailment); and (e) all other amounts. Lender may reorder the application of payments to the extent permitted by law or if an Event of Default exists. Payments received after 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time shall be deemed received on the next Business Day.
:::

## 2.8 Computation; Rate Quotation

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Interest shall be computed on the basis of a 360-day year for the actual number of days elapsed. Upon Borrower's written request (not more frequently than monthly), Lender shall provide a written statement of the Daily Simple SOFR rate then in effect and the resulting Applicable Rate.
:::

## 2.9 Fees

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) **Origination Fee.** Borrower shall pay Lender an origination fee equal to one percent (1.00%) of the Loan Amount, or $475,000, payable in full on the Closing Date and fundable from the Initial Advance in accordance with the Approved Project Budget.

\(b\) **Unused Line Fee.** Borrower shall pay Lender an unused line fee at the rate of twenty-five hundredths of one percent (0.25%) per annum on the average daily undrawn amount of the commitment (calculated as the Loan Amount less the outstanding principal balance of the Loan), payable quarterly in arrears commencing on the first quarterly date following the Closing Date and on the date on which the Loan is paid in full. Borrower acknowledges that the unused line fee is an additional cost of the Loan that is not funded by any line item of the Approved Project Budget and shall be paid from Borrower's own funds.

\(c\) **Exit Fee.** Borrower shall pay Lender an exit fee, payable on the date the Loan is repaid in full, equal to: (i) one-half of one percent (0.50%) of the outstanding principal balance of the Loan at the time of repayment if the Loan is repaid at any time during the Construction Period; (ii) one-quarter of one percent (0.25%) of the outstanding principal balance of the Loan at the time of repayment if the Loan is repaid during the first six (6) months following the Construction Maturity Date; and (iii) zero (0%) if the Loan is repaid after the first six (6) months following the Construction Maturity Date. The exit fee is consideration for Lender's commitment to make the Loan and is not a prepayment premium.

\(d\) **Extension Fee.** If the Extension Option is exercised, Borrower shall pay Lender an extension fee equal to one-quarter of one percent (0.25%) of the then-outstanding principal balance of the Loan on or before the Mini-Perm Maturity Date.

\(e\) **Fees Earned.** All Fees are earned upon receipt, are non-refundable and, except as expressly provided in Section 2.9(a), shall not be paid from Loan proceeds.

\(f\) **Late Charge.** If any payment of principal, interest or Fees is not received within five (5) days after its due date, Borrower shall pay a late charge equal to five percent (5.00%) of the amount of such payment, which late charge is in addition to interest at the Default Rate and is intended to compensate Lender for administrative costs and not as a penalty.
:::

## 2.10 Mandatory Prepayments

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) **Casualty and Condemnation.** Borrower shall prepay the Loan with all net insurance proceeds and condemnation awards not applied to restoration in accordance with the Loan Documents, to the extent required therein.

\(b\) **Capital Events.** Upon any Capital Event, Borrower shall prepay the Loan in an amount equal to one hundred percent (100%) of the net cash proceeds thereof, together with all accrued interest, Fees (including the Exit Fee), and other amounts then due, unless Lender consents in writing to a substitute borrower or to the assumption of the Loan.

\(c\) **Illegality.** If Lender determines that any law, regulation or directive makes it unlawful for Lender to maintain or fund the Loan based on Daily Simple SOFR, Borrower shall prepay the Loan in full within thirty (30) days following written demand, together with all accrued interest, Fees and other amounts due.

\(d\) **Prepayment Premium.** Except for the Exit Fee payable under Section 2.9(c), no prepayment premium shall be payable; provided that the Exit Fee shall be payable upon each prepayment that results in payment in full of the Loan.
:::

## 2.11 Usury Savings

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, no provision of this Agreement or any other Loan Document shall require the payment or permit the collection of interest in excess of the Highest Lawful Rate. If any excess interest is provided for or determined to have been received, (a) the provisions of this Section shall govern and control, (b) neither Borrower nor any Guarantor shall be obligated to pay such excess, (c) any excess actually received by Lender shall be applied to reduce the principal balance of the Loan (or, if the Loan has been paid in full, refunded to Borrower), and (d) the effective rate of interest shall be automatically reduced to the Highest Lawful Rate. To the extent permitted by N.C.G.S. § 24-1.1, the parties intend that the Loan be governed by the provisions thereof applicable to loans in the principal amount of $25,000 or more.
:::

## 2.12 Maturity

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
The entire outstanding principal balance of the Loan, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, Fees and other amounts, shall be due and payable in full on the Maturity Date. Borrower shall not be entitled to any extension of the Maturity Date except pursuant to the Extension Option.
:::

## 2.13 Extension Option

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) **Grant.** Borrower shall have one (1) option (the **"Extension Option"**) to extend the Mini-Perm Maturity Date by six (6) months, to February 28, 2029 (the **"Extended Maturity Date"**), subject to satisfaction of each condition set forth on Exhibit G on or before the date that is sixty (60) days prior to the Mini-Perm Maturity Date.

\(b\) **Notice.** Borrower shall exercise the Extension Option, if at all, by irrevocable written notice delivered to Lender not less than sixty (60) days prior to the Mini-Perm Maturity Date, accompanied by the Extension Fee, the loan estimate or commitment for the Interest Rate Cap Agreement required by Exhibit G, and a certification that each condition on Exhibit G is satisfied.

\(c\) **No Automatic Extension.** If any condition on Exhibit G is not satisfied, the Extension Option shall not be effective and the Loan shall be due and payable on the Mini-Perm Maturity Date.

\(d\) **Drafting Note.** The Term Sheet provides that the Extension Option is "subject to satisfaction of customary conditions to be determined and set forth in the definitive Loan Agreement." The conditions on Exhibit G are drawn from Section 11 of the Credit Memorandum. Borrower has not agreed to them. Note in particular that Exhibit G requires a DSCR of 1.15x while Section 8.3 imposes a continuing covenant of 1.25x; the relationship between the two tests must be resolved (see Cover Memorandum, Issue 24).
:::

## 2.14 Interest Rate Protection

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
If required by Exhibit G or by Section 8.3 as a result of a DSCR shortfall, Borrower shall obtain and maintain one or more Interest Rate Cap Agreements with a counterparty and on terms reasonably acceptable to Lender. Borrower shall collaterally assign each Interest Rate Cap Agreement to Lender, and the counterparty shall acknowledge such assignment. The cost of any Interest Rate Cap Agreement shall be borne by Borrower from its own funds and is not included in the Approved Project Budget.
:::
