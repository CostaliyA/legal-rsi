# ARTICLE III — CONDITIONS PRECEDENT TO CLOSING

## 3.1 Conditions Precedent

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Lender's obligation to make the Initial Advance is subject to the prior or simultaneous satisfaction (or written waiver by Lender) of each of the following conditions, each in form and substance satisfactory to Lender and its counsel:

\(a\) **Loan Documents.** Execution and delivery of this Agreement, the Note, the Deed of Trust, the Assignment of Rents, the Security Agreement, the Construction Contract Assignment, the Architect Assignment, the Pledge Agreements, the Guaranties, the Environmental Indemnity, the Equity Escrow Agreement and each other Loan Document, together with all schedules, exhibits and UCC-1 financing statements required by Lender, in each case duly authorized, executed, acknowledged where required, and delivered.

\(b\) **Senior Loan Committee Approval.** Written approval of the Loan by Lender's Senior Loan Committee, in the amount and on the terms set forth in the Credit Memorandum, which approval shall not have been withdrawn, conditioned in a manner inconsistent with this Agreement, or expired.

\(c\) **Intercreditor Agreement.** Execution and delivery by Lender, Borrower and the Mezzanine Lender of the Intercreditor Agreement, which shall, at a minimum, (i) subordinate the Mezzanine Lender's pledge of membership interests to Lender's Membership Interest Pledge as a first-priority security interest, (ii) impose a standstill period of not less than ninety (90) days, (iii) grant the Mezzanine Lender notice and cure rights and a purchase option at par plus accrued interest, fees and costs, (iv) prohibit any modification of the Mezzanine Documents without Lender's prior written consent, (v) prohibit the Mezzanine Lender from filing or joining in any involuntary petition against Borrower, and (vi) acknowledge the affiliate relationship among Brightleaf Investors Group LLC, Brightleaf Mezzanine Capital LLC and Thomas R. Chen. **This condition may not be waived.**

\(d\) **Organizational Documents; Member Consents.** Delivery of (i) certified copies of Borrower's Articles of Organization and Amended and Restated Operating Agreement, as amended as required by Section 3.1(e) and Section 9.2, (ii) a certificate of good standing from the North Carolina Secretary of State dated not more than thirty (30) days prior to the Closing Date, and (iii) a unanimous written consent of all members of Borrower holding one hundred percent (100%) of the membership interests, expressly authorizing the Loan, the execution and delivery of the Loan Documents, the granting of the Liens (including the Membership Interest Pledge), the amendment of the Operating Agreement required hereby, the recording of the Deed Notice, and the transactions contemplated by the Intercreditor Agreement.

\(e\) **SPE Amendment; Independent Manager.** An amendment to Borrower's Operating Agreement, in form and substance satisfactory to Lender, that (i) incorporates the separateness covenants set forth in Article IX, (ii) requires the affirmative vote of at least one (1) Independent Manager for any voluntary bankruptcy filing, insolvency filing or general assignment for the benefit of creditors, and for any amendment of the bankruptcy-related provisions of the Operating Agreement, and (iii) provides that the Independent Manager may not be removed without Lender's prior written consent. Borrower shall deliver evidence reasonably satisfactory to Lender of the appointment of the Independent Manager concurrently with the Closing Date.

\(f\) **Non-Consolidation Opinion.** Delivery of a reasoned non-consolidation opinion of Calloway & Strauss PLLC, Borrower's counsel, addressed to Lender and dated the Closing Date, opining that, on the basis of the assumptions and qualifications stated therein and applying North Carolina law (with such reference to Delaware and other authority as counsel deems appropriate), a bankruptcy court would not substantively consolidate the assets and liabilities of Borrower with those of any Related Entity.

\(g\) **Equity Escrow Deposit.** Deposit of not less than $2,140,000 in cash into the Equity Escrow Account, together with (i) the Equity Escrow Agreement and (ii) evidence satisfactory to Lender that the source of such funds is not the Mezzanine Loan, any Affiliate of the Mezzanine Lender, Thomas R. Chen, Brightleaf Investors Group LLC, or any entity under common Control with any of the foregoing, including source-of-funds documentation and a certification of Borrower's Managing Member. **Drafting Note:** The Term Sheet does not impose an equity escrow condition, and the Credit Memorandum presents the equity escrow and a reduction of the Loan Amount as alternatives. The escrow does not cure the LTC exceedance unless it displaces Loan proceeds dollar-for-dollar (i.e., unless the Loan Amount is reduced to $45,360,000) or unless Total Project Costs are restated upward. See Cover Memorandum, Issues 1 through 4. Borrower's position on this condition and on the Loan Amount is reserved.

\(h\) **Title Policy and Payoff.** Issuance (or a binding commitment to issue at closing) of the Title Policy in an amount not less than $47,500,000, insuring the Deed of Trust as a valid first-priority Lien on both parcels, subject only to the Permitted Exceptions; and, with respect to the deed of trust in favor of Triangle Commercial Bank recorded in Book 6215, Page 892, Durham County Registry (the **"Prior Deed of Trust"**), delivery of (i) a current payoff letter from the holder thereof dated not more than thirty (30) days prior to the Closing Date, setting forth the outstanding principal balance, accrued interest and per diem, (ii) confirmation that funds sufficient to discharge the Prior Deed of Trust are identified in the sources and uses for the closing and are not required to fund any line item of the Approved Project Budget, and (iii) a recorded or recordable certificate of satisfaction or cancellation, or a title indemnity acceptable to Lender permitting deletion of the Prior Deed of Trust as an exception.

\(i\) **Survey.** Delivery of the Survey, and Lender's and the Title Company's receipt of the Survey in form satisfactory to each of them, deleting or modifying the standard survey exceptions and confirming the legal description in Exhibit A, the location of all easements and improvements, and the metes and bounds description of the Environmental Restricted Area. **Drafting Note:** The legal description in the Title Commitment is internally inconsistent (Parcel 2's northern boundary is described as 297.63 feet, but the same call is described as terminating at the northeast corner of Parcel 1, which lies 305.41 feet from the common corner — a 7.78-foot discrepancy). Exhibit A must be conformed to the Survey. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 17.

\(j\) **Environmental Deliverables.** Delivery of (i) the full Phase I Environmental Site Assessment dated October 28, 2024 (Sentinel Project No. SEC-2024-1147) together with a reliance letter in favor of Lender, (ii) the full Phase II Environmental Site Assessment dated August 30, 2024 (Sentinel Project No. SEC-2024-1089) together with a reliance letter in favor of Lender, (iii) the NFA Letter, (iv) evidence that the Deed Notice has been recorded in the Durham County Registry as required by condition 4 of the NFA Letter, or, if not recorded, a written extension or confirmation from NCDEQ that the deadline has been satisfied or extended, (v) NCDEQ's written approval of the Soil Management Plan, and (vi) the design of the Vapor Mitigation System by a licensed North Carolina professional engineer, together with evidence that the design and its cost are incorporated in the Approved Plans and Specifications and the Approved Project Budget. **Drafting Note:** See Cover Memorandum, Issues 23 through 27; the Credit Memorandum and the Term Sheet assume the Deed Notice has been recorded, the Phase I ESA records no evidence of recording, and the Title Commitment (which searches the public records through December 5, 2024) does not show it as an exception.

\(k\) **Resolution of the Residential Use Restriction.** Evidence satisfactory to Lender of the resolution of the conflict between the non-residential use restriction applicable to the Environmental Restricted Area and the placement of residential units above or within that area, by one or more of: (i) a written modification of the NFA Letter by NCDEQ permitting residential use subject to the Vapor Mitigation System and any additional engineering controls; (ii) redesign of the Project so that no residential unit, and no occupied residential space, is located within or above the Environmental Restricted Area, certified by the Project Architect and confirmed by overlay of the building floor plans on the environmental delineation; or (iii) a written determination of NCDEQ that the restriction applies only to the soils within the restricted area and does not prohibit residential occupancy of enclosed structures constructed above it with the Vapor Mitigation System in operation. At Lender's election, this condition may be satisfied as a condition to Advances for construction within or above the Environmental Restricted Area pursuant to Section 10.6 rather than as a condition to closing.

\(l\) **Brightleaf Historic District Association Approval.** Evidence that the Architectural Review Committee of the Brightleaf Historic District Association, Inc. has approved the Approved Plans and Specifications, or that the sixty (60) day deemed-approval period under Article V of the Declaration recorded in Book 6984, Page 1155, Durham County Registry, has elapsed without written action by the Architectural Review Committee, or a written opinion of Borrower's counsel (reasonably acceptable to Lender) that the Declaration does not require such approval for the Project. **Drafting Note:** The Appraisal states the design has been reviewed and approved by the Association; the Title Commitment states the Title Company has received no evidence of submission or approval. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 20.

\(m\) **Governmental Approvals.** Evidence of all building permits, grading permits, utility approvals and other governmental approvals necessary to commence and complete construction of the Project, and a zoning compliance letter from the City of Durham confirming that the Project is permitted as of right under the MU-D (Mixed Use — Downtown) zoning classification without variance, special use permit, conditional zoning or rezoning.

\(n\) **Construction Documents.** Delivery of the fully executed Construction Contract (GMP of $34,750,000) and Architect Agreement, each in form and substance satisfactory to Lender, together with Lender's receipt of a report from the Construction Inspector approving the Approved Plans and Specifications, the Construction Schedule and the sufficiency of the Approved Project Budget; and delivery of the General Contractor's current financial statements and evidence of payment and performance bonding capacity in amounts and form satisfactory to Lender. **Drafting Note:** The Term Sheet does not require contractor financial statements or bonds; the Credit Memorandum lists them as a diligence item. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 41.

\(o\) **Insurance.** Certificates of insurance and certified copies of policies evidencing the coverages required by Article XI, in form and substance satisfactory to Lender, naming Lender as mortgagee, loss payee and additional insured as its interests may appear.

\(p\) **Financial Statements and Certification.** Delivery of (i) Borrower's financial statements for the most recent fiscal year and interim period, together with a schedule reconciling Borrower's stated net worth of $14,200,000 to its balance sheet and to the assets and liabilities reflected in the Loan Documents, and (ii) current personal financial statements of each Guarantor, together with supporting liquidity evidence and federal income tax returns for the two most recent taxable years. **Drafting Note:** Borrower's stated net worth of $14,200,000 cannot be reconciled on the face of the record to a balance sheet consisting of the Property at a basis of $8,200,000 and the Prior Deed of Trust. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 31.

\(q\) **Legal Opinions.** Delivery of opinions of Borrower's counsel as to due organization, valid existence and good standing; due authorization and execution; enforceability of the Loan Documents; the valid creation and first priority of the Liens granted to Lender (subject to the Permitted Exceptions); the non-consolidation opinion required by Section 3.1(f); and such other matters as Lender may reasonably request.

\(r\) **Mezzanine Loan Closing.** Concurrent closing of the Mezzanine Loan, on terms consistent with the Intercreditor Agreement and the Term Sheet, and delivery of evidence that the Mezzanine Loan proceeds are applied solely in accordance with the sources and uses statement approved by Lender for the closing.

\(s\) **Casualty/Flood Determination.** A standard flood hazard determination confirming that no portion of the Property is located in a Special Flood Hazard Area, or, if any portion is, evidence of flood insurance in the amount required by Lender. **Drafting Note:** The Appraisal identifies FEMA panel 3710406400J (effective May 2, 2019) while the Phase I ESA identifies panel 3720098700J (effective March 2, 2009); both conclude Zone X. A current determination must be obtained. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 16.

\(t\) **Know Your Customer; OFAC.** Completion of Lender's customer due diligence and beneficial ownership verification with respect to Borrower, each member of Borrower, Thomas R. Chen and each Guarantor, with no adverse findings.

\(u\) **Expense Deposit Reconciliation.** Delivery of a statement reconciling the $75,000 expense deposit paid pursuant to Section 22 of the Term Sheet against Lender's costs and expenses incurred to date.

\(v\) **No Material Adverse Change.** No Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred, and no material adverse change shall have occurred in the financial condition of Borrower or any Guarantor, or in general real estate, credit market or interest rate conditions, in each case as determined by Lender in its reasonable discretion.

\(w\) **Closing Costs.** Payment of all Fees due at closing, all costs and expenses of Lender (including the fees and disbursements of Ashford, Cromdale Consulting & Laine LLP) and all title, recording, survey and appraisal costs, in each case to the extent not funded from the Initial Advance in accordance with the Approved Project Budget.

## 3.2 Waiver of Conditions

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Lender may, in its sole discretion, waive any condition precedent to closing in writing, and any such waiver shall be limited to the specific condition waived and shall not constitute a waiver of any other condition, any covenant, or any Event of Default. Lender shall not be deemed to have waived any condition by making the Initial Advance if Lender has not received written notice of the non-satisfaction of such condition.
:::
