# ARTICLE IV — ADVANCES; DISBURSEMENT PROCEDURES; INTEREST RESERVE

## 4.1 Advances Generally

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Lender shall make Advances in accordance with the Draw Schedule attached as Exhibit C. Each Advance shall be in the amount and for the milestone set forth in the Draw Schedule, and the aggregate of all Advances shall not exceed the Loan Amount. Lender's obligation to make any Advance is subject to (a) the satisfaction of each condition in Section 4.2 and (b) the absence of any Event of Default and of any condition that with notice or lapse of time, or both, would constitute an Event of Default. Amounts identified in the Draw Schedule as "contingency" or "developer fee" are not scheduled disbursements; they shall be advanced only upon Lender's written approval following satisfaction of Section 4.10 (contingency) or Section 7.5 (developer fee), respectively. **Drafting Note:** The Draw Schedule as delivered schedules contingency and developer fee amounts in Draws 2 through 9 notwithstanding that those items are reserves under the Approved Project Budget. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 15.
:::

## 4.2 Conditions Precedent to Each Advance

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Lender's obligation to make any Advance (including the Initial Advance and each subsequent Advance) is subject to the satisfaction of each of the following conditions as of the date of the Advance:

\(a\) **No Default.** No Event of Default, and no condition that with notice or lapse of time, or both, would constitute an Event of Default, shall exist under any Loan Document, the Mezzanine Documents or the Intercreditor Agreement.

\(b\) **Representations True.** Each representation and warranty in Article V and in each other Loan Document shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the date of the Advance with the same effect as if made on that date.

\(c\) **Requisition.** Delivery of a Requisition in the form of Exhibit J, executed by Borrower and certified by the Managing Member, not less than fifteen (15) days prior to the requested Advance date, together with the supporting documentation described in Section 4.2(d) through (j).

\(d\) **AIA Application for Payment.** A completed AIA Application and Certificate for Payment (Form G702) and Continuation Sheet (Form G703), certified by the Project Architect and the General Contractor, allocating the requested amount among the line items of the Approved Project Budget.

\(e\) **Construction Inspector's Report.** A report from the Construction Inspector (engaged by Lender at Borrower's expense) confirming (i) achievement of the milestone for the requested Advance, (ii) the percentage of completion claimed, (iii) that the work complies with the Approved Plans and Specifications, (iv) that the remaining undisbursed Loan proceeds and Borrower's committed equity are sufficient to complete the Project free of liens, and (v) the absence of defective work or material cost overruns.

\(f\) **Lien Waivers.** Conditional partial lien waivers in the statutory form prescribed by N.C.G.S. § 44A-23 (for the General Contractor, upon receipt of payment) and N.C.G.S. § 44A-24 (for subcontractors and materialmen) from the General Contractor and each subcontractor and materialman that has furnished labor or materials through the prior draw period, together with a certification from the General Contractor that all such waivers have been obtained and that no claimant has asserted a claim of lien.

\(g\) **Title Date-Down.** A date-down or bring-down endorsement to the Title Policy from the Title Company, effective as of a date not more than ten (10) days prior to the Advance, confirming the continued first-priority status of the Deed of Trust and the absence of any intervening Lien or encumbrance other than the Permitted Exceptions.

\(h\) **Interest Reserve Adequacy Test.** Evidence that the Interest Reserve Adequacy Test in Section 4.5(d) is satisfied, together with Borrower's deposit of any additional amount required by Section 4.5(e).

\(i\) **Equity First.** With respect to every Advance other than the Initial Advance, the Equity Escrow Deposit shall have been fully disbursed and applied to Project Costs in accordance with the Equity Escrow Agreement. **Drafting Note:** The "equity first" requirement of the Credit Memorandum conflicts with the Initial Advance of $9,475,000 made at zero percent (0%) completion, which disburses one hundred percent (100%) of Soft Costs, the entire Interest Reserve and the Origination Fee before any Borrower equity is at risk. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 14.

\(j\) **Insurance; Compliance Certificate.** Evidence of continued compliance with Article XI, and a compliance certificate in the form of Exhibit I certifying as to each financial covenant in Article VIII and each representation in Article V.

\(k\) **Budget Reconciliation.** An updated sources-and-uses reconciliation and construction budget reconciliation demonstrating that (i) the Project remains within the Approved Project Budget, (ii) no cost overrun exists, or, if one exists, that Borrower has deposited the amount of the overrun in accordance with Section 4.10, and (iii) the remaining undisbursed Loan proceeds plus Borrower's available equity are sufficient to complete the Project.

\(l\) **No Material Adverse Change.** No Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred since the Closing Date or the immediately preceding Advance.

\(m\) **Environmental Compliance.** Evidence of compliance with each condition of the NFA Letter and Article X, including, if construction has reached the Environmental Restricted Area, evidence that the conditions of Section 10.6 have been satisfied.
:::

## 4.3 Retainage

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Lender shall retain, from each Advance for Hard Costs, Retainage equal to ten percent (10%) of the Hard Costs covered by such Advance, resulting in aggregate Retainage of $3,475,000 (10% of the $34,750,000 GMP). Retainage shall be released as the final Advance (Draw 9) only upon satisfaction of each of the following: (a) issuance of a final certificate of occupancy by the City of Durham for all residential units and retail space in the Project; (b) delivery of final, unconditional lien waivers from the General Contractor, all subcontractors and all materialmen in the statutory forms prescribed by N.C.G.S. § 44A-23 and § 44A-24; (c) delivery of the Project Architect's certificate of final completion; (d) expiration of the one hundred twenty (120) day period for filing a claim of lien under N.C.G.S. § 44A-12, or, in the alternative, delivery of a lien bond in form and amount satisfactory to Lender; (e) delivery of as-built plans and specifications and all warranties, guaranties and manuals; and (f) confirmation from the Construction Inspector that the Project has been completed in accordance with the Approved Plans and Specifications and that no punch-list item exceeding $[50,000] in the aggregate remains outstanding. **Drafting Note:** The Term Sheet and the Credit Memorandum fix the retainage release at $3,275,000, which is $200,000 less than the 10% Retainage of $3,475,000 required by the same documents. The $200,000 difference is unexplained and must be reconciled. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 13.
:::

## 4.4 Disbursement Mechanics

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) Lender shall use commercially reasonable efforts to fund each approved Advance within ten (10) Business Days after Lender's receipt of a complete and conforming Requisition and all supporting documentation.

\(b\) Lender may, at its election, disburse any Advance directly to the Title Company for disbursement pursuant to the Title Company's closing and disbursement instructions, and Borrower's execution of a Requisition shall constitute irrevocable authorization for such direct disbursement.

\(c\) Lender shall not be obligated to make any Advance in an amount in excess of the lesser of (i) the amount requested and approved and (ii) the amount remaining available under the Loan Amount after reserving (A) the undisbursed Retainage, (B) the Interest Reserve Balance required for the remainder of the Construction Period, and (C) Lender's reasonable estimate of the cost to complete the Project.

\(d\) If Lender determines at any time that the undisbursed Loan proceeds, together with Borrower's committed and available equity, are insufficient to complete the Project free of liens, Lender may (i) suspend further Advances, and (ii) require Borrower or the Guarantors to deposit the deficiency with Lender. Any such deposit shall be held and disbursed by Lender for Project Costs before any further Advance is made.

\(e\) Lender's approval of any Advance shall not constitute approval of the work or a waiver of any defect, and shall not relieve Borrower, the General Contractor or the Project Architect of any obligation.
:::

## 4.5 Interest Reserve

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) **Funding.** On the Closing Date, Lender shall fund the Interest Reserve of $4,850,000 from the proceeds of the Initial Advance, and shall record the Interest Reserve as a ledger sub-account of the Loan (the **"Interest Reserve Account"**). The Interest Reserve is not a segregated deposit of Borrower and does not constitute trust funds; amounts disbursed from the Interest Reserve Account are applied to interest accruing on the Loan and do not reduce the outstanding principal balance of the Loan, except to the extent the Loan Documents provide otherwise.

\(b\) **Application.** So long as the Interest Reserve Balance is sufficient, Lender shall apply the Interest Reserve to interest accruing on the Loan during the Construction Period on each interest payment date. The Interest Reserve shall not be applied to principal, Fees, Default Rate interest, or interest accruing after the Construction Maturity Date.

\(c\) **Statement.** Lender shall deliver to Borrower, concurrently with each monthly interest statement, a reconciliation of the Interest Reserve Balance, the interest accrued during the period, and the projected remaining interest cost through the Construction Maturity Date.

\(d\) **Interest Reserve Adequacy Test.** At each Requisition, and in any event not less frequently than quarterly, Lender shall perform an adequacy test comparing (i) the Interest Reserve Balance to (ii) the projected interest cost through the earlier of the next anticipated Advance date or ninety (90) days from the date of the test, in each case determined at the Applicable Rate then in effect and the then-projected outstanding principal balance (the **"Interest Reserve Adequacy Test"**). **Drafting Note:** The Draw Schedule's own analysis projects a surplus of $221,325 assuming Daily Simple SOFR of 3.50%; the same analysis at 4.35% (the rate used throughout the Credit Memorandum and the Term Sheet) projects a shortfall of approximately $365,830; and the Credit Memorandum projects a shortfall of $565,000. The three results cannot all be correct. See Cover Memorandum, Issues 9 and 10.

\(e\) **Replenishment.** If the Interest Reserve Adequacy Test is not satisfied at any time, Borrower shall, within five (5) Business Days after written notice from Lender (and, if the deficiency is identified in connection with a Requisition, before the requested Advance is funded), deposit into the Interest Reserve Account an amount sufficient to satisfy the test. All such deposits shall be made from Borrower's own funds, and may not be made from Loan proceeds, Mezzanine Loan proceeds, or any Distribution.

\(f\) **Guarantor Backstop.** If Borrower fails to fund any deposit required by Section 4.5(e), the Guarantors shall be obligated to fund such amount on demand pursuant to the Carry Guaranty.

\(g\) **No Waiver.** Lender's acceptance of any deposit or its failure to perform the Interest Reserve Adequacy Test at any time shall not constitute a waiver of Borrower's obligations under this Section, nor a release of the Guarantors under the Carry Guaranty.
:::

## 4.6 Equity Funding

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Borrower shall fund all equity required to complete the Project, including the Equity Escrow Deposit and all cost overruns, from its own funds and from sources approved in writing by Lender. Borrower shall not fund any portion of the Equity Escrow Deposit or any cost overrun from Loan proceeds, Mezzanine Loan proceeds, any Affiliate of the Mezzanine Lender, or any member loan that is secured by the Property or the membership interests in Borrower.
:::

## 4.7 Construction Inspector

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Lender shall engage, at Borrower's expense and pursuant to a scope of work approved by Lender, a third-party construction inspector (the **"Construction Inspector"**) to review the Approved Plans and Specifications, the Construction Schedule, the Approved Project Budget and the progress of construction, and to deliver the reports required by Section 4.2(e) and Section 6.10. Borrower shall provide the Construction Inspector with full access to the Property, the Construction Documents and the General Contractor's records, and shall cause the General Contractor and the Project Architect to cooperate with the Construction Inspector.
:::

## 4.8 Direct Costs of Lender

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
All fees and expenses of the Construction Inspector, the Title Company, the Surveyor, the appraiser, the environmental consultant and Lender's counsel shall be borne by Borrower as provided in Section 18.5, and shall be paid from Borrower's own funds to the extent not included in the Approved Project Budget.
:::

## 4.9 Change Orders

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) Borrower shall deliver to Lender each proposed change order, change directive, construction change or field order under the Construction Contract or the Architect Agreement (each, a **"Change Order"**) not less than five (5) Business Days prior to execution, accompanied by the General Contractor's and Project Architect's written estimate of the cost and schedule impact.

\(b\) Lender's prior written consent shall be required for any Change Order that, individually or in the aggregate with all other Change Orders since the Closing Date, (i) increases the GMP by more than $[100,000], (ii) reduces the scope of the Project in any material respect, (iii) delays Substantial Completion, (iv) affects the structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, life-safety, building-envelope or garage-vapor-mitigation systems, or (v) is inconsistent with the Approved Plans and Specifications or with any condition of the NFA Letter.

\(c\) No Change Order shall be effective, and Borrower shall not be permitted to pay for any Change Order from Loan proceeds, unless (i) the Change Order is approved by Lender in writing and (ii) the cost thereof is funded from the contingency in the Approved Project Budget or from Borrower's equity deposited with Lender.
:::

## 4.10 Contingency and Cost Overruns

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) The contingency line items in the Approved Project Budget ($1,140,000 hard cost contingency and $300,000 soft cost contingency) are reserves. They shall be advanced only upon Lender's written approval of a specific Requisition identifying the cost, its cause and the reason it is not covered by a budgeted line item.

\(b\) Any Project Cost in excess of the Approved Project Budget (a **"Cost Overrun"**) shall be funded by Borrower from its own funds (and, failing that, by the Guarantors pursuant to the Completion Guaranty) before Lender is obligated to make any further Advance. Borrower shall deposit the amount of any Cost Overrun with Lender or the Title Company in immediately available funds upon Lender's written demand.

\(c\) Borrower's obligation to fund Cost Overruns is not limited to the contingency amounts and is not released by Lender's approval of any Advance, Change Order or budget modification.
:::

## 4.11 Suspension and Termination of Advances

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Lender may, without liability, suspend or terminate its obligation to make Advances if (a) any Event of Default exists, (b) any condition in Section 4.2 is not satisfied, (c) Lender determines that the Project cannot be completed in accordance with the Approved Plans and Specifications, the Approved Project Budget and the Construction Schedule within the Loan Amount and Borrower's committed equity, (d) any condition of the NFA Letter is breached, (e) construction has not reached a milestone set forth in the Construction Schedule within [thirty (30)] days after the scheduled date, or (f) the Construction Inspector reports defective work, an unbonded lien, or a cost to complete that exceeds available funds. Suspension shall not constitute a waiver of any Event of Default.
:::
