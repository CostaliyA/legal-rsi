Borrower represents and warrants to Lender, as of the Effective Date, the Closing Date and each date on which an Advance is made, as follows:

## 5.1 Organization; Power; Good Standing

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Borrower is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of North Carolina (File No. 1923847), and has all requisite power and authority to own the Property, to carry on its business as presently conducted and as proposed to be conducted, and to execute, deliver and perform its obligations under the Loan Documents. ## 5.2 Authorization; Enforceability

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) Borrower has delivered to Lender true and complete copies of the Phase I ESA, the Phase II ESA, the NFA Letter and the Deed Notice, and there are no other environmental reports, investigations, notices of violation, orders, consent agreements or claims relating to the Property that have not been delivered to Lender.

# ARTICLE VI — AFFIRMATIVE COVENANTS

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Borrower covenants and agrees that, from the Closing Date and until the Indebtedness is paid in full and the Loan Documents are terminated, Borrower shall:
:::

## 6.1 Existence; Good Standing

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Maintain its existence and good standing as a North Carolina limited liability company; maintain all qualifications, licenses, permits and approvals necessary for the ownership of the Property and the construction and operation of the Project; and remain in compliance with Article IX.
:::

## 6.2 Completion of the Project

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) Construct and complete the Project in a good and workmanlike manner, free of liens and in accordance with the Approved Plans and Specifications, the Approved Project Budget, the Construction Schedule, applicable law and the conditions of the NFA Letter.

\(b\) Achieve Substantial Completion on or before the Construction Maturity Date, and achieve a final certificate of occupancy for all residential units and retail space on or before the date that is ninety (90) days after Substantial Completion. **Drafting Note:** The guaranteed completion date under Section 7.1 of the Term Sheet and under the Completion Guaranty is August 28, 2027, while the Project's projected substantial completion date, as reflected in the Term Sheet, the Credit Memorandum and the Appraisal, is August 31, 2027. The completion obligation as drafted is three (3) days earlier than the Project can be completed on its own schedule. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 28.

\(c\) Cause the General Contractor and each subcontractor to perform in accordance with the Construction Documents; not permit any work to be performed outside the Approved Plans and Specifications except pursuant to an approved Change Order; and not permit any construction to commence before the Deed of Trust has been recorded.

\(d\) Promptly cure any defective work, and replace any defective or non-conforming materials at Borrower's sole cost.

\(e\) Comply with the General Contractor's warranty obligations and enforce and assign to Lender, upon Lender's request, all warranties, guaranties and sureties relating to the Project.

\(f\) Achieve each milestone set forth in Exhibit D within the number of days after the scheduled milestone date specified therein (and in any event not more than ninety (90) days behind the Construction Schedule at any time, consistent with Section 14.1(i)).
:::

## 6.3 Plans and Specifications

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Not modify, amend or deviate from the Approved Plans and Specifications in any material respect without Lender's prior written consent, and not permit any modification that would (a) reduce the number of residential units or retail square footage, (b) alter the unit mix, (c) affect the structural, life-safety, building envelope or vapor mitigation systems, (d) affect compliance with the NFA Letter, or (e) increase Total Project Costs, in each case without Lender's prior written consent. Borrower shall deliver to Lender each revision of the Approved Plans and Specifications promptly upon issuance.
:::

## 6.4 Insurance

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Maintain in full force and effect each insurance policy required by Article XI, and deliver to Lender evidence of renewal not less than ten (10) days prior to the expiration of each policy.
:::

## 6.5 Taxes and Liens

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
