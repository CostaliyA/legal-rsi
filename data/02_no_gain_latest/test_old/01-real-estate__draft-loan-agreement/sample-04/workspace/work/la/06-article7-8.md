# ARTICLE VII — NEGATIVE COVENANTS

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Borrower covenants and agrees that, from the Closing Date and until the Indebtedness is paid in full and the Loan Documents are terminated, Borrower shall not, directly or indirectly, without Lender's prior written consent:
:::

## 7.1 Indebtedness

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Create, incur, assume, guarantee or permit to exist any Indebtedness or other obligation for borrowed money, other than (a) the Loan, (b) the Mezzanine Loan in the original principal amount of $9,200,000 on the terms described in Section 5.12, (c) trade payables incurred in the ordinary course of business and not more than [sixty (60)] days past due, and (d) equipment leases entered into in the ordinary course of business not exceeding $[250,000] in the aggregate. Borrower shall not incur any preferred equity, mezzanine, gap or subordinate financing other than the Mezzanine Loan.
:::

## 7.2 Liens

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Create, incur, assume or permit to exist any Lien on the Property or any other Collateral, or on any membership interest in Borrower, other than (a) the Permitted Exceptions, (b) the Liens granted to Lender, and (c) the second-priority pledge of membership interests in favor of the Mezzanine Lender permitted by the Intercreditor Agreement.
:::

## 7.3 Transfers

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Sell, transfer, convey, assign, lease (other than Leases permitted by Section 6.12), option, mortgage, pledge or otherwise dispose of the Property or any portion thereof or any interest therein, whether directly or indirectly, whether voluntarily or involuntarily, or enter into any agreement to do any of the foregoing.
:::

## 7.4 Changes in Ownership; Organizational Documents

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) Cause, permit or suffer any change in the direct or indirect ownership of the membership interests in Borrower, or any issuance of any additional membership interest, profits interest, option or right to acquire an interest in Borrower, or any transfer, pledge, hypothecation or encumbrance of any membership interest (other than the Membership Interest Pledge and the pledge to the Mezzanine Lender permitted by the Intercreditor Agreement).

\(b\) Amend, modify, restate or waive any provision of Borrower's Articles of Organization or Operating Agreement in any manner that (i) affects the SPE covenants or the Independent Manager provisions required by Section 3.1(e), (ii) affects the distribution waterfall or the capital call provisions, (iii) increases the approval threshold applicable to any action of Borrower, or (iv) could reasonably be expected to impair Lender's rights or the value of the Collateral.
:::

## 7.5 Distributions

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Make any Distribution, except that, provided no Event of Default has occurred and is continuing and no Cost Overrun, Interest Reserve deficiency or unfunded equity obligation exists, Borrower may:
:::

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) pay the Developer Fee solely to the extent and in the manner permitted by Section 6.11;

\(b\) pay its actual, reasonable operating expenses incurred in the ordinary course of business;

\(c\) make distributions of Available Cash to its members during the Mini-Perm Period, if and only if (i) Substantial Completion has occurred and a final certificate of occupancy has been issued for all residential units and retail space, (ii) occupancy of at least [85]% of the residential units has been achieved, (iii) the DSCR is not less than 1.25x and the Debt Yield is not less than [8.50]%, in each case calculated on the most recent trailing twelve-month period, (iv) no Interest Reserve deficiency exists and all required deposits have been made, and (v) Borrower has delivered a compliance certificate demonstrating the foregoing; and

\(d\) nothing in this Section 7.5 shall permit, at any time, (i) any repayment of, or payment of interest on, any member loan or member advance, including any advance used to acquire the Property or to fund the environmental remediation of the Property, except from Borrower's own funds in compliance with this Section and after satisfaction of the conditions in clause (c) above, or (ii) any Distribution funded, directly or indirectly, from Loan proceeds or Mezzanine Loan proceeds.

**Drafting Note:** Section 14 of the Term Sheet prohibits Distributions during the Construction Period "except for payment of the developer fee in accordance with the approved Project Budget," but the Approved Project Budget identifies Borrower itself as the payee of the Developer Fee, so the carve-out does not in terms authorize any payment to a member. The carve-out also permits $1,835,000 of Loan proceeds to be applied to a fee payable to the Managing Member-controlled Borrower before any equity is at risk. Lender's underwriting assumes that no Distributions occur during the Construction Period. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 7.
:::

## 7.6 Affiliate Transactions

# ARTICLE VIII — FINANCIAL COVENANTS

## 8.1 Loan-to-Cost Ratio

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
The Loan-to-Cost Ratio shall not exceed eighty percent (80%) at any time. **"Loan-to-Cost Ratio"** or **"LTC"** means the ratio of (a) the outstanding principal balance of the Loan to (b) Total Project Costs. **"Total Project Costs"** means the aggregate of all costs and expenses incurred or to be incurred in connection with the acquisition, development, construction and completion of the Project, as set forth in the Approved Project Budget as in effect from time to time, including the cost of the Property, Hard Costs, Soft Costs, the Interest Reserve, the Origination Fee, the Developer Fee and contingency, and excluding, solely for purposes of this ratio, the Mezzanine Loan.

Testing: As of the Closing Date, the Loan-to-Cost Ratio is $47,500,000 ÷ $56,700,000 = 83.77%, which exceeds the eighty percent (80%) maximum. At an eighty percent (80%) Loan-to-Cost Ratio, the maximum Loan Amount would be $45,360,000, a reduction of $2,140,000. **Drafting Note:** The Condition Precedent in Section 3.1(g) requires a $2,140,000 Equity Escrow Deposit but does not reduce the Loan Amount. Because the Approved Project Budget allocates one hundred percent (100%) of Total Project Costs to the Loan and the Mezzanine Loan, an Equity Escrow Deposit that does not displace Loan proceeds has no effect on the Loan-to-Cost Ratio as defined above; it creates a corresponding surplus of sources over uses. Lender's credit approval contemplates that the escrowed equity will be applied to Project Costs ahead of Loan Advances; that result is achievable only if the Loan Amount is simultaneously reduced to $45,360,000 or Total Project Costs are restated upward. Borrower's and Lender's positions on the Loan Amount are reserved pending resolution. See Cover Memorandum, Issues 1 through 4.
:::

## 8.2 Loan-to-Value Ratio

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) The Loan-to-Value Ratio determined on an as-completed basis shall not exceed sixty-five percent (65%). **"Loan-to-Value Ratio"** or **"LTV"** means the ratio of the outstanding principal balance of the Loan to the appraised value of the Property on the applicable basis, as determined by an MAI appraisal conforming to the requirements of FIRREA and Lender's appraisal policy.

\(b\) As of the Closing Date, (i) the as-completed Loan-to-Value Ratio is $47,500,000 ÷ $72,800,000 = 65.25%, which exceeds the sixty-five percent (65%) maximum by $180,000 in principal amount ($72,800,000 × 65% = $47,320,000); and (ii) the as-stabilized Loan-to-Value Ratio is $47,500,000 ÷ $76,200,000 = 62.34%.

\(c\) Lender may obtain a new or updated appraisal at any time at Borrower's expense, and Borrower shall reimburse Lender for the cost thereof within ten (10) days after demand.

**Drafting Note:** The Term Sheet limits the as-completed Loan-to-Value Ratio to 65% (Sections 4 and 6.2). The Credit Memorandum applies a 70% policy limit and reports the as-completed ratio as a "Pass" at 65.25%. The executed Term Sheet governs as between the parties, so the Loan as sized exceeds the as-completed limit. The same $2,140,000 reduction that cures the Loan-to-Cost exceedance would also reduce the as-completed Loan-to-Value Ratio to 62.31% and the as-stabilized ratio to 59.53%. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 2.
:::

## 8.3 Debt Service Coverage Ratio

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) The DSCR shall not be less than 1.25x, tested (i) at the commencement of the Mini-Perm Period and (ii) annually thereafter, in each case based on the Net Operating Income for the most recent twelve (12) month period.

\(b\) **Cure.** If the DSCR is less than 1.25x at any test date, Borrower shall, at Lender's election exercised in writing within thirty (30) days after delivery of the applicable compliance certificate, either (i) make a principal curtailment sufficient to restore the DSCR to 1.25x, or (ii) deposit cash collateral in an amount determined by Lender sufficient to restore the DSCR to 1.25x. Any principal curtailment shall be accompanied by payment of the Exit Fee allocable to the amount curtailed.

\(c\) **Drafting Note.** The first test date precedes stabilization. Substantial Completion is projected for August 31, 2027, and stabilization (95% occupancy) is projected for approximately May 2028; the Mini-Perm Period commences August 29, 2027. A test based on trailing twelve-month actual Net Operating Income at the commencement of the Mini-Perm Period would necessarily reflect a partially leased project and would be very likely to fail. Lender's practice is to test on a stabilized basis with a lease-up adjustment. Borrower and Lender must agree on the test basis. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 30.
:::

## 8.4 Debt Yield

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
The Debt Yield shall not be less than [eight and fifty hundredths percent (8.50%)], tested at the commencement of the Mini-Perm Period and annually thereafter. This covenant does not appear in the Term Sheet and is proposed by Lender to address the absence of any coverage test that measures the Loan against the Project's income capacity independently of the interest rate. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 30.
:::

## 8.5 Borrower Net Worth

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Borrower shall maintain a minimum net worth of not less than $12,000,000, tested annually within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, and certified in a compliance certificate. For purposes of this covenant, net worth shall be determined in accordance with GAAP.

**Drafting Note:** Borrower's asserted net worth of $14,200,000 cannot be reconciled to the balance sheet implicit in the record. Borrower's only identified asset is the Property, acquired for $8,200,000 and encumbered by the Prior Deed of Trust in the original principal amount of $3,400,000. That balance sheet would produce a net worth of approximately $4,800,000. Following closing, Borrower's assets and liabilities increase roughly dollar-for-dollar with Loan and Mezzanine Loan advances, so the covenant would not be cured by construction. The covenant as drafted would therefore be breached on the Closing Date unless the capital structure is restated or the covenant is redefined to permit add-backs for the Property at appraised value and for contributions. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 31.
:::

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}

As of the Closing Date, the Guarantors' combined liquidity is approximately $9,300,000 ($6,100,000 attributable to Elena Vasquez-Torres and $3,200,000 attributable to Reginald K. Osei), which satisfies the covenant with a cushion of approximately $4,300,000.
:::

## 8.8 Testing Mechanics; Calculations; Adjustments

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) Each financial covenant shall be tested as of the last day of the applicable period and evidenced by a compliance certificate in the form of Exhibit I delivered within the time period specified in Exhibit F.

\(b\) Net Operating Income shall be determined on an actual basis for the trailing twelve (12) month period, and shall be annualized only if the Project has been in operation for fewer than twelve (12) months, in which case Net Operating Income shall be annualized by dividing the Net Operating Income for the period of operation by the number of months of operation and multiplying by twelve. Annualization shall not be permitted after the Project has been in operation for twelve (12) months.

\(c\) Excluded from Net Operating Income: (i) rent from any tenant that is more than sixty (60) days in arrears, (ii) rent from any tenant that is a Related Entity, (iii) any revenue from a unit or space that is not lawfully occupied (including any residential unit within or above the Environmental Restricted Area as to which a certificate of occupancy has not been issued), (iv) security deposits and non-recurring income, and (v) the amount of any CC&R assessment payable to the Brightleaf Historic District Association in excess of the amount reflected in Borrower's operating budget.

\(d\) In determining compliance, Lender may make adjustments for non-recurring items, above-market or below-market leases, and items that Lender reasonably determines do not reflect normalized operations.

**Drafting Note:** The Appraised Value and the Appraiser's projected Stabilized Net Operating Income of $4,876,800 do not include the annual assessment payable to the Brightleaf Historic District Association under the Declaration in Book 6984, Page 1155, which is $0.12 per square foot of gross building area, or approximately $34,500 per annum for 287,500 square feet. Adjusting Net Operating Income for that assessment reduces it to approximately $4,842,300, reduces the DSCR to approximately 1.30x and reduces the indicated as-stabilized value at the appraiser's 6.40% capitalization rate to approximately $75,660,900 (and the Loan-to-Value Ratio to approximately 62.78%). See Cover Memorandum, Issue 29.
:::
