# ARTICLE X — ENVIRONMENTAL MATTERS

## 10.1 Acknowledgement of Environmental Condition

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Borrower acknowledges that (a) the Phase I ESA identified one Recognized Environmental Condition at the Property, consisting of residual petroleum contamination from a former 2,000-gallon diesel underground storage tank located in the northeast corner of Parcel 0821-04-72-4102; (b) remediation consisting of excavation and off-site disposal of impacted soil was completed in September 2024; (c) residual contamination remains in place in the Environmental Restricted Area at concentrations exceeding NCDEQ residential Preliminary Soil Remediation Goals for TPH-DRO and benzene in soil, and benzene in soil gas exceeds NCDEQ residential vapor intrusion screening levels; and (d) the NFA Letter is conditional, and the Property is not a "clean closure" site.
:::

## 10.2 Compliance with the NFA Letter

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Borrower shall comply with each condition of the NFA Letter, including:
:::

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) **Vapor Mitigation System.** Design, install, commission, operate and maintain a passive sub-slab depressurization system with conversion capability to active depressurization (the **"Vapor Mitigation System"**) beneath each enclosed structure constructed within or over the Environmental Restricted Area, in accordance with the NFA Letter, the guidelines of the NCDEQ Division of Waste Management and the design of a professional engineer licensed in North Carolina. Borrower shall deliver to Lender and to NCDEQ as-built drawings and a system performance verification report within ninety (90) days after completion of the building foundation in the affected area, and shall deliver a copy of the system's operating and maintenance manual to Lender.

\(b\) **Land Use Restriction.** Restrict the Environmental Restricted Area to non-residential use, and not construct, occupy, lease or permit the occupancy of any residential unit or other sensitive use within or above the Environmental Restricted Area unless and until the NFA Letter is modified in accordance with Section 10.4.

\(c\) **Soil Management Plan.** Submit a soil management plan to NCDEQ for approval, and comply with it, prior to any excavation, grading or other subsurface disturbance within the Environmental Restricted Area. Borrower shall cause the Soil Management Plan to be incorporated into the Construction Documents and delivered to the General Contractor and each subcontractor performing work in the affected area.

\(d\) **Deed Notice.** Record, and maintain of record, a deed notice in the Durham County Registry of Deeds in the form required by the NFA Letter, describing the Environmental Restricted Area by metes and bounds (or by reference to a recorded plat), the nature and extent of residual contamination, and the non-residential use restriction. Borrower shall deliver to Lender a recorded copy, together with evidence of the recording date, promptly following recording.

\(e\) **Reporting.** Deliver to Lender, within ten (10) Business Days after delivery or receipt, copies of all correspondence, submissions, approvals, notices and reports exchanged with NCDEQ relating to the Property, and annual certifications of continued compliance with each condition of the NFA Letter.
:::

## 10.3 No Reopening; No Adverse Modification

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Borrower shall not take, and shall not permit any tenant or contractor to take, any action that would cause NCDEQ to reopen the regulatory file for Site ID IHSB-24-1203, revoke or modify the NFA Letter adversely, or require additional investigation or remediation, including any change in land use, any unpermitted subsurface disturbance, or any failure to maintain the Vapor Mitigation System.
:::

## 10.4 Modification of the NFA Letter

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Borrower may seek a modification of the NFA Letter to permit residential use of the Environmental Restricted Area only with Lender's prior written consent (which may be conditioned on Lender's review and approval of the proposed scope of work, the additional investigation and risk assessment, the engineering controls and the cost thereof). Borrower shall bear all costs of any such modification and shall deposit the amount of any additional cost with Lender before it is incurred. No modification shall be deemed effective for purposes of Section 10.2(b) until NCDEQ has issued a written modification and Borrower has delivered a copy to Lender.
:::

## 10.5 Environmental Indemnity; Pollution Legal Liability Insurance

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Borrower and the Guarantors shall indemnify Lender as provided in the Environmental Indemnity, which shall be unlimited in amount and shall survive repayment of the Loan, reconveyance of the Deed of Trust, foreclosure, deed in lieu of foreclosure and any bankruptcy proceeding. Lender recommends, and may require as a condition to the Extension Option, that Borrower obtain a pollution legal liability insurance policy in an amount and on terms reasonably acceptable to Lender, naming Lender as an additional insured, covering third-party claims, the cost of any NFA modification, additional remediation and unknown conditions discovered during construction.

## 10.6 Condition to Advances in the Environmental Restricted Area

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, Lender shall not be obligated to make any Advance for Hard Costs attributable to construction within or above the Environmental Restricted Area unless and until (a) the conditions in Section 3.1(k) have been satisfied, or (b) the NFA Letter has been modified in accordance with Section 10.4 to permit the planned use, and (c) NCDEQ has approved the Soil Management Plan and the Vapor Mitigation System design, and (d) the Construction Inspector has confirmed that the work will be performed in accordance with the approved Soil Management Plan.

Borrower shall, at its sole cost, promptly undertake and diligently complete all investigation, remediation, removal and response actions required by applicable Environmental Laws or by NCDEQ, and shall deliver to Lender all reports and confirmations relating thereto.

## 13.4 Guaranty Terms

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Each Guaranty shall (a) be a continuing, absolute and unconditional guaranty of payment and not of collection, (b) waive all defenses of suretyship, including the defenses of marshalling, election of remedies, and the release or modification of any other obligor or collateral, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, (c) not be released, diminished or impaired by any foreclosure, deed in lieu of foreclosure, or deficiency following foreclosure, (d) not be released by any modification or extension of the Loan Documents, (e) be governed by North Carolina law, and (f) survive the repayment of the Loan to the extent of any indemnity or reimbursement obligation that by its terms survives.

**Drafting Note:** North Carolina is a judicial foreclosure state and, following a power-of-sale foreclosure, a deficiency is recoverable only as permitted by N.C.G.S. § 45-21.36, which requires the court to credit the fair market value of the property against the debt if the property was sold for less than its fair market value.
