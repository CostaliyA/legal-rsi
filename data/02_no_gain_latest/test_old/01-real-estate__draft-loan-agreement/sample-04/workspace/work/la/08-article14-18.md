# ARTICLE XIV — EVENTS OF DEFAULT

## 14.1 Events of Default

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Each of the following shall constitute an **"Event of Default"** under this Agreement and the other Loan Documents:
:::

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) **Payment.** Borrower fails to pay any principal, interest, Fee or other amount due under any Loan Document when due, and such failure continues for five (5) days after the due date.

\(b\) **Interest Reserve Deficiency.** Borrower fails to fund any deposit required by Section 4.5(e) within five (5) Business Days after written notice.

\(c\) **Representations.** Any representation or warranty made by Borrower or any Guarantor in any Loan Document or in any certificate delivered thereunder proves to have been false or misleading in any material respect when made or deemed made.

\(d\) **Covenants (Non-Monetary).** Borrower fails to observe or perform any covenant or agreement contained in any Loan Document (other than a covenant described in another clause of this Section) and such failure continues for thirty (30) days after the earlier of (i) written notice from Lender and (ii) the date Borrower knew or reasonably should have known of the failure; provided that if such failure is capable of cure but cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days and Borrower is diligently pursuing cure, the cure period shall be extended by an additional thirty (30) days.

\(e\) **Specified Covenants Not Subject to Cure.** Borrower breaches any covenant in Section 4.5(f), Article IX, Article X, Section 6.11, Article VII or Article VIII, and, in the case of a breach of Article VIII, such breach continues for thirty (30) days after Lender's written notice.

\(f\) **Voluntary Bankruptcy.** Borrower, any Guarantor or any member of Borrower commences any case, proceeding or other action under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization, receivership or similar law, consents to the appointment of a receiver or trustee, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or admits in writing its inability to pay its debts generally as they become due.

\(g\) **Involuntary Bankruptcy.** Any case, proceeding or other action is commenced against Borrower, any Guarantor or any member of Borrower seeking to have an order for relief entered or seeking reorganization, liquidation or appointment of a receiver, trustee or custodian, and such case is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

\(h\) **Material Adverse Change.** A Material Adverse Effect occurs, as determined by Lender in its reasonable discretion.

\(i\) **Construction Delay.** The Project is more than ninety (90) days behind the Construction Schedule at any time during the Construction Period (a **"Construction Delay Default"**), or Borrower fails to achieve Substantial Completion on or before the date required by Section 6.2(b).

\(j\) **Abandonment.** Borrower or the General Contractor abandons the Project or suspends construction for more than thirty (30) consecutive days (other than as a result of a force majeure event approved by Lender).

\(k\) **Insurance.** Borrower fails to maintain any insurance required by Article XI.

\(l\) **Liens.** Any Lien (other than a Permitted Exception) is filed, recorded or otherwise attaches to the Property or any other Collateral and is not released or bonded over within thirty (30) days.

\(m\) **Cross-Default; Mezzanine.** A default or event of default occurs under (i) the Mezzanine Documents or the Intercreditor Agreement, (ii) any other document evidencing Indebtedness of Borrower or any Guarantor, or (iii) the Construction Contract, the Architect Agreement or any other material agreement to which Borrower is a party, and in each case is not cured within any applicable cure period.

\(n\) **Environmental.** Borrower fails to comply with any condition of the NFA Letter, any Environmental Law or Article X, any release of a hazardous substance occurs at the Property, or NCDEQ or any other authority issues any order, directive, notice of violation or lien relating to the Property that is not stayed or discharged within thirty (30) days.

\(o\) **Judgments.** One or more money judgments are entered against Borrower or any Guarantor in an aggregate amount exceeding $500,000 (not covered by insurance as to which the insurer has acknowledged coverage in writing) and remains unsatisfied, unbonded or unstayed for thirty (30) days.

\(p\) **Guarantor.** Any Guarantor revokes, terminates, disaffirms, contests or disclaims any obligation under any Guaranty, any Guaranty becomes unenforceable in any material respect, any Guarantor fails to comply with Section 8.6 or 8.7, or any Guarantor commences any action seeking to limit its liability under any Guaranty.

\(q\) **Guarantor Financial Decline.** The combined net worth of the Guarantors decreases by more than [thirty-five percent (35%)] from the combined net worth reflected in the most recent personal financial statements delivered to Lender, or any Guarantor's net worth decreases below the threshold established under Section 8.7.

\(r\) **Failure to Deliver Reports.** Borrower fails to deliver any financial statement, compliance certificate, construction progress report, interest reserve reconciliation or other report required by Exhibit F, and such failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after written notice.

\(s\) **SPE.** Borrower breaches any covenant in Article IX, or any Related Entity takes any action inconsistent with Borrower's separate existence.

\(t\) **Change of Control.** Any change in the direct or indirect ownership or Control of Borrower or of any member of Borrower occurs without Lender's prior written consent, including any change resulting from the exercise of remedies by the Mezzanine Lender, other than as permitted by the Intercreditor Agreement.

\(u\) **Fraud.** Borrower, any Guarantor or any Related Entity engages in fraud, misappropriation of Loan proceeds or rents, or willful misconduct in connection with the Loan.

\(v\) **Other.** Borrower fails to perform any other obligation under any Loan Document, or any other event occurs that is expressly identified as an Event of Default in any Loan Document.
:::

## 14.2 Notice and Cure

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Each notice and cure period in this Article is the exclusive notice and cure period applicable to the corresponding Event of Default, and time is of the essence. Lender's acceptance of any late or partial payment, or its failure to object to any act or omission, shall not constitute a waiver of any Event of Default. Lender shall not be required to give notice of any Event of Default described in Sections 14.1(f), (g) or (u), or of any Event of Default arising from the failure to pay an amount after the expiration of the applicable grace period.

# ARTICLE XV — REMEDIES

## 15.1 Remedies Upon Default

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of any Event of Default, Lender may, in addition to all other rights and remedies available at law or in equity, exercise any one or more of the following remedies, concurrently or successively, without notice or demand except as required by applicable law:
:::

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) declare the entire unpaid principal balance of the Loan, together with all accrued and unpaid interest (including interest at the Default Rate), Fees (including the Exit Fee), costs and other amounts, immediately due and payable, whereupon the same shall be immediately due and payable without presentment, demand, protest or notice of any kind, all of which Borrower and each Guarantor waive;

\(b\) cease making Advances and terminate the commitment;

\(c\) apply the Default Rate to all outstanding amounts;

\(d\) foreclose the Deed of Trust by exercise of the power of sale contained therein in accordance with Part 6 of Article 2A of Chapter 45 of the North Carolina General Statutes (N.C.G.S. § 45-21.1 et seq.), including the filing of a notice of hearing and the obtaining of an order authorizing the sale under N.C.G.S. § 45-21.16;

\(e\) institute an action for judicial foreclosure and for the appointment of a receiver under N.C.G.S. § 1-502, and Borrower consents to the appointment of a receiver without bond;

\(f\) exercise all rights and remedies of a secured party under Article 9 of the UCC, including with respect to the pledged membership interests, and, upon foreclosure of the Membership Interest Pledge, exercise all rights of the pledgors as members of Borrower, including the right to vote the pledged interests, to remove and replace the Managing Member, and, subject to Section 9.3, to control the management of Borrower;

\(g\) collect the rents and enforce the Assignment of Rents, and require Borrower to deposit all rents into an account controlled by Lender;

\(h\) exercise all rights under the Construction Contract Assignment and the Architect Assignment, and take possession of the Property, the Approved Plans and Specifications, the Construction Documents and all materials, and engage a replacement general contractor to complete the Project;

\(i\) exercise all rights and remedies under the Guaranties and the Environmental Indemnity;

\(j\) set off any amounts held by Lender (including the Interest Reserve Balance, any Equity Escrow Deposit and any deposit held under Section 4.10) against the Indebtedness, to the extent permitted by applicable law; and

\(k\) exercise all other rights and remedies available under the Loan Documents, at law or in equity.
:::

## 15.2 Cumulative Remedies; No Waiver

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
All remedies are cumulative and not exclusive. No delay or failure by Lender in exercising any right or remedy shall operate as a waiver, and no single or partial exercise shall preclude any other or further exercise. Lender may exercise its remedies without regard to the value of any collateral and without any obligation to marshal assets.
:::

## 15.3 Deficiency; Fair Market Value

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Following a foreclosure sale conducted under the power of sale, Lender may seek a deficiency judgment in accordance with N.C.G.S. § 45-21.36. In any action for a deficiency, Borrower and each Guarantor shall be entitled to the benefit of the fair market value credit provided by that statute if the property is sold for less than its fair market value, and the amount of any deficiency shall be determined in accordance with that statute. Nothing in this Section limits the independent obligations of the Guarantors under the Completion Guaranty, the Carry Guaranty, the Partial Repayment Guaranty (as to the Guaranteed Amount) or the Environmental Indemnity, each of which is a separate obligation and not a deficiency obligation.

## 15.4 Right to Cure by Guarantors

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Lender shall accept performance by any Guarantor of Borrower's obligations under the Loan Documents to the extent such performance cures the applicable Event of Default, and such acceptance shall not release any other obligor. Lender shall provide each Guarantor with copies of each notice of default and each notice of acceleration delivered to Borrower.
:::

## 15.5 Enforcement Costs

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Borrower shall reimburse Lender for all costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees, expert witness fees, appraisal costs, and the costs of any receiver) incurred in enforcing or attempting to enforce the Loan Documents or in protecting or realizing upon the Collateral, whether or not any action or proceeding is commenced, and such amounts shall bear interest at the Default Rate and shall be secured by the Loan Documents.

## 16.4 Mezzanine Debt Service

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}

If the Mezzanine Loan bears interest that is paid in kind or accrued during the Construction Period, the accreted amount shall be disclosed to Lender quarterly and shall be included in each refinancing and take-out analysis delivered to Lender. **Drafting Note:** The Credit Memorandum describes the Mezzanine Loan as accruing interest and being paid in kind during construction and cash-paid during the Mini-Perm Period. At 12.50% per annum, the Mezzanine Loan would accrete approximately $2,875,000 over a thirty-month construction period, and cash interest during the twelve-month Mini-Perm Period would be approximately $1,509,000 on an accreted balance of approximately $12,075,000. Neither amount is funded in the Approved Project Budget, and no covenant in the Term Sheet measures total debt service coverage on a combined basis. On the Appraiser's Stabilized Net Operating Income of $4,876,800, combined senior and mezzanine debt service would produce coverage of approximately 0.93x. See Cover Memorandum, Issue 6.
