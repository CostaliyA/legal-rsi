THIS LOAN AGREEMENT (this **"Agreement"**) is made and entered into as of February [●], 2025 (the **"Effective Date"**), by and between **PINNACLE SAVINGS BANK, N.A.**, a nationally chartered savings bank organized under the laws of the United States and regulated by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, with its principal office at 215 South Tryon Street, 18th Floor, Charlotte, North Carolina 28202 (together with its successors and assigns, **"Lender"**), and **HARBORVIEW CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company, with North Carolina Secretary of State File No. 1923847 and its principal office at 400 West Main Street, Suite 310, Durham, North Carolina 27701 (together with its permitted successors and assigns, **"Borrower"**).

## RECITALS

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
A. Borrower is the fee simple owner of two contiguous parcels of real property located at 601 and 615 Foster Street, Durham, Durham County, North Carolina 27701, identified by Durham County Parcel Identification Numbers 0821-04-72-3341 and 0821-04-72-4102, comprising approximately 3.47 acres in the aggregate, together with all improvements, fixtures and appurtenances thereto (collectively, the **"Property"**).

B. Borrower has requested that Lender make available to Borrower a construction-to-mini-permanent loan in the principal amount of Forty-Seven Million Five Hundred Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($47,500,000) to finance a portion of the costs of constructing the Project (as defined below) and to fund the Interest Reserve (as defined below), and Lender is willing to do so on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

C. The Loan is being made on the basis of, and in reliance upon, the Term Sheet dated January 10, 2025 and executed by Lender and Borrower (the **"Term Sheet"**), and the Senior Loan Committee Credit Memorandum dated January 22, 2025 relating to Lender's Loan No. CRE-2025-0342 (the **"Credit Memorandum"**). This Agreement sets forth the definitive terms and conditions of the Loan. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and the Term Sheet, this Agreement shall control as between Lender and Borrower, except that the fees, rates, maturities, financial covenants and other economic terms set forth in the Term Sheet shall not be modified except as expressly provided herein or as approved in writing by Lender's Senior Loan Committee.

D. Concurrently with the closing of the Loan, Brightleaf Mezzanine Capital LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company (North Carolina Secretary of State File No. 2103988) (the **"Mezzanine Lender"**), will make a mezzanine loan to Borrower in the original principal amount of $9,200,000 (the **"Mezzanine Loan"**), which Mezzanine Loan will be secured by a pledge of one hundred percent (100%) of the membership interests in Borrower, subordinated to the Membership Interest Pledge granted to Lender pursuant to this Agreement and the other Loan Documents.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Lender and Borrower agree as follows:

# ARTICLE II — THE LOAN

## 2.1 The Commitment

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the other Loan Documents, and in reliance on the representations, warranties and covenants of Borrower and the Guarantors, Lender agrees to make Advances to Borrower from time to time during the Construction Period in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed Forty-Seven Million Five Hundred Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($47,500,000), in accordance with the Draw Schedule attached as Exhibit C. The Loan is a single-advance, non-revolving construction facility permitting multiple disbursements in accordance with Article IV. No principal amount repaid or prepaid may be re-borrowed. Borrower's obligation to repay the Loan is evidenced by the Note and secured by the Security Documents.
:::

## 2.2 The Note

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
The Loan shall be evidenced by the Note, which shall bear interest and be payable as provided in this Agreement and in the Note. In the event of any conflict between the Note and this Agreement, this Agreement shall control.
:::

## 2.3 Purpose

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
The proceeds of the Loan shall be used solely to (a) pay Hard Costs and Soft Costs set forth in the Approved Project Budget, (b) fund the Interest Reserve, and (c) pay the Origination Fee to the extent permitted by the Approved Project Budget. No proceeds of the Loan shall be used (i) to pay any Distribution, (ii) to repay, reimburse or refinance any capital contribution, member loan or other advance made by any member of Borrower (including any advance used to acquire the Property or to fund the remediation of the Property) except as expressly permitted in Section 7.5(f), (iii) to pay any amount in respect of the Mezzanine Loan, or (iv) for any purpose that would cause the Loan-to-Cost Ratio or the Loan-to-Value Ratio to exceed the limitations in Article VIII.
:::

## 2.4 Interest

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) **Applicable Rate.** Interest on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall accrue at all times at the Applicable Rate.

\(b\) **Payment.** Interest shall be calculated on the basis of a 360-day year and the actual number of days elapsed, and shall be payable monthly in arrears on the first (1st) Business Day of each calendar month, commencing on the first Business Day of the first full calendar month following the Closing Date.

\(c\) **Construction Period Interest; Interest Reserve.** During the Construction Period, and for so long as the Interest Reserve Balance is sufficient to pay interest then due, interest shall be funded by disbursements from the Interest Reserve Account as provided in Section 4.5. When and to the extent the Interest Reserve Balance is insufficient to pay interest when due, Borrower shall pay such interest from its own funds, and the Guarantors shall be obligated to do so pursuant to the Carry Guaranty, in each case without notice, demand or grace period beyond the third (3rd) Business Day following such insufficiency.

\(d\) **Mini-Perm Period Interest.** From and after the Construction Maturity Date, interest shall be payable by Borrower monthly in arrears from Borrower's own funds. No portion of the Interest Reserve may be applied to interest accruing after the Construction Maturity Date.
:::

## 2.5 SOFR; Benchmark Replacement; Conforming Changes

::: {custom-style="Body Text"}
\(a\) **Daily Simple SOFR.** If Lender determines that Daily Simple SOFR cannot be determined in accordance with its definition for any day, the rate for that day shall be the most recently published Daily Simple SOFR, and the next Business Day on which Daily Simple SOFR is determinable shall apply. Lender's determination of the Applicable Rate shall be conclusive absent manifest error.

\(b\) **Benchmark Transition.** If Lender determines in its reasonable discretion that (i) Daily Simple SOFR has been permanently or indefinitely discontinued or is no longer representative, (ii) the administrator of Daily Simple SOFR has made a public statement to that effect, or (iii) the regulatory supervisor of the administrator has made a public statement to that effect, then Lender may designate a replacement benchmark rate, giving due consideration to (A) any replacement rate or successor rate then recommended for the applicable loan type by the Alternative Reference Rates Committee ("**ARRC**") and (B) any evolving or then-prevailing market convention for syndicated and commercial real estate construction loans in the United States for such replacement. Lender shall determine any spread adjustment (including any ARRC-recommended spread adjustment) and any conforming changes to the definition of Business Day, the interest payment dates, the lookback period and the timing and frequency of rate determinations. Lender shall provide Borrower with not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any such designation.

\(c\) **Conforming Changes.** Lender may make conforming changes to the timing and manner of determining the Applicable Rate, the interest payment dates and the lookback period, and such changes shall become effective without further consent of Borrower; provided that no such change shall increase the aggregate amount of interest payable by more than the sum of the applicable spread adjustment plus the replacement benchmark rate would produce.

\(d\) **SOFR Floor.** The SOFR Floor shall apply notwithstanding any discontinuation, replacement or negative rate.
