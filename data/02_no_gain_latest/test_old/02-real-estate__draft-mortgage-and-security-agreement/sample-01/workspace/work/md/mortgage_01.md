# RECITALS

**A. The Loan.** Mortgagor has requested that Mortgagee make the Loan to Mortgagor in the original principal amount of \$14,750,000, designated Calverley Heritage Bank Commercial Real Estate Loan No. 2025-CHS-0047, the proceeds of which will be used to finance (i) the acquisition of the Land and Improvements from Hampstead Village Holdings LLC, a South Carolina limited liability company, pursuant to a General Warranty Deed to be recorded in the RMC Office simultaneously with this Mortgage, and (ii) the Project. The Loan is evidenced by the Note and is made on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Term Sheet and the other Loan Documents.

**B. Disbursement Structure.** The Loan proceeds are to be disbursed in two components: (i) the Initial Advance of \$12,500,000 at closing, to be applied toward the purchase price for the Property, closing costs and funded reserves; and (ii) the Renovation Holdback of \$2,250,000, to be held by Mortgagee and disbursed on a Draw basis in accordance with **Article 10** for Renovation Costs, against a total Approved Renovation Budget of \$3,150,000, with the balance of \$900,000 of Renovation Costs to be funded by Mortgagor from the Borrower Equity Contribution.

**C. Guaranties and Environmental Indemnity.** The Loan is additionally secured by the Guaranty Agreement, including the Completion Guaranty and the interest-shortfall payment guaranty, and by the Environmental Indemnity Agreement, in each case executed by Guarantor and, where applicable, Mortgagor, and the obligations of Mortgagor under the Loan Documents are further supported by the Completion Guaranty with respect to completion of the Project.

**D. Title.** The Title Insurer has issued Commitment No. PT-2025-04-1178, effective February 28, 2025, committing to issue the Title Policy in the amount of \$14,750,000 insuring the Lien of this Mortgage as a first-priority lien on the Land and Improvements, subject only to the Permitted Exceptions.

**E. Environmental Condition.** The Phase I Environmental Site Assessment prepared by Clearfield Environmental Consulting LLC (Report No. CLR-2025-0119, dated January 28, 2025) identified no Recognized Environmental Conditions and one Controlled Recognized Environmental Condition affecting Lot 7 (Tax Map No. 460-12-04-007), arising from a historical release at the adjoining property municipally known as 518 King Street, which condition has been addressed under SCDHEC oversight through a No Further Action determination dated August 12, 2021 (SCDHEC Site ID SC-29403-0772) and is managed through the Land Use Restriction recorded in Deed Book 1203, Page 89, in the RMC Office. This Mortgage contains express covenants and an Event of Default addressing compliance with the Land Use Restriction, as required by the Title Insurer's special instructions and by **Section 5.4** of the Term Sheet.

**F. Purpose of Instrument.** This Mortgage is given to secure the Indebtedness, including the Renovation Holdback disbursements, and is intended to constitute (i) a mortgage of real property under the laws of the State of South Carolina, (ii) a security agreement under Article 9 of the UCC, (iii) a financing statement filed as a fixture filing, and (iv) an absolute and present assignment of the Leases, Rents, issues and profits under the South Carolina Uniform Assignment of Rents Act, Chapter 3, Title 29 of the South Carolina Code of Laws, as amended.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the Loan and the covenants and agreements contained in the Loan Documents, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Mortgagor hereby grants, bargains, sells, conveys, mortgages, pledges, assigns, transfers and sets over to Mortgagee, and grants to Mortgagee a security interest in, all of the following property, rights, interests and privileges, whether now owned or hereafter acquired (collectively, the "**Property**"), TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the same unto Mortgagee, its successors and assigns, upon the terms and conditions of this Mortgage:
