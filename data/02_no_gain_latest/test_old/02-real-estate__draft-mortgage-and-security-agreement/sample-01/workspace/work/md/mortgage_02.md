All such amounts are secured by this Mortgage with the same priority as the original principal amount of the Loan to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, and Mortgagor acknowledges that this Mortgage is given in part to secure present and future advances.

**2.6 Permitted Exceptions.** The Lien of this Mortgage is a first-priority lien on the Property, subject only to the Permitted Exceptions set forth in **Exhibit B**. Mortgagor shall not, and shall not permit any related Person to, cause, permit, suffer or agree to any Lien on the Property other than the Permitted Exceptions, and shall not modify, extend, renew, expand or increase the burden of any Permitted Exception without Mortgagee's prior written consent. Without limiting the foregoing, Mortgagor shall not permit any mechanic's, materialmen's, laborer's, architect's, engineer's or other lien or claim for construction or improvement to attach to the Property, and shall keep the Property free of all such Liens, or shall discharge or bond over the same in a manner satisfactory to Mortgagee and the Title Insurer within thirty (30) days after Mortgagor receives notice of the filing thereof.

**2.7 Mechanics' Liens and Statutory Rights.** Nothing in this Mortgage shall be construed to constitute the consent or request of Mortgagee, express or implied, by inference or otherwise, to any contractor, subcontractor, laborer or materialman for the performance of any labor or the furnishing of any service or material to or for the Property, or to any construction, alteration or repair of the Property by or on behalf of Mortgagor. Mortgagor shall not request, permit or authorize any Person to claim, any mechanic's or materialmen's lien against the Property or the Rents, and Mortgagor shall post and maintain upon the Property all notices required by law to protect Mortgagee's interest.

# ARTICLE 4 — FUTURE ADVANCES AND PRIORITY

**4.1 Future Advances.** This Mortgage is given to secure not only the Initial Advance but also all future advances and disbursements made by Mortgagee under the Loan Documents, including, without limitation, the Renovation Holdback and all Disbursements made under **Article 10**, and all protective advances made under **Article 11**. All such obligations and advances, whether obligatory or optional, and whether or not made pursuant to a commitment in existence on the date of recording of this Mortgage, together with all interest thereon, shall be secured by this Mortgage and shall have priority as of the date and time of recording of this Mortgage to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

**4.2 Renovation Holdback as Obligatory Advance.** Mortgagor and Mortgagee acknowledge that the Renovation Holdback constitutes an obligatory advance under a commitment existing at the time this Mortgage is recorded, and that this Mortgage secures the Renovation Holdback with the same priority as the Initial Advance. **4.4 No Waiver of Priority.** No increase in the amount secured, no advance made after the occurrence of an Event of Default, no forbearance, and no other act or omission of Mortgagee shall impair, waive, subordinate or otherwise affect the priority of this Mortgage.

# ARTICLE 5 — ABSOLUTE ASSIGNMENT OF LEASES, RENTS, ISSUES AND PROFITS

**5.1 Absolute Present Assignment.** Mortgagor hereby unconditionally, absolutely and presently grants, bargains, sells, conveys, transfers, assigns and sets over to Mortgagee, without reservation and without any limitation other than as expressly set forth in this Article 5, all of Mortgagor's right, title and interest in, to and under (a) each and every Lease now existing or hereafter arising affecting the Property, including, without limitation, each Lease identified in the Rent Roll, together with all extensions, renewals, amendments, restatements, replacements and guaranties thereof, and (b) all Rents, issues, profits, income, revenues and other sums now or hereafter due, payable or accruing under or with respect to any Lease or the use, occupancy or enjoyment of the Property. This assignment is an absolute and present assignment and not a collateral assignment or an assignment as additional security, and it is effective immediately upon execution of this Mortgage, without regard to any act, event, condition or default, and without the necessity of any further instrument or action by Mortgagor. This assignment is intended to constitute an absolute assignment of rents within the meaning of the South Carolina Uniform Assignment of Rents Act, Chapter 3, Title 29 of the South Carolina Code of Laws, as amended (the "**Assignment of Rents Act**").

**5.2 Perfection; Authority to Assign.** Mortgagor acknowledges that, under the Assignment of Rents Act, this assignment of Rents is perfected upon recording of this Mortgage in the RMC Office, and that Mortgagee's right to enforce the assignment, including its right to collect the Rents, arises upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default and upon written notice to the applicable Tenants. Mortgagor represents, warrants and covenants that (a) it is the owner of and has the full right, power and authority to absolutely and presently assign each Lease and all Rents; (b) it has not previously assigned, pledged, hypothecated or otherwise encumbered any Lease or any Rents, and has granted no Person any right to collect any Rents other than the revocable license granted in **Section 5.3**; (c) each Lease is in full force and effect, and neither Mortgagor nor, to Mortgagor's knowledge, any Tenant is in default thereunder; (d) no Rents have been prepaid more than one (1) month in advance other than as disclosed in the Rent Roll; and (e) specifically and without limitation of the foregoing, Mortgagor has the full right and authority to absolutely assign all Rents under that certain lease with Lowcountry Provisions Inc. (Bay 1, 520 King Street), notwithstanding that such Lease contains no assignment or subletting provision.

**5.3 Revocable License to Mortgagor.** So long as no Event of Default has occurred and is continuing, Mortgagor shall have a revocable, non-assignable license to collect, use and enjoy the Rents in the ordinary course of the operation of the Property, subject to the terms of the Loan Documents. The license granted in this Section 5.3 is granted solely for the purpose of permitting Mortgagor to operate the Property and is not a retransfer or reassignment of the Rents. The license shall be deemed revoked automatically, without further notice, upon the occurrence of an Event of Default, and Mortgagee may, in addition, revoke the license by written notice to Mortgagor.

**5.4 Enforcement Upon Default.** Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, and upon written notice from Mortgagee to the Tenants (or any of them), Mortgagee shall be entitled, without the necessity of obtaining a court order, appointing a receiver or taking any other action, to (a) collect all Rents directly from the Tenants, (b) enforce the Leases and institute and prosecute all proceedings in the name of Mortgagor or Mortgagee, as Mortgagee may elect, for the collection of Rents, the enforcement of any Lease, the eviction of any Tenant, the recovery of possession or the protection or preservation of the Property, (c) settle, compromise, release, extend or modify in any respect any claim or right under any Lease, in Mortgagee's sole discretion, and (d) do all such other acts and things as Mortgagee may deem necessary or advisable to enforce the assignment made in this Article 5. Mortgagor hereby irrevocably constitutes and appoints Mortgagee as Mortgagor's attorney-in-fact, coupled with an interest, with full power of substitution, for the purposes described in this Section 5.4, which power of attorney may be exercised only upon and during the continuance of an Event of Default. Each Tenant is hereby authorized and directed, upon receipt of written notice from Mortgagee, to pay all Rents directly to Mortgagee or its designee, and Mortgagor hereby irrevocably authorizes and directs each Tenant so to pay, and agrees that any payment made in accordance with such notice shall discharge the Tenant's obligations with respect to such amounts.

**5.5 Tenants Protected; No Liability of Mortgagee.** No Tenant shall be liable to Mortgagor for any Rents paid to Mortgagee in accordance with this Article 5. Mortgagee shall have no obligation to perform any obligation of Mortgagor as landlord under any Lease, to pay or discharge any operating expense, or to maintain or repair the Property, unless and until Mortgagee has actually entered into possession of the Property, and Mortgagor shall remain solely responsible for all landlord obligations under the Leases. The exercise by Mortgagee of any right under this Article 5 shall not constitute an assumption by Mortgagee of any obligation of Mortgagor under any Lease, shall not constitute Mortgagee a mortgagee in possession with respect to obligations to Tenants, and shall not be deemed to constitute an attornment by any Tenant to Mortgagee.

**5.6 Subordination of Leases; Estoppels; SNDAs.** Each Lease now or hereafter existing is and shall be subject and subordinate to this Mortgage and to all renewals, extensions, modifications and consolidations hereof, and Mortgagee may, at its election, require that any Lease be so subordinated in form reasonably acceptable to Mortgagee, *provided* that Mortgagee shall, upon the request of Mortgagor, enter into a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement ("**SNDA**") with any Tenant that is not in default under its Lease, in form reasonably acceptable to Mortgagee. Mortgagor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, and shall deliver to Mortgagee within the applicable time period, (a) an estoppel certificate from each Tenant in accordance with Section 12.1 of the Term Sheet, and (b) an executed SNDA from each Tenant in accordance with Section 12.1 of the Term Sheet. Mortgagor acknowledges that the leases identified on the Rent Roll with Harmon & Wren Design Studio LLC and Tidemark Consulting Group LLC each contain a subordination and estoppel provision, and that the lease with Lowcountry Provisions Inc. contains neither an assignment or subletting provision nor an estoppel certificate or SNDA obligation. Accordingly, Mortgagor shall (i) cause Lowcountry Provisions Inc. to deliver an estoppel certificate and an SNDA as a condition to closing, and (ii) thereafter use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, at the next renewal, extension or amendment of the Lowcountry Provisions Inc. lease (and in any event no later than the tenant's exercise of its renewal option, for which notice is due on or before March 31, 2027), a lease amendment adding customary assignment and subletting provisions, a consent to this assignment of Rents, an estoppel certificate obligation and an SNDA obligation, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Mortgagee, and shall deliver a copy of each such amendment to Mortgagee within five (5) Business Days after execution.

**5.7 Security Deposits.** Mortgagor shall hold all security deposits and advance rentals in trust for the Tenants under the applicable Leases and shall not apply any security deposit except in accordance with the applicable Lease and applicable law. Mortgagor acknowledges that security deposits remain the property of the Tenants and are not the property of Mortgagee; the Lien and assignment granted under this Mortgage extend to Mortgagor's interest in such deposits, subject in all respects to the rights of the Tenants. Upon Mortgagee's request following an Event of Default, Mortgagor shall deliver to Mortgagee an accounting of all security deposits held, together with all documentation necessary to transfer such deposits (or the obligation to return them) to Mortgagee or to a successor owner of the Property.

**5.8 New Leases.** All Leases hereafter executed by Mortgagor shall be subject in all respects to this Mortgage and to the Assignment of Rents Act and shall, unless Mortgagee otherwise consents in writing, contain a provision (a) subordinating the Lease to this Mortgage and to any renewals, extensions and modifications thereof, (b) requiring the Tenant to attorn to any purchaser at foreclosure or to any successor owner of the Property, (c) requiring the Tenant to deliver estoppel certificates within ten (10) Business Days of request, and (d) requiring the Tenant to pay all Rents to Mortgagee upon notice following an Event of Default. Mortgagor shall deliver a fully executed copy of each Lease, and of each amendment, renewal, extension, termination or surrender thereof, to Mortgagee within ten (10) Business Days after execution or receipt, as required by Section 7.3(e) of the Term Sheet and **Section 7.9**.

**5.9 No Merger; Continuing Assignment.** This Article 5 shall not merge with or into any decree of foreclosure, any deed given in lieu of foreclosure, or any other instrument, and shall remain in full force and effect until the Indebtedness has been paid in full and this Mortgage has been released of record. No collection of Rents by Mortgagee, and no application of Rents to the Indebtedness, shall cure any Event of Default, satisfy any provision of this Mortgage, or affect the Lien of this Mortgage on the Property.
