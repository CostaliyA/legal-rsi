# ARTICLE 8 — INSURANCE

**(e) Workers' compensation insurance** in the amounts required by the laws of the State of South Carolina, together with employer's liability insurance of not less than \$1,000,000, covering all employees of Mortgagor and, to the extent not covered under the general contractor's policies, all persons engaged in the Project;

**(f) Umbrella or excess liability insurance** in an amount not less than \$5,000,000, providing excess coverage over the commercial general liability, automobile liability and employers' liability policies, naming Mortgagee as an additional insured; and

**(g) Business income and rent loss insurance** covering not less than twelve (12) months of actual loss of Rents and operating expenses following a casualty, and such other insurance, in such amounts and covering such risks, as Mortgagee may reasonably require from time to time consistent with prudent institutional lending practices for comparable mixed-use commercial properties.

**8.2 Policy Requirements.** Each policy required under **Section 8.1** shall (a) be issued by an insurer acceptable to Mortgagee and authorized to do business in South Carolina; (b) contain a provision requiring not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Mortgagee of cancellation, non-renewal or material modification (and ten (10) days' prior written notice of cancellation for non-payment of premium); (c) contain a standard non-contributory mortgagee clause in favor of Mortgagee; (d) provide that Mortgagee shall receive not less than ten (10) days' prior written notice of the effective date of any endorsement restrictive to Mortgagee; (e) waive any right of subrogation against Mortgagee; and (f) be primary and non-contributory with respect to any insurance maintained by Mortgagee. Mortgagor shall deliver to Mortgagee, on or before the Closing Date and thereafter at least thirty (30) days prior to each expiration, certificates of insurance and, upon Mortgagee's request, certified copies of the policies (including all endorsements).

**8.3 Deductibles; Self-Insurance.** Deductibles and self-insured retentions shall not exceed \$50,000 per occurrence without Mortgagee's prior written consent, and Mortgagor shall bear the cost of all deductibles and retentions. Mortgagor shall not self-insure any risk without Mortgagee's prior written consent, which may be withheld in Mortgagee's sole discretion.

**8.4 Evidence of Coverage; Mortgagee's Right to Insure.** If Mortgagor fails to obtain, maintain or renew any required insurance, or fails to deliver evidence of coverage within ten (10) days after Mortgagee's request, Mortgagee may, at its option and without obligation to do so, obtain such insurance (including "force-placed" coverage on terms acceptable to Mortgagee, whether or not equivalent to the coverage required by this Article), and all amounts paid by Mortgagee for such insurance, together with interest at the Default Rate from the date of payment, shall be added to the Indebtedness and secured by this Mortgage. Mortgagor acknowledges that any insurance obtained by Mortgagee may be more expensive and may provide less coverage than the insurance required under this Article, and Mortgagor shall not be relieved of any obligation under this Article by Mortgagee's purchase of such insurance.

**8.5 Application of Proceeds.** All proceeds of insurance (other than proceeds of liability insurance payable to third parties, and proceeds of insurance maintained by a Tenant under its Lease and payable to such Tenant) shall be paid to and held by Mortgagee and applied in accordance with **Article 14**.

**8.6 Separate Insurance.** Mortgagor shall not take out separate insurance concurrently with or contributing in the event of loss with any insurance required under this Article, unless Mortgagee is named as an additional insured or loss payee thereon, and Mortgagor shall immediately notify Mortgagee of the issuance of any such separate insurance and deliver copies of the policies and endorsements.

# ARTICLE 9 — ESCROWS, RESERVES AND IMPOUNDS

**9.1 Tax and Insurance Escrow.** Mortgagor shall deposit with Mortgagee, on or before the Closing Date and thereafter on each Payment Date, an amount equal to one-twelfth (1/12) of the estimated annual Impositions (covering all three tax parcels, Tax Map Nos. 460-12-04-007, 460-12-04-008 and 460-12-04-009) and Insurance Premiums, as reasonably estimated by Mortgagee from time to time. Mortgagee shall hold such deposits in escrow, without interest unless required by applicable law, and shall apply them toward the payment of Impositions and Insurance Premiums as they become due. Mortgagee may adjust the monthly escrow deposit periodically based on actual or anticipated tax assessments and insurance premiums, and Mortgagor shall fund any deficiency within ten (10) days after written demand. If Mortgagor fails to fund a deficiency, Mortgagee may, at its option, advance the deficiency and add it to the Indebtedness. Mortgagee's obligation to pay Impositions and Insurance Premiums from the escrow is limited to the funds then held in the escrow, and Mortgagee shall have no liability for any penalty or interest arising from insufficient escrow funds attributable to Mortgagor's failure to fund.

**9.2 Capital Replacement Reserve.** Mortgagor shall deposit with Mortgagee, on each Payment Date, the sum of \$645.83 (calculated as \$0.25 per square foot per year multiplied by 31,000 square feet of gross building area, or \$7,750 per year, divided by twelve months) into the Capital Replacement Reserve. Funds in the Capital Replacement Reserve shall be disbursed to Mortgagor upon Mortgagee's prior written approval of qualifying capital replacement expenditures for the Property, subject to Mortgagee's reasonable review and approval procedures, and upon Mortgagee's receipt of invoices, lien waivers and such other supporting documentation as Mortgagee may reasonably require. Mortgagee may, in its reasonable discretion, adjust the monthly deposit amount based on a physical needs assessment or the actual capital replacement requirements of the Property.

**9.3 Tenant Improvement and Leasing Commission Reserve.** Mortgagor shall deposit with Mortgagee on the Closing Date the sum of \$150,000 into the Tenant Improvement and Leasing Commission Reserve. Disbursements from such reserve shall be subject to Mortgagee's prior written approval and shall be used exclusively for tenant improvement costs and leasing commissions incurred in connection with the leasing of vacant space at the Property (Bay 2 and 2nd Floor Suite B). Upon any draw from such reserve, Mortgagor shall replenish the reserve to the full amount of \$150,000 from Mortgagor's own funds within thirty (30) days after Mortgagee's written demand. Mortgagor acknowledges that the Tenant Improvement and Leasing Commission Reserve is separate from, and in addition to, the tenant improvement allowance line item in the Approved Renovation Budget, and that neither may be applied to the same cost.

**9.4 General Provisions Applicable to Escrow Funds.** (a) All Escrow Funds shall be held by Mortgagee and may be commingled with other funds of Mortgagee, and Mortgagor shall have no right to any interest or earnings thereon except as required by applicable law. (b) The Escrow Funds are pledged to Mortgagee as additional Collateral for the Indebtedness, and Mortgagor hereby grants to Mortgagee a security interest in all Escrow Funds and in all accounts in which they are held. (c) Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, Mortgagee may apply all or any portion of the Escrow Funds to the Indebtedness in such order as Mortgagee may elect. (d) Mortgagee shall not be obligated to pay any Imposition, Insurance Premium, capital expenditure, tenant improvement allowance or leasing commission from the Escrow Funds if (i) an Event of Default has occurred and is continuing, (ii) the amount on deposit is insufficient, or (iii) Mortgagor has failed to deliver the documentation required under the applicable Loan Document. (e) Mortgagor's failure to make any required deposit into any escrow or reserve within five (5) Business Days after its due date shall constitute an Event of Default. (f) Upon payment in full of the Indebtedness, Mortgagee shall return any remaining Escrow Funds to Mortgagor, less any amounts required to be retained by Mortgagee for indemnity obligations that expressly survive repayment. (g) Mortgagee may, in its discretion, waive the escrow requirements of this Article 9 in writing, and any such waiver shall not affect Mortgagee's right to reinstate the requirements upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

# ARTICLE 10 — RENOVATION HOLDBACK AND DISBURSEMENT PROCEDURES

**10.1 The Renovation Holdback.** Of the total Loan commitment of \$14,750,000, the sum of \$2,250,000 constitutes the Renovation Holdback, to be held by Mortgagee and disbursed to Mortgagor on a Draw basis solely for the payment of Renovation Costs in accordance with the Approved Renovation Budget, the Draw Schedule and this Article 10. No interest shall accrue or be payable on any undrawn portion of the Renovation Holdback. Mortgagor acknowledges that the Approved Renovation Budget is \$3,150,000, that the Renovation Holdback represents 71.43% of that budget, and that the remaining \$900,000 of Renovation Costs (representing 28.57% of the Approved Renovation Budget) is the Borrower Equity Contribution to be funded by Mortgagor from its own funds and not from the proceeds of the Loan.

**10.2 Conditions Precedent to Each Draw.** Mortgagee shall have no obligation to make any disbursement from the Renovation Holdback unless each of the following conditions has been satisfied (or waived in writing by Mortgagee):

**(a)** no Event of Default, and no event that with notice or lapse of time or both would constitute an Event of Default, has occurred and is continuing, and each representation and warranty of Mortgagor in this Mortgage remains true and correct in all material respects as of the date of the Draw Request and the date of disbursement;

**(b)** Mortgagee has received a complete Draw Request satisfying **Section 10.3** not less than ten (10) Business Days prior to the requested disbursement date;

**(c)** the work for which payment is requested has been completed in accordance with the Plans and Specifications and the Approved Renovation Budget, free and clear of all mechanic's, materialmen's and other Liens and claims, and has been inspected and approved by Mortgagee's inspector in accordance with **Section 10.3(e)**;

**(d)** Mortgagor has funded, and there is on deposit with Mortgagee or otherwise evidenced to Mortgagee's satisfaction, the portion of the Borrower Equity Contribution allocable to the work covered by the Draw Request, such that at no time shall the cumulative amount disbursed from the Renovation Holdback exceed 71.43% of the cumulative Renovation Costs then incurred and paid or payable;

**(e)** the Title Insurer has issued the date-down endorsement required by **Section 10.3(g)**;

**(f)** all permits, approvals and certificates of occupancy required for the work covered by the Draw Request have been obtained, including any approval of the Board of Architectural Review of the City of Charleston required for the work;

**(g)** if the work covered by the Draw Request involves or is reasonably likely to affect the floor slab of Bay 1 on Lot 7, Mortgagor has satisfied each of the requirements of Section 7.6(c)(iii) (including prior written notice to SCDHEC and delivery of the environmental professional's pre- and post-work certification);

**(h)** the Title Policy (or a commitment to issue the same) remains in full force and effect, and the future advance endorsement contemplated by **Section 4.2** remains available; and

**(i)** Mortgagor has delivered to Mortgagee a then-current budget comparison and construction schedule and such other information as Mortgagee may reasonably request.

**10.3 Draw Requests.** Each disbursement from the Renovation Holdback (a "**Draw**") shall be made pursuant to a written draw request (a "**Draw Request**"), and Draws shall be made no more frequently than once per calendar month. Each Draw Request shall be submitted in writing, in form reasonably satisfactory to Mortgagee, and shall be accompanied by:

**(a)** an AIA G702/G703 Application and Certificate for Payment, certified by Mortgagor's architect of record, detailing the work completed to date, the amounts previously paid, the amount currently requested and the percentage of completion of each line item of the Approved Renovation Budget;

**(b)** a schedule of values, updated to the date of the Draw Request, reconciling all amounts requested and disbursed to the Approved Renovation Budget and the Draw Schedule;

**(c)** conditional lien waivers from Beaufort Builders Group LLC and each subcontractor or materialman performing work or supplying materials covered by the current Draw Request, and unconditional lien waivers from Beaufort Builders Group LLC and each subcontractor or materialman for all work and materials covered by all prior Draws; provided that lien waivers shall be required from each subcontractor or materialman whose work or materials exceed \$25,000, and Mortgagor shall obtain and deliver such partial or full releases, affidavits and sworn statements as Mortgagee may reasonably require;

**(d)** a certification of Mortgagor, executed by an authorized representative, that the work has been completed in accordance with the Plans and Specifications, that all amounts requested have been or will promptly be paid to the Persons entitled thereto, that no Lien has been filed or is threatened against the Property, and that all conditions in **Section 10.2** have been satisfied;

**(e)** evidence of Mortgagee's satisfactory inspection of the work in progress, which inspection may be conducted by a third-party inspector or architect retained by Mortgagee at Mortgagor's expense;

**(f)** evidence of the Borrower Equity Contribution allocable to the work covered by the Draw Request, together with a reconciliation demonstrating compliance with **Section 10.2(d)**;

**(g)** a date-down endorsement to the Title Policy from the Title Insurer confirming that no intervening mechanic's or materialmen's lien, or other Lien or encumbrance, has been filed against the Property since the date of this Mortgage or the most recent date-down endorsement, and confirming the continued first priority of this Mortgage (including as to the amounts then being advanced); and

**(h)** such other documentation as Mortgagee may reasonably require, including invoices, bills of sale, paid receipts, contractor's affidavits, and evidence of insurance compliance by the general contractor and subcontractors.

Mortgagee may rely conclusively on each Draw Request and each certificate delivered in connection therewith, and shall have no duty to verify the accuracy of any request or certificate or to inspect the Property.

**10.4 Disbursement; Retainage.** Mortgagee shall disburse the amount properly requested (subject to **Section 10.5**) within ten (10) Business Days after Mortgagee's receipt of a complete and conforming Draw Request and satisfaction of the conditions in **Section 10.2**, by wire transfer to Mortgagor or, at Mortgagee's election, directly to Beaufort Builders Group LLC, any subcontractor, materialman or other Person entitled to payment. Mortgagee shall retain ten percent (10%) of each disbursement from the Renovation Holdback as retainage until Substantial Completion has been achieved in accordance with **Section 10.8**. Retainage shall be held by Mortgagee (and not by Mortgagor or the general contractor unless Mortgagee otherwise agrees in writing) and shall not bear interest. No disbursement shall exceed the lesser of (a) the amount requested and (b) the cost of the work actually completed and approved, less prior payments and retainage.

**10.5 Mortgagee's Right to Withhold.** Mortgagee may decline to fund all or any portion of a Draw Request if (a) any condition in **Section 10.2** has not been satisfied; (b) the Draw Request or any supporting certificate is incomplete, inaccurate or inconsistent with the Approved Renovation Budget or the Plans and Specifications; (c) the work is defective or not in accordance with the Plans and Specifications, or Mortgagee's inspector has not approved the work; (d) a mechanic's, materialmen's or other Lien has been filed against the Property and has not been discharged or bonded over to Mortgagee's satisfaction; (e) Mortgagor is in default under the GC Contract or any other construction contract; (f) Mortgagee reasonably determines that the undisbursed Renovation Holdback, together with the unfunded portion of the Borrower Equity Contribution, is insufficient to complete the Project; or (g) a Material Adverse Effect has occurred. Mortgagee's decision to fund any Draw notwithstanding the existence of any such condition shall not constitute a waiver of Mortgagee's rights with respect to that or any other condition or Draw.

**10.6 Cost Overruns.** Mortgagor shall be solely responsible, from Mortgagor's own funds and not from the proceeds of the Loan, for all Renovation Costs in excess of the Renovation Holdback and for all Renovation Costs of every kind in excess of the Approved Renovation Budget, including any increase in the GC contract sum, any change order costs, any costs arising from concealed or unforeseen conditions, any design or construction defects, any code-compliance or Board of Architectural Review requirements, and any costs arising from delay. Mortgagor shall deposit any such excess funds with Mortgagee or otherwise demonstrate the availability of such funds to Mortgagee's satisfaction before Mortgagee is obligated to make any further Draw. Mortgagor's obligation under this Section 10.6 is guaranteed by Guarantor under the Completion Guaranty.

**10.7 Use of Renovation Holdback; No Reallocation.** The Renovation Holdback shall be used solely for Renovation Costs in accordance with the line-item allocations set forth in **Exhibit C**. Mortgagor shall not reallocate amounts among line items (other than de minimis reallocations not exceeding five percent (5%) of the total Approved Renovation Budget, and then only with prior written notice to Mortgagee) without Mortgagee's prior written consent. No portion of the Renovation Holdback may be used for (a) any cost incurred before the Closing Date, (b) any cost not included in the Approved Renovation Budget, (c) any payment to a Related Entity except for arm's-length development or asset management fees disclosed in the Approved Renovation Budget and approved by Mortgagee, or (d) any cost relating to a Tenant improvement for a Lease that Mortgagee has not approved where such approval is required under **Section 7.3**.

**10.8 Substantial Completion and Release of Retainage.** Upon Substantial Completion, Mortgagee shall release the retainage held under **Section 10.4** within thirty (30) days after Mortgagee's receipt of (a) an architect's certificate of Substantial Completion, (b) the certificate of occupancy (or equivalent) issued by the City of Charleston for the renovated portions of the Property, (c) final unconditional lien waivers from Beaufort Builders Group LLC and from each subcontractor and materialman, (d) a final title date-down endorsement confirming no intervening Liens, (e) evidence of the replacement of builder's risk insurance with the permanent property insurance required by **Section 8.1(a)**, and (f) a certification by Mortgagor that the Project has been completed in accordance with the Plans and Specifications and the Approved Renovation Budget.

**10.9 Substantial Completion Deadline; No Extension.** Mortgagor shall achieve Substantial Completion on or before June 15, 2026. The Substantial Completion Deadline shall not be extended by any event, including any delay caused by weather, labor disturbance, governmental action, casualty or other event beyond Mortgagor's control, except by a written extension executed by Mortgagee in its sole discretion; Mortgagee may, but shall not be obligated to, consider any such event in determining whether to grant an extension if Mortgagor has given Mortgagee prompt written notice of the event, has diligently pursued all reasonable measures to mitigate the delay, and is not otherwise in default. Mortgagee's failure to declare an Event of Default upon the passage of the Substantial Completion Deadline shall not constitute a waiver or an extension of the deadline.

**10.10 Completion by Mortgagee.** If Mortgagor fails to achieve Substantial Completion by the Substantial Completion Deadline, or if Mortgagee takes possession of the Property following an Event of Default, Mortgagee may (but shall not be obligated to), at Mortgagor's sole cost and expense, complete the Project or cause the Project to be completed, using the undrawn portion of the Renovation Holdback and any other Escrow Funds for that purpose and advancing such additional funds as Mortgagee deems necessary. All amounts so expended or advanced shall be added to the Indebtedness, shall bear interest at the Default Rate from the date of expenditure, and shall be secured by this Mortgage. Mortgagor hereby grants to Mortgagee, effective upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, an irrevocable license to enter upon the Property and to use the Plans and Specifications, the GC Contract, all permits, all materials and equipment at the Property and all of Mortgagor's rights under the GC Contract and the construction documents for the purpose of completing the Project, and Mortgagor shall execute such assignments, consents and acknowledgments as Mortgagee may request to evidence the foregoing, including a collateral assignment of the GC Contract and the consent of Beaufort Builders Group LLC thereto.

**10.11 Undisbursed Renovation Holdback.** Upon Substantial Completion and the satisfaction of the conditions in **Section 10.8**, any portion of the Renovation Holdback that remains undisbursed shall no longer be available to Mortgagor and shall be applied by Mortgagee, at Mortgagee's election, to the outstanding principal balance of the Note (without premium, penalty or Yield Maintenance Premium) or retained by Mortgagee as additional Collateral for the Indebtedness.
