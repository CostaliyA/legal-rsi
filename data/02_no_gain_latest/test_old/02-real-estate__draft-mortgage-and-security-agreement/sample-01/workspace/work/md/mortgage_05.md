# ARTICLE 12 — EVENTS OF DEFAULT

**12.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following shall constitute an "**Event of Default**" under this Mortgage:

**(a) Payment.** Failure by Mortgagor to pay any installment of principal, interest, the Late Charge, any escrow deposit, or any other amount due under this Mortgage, the Note or any other Loan Document on the date when due, subject to the cure period in **Section 12.2(a)**;

**(b) Covenant breach.** Breach by Mortgagor of any covenant, agreement, representation, warranty or obligation contained in this Mortgage or any other Loan Document, subject to the cure periods in **Section 12.2** where applicable;

**(c) Misrepresentation.** Any representation or warranty made by Mortgagor or Guarantor in this Mortgage, in any other Loan Document, or in any certificate, financial statement, Draw Request, rent roll or other document furnished to Mortgagee proves to have been false, misleading or inaccurate in any material respect when made or deemed made;

**(d) Insolvency.** The filing by or against Mortgagor, Guarantor or any Related Entity of any petition in bankruptcy, reorganization, arrangement or insolvency proceeding; the appointment of a receiver, trustee, custodian, conservator or liquidator for Mortgagor, Guarantor, any Related Entity or any substantial part of the property or assets of any of them; any assignment for the benefit of creditors; any admission in writing of inability to pay debts generally as they become due; or any corporate or limited liability company action taken to authorize any of the foregoing;

**(e) Cross-default.** A default by Mortgagor, Guarantor or any Related Entity under any other indebtedness for borrowed money in an amount exceeding \$250,000 in the aggregate, which default is not cured within any applicable cure or grace period, or the acceleration of any such indebtedness;

**(f) Insurance.** Failure by Mortgagor to maintain, or to deliver evidence of, any insurance required under **Article 8**, or failure to fund any insurance escrow deposit required under **Section 9.1**;

**(g) Transfer violation.** Any Transfer in violation of **Section 7.5**, including any change in Control of Mortgagor;

**(h) Substantial Completion.** Failure by Mortgagor to achieve Substantial Completion on or before the Substantial Completion Deadline;

**(i) Debt service coverage.** The Debt Service Coverage Ratio falling below 1.25 to 1.00 for two (2) consecutive calendar quarters after the commencement of DSCR testing under Section 7.8(a);

**(j) SPE covenants.** Breach by Mortgagor of any SPE Covenant set forth in **Section 7.4** or of any comparable provision of its organizational documents;

**(k) Environmental and Land Use Restriction.** Breach by Mortgagor of any covenant in **Section 7.6**, including any failure to comply with the Land Use Restriction, any use of the ground floor of Lot 7 for residential purposes, any penetration, removal or compromise of the vapor barrier beneath the Bay 1 slab without satisfaction of the conditions in Section 7.6(c)(iii), or any amendment, modification, termination or release of the Land Use Restriction;

**(l) Material adverse change.** A Material Adverse Effect that, in Mortgagee's reasonable judgment, materially impairs the ability of Mortgagor to perform its obligations under the Loan Documents or of Guarantor to perform its obligations under the Guaranty Agreement;

**(m) Guarantor.** Failure by Guarantor to perform any obligation under the Guaranty Agreement, the Completion Guaranty or the Environmental Indemnity Agreement, the death or incapacity of Guarantor, or any transfer by Guarantor of a material portion of Guarantor's assets other than as permitted thereunder;

**(n) Loan Documents.** Any Loan Document ceases to be in full force and effect or is declared void, invalid or unenforceable, or Mortgagor or Guarantor asserts in writing that any Loan Document is not binding or enforceable, or any Lien purported to be created by this Mortgage ceases to be a valid, perfected first-priority Lien (subject only to the Permitted Exceptions);

**(o) Construction.** Failure by Mortgagor to comply with **Article 10**, any material breach or default by Mortgagor under the GC Contract, abandonment of the Project for a period of thirty (30) consecutive days, or failure to commence the Project within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date;

**(p) Liens and judgments.** The filing of any mechanic's, materialmen's, judgment or other Lien against the Property that is not discharged or bonded over to Mortgagee's satisfaction within thirty (30) days after Mortgagor receives notice thereof, or the entry of any judgment against Mortgagor or Guarantor in excess of \$250,000 that remains unsatisfied, unbonded or unstayed for thirty (30) days;

**(q) Permits and abandonment.** The revocation, suspension or loss of any permit, license or certificate of occupancy necessary for the use or occupancy of the Property, or the abandonment or discontinuation of the operation of the Property for a period of thirty (30) consecutive days (other than as a result of casualty or Condemnation, in which case **Article 14** shall govern);

**(r) Estoppels and SNDAs.** Failure by Mortgagor to deliver any estoppel certificate or SNDA required under **Section 5.6** within the applicable time period; and

**(s) Other.** The occurrence of any other event or condition that constitutes an Event of Default under any other Loan Document.

**12.2 Cure Periods.**

**(a) Payment defaults.** Mortgagor shall have five (5) Business Days after the date on which payment was due to cure a payment default described in **Section 12.1(a)**. The Late Charge provided in **Section 15.4** may be assessed independently of, and shall not extend, this cure period.

**(b) Non-monetary defaults.** For a default described in **Section 12.1(b)** that is not a payment default, an SPE default, a Transfer default, an insurance default or an environmental or Land Use Restriction default, Mortgagor shall have thirty (30) days after written notice from Mortgagee to Mortgagor to cure such default; *provided* that if the default is of a nature that it cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days and Mortgagor has commenced and is diligently pursuing the cure, the cure period shall be extended for an additional period not to exceed sixty (60) days (for a total cure period not to exceed ninety (90) days), and *provided further* that no such extension shall be available if the failure to cure would reasonably be expected to result in a Material Adverse Effect or in the loss, impairment or subordination of Mortgagee's Lien.

**(c) No cure periods.** No cure period shall apply to (i) any breach of the SPE Covenants in **Section 7.4**, (ii) any Transfer in violation of **Section 7.5**, (iii) any breach of **Section 7.6** relating to the Land Use Restriction or the vapor barrier, (iv) any misrepresentation described in **Section 12.1(c)**, (v) any event described in **Section 12.1(d)** or **Section 12.1(n)**, or (vi) any failure to maintain insurance required under **Article 8**. Each such event shall constitute an immediate Event of Default, without notice or opportunity to cure, in accordance with Sections 11.2(c) and 10.3 of the Term Sheet.

**(d) No notice required for acceleration after cure period.** Once any applicable cure period has expired without cure, the Event of Default shall be deemed continuing and Mortgagee may exercise its remedies without further notice, except as required by applicable law.

**12.3 Automatic Acceleration.** Upon the occurrence of any event described in **Section 12.1(d)** with respect to Mortgagor, the entire Indebtedness shall immediately become due and payable, without notice, presentment, demand, protest or other action of any kind, all of which are hereby expressly waived by Mortgagor to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

**12.4 Notice of Default.** Any notice from Mortgagee under this Article 12 shall be given in accordance with **Section 18.1** and shall specify the nature of the asserted default and, where applicable, the cure period. Mortgagee's failure to give a notice of default shall not constitute a waiver of any Event of Default or of any remedy, except to the extent a notice is expressly made a condition to the existence of an Event of Default under this Article 12.

# ARTICLE 13 — REMEDIES

**13.1 Remedies Upon Default.** Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, Mortgagee may, in its sole discretion and in addition to all other rights and remedies available at law or in equity, exercise any one or more of the following remedies, concurrently or successively, without notice to Mortgagor except as required by applicable law:

**(a)** declare the entire Indebtedness immediately due and payable and demand payment thereof;

**(b)** impose the Default Rate on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan and on all other amounts due, from the date of the Event of Default until the Event of Default is cured or the Indebtedness is paid in full, whichever first occurs;

**(c)** foreclose this Mortgage by judicial proceedings in accordance with **Section 13.2**;

**(d)** enforce the Assignment of Rents in accordance with **Article 5**, including by giving notice to the Tenants to pay all Rents directly to Mortgagee;

**(e)** apply for the appointment of a receiver in accordance with **Section 13.3**;

**(f)** exercise all rights and remedies of a secured party under the UCC, including the right to take possession of, and to sell or otherwise dispose of, the personal property Collateral;

**(g)** enter upon and take possession of the Property and operate, manage, lease, repair, restore, complete and otherwise deal with the Property, and collect and apply the Rents, in each case in accordance with **Section 13.7**;

**(h)** apply all Escrow Funds to the Indebtedness in such order as Mortgagee may elect;

**(i)** institute and prosecute any action for specific performance, injunctive relief, the appointment of a receiver, damages, or the enforcement of any covenant or obligation under the Loan Documents;

**(j)** exercise any power of attorney granted under **Section 11.5** or **Section 5.4**; and

**(k)** pursue a deficiency judgment against Mortgagor or Guarantor to the extent permitted by **Section 13.9** and applicable law.

**13.2 Judicial Foreclosure; No Power of Sale.** South Carolina is a judicial foreclosure state. Accordingly, this Mortgage does not grant, and shall not be construed to grant, to Mortgagee any power of sale, right of non-judicial foreclosure, or right to sell the Property without judicial proceedings. Mortgagee's remedy of foreclosure shall be exercised exclusively by an action for foreclosure and sale in the Court of Common Pleas for Charleston County, South Carolina (or, at Mortgagee's election, in any other court of competent jurisdiction), in accordance with the laws of the State of South Carolina. Mortgagor, for itself and for each Person claiming by, through or under it, hereby submits to the jurisdiction of such courts for purposes of any such action or proceeding.

**13.3 Receivership.** Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, Mortgagee shall be entitled, as a matter of right and without notice to Mortgagor (to the extent permitted by applicable law) and without regard to the value of the Property, the adequacy of the security for the Indebtedness, the solvency of Mortgagor or any other Person, or the existence of any other remedy, to the appointment of a receiver (which may be Mortgagee or an Affiliate of Mortgagee) for the Property. Mortgagor irrevocably consents to the appointment of such receiver and to the exercise by the receiver of all of the powers customarily exercised by a receiver of a commercial property, including the power to collect Rents, to lease space, to incur obligations for repairs and operations, to complete the Project, to prosecute and defend actions relating to the Property, and to apply the Rents and other income of the Property to the costs of operation and to the Indebtedness. The appointment of a receiver shall not constitute an election of remedies or a waiver of any right of Mortgagee, including the right to foreclose. Any receiver appointed under this Section shall be entitled to reasonable compensation and to indemnification from the Property, and Mortgagor shall pay all costs of the receivership.

**13.4 Enforcement of Leases and Rents.** Without limiting **Article 5**, Mortgagee may, upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, (a) institute and prosecute all actions and proceedings in the name of Mortgagor or Mortgagee, as Mortgagee may elect, for the collection of Rents and the enforcement of any Lease, (b) recover possession of the Property from any Tenant, (c) settle, compromise, extend, modify or terminate any Lease, (d) evict any Tenant, and (e) exercise any right of the landlord under any Lease, and Mortgagor shall cooperate fully and shall not take any action inconsistent with Mortgagee's exercise of such rights. Mortgagee shall apply all Rents collected to the Indebtedness in accordance with **Section 13.8**, after deducting all costs of collection, operation and maintenance.

**13.5 UCC Remedies.** Mortgagee may exercise all rights and remedies of a secured party under the UCC, including the right to notify account debtors and other Persons obligated on any Collateral to make payment directly to Mortgagee, the right to take possession of the Collateral, and the right to sell, lease, license or otherwise dispose of the Collateral at public or private sale. Mortgagor agrees that ten (10) days' written notice of the time and place of any public sale or of the time after which any private sale or other disposition is to be made shall constitute reasonable notification. Mortgagor waives, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, any right to require Mortgagee to marshal assets, to exhaust any remedy against any other Person or property, or to pursue any particular remedy before pursuing another. Any proceeds of the Collateral shall be applied in accordance with **Section 13.8**.

**13.6 Cumulative Remedies; No Waiver.** All rights and remedies of Mortgagee under this Mortgage, the other Loan Documents and applicable law are cumulative and not exclusive, and may be exercised concurrently or successively. No failure or delay by Mortgagee in exercising any right or remedy shall operate as a waiver thereof, and no single or partial exercise shall preclude any other or further exercise. Acceptance of any payment after an Event of Default, or after the acceleration of the Indebtedness, shall not constitute a waiver of the Event of Default or of the acceleration, and Mortgagee may retain such payment and apply it as provided in **Section 15.5** without prejudice to its rights. No waiver, consent or amendment shall be effective unless in writing signed by Mortgagee, and no waiver shall be effective for any purpose other than the specific instance and purpose for which it is given.

**13.7 Mortgagee in Possession.** If Mortgagee enters into possession of the Property, Mortgagee may (a) hold, manage, lease, operate, insure, repair, restore, complete and otherwise deal with the Property; (b) collect and apply the Rents; (c) expend such sums as Mortgagee deems necessary for the operation, maintenance, repair, restoration, completion and preservation of the Property; (d) execute and terminate Leases and other agreements relating to the Property; and (e) take any other action necessary to preserve and protect the Property and the Indebtedness. Mortgagee shall not be liable for any loss, cost or damage to Mortgagor or any other Person arising from Mortgagee's possession and operation of the Property except to the extent caused by Mortgagee's gross negligence or willful misconduct, and Mortgagee shall not be obligated to incur any expense or take any action that Mortgagee determines not to be prudent. Mortgagee's entry into possession shall not constitute a waiver of any Event of Default or of Mortgagee's right to foreclose.

**13.8 Application of Proceeds.** All amounts received or collected by Mortgagee, whether from the foreclosure or other sale of the Property, the Collateral, the Rents, the Escrow Funds, insurance, Condemnation, any guaranty or indemnity, or otherwise, shall be applied in the following order of priority: (a) first, to the costs and expenses of collection, enforcement, foreclosure, receivership, sale, possession and protection of the Property, including reasonable attorneys' fees, court costs, trustee's and receiver's fees and expert fees; (b) second, to the payment of any protective advances or other advances made by Mortgagee under **Article 11**; (c) third, to the Late Charge, the Yield Maintenance Premium (to the extent payable) and any other fees and charges; (d) fourth, to interest at the Default Rate and any other interest accrued on the Indebtedness; (e) fifth, to the unpaid principal balance of the Loan; and (f) sixth, to any remaining Indebtedness and then to Mortgagor or as otherwise required by applicable law. Mortgagee may apply amounts in any other order to the extent permitted by applicable law, and Mortgagor waives any right to direct the application of payments.

**13.9 Deficiency Judgment.** If the proceeds of any foreclosure sale or other disposition of the Collateral are insufficient to pay the Indebtedness in full, Mortgagee may seek a deficiency judgment against the parties liable therefor (including Guarantor under the Guaranty Agreement) to the extent permitted by applicable law and subject to the provisions of **Section 18.14**.

**13.10 Deed in Lieu of Foreclosure.** Mortgagee may accept a deed, assignment or other conveyance of the Property in lieu of foreclosure, and no such acceptance shall be deemed to constitute a release of Mortgagor, an election of remedies, a merger of this Mortgage into any deed, or a satisfaction of all or any part of the Indebtedness, except to the extent of the amount actually credited against the Indebtedness in writing by Mortgagee.

**13.11 Costs and Attorneys' Fees.** Mortgagor shall pay on demand all costs and expenses incurred by Mortgagee in connection with the enforcement of this Mortgage or any other Loan Document, including reasonable attorneys' fees and disbursements (whether or not litigation is commenced), court costs, expert witness fees, appraisal fees, receiver's fees, title search and title insurance charges, and costs of publication, auction or sale. If Mortgagee retains counsel to collect any amount due or to enforce any right or remedy, Mortgagor shall pay such fees and costs, which shall be secured by this Mortgage and shall bear interest at the Default Rate until paid.

# ARTICLE 14 — CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

**14.1 Notice; Mortgagee's Rights.** Mortgagor shall give Mortgagee prompt written notice of any casualty to the Property or any Condemnation, and of any claim, settlement offer or award relating thereto. Mortgagee shall have the right, but not the obligation, to participate in and approve any adjustment, settlement or compromise of any insurance claim or Condemnation award, and Mortgagor shall not settle any claim or award exceeding \$250,000 without Mortgagee's prior written consent. Mortgagor shall execute such assignments of claims and proofs of loss as Mortgagee may require.

**14.2 Proceeds Held by Mortgagee.** All insurance proceeds and Condemnation awards (other than those payable directly to a Tenant under its Lease and not attributable to the landlord's interest) shall be paid to and held by Mortgagee, in its discretion, as additional Collateral for the Indebtedness. Mortgagee is hereby authorized to collect and receipt for such proceeds and awards and to endorse any instrument in payment thereof.

**14.3 Restoration.** Mortgagor may elect to restore the Property with the insurance proceeds or Condemnation award if, and only if, each of the following conditions is satisfied: (a) no Event of Default has occurred and is continuing; (b) the Property can be restored to a condition at least equivalent to its condition immediately prior to the casualty, in compliance with applicable law and the Plans and Specifications, within twelve (12) months after the casualty; (c) the proceeds or award, together with any additional funds committed by Mortgagor and evidenced to Mortgagee's satisfaction, are sufficient to complete the restoration; (d) the Leases and the economic viability of the Property will not be materially adversely affected; and (e) Mortgagee has approved the restoration plans and budget, which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld. If Mortgagor elects to restore and satisfies these conditions, Mortgagee shall make the proceeds available for the restoration in accordance with disbursement procedures substantially equivalent to those in **Article 10**, and Mortgagor shall complete the restoration diligently and free of Liens.

**14.4 Application to Indebtedness.** If Mortgagor does not elect to restore, or if any condition in **Section 14.3** is not satisfied, Mortgagee may apply all or any portion of the proceeds or award to the Indebtedness in the order set forth in **Section 13.8**, without payment of any prepayment premium, penalty or Yield Maintenance Premium. Any amounts applied to principal shall be applied to the installments of principal in the inverse order of their maturity and shall not reduce the amount of any monthly installment, change the amortization schedule or change the Maturity Date.

**14.5 Total Condemnation.** If all or substantially all of the Property is taken by Condemnation, or if the remaining Property is not economically viable in Mortgagee's reasonable judgment, the entire Indebtedness shall, at Mortgagee's election, become immediately due and payable, and Mortgagee may apply the Condemnation award as provided in **Section 14.4**.

**14.6 Insufficient Proceeds.** If the proceeds or award are insufficient to pay all costs of restoration and all amounts due under the Loan Documents, Mortgagor shall pay the deficiency from its own funds. Mortgagee shall have no liability for any failure to collect any insurance proceeds or Condemnation award, or for any delay in collection, and Mortgagor shall remain obligated for the full amount of the Indebtedness regardless of any casualty or Condemnation.
