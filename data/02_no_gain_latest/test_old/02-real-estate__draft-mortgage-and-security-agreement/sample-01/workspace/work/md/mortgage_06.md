# ARTICLE 16 — PREPAYMENT

**16.1 Lockout Period — No Prepayment.** The Loan may not be prepaid in whole or in part at any time during the Lockout Period (the period from the Closing Date through the day immediately preceding the twenty-fourth (24th) monthly anniversary of the date of the Note). No voluntary prepayment of principal shall be permitted during the Lockout Period, and any attempted prepayment during the Lockout Period shall be void and of no effect and shall not discharge or reduce the Indebtedness; *provided* that the foregoing shall not limit the application of insurance proceeds or Condemnation awards in accordance with **Article 14**, which shall be applied at par without any prepayment premium, penalty or Yield Maintenance Premium.

**16.2 Yield Maintenance Period.** From the expiration of the Lockout Period through the forty-eighth (48th) monthly anniversary of the date of the Note (the "**Yield Maintenance Period**"), the Loan may be prepaid in whole, but not in part, upon not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Mortgagee, and upon payment, in addition to all other amounts due, of a prepayment premium (the "**Yield Maintenance Premium**") equal to the greater of:

> **(a)** one percent (1.00%) of the principal amount of the Loan being prepaid; and
>
> **(b)** the excess, if any, of (i) the present value, discounted monthly at the Treasury Rate, of the aggregate interest that would have accrued on the principal amount being prepaid during the period from the date of prepayment through the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, computed at the rate per annum equal to the Note rate in effect on the date of prepayment, over (ii) the present value, discounted monthly at the Treasury Rate, of the aggregate interest that would have accrued on such principal amount during the same period, computed at a rate per annum equal to the Treasury Rate plus fifty (50) basis points (0.50%);
>
> *provided* that the amount determined under this clause (b) shall in no event be less than zero, and shall be zero if the Note rate in effect on the date of prepayment is less than or equal to the Treasury Rate plus fifty (50) basis points.

For purposes of this Section 16.2: (i) the "**Treasury Rate**" means the yield to maturity, as of the second (2nd) Business Day preceding the date of prepayment, of the United States Treasury security having a maturity date closest to, but not exceeding, the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, as reported in Federal Reserve Statistical Release H.15 — Selected Interest Rates (or any successor publication) under the heading "Treasury Constant Maturities"; if the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period does not correspond exactly to a published maturity, the Treasury Rate shall be determined by linear interpolation between the yields reported for the two nearest published maturities (or, if the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period is later than the longest published maturity, the yield on the longest published maturity); (ii) discounting shall be computed monthly using a periodic rate equal to the Treasury Rate divided by twelve (12) and the number of whole and partial months remaining in the Yield Maintenance Period; (iii) if the Treasury Rate is not published for the applicable date, the Treasury Rate shall be determined on the basis of the most recent publication available, as reasonably determined by Mortgagee; and (iv) Mortgagee's computation of the Yield Maintenance Premium shall be conclusive absent manifest error and shall be set forth in a written calculation delivered to Mortgagor not less than five (5) Business Days prior to the date of prepayment.

**16.3 Open Prepayment Period.** During the final twelve (12) months of the term of the Loan (that is, from and after the forty-eighth (48th) monthly anniversary of the date of the Note through the Maturity Date), the Loan may be prepaid in whole or in part at any time and from time to time, without payment of any prepayment premium, penalty or yield maintenance amount, upon not less than fifteen (15) days' prior written notice to Mortgagee.

**16.4 Mechanics of Prepayment.** Each notice of prepayment shall be in writing, shall be irrevocable, shall specify the date and amount of the prepayment, and shall be accompanied by the payment, on the date specified, of the principal amount being prepaid together with all accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid, the Yield Maintenance Premium (if applicable), the Late Charge and all other amounts then due under the Loan Documents. No prepayment shall be made on a date other than a Payment Date, and no partial prepayment shall be permitted during the Lockout Period or the Yield Maintenance Period. Any partial prepayment permitted under **Section 16.3** shall be in a minimum amount of \$500,000 and in increments of \$100,000 in excess thereof. All partial prepayments shall be applied to the installments of principal in the inverse order of their maturity, shall not reduce the amount of any monthly installment of principal and interest, shall not change the amortization schedule, and shall not change the Maturity Date.

**16.5 Involuntary Prepayments.** Any application of insurance proceeds or Condemnation awards to the Indebtedness under **Article 14**, and any application of Escrow Funds, Rents or other Collateral to the Indebtedness at any time when no Event of Default has occurred and is continuing, shall be made at par, without any prepayment premium, penalty or Yield Maintenance Premium.

**16.6 Prepayment After Acceleration.** If Mortgagee accelerates the Indebtedness following an Event of Default and Mortgagor tenders payment of the entire Indebtedness, the Yield Maintenance Premium (computed as if the payment were a voluntary prepayment on the date of tender) shall be due and payable to the extent permitted by applicable law. **16.7 Nature of Premium.** Mortgagor acknowledges that (a) the Yield Maintenance Premium is intended to compensate Mortgagee for the loss of its investment yield caused by an early repayment of the Loan; (b) the amount of such loss is difficult and impractical to ascertain and depends upon the movement of interest rates and market conditions after the date of this Mortgage; (c) the formula set forth in **Section 16.2** is a reasonable, objective, mathematically determinate method of estimating such loss; and (d) the Yield Maintenance Premium is not a penalty or a liquidated damages provision, but is consideration for the making of the Loan.
