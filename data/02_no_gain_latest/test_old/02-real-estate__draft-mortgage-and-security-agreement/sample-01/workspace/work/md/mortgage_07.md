# EXHIBIT E — SCDHEC LAND USE RESTRICTION AND COMPLIANCE PROTOCOL

**Part 1 — The Land Use Restriction**

The land use restriction and institutional control recorded in Deed Book 1203 at Page 89, RMC Office for Charleston County, South Carolina, was imposed by SCDHEC as a condition of its No Further Action determination dated August 12, 2021 for SCDHEC Site ID SC-29403-0772, relating to a historical release of perchloroethylene and petroleum-based solvents from the former dry-cleaning operation at the adjoining property municipally known as 518 King Street. The Land Use Restriction runs with the land and binds all current and future owners, tenants, lessees, successors and assigns of Lot 7 (Tax Map No. 460-12-04-007). It imposes two obligations:

| Requirement | Description |
|:---|:---|
| **(a) Prohibition on residential use** | The ground floor of Lot 7 (comprising Bay 1, 520 King Street, approximately 3,800 square feet) shall not be converted to or used for residential purposes. Current use as a specialty grocery is consistent with the restriction. |
| **(b) Vapor barrier maintenance** | The owner must maintain, in good condition and working order, the vapor barrier system installed beneath the concrete floor slab of Bay 1. The vapor barrier shall not be penetrated, removed or compromised without SCDHEC's prior written approval. Any renovation, construction, demolition or alteration affecting the Bay 1 floor slab must include inspection and, if necessary, repair or replacement of the vapor barrier, with prior written notification to SCDHEC. |

The No Further Action determination is contingent upon continued compliance with these institutional controls. Non-compliance may result in SCDHEC reopening the site for additional investigation, remediation or other regulatory action at the expense of the then-current owner.
