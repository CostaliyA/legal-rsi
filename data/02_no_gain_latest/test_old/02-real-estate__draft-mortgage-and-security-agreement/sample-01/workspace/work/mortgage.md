# RECITALS

**A. The Loan.** Mortgagor has requested that Mortgagee make the Loan to Mortgagor in the original principal amount of \$14,750,000, designated Calverley Heritage Bank Commercial Real Estate Loan No. 2025-CHS-0047, the proceeds of which will be used to finance (i) the acquisition of the Land and Improvements from Hampstead Village Holdings LLC, a South Carolina limited liability company, pursuant to a General Warranty Deed to be recorded in the RMC Office simultaneously with this Mortgage, and (ii) the Project. The Loan is evidenced by the Note and is made on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Term Sheet and the other Loan Documents.

**B. Disbursement Structure.** The Loan proceeds are to be disbursed in two components: (i) the Initial Advance of \$12,500,000 at closing, to be applied toward the purchase price for the Property, closing costs and funded reserves; and (ii) the Renovation Holdback of \$2,250,000, to be held by Mortgagee and disbursed on a Draw basis in accordance with **Article 10** for Renovation Costs, against a total Approved Renovation Budget of \$3,150,000, with the balance of \$900,000 of Renovation Costs to be funded by Mortgagor from the Borrower Equity Contribution.

**C. Guaranties and Environmental Indemnity.** The Loan is additionally secured by the Guaranty Agreement, including the Completion Guaranty and the interest-shortfall payment guaranty, and by the Environmental Indemnity Agreement, in each case executed by Guarantor and, where applicable, Mortgagor, and the obligations of Mortgagor under the Loan Documents are further supported by the Completion Guaranty with respect to completion of the Project.

**D. Title.** The Title Insurer has issued Commitment No. PT-2025-04-1178, effective February 28, 2025, committing to issue the Title Policy in the amount of \$14,750,000 insuring the Lien of this Mortgage as a first-priority lien on the Land and Improvements, subject only to the Permitted Exceptions.

**E. Environmental Condition.** The Phase I Environmental Site Assessment prepared by Clearfield Environmental Consulting LLC (Report No. CLR-2025-0119, dated January 28, 2025) identified no Recognized Environmental Conditions and one Controlled Recognized Environmental Condition affecting Lot 7 (Tax Map No. 460-12-04-007), arising from a historical release at the adjoining property municipally known as 518 King Street, which condition has been addressed under SCDHEC oversight through a No Further Action determination dated August 12, 2021 (SCDHEC Site ID SC-29403-0772) and is managed through the Land Use Restriction recorded in Deed Book 1203, Page 89, in the RMC Office. This Mortgage contains express covenants and an Event of Default addressing compliance with the Land Use Restriction, as required by the Title Insurer's special instructions and by **Section 5.4** of the Term Sheet.

**F. Purpose of Instrument.** This Mortgage is given to secure the Indebtedness, including the Renovation Holdback disbursements, and is intended to constitute (i) a mortgage of real property under the laws of the State of South Carolina, (ii) a security agreement under Article 9 of the UCC, (iii) a financing statement filed as a fixture filing, and (iv) an absolute and present assignment of the Leases, Rents, issues and profits under the South Carolina Uniform Assignment of Rents Act, Chapter 3, Title 29 of the South Carolina Code of Laws, as amended.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the Loan and the covenants and agreements contained in the Loan Documents, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Mortgagor hereby grants, bargains, sells, conveys, mortgages, pledges, assigns, transfers and sets over to Mortgagee, and grants to Mortgagee a security interest in, all of the following property, rights, interests and privileges, whether now owned or hereafter acquired (collectively, the "**Property**"), TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the same unto Mortgagee, its successors and assigns, upon the terms and conditions of this Mortgage:

# ARTICLE 16 — PREPAYMENT

**16.1 Lockout Period — No Prepayment.** The Loan may not be prepaid in whole or in part at any time during the Lockout Period (the period from the Closing Date through the day immediately preceding the twenty-fourth (24th) monthly anniversary of the date of the Note). **16.2 Yield Maintenance Period.** From the expiration of the Lockout Period through the forty-eighth (48th) monthly anniversary of the date of the Note (the "**Yield Maintenance Period**"), the Loan may be prepaid in whole, but not in part, upon not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Mortgagee, and upon payment, in addition to all other amounts due, of a prepayment premium (the "**Yield Maintenance Premium**") equal to the greater of:

> **(a)** one percent (1.00%) of the principal amount of the Loan being prepaid; and
>
> **(b)** the excess, if any, of (i) the present value, discounted monthly at the Treasury Rate, of the aggregate interest that would have accrued on the principal amount being prepaid during the period from the date of prepayment through the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, computed at the rate per annum equal to the Note rate in effect on the date of prepayment, over (ii) the present value, discounted monthly at the Treasury Rate, of the aggregate interest that would have accrued on such principal amount during the same period, computed at a rate per annum equal to the Treasury Rate plus fifty (50) basis points (0.50%);
>
> *provided* that the amount determined under this clause (b) shall in no event be less than zero, and shall be zero if the Note rate in effect on the date of prepayment is less than or equal to the Treasury Rate plus fifty (50) basis points.

For purposes of this Section 16.2: (i) the "**Treasury Rate**" means the yield to maturity, as of the second (2nd) Business Day preceding the date of prepayment, of the United States Treasury security having a maturity date closest to, but not exceeding, the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, as reported in Federal Reserve Statistical Release H.15 — Selected Interest Rates (or any successor publication) under the heading "Treasury Constant Maturities"; if the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period does not correspond exactly to a published maturity, the Treasury Rate shall be determined by linear interpolation between the yields reported for the two nearest published maturities (or, if the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period is later than the longest published maturity, the yield on the longest published maturity); (ii) discounting shall be computed monthly using a periodic rate equal to the Treasury Rate divided by twelve (12) and the number of whole and partial months remaining in the Yield Maintenance Period; (iii) if the Treasury Rate is not published for the applicable date, the Treasury Rate shall be determined on the basis of the most recent publication available, as reasonably determined by Mortgagee; and (iv) Mortgagee's computation of the Yield Maintenance Premium shall be conclusive absent manifest error and shall be set forth in a written calculation delivered to Mortgagor not less than five (5) Business Days prior to the date of prepayment.

**16.3 Open Prepayment Period.** During the final twelve (12) months of the term of the Loan (that is, from and after the forty-eighth (48th) monthly anniversary of the date of the Note through the Maturity Date), the Loan may be prepaid in whole or in part at any time and from time to time, without payment of any prepayment premium, penalty or yield maintenance amount, upon not less than fifteen (15) days' prior written notice to Mortgagee.

**16.4 Mechanics of Prepayment.** Each notice of prepayment shall be in writing, shall be irrevocable, shall specify the date and amount of the prepayment, and shall be accompanied by the payment, on the date specified, of the principal amount being prepaid together with all accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid, the Yield Maintenance Premium (if applicable), the Late Charge and all other amounts then due under the Loan Documents. No prepayment shall be made on a date other than a Payment Date, and no partial prepayment shall be permitted during the Lockout Period or the Yield Maintenance Period. All partial prepayments shall be applied to the installments of principal in the inverse order of their maturity, shall not reduce the amount of any monthly installment of principal and interest, shall not change the amortization schedule, and shall not change the Maturity Date.

**16.5 Involuntary Prepayments.** Any application of insurance proceeds or Condemnation awards to the Indebtedness under **Article 14**, and any application of Escrow Funds, Rents or other Collateral to the Indebtedness at any time when no Event of Default has occurred and is continuing, shall be made at par, without any prepayment premium, penalty or Yield Maintenance Premium.
