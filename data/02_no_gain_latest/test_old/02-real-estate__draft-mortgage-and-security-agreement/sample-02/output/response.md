# Basis

The Term Sheet dated April 1, 2025 was treated as the controlling commercial instrument.
Every figure and date in the record was recomputed from its components; every
cross-reference in the Mortgage was machine-checked (127 sections, 18 articles, no
unresolved references).

# Principal findings recorded in the memorandum

- **The appraisal is internally irreconcilable.** Its stated income approach ($338,000
  as-is NOI at 6.25%; $587,000 stabilized at 5.85%) supports roughly $5.4m and $10.0m
  against concluded values of $18.2m and $23.5m — implied cap rates of 1.86% and 2.50%.
- **Occupancy is 56.13% (17,400 SF), not the 68% / 21,400 SF** recited in the Term Sheet
  and the appraisal; the error is embedded in the appraisal's rent and vacancy analysis.
- **The 1.25x DSCR covenant is unachievable** on the record's own projections: 0.55x
  amortizing / 0.72x interest-only in the best-case stabilization (NOI of $1,405,299
  would be required), so the covenant generates its own Event of Default at the first test.
- **The interest-shortfall guaranty expires at month 14; DSCR testing begins at month 24**,
  leaving ~$457,000 of projected unfunded debt service in the gap.
- **The 10% retainage required by Term Sheet §8.4 is absent from the draw schedule**
  (~$225,000 overstatement of contractor cash), and the draw schedule does not reconcile
  to the budget detail ($50,000 HVAC / $50,000 elevator reallocation, $5,000 overrun).
- **The Anson Lane strip** was conveyed by quitclaim, the title insurer reserves the right
  to limit coverage by endorsement, and the title commitment and Phase I ESA describe the
  strip's extent differently.
- **Sources and uses are ~$352,075 short** of closing costs and required closing-date reserves.

# Drafting resolutions flagged for instruction

Where the record was ambiguous the memorandum records the resolution adopted and its
authority — notably the yield-maintenance formula (the 1.00% floor was retained from the
Term Sheet, and the 50 bp placement departed from it under supervision instruction),
the DSCR first-test date, the net worth covenant obligor, and the additions beyond the
Term Sheet (change of control, independent manager, LUR covenants, DSCR cure reserve,
DACA, pari passu equity funding).
