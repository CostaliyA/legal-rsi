and entered into as of April 15, 2025 (the “Closing Date”), by and between
PALMETTO GATEWAY PARTNERS LLC, a South Carolina limited liability

RECITALS
A. Lender has agreed to make a loan to Mortgagor in the original principal
amount of Fourteen Million Seven Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100
Dollars ($14,750,000.00) (the “Loan”), to finance, in part, the acquisition and
renovation of the real property and improvements described in Exhibit A
attached hereto and made a part hereof (the “Property”).

B. The Loan is evidenced by a promissory note of even date herewith made by
Mortgagor payable to the order of Lender in the original principal amount of
$14,750,000.00 (as amended, restated, extended, supplemented or otherwise
modified from time to time, the “Note”), and is further evidenced, secured
and/or guaranteed by the other Loan Documents (as defined in Section 1.1).

C. The Loan proceeds are to be disbursed in two components: (i) an initial
advance of Twelve Million Five Hundred Thousand and No/100 Dollars
($12,500,000.00) to be made on the Closing Date (the “Initial Advance”), and
(ii) a Renovation Holdback in the amount of Two Million Two Hundred Fifty
Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($2,250,000.00), to be disbursed in periodic
draws in accordance with Article 8 to fund a portion of the Renovation Work
described in the Renovation Budget.

D. The Renovation Holdback constitutes a series of obligatory future advances
made by Lender to Mortgagor pursuant to the Loan Documents, and this
Mortgage is intended to secure, and to give record notice that it secures, such
future advances together with the Initial Advance, all interest thereon and all
other Secured Obligations, in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed
$14,750,000.00 at any one time outstanding, plus interest, costs, expenses and
other Secured Obligations.

rate equal to the Treasury Rate divided by twelve (12)), and with the final
