## The instrument

The Mortgage is drafted to the **executed Term Sheet** (the binding commitment), implementing the March 18 markup notes except where those notes depart from the Term Sheet — each departure is flagged in the memorandum rather than silently resolved. Principal features:

- **Granting clauses** covering all three tax parcels plus the vacated Anson Lane strip, improvements, fixtures, personal property (with a UCC fixture filing), and reserves; obligatory future advances recited so the holdback advances share first-lien priority; **no power of sale** (South Carolina is a judicial foreclosure state) with a defeasance clause accommodating either mortgage theory.
- **Article 4 — absolute and present assignment** of leases and rents (not collateral), perfected on recording, enforceable on default without a court order, with a revocable license to collect until default, and lease-specific provisions for the Lowcountry Provisions lease (no assignment or estoppel clause), the Harmon & Wren Bay 2 right of first refusal, the grocery exclusivity, and the $69,183 of security deposits.
- **Article 7 — SPE/bankruptcy remoteness** (independent manager, separateness covenants, single-purpose restriction, Key Principal control) with the Operating Agreement Amendment as a condition of the representations being true when made.
- **Article 9 — SCDHEC Land Use Restriction** covenants (no residential use, vapor barrier maintenance, annual and pre/post-work inspections, advance SCDHEC notice before any Bay 1 slab work, contractor notification, preservation of CERCLA protections) with a defined Event of Default.
- **Article 10 — renovation holdback** draw mechanics (AIA G702/G703, lien waivers, title date-downs, inspection, equity ratability, 10% retainage, uncapped completion guaranty, Lender's right to complete).
- **Article 17 — non-recourse with full-recourse carve-outs**, and environmental liability expressly full recourse.

## What the verification work found

Recomputation of the record (not acceptance of its summaries) produced findings that change the risk profile and are set out with full arithmetic in the memorandum:

1. **The 1.25x DSCR covenant cannot be satisfied.** Stabilized best-case NOI of $622,772 is 0.72x interest-only and 0.55x on amortizing debt service; 1.25x would require NOI of $1,405,299 — 193% of total potential gross income.
2. **The appraisal's income approach does not reconcile** to its own value conclusions (implied cap rates of 1.82%–1.90% as-is and 2.48%–2.52% stabilized, against stated selections of 6.25% and 5.85%).
3. **Occupancy is 56.13%, not 68%**, and contractual in-place rent at closing is $422,966, not $415,100.
4. **A $352,075 sources-and-uses gap** before any third-party closing cost: the initial advance plus non-renovation equity equals the purchase price exactly, leaving the TI/LC reserve, origination fee and recording tax unfunded.
5. **The draw schedule does not tie to the budget** (equity $905,000 v. $900,000; HVAC +$50,000 / elevator −$50,000; no retainage implemented), and contingency is 3.17% on a $3.15 million renovation of a 1952 building.
6. **Governance gaps**: the Operating Agreement lacks every SPE provision, a bankruptcy filing is not a Major Decision, and the 75% Major Decision threshold means GDG (72%) cannot authorize this loan — member consent is a missing closing condition.
7. **The Anson Lane strip is a quitclaim conveyance** with unquantified title coverage, and the Lowcountry lease carries no assignment, estoppel or SNDA obligation although estoppels and SNDAs are closing conditions.

## Flagged on the face of the drafts

The metes-and-bounds description cannot be drafted from the record and must be inserted from the re-certified ALTA/NSPS survey in substitution for the plat-reference description. Statutory citations taken from the file (South Carolina Uniform Assignment of Rents Act; the notice of nonresponsibility provision) are flagged for verification against the current Code before the drafts are released. Several instruments referenced in the record — the purchase agreement, the executed construction contract, the unredacted Operating Agreement, the three leases, the full Phase I report, the survey and the Note/Guaranty/Environmental Indemnity forms — were not in the materials furnished and are listed as outstanding items.
