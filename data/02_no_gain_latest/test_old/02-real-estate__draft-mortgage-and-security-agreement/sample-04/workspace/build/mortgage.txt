@H1:ARTICLE 3 — THE LOAN; PAYMENTS; PREPAYMENT
@H2:3.1|The Note and the Note Rate.|The Loan is evidenced by the Note in the original principal amount of $14,750,000.00. The Loan bears interest at a floating rate per annum equal to Daily SOFR plus two hundred eighty-five basis points (2.85%) (the "**Note Rate**"), subject to a floor: in no event shall the SOFR component used in determining the Note Rate be less than three percent (3.00%) per annum. Accordingly, the Note Rate shall in no event be less than five and eighty-five hundredths percent (5.85%) per annum. "**Daily SOFR**" means the Secured Overnight Financing Rate as published by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York (or a successor administrator) on the applicable Business Day, or if such rate is not published on such Business Day, the rate published on the immediately preceding Business Day. The conventions for compounding, lookback, rounding, and business-day adjustment applicable to Daily SOFR shall be as set forth in the Note. If at any time the Note Rate cannot be determined because Daily SOFR is unavailable or is permanently or indefinitely discontinued, the Note Rate shall be determined in accordance with the benchmark replacement provisions of the Note, and Lender and Borrower shall negotiate in good faith any conforming changes to this Mortgage reasonably necessary to give effect to such benchmark replacement.
@H2:3.2|Payment Dates; Maturity.|Accrued interest on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall be due and payable monthly in arrears on the fifteenth (15th) day of each calendar month, commencing May 15, 2025, and on the Maturity Date (each a "**Payment Date**"). The entire outstanding principal balance of the Loan, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts due under the Loan Documents, shall be due and payable in full on the Maturity Date, without notice, presentment, or demand, all of which are hereby waived.
@H2:3.3|Interest-Only Period; Amortization.|The Loan shall be interest-only for the first twenty-four (24) months following the Closing Date (the "**Interest-Only Period**"), commencing with the Payment Date occurring on May 15, 2025 and ending on or about April 15, 2027. During the Interest-Only Period, each monthly payment shall consist solely of accrued interest on the outstanding principal balance. Commencing with the Payment Date occurring on May 15, 2027, and continuing through and including the Maturity Date, monthly payments of principal and interest shall be due in amounts sufficient to fully amortize the then-outstanding principal balance over a twenty-five (25) year amortization schedule at the then-applicable Note Rate, with the unpaid principal balance due on the Maturity Date. Following any Advance made after the commencement of amortization, Lender shall recalculate the monthly installment based upon the then-outstanding principal balance, the then-applicable Note Rate, and the remaining amortization period.
@H2:3.4|Default Rate.|Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, the outstanding principal balance of the Loan and, to the extent permitted by Applicable Law, all other amounts due under the Loan Documents, shall bear interest at the Default Rate, being the Note Rate plus five percent (5.00%) per annum. @H2:3.5|Late Charge.|If any installment of principal, interest, or other amount due under the Loan Documents is not received by Lender within ten (10) calendar days after its due date, Borrower shall pay to Lender, on demand, a late charge equal to five percent (5.00%) of the overdue amount. @H2:3.9|Yield Maintenance Premium.|The "**Yield Maintenance Premium**" payable in connection with any prepayment permitted under Section 3.8 shall equal the greater of:
@I:(a) one percent (1.00%) of the principal amount of the Loan prepaid; and
@I:(b) the present value, as of the date of prepayment, of the excess, if any, of (i) the aggregate amount of interest that would have accrued on the principal amount prepaid from the date of prepayment through the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period at the Note Rate in effect on the date of prepayment, over (ii) the aggregate amount of interest that would accrue on such principal amount over the same period at a rate per annum equal to the Treasury Rate plus fifty (50) basis points (0.50%), with each such amount discounted to present value at the Treasury Rate, calculated on a monthly basis over the number of whole and partial months remaining from the date of prepayment through the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period.
@I:"**Treasury Rate**" means the yield to maturity, as of the second (2nd) Business Day preceding the date of prepayment, of the United States Treasury security having a maturity date closest to, but not exceeding, the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, as reported in Federal Reserve Statistical Release H.15 (Selected Interest Rates) or any successor publication. If no published maturity exactly matches the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period, the Treasury Rate shall be determined by linear interpolation between the yields of the two published maturities nearest to, one shorter than and one longer than, the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period. If Federal Reserve Statistical Release H.15 is no longer published, the Treasury Rate shall be determined by reference to the yield on the corresponding on-the-run United States Treasury obligation as reported by a nationally recognized pricing service selected by Lender in good faith.
@I:The Yield Maintenance Premium shall be calculated by Lender in good faith using the foregoing formula, and Lender's written calculation, delivered to Borrower together with reasonable supporting detail, shall be conclusive and binding on Borrower absent manifest error. In no event shall the Yield Maintenance Premium be less than zero; however, because the premium equals the greater of the amount described in clause (a) and the amount described in clause (b), the premium payable during the Yield Maintenance Period shall in no event be less than one percent (1.00%) of the principal amount prepaid. **Exhibit D** sets forth a worked illustration of the calculation, which is included for illustrative purposes only and does not modify the operation of this Section 3.9.

@H1:EXHIBIT D — ILLUSTRATION OF YIELD MAINTENANCE PREMIUM CALCULATION
@P:This Exhibit is included for illustrative purposes only. It does not modify, limit, or expand Section 3.9, which controls the calculation of the Yield Maintenance Premium.
@P:**Assumed facts (hypothetical only):** Closing Date: April 15, 2025. Prepayment Date: April 15, 2028 (month 36). Principal prepaid: $14,750,000.00. Note Rate on the Prepayment Date: 5.85% per annum. Last day of the Yield Maintenance Period: April 15, 2029 (month 48). Remaining months from the Prepayment Date to the last day of the Yield Maintenance Period: 12. Treasury Rate (interpolated to a 12-month remaining term as of the second Business Day preceding the Prepayment Date): 4.25% per annum.
@TABLE:1.4,2.9,2.2
Step|Computation|Result
1. Clause (a) amount|1.00% × $14,750,000.00|$147,500.00
2. Interest that would accrue at the Note Rate through month 48|$14,750,000.00 × 5.85% × (12/12)|$862,875.00
3. Interest that would accrue at Treasury Rate + 0.50%|$14,750,000.00 × 4.75% × (12/12)|$700,625.00
4. Excess (Step 2 − Step 3)|$862,875.00 − $700,625.00|$162,250.00
5. Present value of Step 4, discounted monthly at the Treasury Rate (4.25%), treating the annual excess as twelve equal monthly amounts of $13,520.83|Monthly discount rate 0.354167%; annuity factor 11.728260; $13,520.83 × 11.728260|$158,575.79
6. Yield Maintenance Premium = greater of Step 1 and Step 5|Greater of $147,500.00 and $158,575.79|$158,575.79
@ENDTABLE
@P:If the Treasury Rate in the example above were 6.50% (that is, above the Note Rate), Step 4 would be negative and Step 5 would be less than zero. The Yield Maintenance Premium would then equal the greater of Step 1 ($147,500.00) and zero, and would therefore be $147,500.00.
