# What the lease does

Built from the Term Sheet's business terms but drafted to be financeable and to protect the leasehold:

- **Reverter repriced** (Art. 7): no automatic termination; 180-day Tenant cure plus an independent 180-day Leasehold Mortgagee cure; Reverter Compensation = greater of appraised FMV or Net Hard Costs, net of Landlord Damages capped at 24 months' Base Rent, with mitigation duty, escrow/no-possession until paid, rescission right, and a limited draw on the security deposit.
- **Lender protections** (Art. 10) drafted to Bayside's Section 5 checklist rather than the Term Sheet summary: no-consent leasehold mortgage, simultaneous notices, independent cure periods, New Lease on *any* termination (30-day execution, equal priority, sublease recognition, improvements included), no surrender/amendment without consent, foreclosure/deed-in-lieu without consent, SNDA, bankruptcy/§365.
- **Percentage rent** (Art. 4): Breakpoint indexed in lockstep with Base Rent; cash-basis Gross Revenues with 17 closed-list exclusions; audit lookback and deemed acceptance.
- **Tax abatement** (Art. 6): runs from Rent Commencement; recorded City agreement as a closing condition; dollar-for-dollar Base Rent offset and termination right on shortfall.
- **Definitional fix**: "Lease Year" replaced with distinct "Term Year" / "Rent Year" to resolve the Term Sheet's three-year ambiguity.
- Also: ELUR ground-floor program and DEEP process (Art. 5, 9.3), remediation cost bands and credit acceleration/cash-out, mechanics' lien as a Landlord obligation (not a Permitted Exception), recorded ROFR amendment with eight carve-outs, objective LOC step-down with deemed approval, casualty/condemnation valuation mechanics, tenant-favourable default remedies, and Term Sheet supersession.

# Key findings the memo surfaces (beyond the client's own list)

1. **Title Commitment Exhibit A is arithmetically false** — its own courses (580.00 ft × 480.00 ft) enclose ~279,535 SF (6.42 acres) against a stated 139,392 SF, and describe a different surveyor/drawing than the Survey in the file (435.22 ft × 320.47 ft).
2. **Bulkhead** stated at ~480 LF in four documents but the surveyed eastern boundary is 320.47 ft; pedestrian easement area computed on the 480-ft assumption (7,200 SF vs 4,807 SF); structure described inconsistently (concrete/timber vs steel sheet pile); no cost allocated anywhere in the record.
3. **Recorded 2019 ROFR has no mortgage/foreclosure carve-out** and reaches "any interest" in the leasehold — a direct conflict with Bayside §§5.1 and 5.6 that only a recorded amendment can cure.
4. **Tax abatement is not binding on the tax levying entity**; worth ~$46.4M nominal over 15 years, of which ~$4.6M is lost by starting the clock at Lease Commencement rather than Rent Commencement.
5. **Percentage rent with a fixed Breakpoint** runs from ~0.9% to ~30% of Base Rent by Year 99 (~$146M more than an indexed construct on an illustrative revenue path).
6. **ESA contingency is mislabelled**: the "10%" lines are 5.71% and 4.76%, understating the estimate by $102,500 before the excluded RAP, SMP, DEEP fees, verification, dewatering, and bulkhead costs.
7. **Schedule conflict**: Milestone 3 coincides exactly with Bayside's final extended maturity; Coventry's completion guaranty runs to month 48; Phase 2 (2032) falls outside the loan term.
8. Plus: mechanics' lien treated as a Permitted Exception though Title Requirement No. 7 bars policy issuance; three conflicting AMI geographies; blank/unexecuted Resolution certification; City not a party to the abatement; no Landlord environmental indemnity; unexecuted Bayside term sheet that expired 6/27/2025.
