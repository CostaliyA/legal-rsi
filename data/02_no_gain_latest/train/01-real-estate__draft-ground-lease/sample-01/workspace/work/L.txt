RECITALS
A. Landlord holds fee simple title to that certain parcel of land located at 10-30
Pier Street, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801, identified on the records of the Port
Calloway Tax Assessor as Parcel No. 127-003-0045, consisting of approximately 3.2
acres (139,392 square feet) and more particularly described on Exhibit A (the
“Premises”).
B. Landlord acquired the Premises through eminent domain proceedings
concluded in 2012 for the purpose of implementing the waterfront revitalization
plan for the Port Calloway Waterfront Revitalization District, and the Premises are
currently unimproved and are subject to the Environmental Land Use Restriction
recorded March 22, 2018, at Volume 412, Page 88 of the Port Calloway Land
Records (the “ELUR”).
C. Tenant desires to lease the Premises from Landlord and to develop, construct,
own (for the Term), operate, and maintain thereon a mixed-use project consisting
of a residential rental tower, a Class A office building, ground-floor retail space,
structured and/or below-grade accessory parking, and publicly accessible
waterfront open space (the “Project”), and Landlord desires to lease the Premises
to Tenant for such purposes, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in
this Lease.

D. Landlord and Tenant entered into that certain binding term sheet dated June
10, 2025 (the “Term Sheet”) setting forth the principal business terms for this
Lease, and Landlord’s Board of Commissioners adopted Resolution No. 2025-047
on June 5, 2025, authorizing the execution and delivery of this Lease.
E. The Project will be financed in part with a construction loan from Bayside
Federal Lending Corp. (“Bayside”) secured by a first-priority leasehold mortgage
on Tenant’s leasehold interest in the Premises and the Improvements, and the
parties intend that this Lease constitute a financeable, institutional-quality ground
lease that satisfies the requirements of Bayside and of any successor or additional
Leasehold Mortgagee during the Term.

ARTICLE 3. BASE RENT

Section 3.1 Initial Base Rent.
Commencing on the Rent Commencement Date, Tenant shall pay initial annual
Base Rent of Two Million Eight Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars
($2,850,000) (approximately $20.45 per square foot per annum based on 139,392
square feet), payable in equal monthly installments of Two Hundred Thirty-Seven
Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($237,500) on or before the first day of each
calendar month, without notice or demand.

Section 3.2 Fixed Escalation (Rent Years 1 through 15).
Base Rent shall escalate by two and one-half percent (2.5%) per annum,
compounded annually, applied in five (5) year steps from the initial Base Rent, so
that annual Base Rent is as follows:

Rent Years              Annual Base Rent               Monthly Installment (annual
                                                       amount / 12)
1 through 5             $2,850,000                     $237,500.00
6 through 10            $3,224,513                     $268,709.42
11 through 15           $3,648,241                     $304,020.08
The amounts in the table are computed as $2,850,000 multiplied by 1.025 raised to
the fifth and tenth powers, respectively, and rounded to the nearest whole dollar. If
any amount stated in the table differs from the amount produced by that formula,
the formula controls, and the monthly installment shall in all events equal one-
twelfth of the annual amount determined under the formula, rounded to the

EXHIBIT G — AFFORDABLE HOUSING REQUIREMENTS
  1. Minimum percentage. Not less than fifteen percent (15%) of the
     residential dwelling units in the Project shall be Affordable Units, calculated
     on the total unit count as finally designed and thereafter as adjusted under
     Section 9.4(b), with fractions rounded up to the next whole unit.
  2. Income eligibility. Affordable Units shall be available to households
     earning at or below eighty percent (80%) of Area Median Income,
     determined annually by HUD for the metropolitan statistical area applicable
     to Port Calloway (Section 9.4(c)).
  3. Rents. Rents for Affordable Units shall not exceed the applicable HUD-
     published maximum rent for the unit size at the required income level,
     inclusive of utilities through a utility allowance, and shall be maintained for
     the duration of the Term.
  4. Location. Affordable Units shall be located on the second floor and above
     (Section 9.3(d)) and distributed proportionately by unit type and across
     floors (Section 9.4(d)).
  5. Design. Affordable Units shall be comparable in size, finish, and access to
     amenities to market-rate units of the same bedroom count, and shall not be
     distinguishable from the exterior.
  6. Marketing and verification. Tenant shall maintain an affirmative
     marketing plan, verify household income at initial occupancy and annually,
     and deliver an annual compliance certificate to Landlord (Section 9.4(e)).
  7. Duration. The requirement runs for the duration of the Term and with the
     leasehold interest.
  8. Enforcement. Notice and 60-day cure; remedies limited to specific
     performance and injunctive relief; no default, reverter, forfeiture, or draw on
     the Security Deposit (Section 9.4(f)).
  9. Conforming amendment. The parties shall execute an amended or
     supplemental declaration conforming the recorded Affordable Housing
     Restrictive Covenant (Volume 408, Page 230) to this Exhibit, including the
     HUD area determination, the recalculation methodology, the monitoring
     entity, the floor-location requirement, and the enforcement and cure
     provisions.
