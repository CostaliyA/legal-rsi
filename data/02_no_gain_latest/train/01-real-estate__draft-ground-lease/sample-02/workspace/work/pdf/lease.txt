This GROUND LEASE AGREEMENT (this "Lease") is made and entered into as of
______, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between CITY OF PORT CALLOWAY
REDEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY, a public body corporate and politic organized and
existing under the provisions of Connecticut General Statutes § 8-125 et seq., having
its principal offices at 75 Municipal Plaza, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801 (the
"Landlord" or the "Authority"), and MERIDIAN GATEWAY HOLDINGS LLC, a
Delaware limited liability company duly formed on August 14, 2019, registered to do
business in the State of Connecticut, having its principal place of business at 400
Harborview Drive, Suite 1200, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801 (the "Tenant").

                                               RECITALS

A.     Landlord holds fee simple title to that certain parcel of land located at 10–30 Pier
       Street, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801, identified as Assessor's Parcel No. 127-
       003-0045, containing approximately 3.2 acres (139,392 square feet), as more
       particularly described on Exhibit A attached hereto (the "Premises"), which
       Premises Landlord acquired through eminent domain proceedings concluded in
       2012.
B.     The Premises are located within the Port Calloway Waterfront Revitalization
       District and are zoned WRD-3 (Mixed-Use High Density) under the Port Calloway
       Zoning Regulations, and are subject to that certain Environmental Land Use
       Restriction recorded March 22, 2018, at Volume 412, Page 88 of the Port
       Calloway Land Records (the "ELUR") and to the other matters described on
       Exhibit B.
C.     Tenant intends to develop on the Premises a mixed-use project consisting of a
       twenty-eight (28) story residential rental tower, a six (6) story Class A office
       building, ground-floor retail space, accessory structured and/or below-grade
       parking, and not less than eight thousand (8,000) square feet of publicly
       accessible waterfront open space, together with related amenities and
       improvements (collectively, the "Project"), at an estimated total project cost of
       Four Hundred Twenty-Five Million Dollars ($425,000,000).
D.     Landlord and Tenant are parties to that certain binding term sheet dated June 10,
       2025 (the "Term Sheet") setting forth the principal business terms of this Lease,
       and Landlord's Board of Commissioners adopted Resolution No. 2025-047 on
       June 5, 2025, authorizing the Executive Director to negotiate, execute and deliver
       this Lease.
E.     Tenant has obtained a construction loan commitment from Bayside Federal
       Lending Corp. in the principal amount of Two Hundred Eighty-Five Million
       Dollars ($285,000,000), and equity commitments from Coventry Capital Partners
       LP in the amount of Ninety-Five Million Dollars ($95,000,000) and from Tenant's
       members in the amount of Forty-Five Million Dollars ($45,000,000), each of
       which is conditioned upon this Lease being in form and substance satisfactory to
       the construction lender and containing the leasehold mortgage protections set
       forth in Article 9.
F.     The environmental condition of the Premises has been evaluated in the Phase I
       Environmental Site Assessment dated March 15, 2025 and the Phase II
       Environmental Site Assessment dated May 2, 2025, each prepared by Halcyon
