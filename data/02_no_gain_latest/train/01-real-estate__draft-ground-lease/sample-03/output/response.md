## What the lease does

Drafted for the tenant, and built to satisfy every one of Bayside's seven required ground lease provisions (its §5). The four client priorities are implemented as follows:

- **Leasehold protection** — Art. 5 (Improvement Compensation: FMV buyout or unreimbursed hard/soft cost reimbursement at Tenant's election), §5.7 (no reverter termination effective until compensation is paid; non-payment waives the default), §19.2 (exclusive remedies, no acceleration, no forfeiture).
- **Financeability** — Art. 17 in full (mortgage consent without fee, SNDA, Bayside §5.3-form New Lease on *any* termination, consecutive 60/90/180-day mortgagee cure, no voluntary surrender, foreclosure free of the ROFR, bankruptcy protections), §3.9 and §17.11 (fee-mortgage subordination), §18.6 (recorded ROFR amendment).
- **Tax abatement** — §8.6 (abatement period runs from the *Rent* Commencement Date and is computed on actual assessed value), §8.7 (cap on Tenant's tax bill, Landlord pays or Tenant offsets, no default if the City fails).
- **Reverter** — Art. 5 throughout, including §5.11(d) (fair-value fallback if the article is struck down as a penalty).

## Key findings in the memo

1. **The Title Commitment's legal description describes a different parcel** — its metes and bounds compute to 279,535 SF (6.417 ac) against the stated 139,392 SF (3.2 ac) and close at 1:1,927, not the 1:15,000 the surveyor certified. Every bearing, distance and the surveyor's identity differ from the survey of record.
2. **The recorded 2019 Right of First Refusal has no foreclosure carve-out** (the title company says so expressly) — a standalone financeability defect that lease drafting cannot cure; the Authority must record an amendment.
3. **No binding financing commitment** — the Bayside term sheet is expressly non-binding except §§11–12 and expired 6/27/25.
4. **Tax abatement burns three construction years** and, as written, would charge $1,546,375/yr on a vacant lot assessed at $4.2M — quantified exposure of **$4,201,275**; and the abatement is a *City* commitment with no instrument, no Board of Finance action and no identified statutory basis.
5. **ELUR vs. the Project's ground-floor lobby/amenity program** — Halcyon flags the ambiguity and recommends a DEEP interpretation before design and lease are fixed.
6. **The bulkhead** — fair-to-poor, ~480 LF, excluded from the $2.4M remediation estimate, unallocated in the Term Sheet, and described three different ways in three documents; plus a 480 LF vs. 320.47' waterfront-frontage conflict that makes the 8,000 SF open space geometrically impossible in a 15-foot strip.
7. **Base rent has a 13.14% cliff at the first reset** ($3,648,200 → $4,127,650) because "compounding annually" and "adjustments at five-year intervals" cannot both be true.
8. **Percentage rent breakpoint fixed at $28.5M for 99 years** — indexed in the draft; the client's concern is confirmed and quantified.

Every figure in the record was independently recomputed (Part III of the memo): one genuine arithmetic error, one survey/title error, one geometric inconsistency, and a handful of immaterial roundings. All matter — base rent, remediation credit, LOC, sources/uses, tax phases, and all four milestone dates — ties exactly.

The memo closes with a 24-row negotiation matrix that flags, item by item, where the draft departs from the Term Sheet so nothing is a surprise in the room.
