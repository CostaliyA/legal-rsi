# PREAMBLE AND RECITALS

**THIS GROUND LEASE AGREEMENT** (this **"Lease"**) is made and entered into as of the \_\_\_\_ day of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, 2025 (the **"Effective Date"**), by and between:

**CITY OF PORT CALLOWAY REDEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY**, a public body corporate and politic organized and existing under the provisions of the Connecticut General Statutes, Section 8-125 *et seq.*, with its principal offices located at 75 Municipal Plaza, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801, as landlord (the **"Authority"** or **"Landlord"**); and

**MERIDIAN GATEWAY HOLDINGS LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company duly formed on August 14, 2019, and authorized to transact business in the State of Connecticut, with its principal place of business at 400 Harborview Drive, Suite 1200, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801, as tenant (the **"Tenant"**).

## RECITALS

**A.** Landlord holds fee simple title to a certain parcel of land consisting of approximately 3.2 acres (139,392 square feet) located at 10-30 Pier Street, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801, identified on the records of the Port Calloway Tax Assessor as Parcel No. 127-003-0045 (the **"Premises"**), which Landlord acquired by final judgment of condemnation entered in 2012.

**B.** Landlord is authorized by its enabling legislation and by Resolution No. 2025-047, adopted by its Board of Commissioners on June 5, 2025, to lease the Premises to Tenant for a term of ninety-nine (99) years and to execute and deliver this Lease and the related documents contemplated hereby.

**C.** On June 10, 2025, Landlord and Tenant executed a binding-in-part term sheet (the **"Term Sheet"**) setting forth the principal business terms upon which the parties agreed to negotiate and execute this Lease. The Term Sheet contemplated that, upon execution and delivery of this Lease, this Lease would supersede the Term Sheet in its entirety.

**D.** Tenant has agreed to develop on the Premises a mixed-use waterfront project consisting of a twenty-eight (28) story residential rental tower, a six (6) story Class A office building, ground-floor retail space, structured and/or below-grade accessory parking, and a minimum of 8,000 square feet of publicly accessible open space on the waterfront, at a total development cost of approximately $425,000,000 (the **"Project"**).

**E.** Tenant intends to finance the Project with a construction loan from Bayside Federal Lending Corp. in the original principal amount of $285,000,000, preferred equity from Coventry Capital Partners LP in the amount of $95,000,000, and co-investment capital from Tenant's members in the amount of $45,000,000. The Parties acknowledge that the availability of such financing is conditioned upon this Lease containing the Leasehold Mortgage protections set forth in Article 17, and that such financing is essential to Tenant's performance of its obligations hereunder.

**F.** The Premises are subject to an Environmental Land Use Restriction recorded March 22, 2018 in Volume 412, Page 88 of the Port Calloway Land Records (the **"ELUR"**), and to residual petroleum hydrocarbon contamination documented in the Phase I Environmental Site Assessment dated March 15, 2025 and the Phase II Environmental Site Assessment dated May 2, 2025, each prepared by Halcyon Environmental Consultants Inc. The Parties intend that this Lease allocate responsibility for the investigation, remediation, and long-term management of the environmental conditions at the Premises as set forth in Article 9.

**G.** The Premises lie within FEMA Flood Zone AE, with a Base Flood Elevation of twelve (12) feet NAVD88, and the Parties intend that the Project be designed and constructed to provide freeboard above the Base Flood Elevation as required by this Lease and applicable law.

**H.** The Parties acknowledge that the public benefits of the Project — including the remediation and productive reuse of a contaminated and underutilized waterfront parcel, the creation of housing (including affordable housing), the construction of a publicly accessible waterfront open space connected to the existing promenade, the generation of employment, and the expansion of the municipal tax base — constitute good and valuable consideration for Landlord's entry into this Lease and for the tax abatement contemplated by Article 8.

**Section 6.6 CPI Mechanics.**

>
> (c) If the CPI is rebased (that is, the index is re-referenced to a new base year), the Parties shall apply the conversion factor published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics so that the comparison reflects equivalent index levels.
>
> (d) If the CPI ceases to be published and no successor index is published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Parties shall select a comparable index of consumer prices for the Northeast Region published by a recognized governmental or financial authority; if the Parties cannot agree within thirty (30) days, the selection shall be made by the Appraiser appointed pursuant to Section 5.6(a), whose determination shall be final and binding.

**Section 6.7 Net Lease; Limited Offsets.** This Lease is a net lease, except as expressly provided in this Lease. # ARTICLE 7 — PERCENTAGE RENT

**Section 7.1 Obligation to Pay Percentage Rent.** Beginning with Rent Year 6 and continuing for the remainder of the Term, Tenant shall pay to Landlord additional rent (**"Percentage Rent"**) equal to two percent (2.0%) of the amount by which the Gross Revenues of the Project for such Rent Year exceed the Breakpoint for such Rent Year. Percentage Rent shall be paid in accordance with Section 7.7.

**Section 7.2 Definition of Gross Revenues.** **"Gross Revenues"** means the total gross revenues actually received by Tenant and its Affiliates from the operation of the Project during the applicable Rent Year, determined on a cash basis, including:

> (a) residential rental income, including base rent, amenity fees, pet fees, parking income attributable to residents, and other residential charges;
>
> (b) office rental income, including base rent and additional rent;
>
> (c) retail rental income, including base rent, additional rent, and percentage rent received from retail subtenants;
>
> (d) parking revenues from the parking facilities within the Project;
>
> (e) income from antenna, rooftop, signage, telecommunications, and similar licenses; and
>
> (f) income from vending, laundry, package lockers, co-working, short-term rental, event, and other ancillary operations conducted at the Premises.

**Section 7.3 Exclusions from Gross Revenues.** Gross Revenues shall **exclude**, and Tenant shall have no obligation to pay Percentage Rent with respect to, each of the following:

> (a) security deposits, except to the extent applied to rent and actually received as revenue;
>
> (b) utility reimbursements and pass-throughs, including recoveries of electricity, gas, water, sewer, trash, and telecommunications charges;
>
> (c) common area maintenance, real estate tax, insurance, and operating expense pass-throughs and recoveries from subtenants, including any administrative or management fee imposed on such pass-throughs;
>
> (d) proceeds of insurance, including business interruption proceeds and proceeds received on account of a Casualty;
>
> (e) condemnation awards;
>
> (f) capital contributions, equity contributions, loans, and other financing proceeds;
>
> (g) proceeds of the sale or other disposition of the Project or any portion thereof or interest therein, and proceeds of any sale of Tenant's leasehold estate;
>
> (h) interest income and investment earnings on reserves and deposits;
>
> (i) lease termination payments, buy-out payments, and settlement proceeds received from subtenants;
>
> (j) amounts received from any warranty, guaranty, indemnity, or claim;
>
> (k) sales, use, occupancy, hotel, and amusement taxes, and any other taxes or governmental charges collected from subtenants and remitted to a taxing authority;
>
> (l) bad debt recoveries to the extent previously written off and excluded from Gross Revenues;
>
> (m) refunds, rebates, and credits (other than refunds of amounts previously included in Gross Revenues);
>
> (n) employee, guest, and resident concession and discount amounts to the extent not collected;
>
> (o) amounts received from the sale of affordable housing tax credits, low-income housing tax credits, or any similar tax incentive;
>
> (p) grants, subsidies, and incentive payments received from any Governmental Authority; and
>
> (q) any amounts that are not true revenue to Tenant, including amounts collected on behalf of third parties.

**In addition, Gross Revenues shall be determined without including any revenue from a business or activity that is not conducted at the Premises.** Tenant may satisfy its obligations under this Article 7 by delivering a statement of Gross Revenues prepared on the same basis as the Project's audited or reviewed annual financial statements, consistently applied.

**Section 7.4 Deductions from Gross Revenues.** In calculating Gross Revenues, Tenant may deduct (a) amounts actually written off as uncollectible during the Rent Year in accordance with Tenant's customary accounting practices, and (b) the amount of any rent concession, free rent, or abatement actually granted to a subtenant.

**Section 7.5 Indexation of the Breakpoint.** **The Breakpoint shall be adjusted on each Reset Date** by multiplying the Breakpoint in effect immediately prior to such Reset Date by the same fraction used to calculate the CPI-based adjustment to Base Rent under Section 6.5(b) for such Reset Date. **In no event shall the Breakpoint be reduced below $28,500,000.** The Breakpoint as so adjusted shall apply to the Rent Year beginning on the applicable Reset Date and to each Rent Year thereafter until the next Reset Date. Tenant shall deliver to Landlord, together with the statement required by Section 6.10, a statement setting forth the Breakpoint as adjusted.

**The Parties acknowledge that the purpose of this Section 7.5 is to preserve over the full ninety-nine (99) year Term the economic relationship between the Breakpoint and project revenues that the Parties negotiated in 2025, so that Percentage Rent operates as a participation in the Project's real growth rather than as a levy on inflationary revenue increases.**

**Section 7.6 Partial Rent Years.** If the Rent Commencement Date does not fall on the first day of a calendar month, or if a Rent Year is otherwise less than a full twelve (12) month period, Percentage Rent for such period shall be computed by annualizing Gross Revenues and the Breakpoint for such period and then prorating the resulting amount by the ratio of the number of days in such period to three hundred sixty-five (365).

**Section 7.7 Calculation, Statement, and Payment.** Percentage Rent for each Rent Year shall be calculated annually and shall be due and payable within ninety (90) days after the end of such Rent Year. Each payment shall be accompanied by a certified statement of Gross Revenues for the applicable Rent Year, prepared in accordance with Section 7.2 on a basis consistent with the Project's annual financial statements, and signed by an authorized officer of Tenant. **If Tenant fails to deliver the statement and pay any Percentage Rent when due and such failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Landlord, the unpaid amount shall bear interest in accordance with Section 6.8, but such failure shall not of itself constitute an Event of Default until such thirty (30) day period has expired.**

**Section 7.8 Audit.** Landlord shall have the right, exercisable not more than once in any Rent Year and not more than once with respect to any Rent Year, to audit Tenant's books and records relating to Gross Revenues for the two (2) Rent Years most recently ended, upon not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Any such audit shall be conducted by an independent certified public accountant reasonably acceptable to Tenant, during normal business hours, at Tenant's principal place of business or remotely, and in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with the operation of the Project. **The following shall apply to any audit:**

> (a) **Cost.** If the audit discloses an underpayment of Percentage Rent of five percent (5%) or more for the audited Rent Year, Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for the reasonable out-of-pocket cost of the audit. If the audit discloses an underpayment of less than five percent (5%), or discloses an overpayment, Landlord shall bear the full cost of the audit.
>
> (b) **Payment or Credit.** Any underpayment disclosed by an audit shall be paid by Tenant within thirty (30) days after delivery of the auditor's report, together with interest at the rate set forth in Section 6.8 from the original due date. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next installment of Base Rent or, if no further Rent is payable, refunded within thirty (30) days.
>
> (c) **Records.** Tenant shall maintain complete and accurate books and records relating to Gross Revenues for not less than three (3) years following the end of each Rent Year, and Landlord's audit right with respect to any Rent Year shall expire twenty-four (24) months after the delivery of the statement for such Rent Year.
>
> (d) **Confidentiality.** Landlord shall, and shall cause its auditor to, keep all information obtained in any audit confidential and shall not disclose it except to its Board, its attorneys, its auditors, or as required by the Connecticut Freedom of Information Act or other applicable law. Landlord shall assert all available exemptions, including Conn. Gen. Stat. § 1-210(b)(5) (trade secrets and commercial or financial information), before disclosing any such information.
>
> (e) **No Landlord Audit of Subtenant Records.** Landlord's audit right extends only to Tenant's records, and Landlord shall have no right to audit Tenant's subtenants directly.

**Section 7.9 No Percentage Rent Below Breakpoint.** No Percentage Rent shall be payable for any Rent Year in which Gross Revenues do not exceed the Breakpoint for such Rent Year. Percentage Rent shall not be cumulative, and a shortfall in one Rent Year shall not be carried forward.

**Section 7.10 Relationship to Base Rent.** Percentage Rent is additional rent and is independent of Base Rent. Tenant shall not be entitled to credit Base Rent against Percentage Rent, or Percentage Rent against Base Rent, except pursuant to an Express Offset.

> (a) **Applicable Percentage; Abatement Period.** For each Rent Year during the fifteen (15) year period commencing on the Rent Commencement Date (the **"Abatement Period"**), Tenant's obligation for Taxes on the Premises and the Improvements shall be calculated on the following percentage of the actual assessed value of the Premises and the Improvements (the **"Applicable Percentage"**):
>
> > | Rent Year(s) of Abatement Period | Applicable Percentage |
> > |---|---|
> > | 1 – 5 | 25% |
> > | 6 – 10 | 50% |
> > | 11 – 15 | 75% |
> > | 16 and thereafter | 100% |
>
> (b) **Commencement of Abatement Period.** **The Abatement Period shall commence on the Rent Commencement Date and not on the Lease Commencement Date.** The Parties agree that the tax abatement program was provided to make the Project's operating economics viable during lease-up and stabilization, and that a construction period of up to thirty-six (36) months precedes the Rent Commencement Date.

> (d) **Use of Actual Assessments.** Taxes during and after the Abatement Period shall be calculated by multiplying the actual assessed value of the Premises and the Improvements, as determined by the Port Calloway Tax Assessor, by the actual mill rate then in effect, and then by the Applicable Percentage. **The Parties acknowledge that the projections in the Term Sheet (post-development assessed value of $178,000,000 and mill rate of 34.75 mills, yielding $6,185,500 per annum at full assessment) were estimates only, that the actual assessed value and mill rate will vary over the Term, and that Taxes shall be calculated on the actual assessed value and actual mill rate.**
>
> (e) **Estimated Savings.** For illustration only, the Parties acknowledge that the Term Sheet estimated annual Taxes during the first phase of the abatement at $1,546,375, during the second phase at $3,092,750, during the third phase at $4,639,125, and at full assessment at $6,185,500, in each case based on a projected assessed value of $178,000,000 and a mill rate of 34.75 mills.

**Section 8.7 Failure to Implement or Maintain Abatement.** If, for any reason other than Tenant's own act or omission, the tax abatement program described in Section 8.6 is not implemented as of the Rent Commencement Date, or ceases to be available at any time during the Abatement Period, then:

**Section 8.8 Change in Tax Regime.** If any Governmental Authority imposes a new or additional tax, assessment, charge, or fee on the Premises, the Improvements, or the leasehold estate that is not a Tax as defined in Section 8.2 during the Term, Tenant shall pay the same (subject to Article 9.2; but Tenant's obligation under this Section 8.8 shall be subject to the following):
