@TITLE GROUND LEASE AGREEMENT
@P This Ground Lease Agreement (this "**Lease**") is made and entered into as of the ______ day of ______________, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **CITY OF PORT CALLOWAY REDEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY**, a public body corporate and politic organized and existing under the provisions of the Connecticut General Statutes, Section 8-125 *et seq.*, having its principal offices at 75 Municipal Plaza, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801 ("**Landlord**" or the "**Authority**"), and **MERIDIAN GATEWAY HOLDINGS LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company duly formed on August 14, 2019, registered to transact business in the State of Connecticut, Employer Identification Number 84-2917635, having its principal place of business at 400 Harborview Drive, Suite 1200, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801 ("**Tenant**").
@H2 RECITALS
@P **A.** Landlord holds fee simple title to that certain parcel of land situated in the City of Port Calloway, County of New Haven, State of Connecticut, known as 10-30 Pier Street, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801, identified as Assessor's Parcel No. 127-003-0045, containing approximately 3.2 acres (139,392 square feet), more particularly described on **Exhibit A** attached hereto and made a part hereof (the "**Premises**"). Landlord acquired fee simple title to the Premises by final judgment of condemnation entered in 2012 in the Superior Court of the State of Connecticut.
@P **B.** The Premises are situated within the Port Calloway Waterfront Revitalization District and are zoned WRD-3 (Mixed-Use High Density). The Premises are unimproved, are encumbered by that certain Environmental Land Use Restriction recorded March 22, 2018, at Volume 412, Page 88 of the Port Calloway Land Records (the "**ELUR**"), and contain residual petroleum hydrocarbon contamination attributable to their historical use as a ship repair and dry dock facility from approximately 1942 through 1987, as more particularly described in the Phase I Environmental Site Assessment dated March 15, 2025 and the Phase II Environmental Site Assessment dated May 2, 2025 prepared by Halcyon Environmental Consultants Inc.
@P **C.** Tenant desires to lease the Premises from Landlord for a term of ninety-nine (99) years, and to design, finance, construct, and operate thereon a mixed-use waterfront development consisting of a residential rental tower, a Class A office building, ground-floor retail space, accessory parking, and public open space, and Landlord desires to lease the Premises to Tenant for such purpose, upon and subject to the terms and conditions of this Lease.
@P **D.** The Authority's Board of Commissioners, by Resolution No. 2025-047 duly adopted at a properly noticed special meeting held on June 5, 2025, has authorized the Executive Director of the Authority to negotiate, execute, and deliver this Lease on behalf of Landlord, and has found that the lease of the Premises to Tenant serves a valid public purpose and is consistent with Landlord's adopted waterfront revitalization plan.
@P **E.** The parties entered into that certain binding Term Sheet dated June 10, 2025 (the "**Term Sheet**") setting forth the principal business terms of this Lease. This Lease supersedes the Term Sheet in its entirety and, except as expressly provided in the Term Sheet with respect to exclusivity, the Term Sheet shall be of no further force or effect upon execution of this Lease.
@P **NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Landlord and Tenant agree as follows:

@H2 3.6 Affordable Housing Covenant
@C1 (a) **Covenant.** Not less than fifteen percent (15%) of the residential dwelling units in the Project (the "**Affordable Housing Units**") shall be designated as affordable housing units available to households earning at or below eighty percent (80%) of Area Median Income ("**AMI**"), at rents (including utilities) not exceeding the applicable HUD maximum rent, for the duration of the Term. The Affordable Housing Units shall be distributed proportionately among unit types and shall be comparably finished to market-rate units. The number of Affordable Housing Units shall be calculated as fifteen percent (15%) of the total number of residential dwelling units in the Project as set forth in the Project Program and, upon any change in the number of residential dwelling units in the Improvements (whether by renovation, conversion, partial demolition, alteration, or otherwise), shall be recalculated so that not less than fifteen percent (15%) of the then-existing residential dwelling units are Affordable Housing Units.
@C1 (b) **AMI Standard.** References in this Lease to AMI shall mean the median income for the New Haven-Milford, Connecticut Metropolitan Statistical Area as published annually by the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development for the Section 8 program, or such other HUD-defined area as may then be applicable to the City of Port Calloway.
@C1 (c) **Location; ELUR.** The parties acknowledge that the ELUR restricts residential use on the ground floor of any structure on the Premises. Accordingly, all Affordable Housing Units (and all other dwelling units) shall be located on floors above the ground floor of the Residential Tower. Tenant shall not be in default of this Section 3.6 by reason of the placement of Affordable Housing Units if such placement complies with Section 3.9.
@C1 (d) **Compliance and Monitoring.** Within ninety (90) days after the Rent Commencement Date and annually thereafter, Tenant shall deliver to Landlord a rent roll identifying the Affordable Housing Units, the income qualifications of the occupying households, and the rents charged. Tenant shall implement and maintain reasonable income verification procedures consistent with HUD guidance. Landlord may inspect Tenant's books and records relating to the Affordable Housing Units upon reasonable prior notice, not more than once in any twelve (12) month period, and may require Tenant to engage an independent monitor reasonably acceptable to both parties if Landlord reasonably determines that a material non-compliance has occurred.
@C1 (e) **Enforcement and Cure.** If Tenant fails to comply with this Section 3.6 in any material respect, Landlord shall give written notice specifying the failure, and Tenant shall have ninety (90) days to cure (or, if cure cannot reasonably be completed within ninety (90) days, such longer period as is reasonably necessary, not to exceed one hundred eighty (180) days, provided Tenant commences cure within such ninety (90) day period and diligently prosecutes it to completion). Landlord's sole remedies for a breach of this Section 3.6 after the expiration of such cure period shall be specific performance and actual damages; a breach of this Section 3.6 shall not constitute a basis for exercising the Reverter Right, for terminating this Lease, or for drawing on the Security Deposit, except to the extent of damages actually incurred.
@C1 (f) **Prior Recorded Declaration.** The parties acknowledge the Declaration of Restrictive Covenants dated November 15, 2019 and recorded at Volume 408, Page 230 of the Port Calloway Land Records. Landlord represents that (i) such declaration is the only recorded instrument imposing affordability requirements on the Premises, and (ii) the covenant set forth in this Section 3.6 satisfies such declaration. Tenant's obligations under this Section 3.6 are undertaken in satisfaction of both this Lease and such declaration, and Landlord shall not enforce such declaration separately in a manner inconsistent with this Section 3.6.
@NOTE Drafting Note: neither Section 4 of the Term Sheet nor the recorded Declaration of Restrictive Covenants (Vol. 408, Pg. 230) addresses recalculation of the 15% requirement if the unit count changes, income verification, the monitoring or enforcement entity, the consequences of non-compliance, or the distribution and floor location of the Affordable Housing Units. Three different AMI areas are referred to in the record — "Port Calloway Metropolitan Statistical Area" (Term Sheet), "New Haven-Milford" (recorded covenant) and "Bridgeport-Stamford-Norwalk" (survey). @H1 ARTICLE 8 — ENVIRONMENTAL MATTERS; REMEDIATION
@H2 8.1 Acknowledgment of Environmental Conditions
