@TITLE GROUND LEASE AGREEMENT
@P This Ground Lease Agreement (this "**Lease**") is made and entered into as of the ______ day of ______________, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **CITY OF PORT CALLOWAY REDEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY**, a public body corporate and politic organized and existing under the provisions of the Connecticut General Statutes, Section 8-125 *et seq.*, having its principal offices at 75 Municipal Plaza, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801 ("**Landlord**" or the "**Authority**"), and **MERIDIAN GATEWAY HOLDINGS LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company duly formed on August 14, 2019, registered to transact business in the State of Connecticut, Employer Identification Number 84-2917635, having its principal place of business at 400 Harborview Drive, Suite 1200, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801 ("**Tenant**").
@H2 RECITALS
@P **A.** Landlord holds fee simple title to that certain parcel of land situated in the City of Port Calloway, County of New Haven, State of Connecticut, known as 10-30 Pier Street, Port Calloway, Connecticut 06801, identified as Assessor's Parcel No. 127-003-0045, containing approximately 3.2 acres (139,392 square feet), more particularly described on **Exhibit A** attached hereto and made a part hereof (the "**Premises**"). Landlord acquired fee simple title to the Premises by final judgment of condemnation entered in 2012 in the Superior Court of the State of Connecticut.
@P **B.** The Premises are situated within the Port Calloway Waterfront Revitalization District and are zoned WRD-3 (Mixed-Use High Density). The Premises are unimproved, are encumbered by that certain Environmental Land Use Restriction recorded March 22, 2018, at Volume 412, Page 88 of the Port Calloway Land Records (the "**ELUR**"), and contain residual petroleum hydrocarbon contamination attributable to their historical use as a ship repair and dry dock facility from approximately 1942 through 1987, as more particularly described in the Phase I Environmental Site Assessment dated March 15, 2025 and the Phase II Environmental Site Assessment dated May 2, 2025 prepared by Halcyon Environmental Consultants Inc.
@P **C.** Tenant desires to lease the Premises from Landlord for a term of ninety-nine (99) years, and to design, finance, construct, and operate thereon a mixed-use waterfront development consisting of a residential rental tower, a Class A office building, ground-floor retail space, accessory parking, and public open space, and Landlord desires to lease the Premises to Tenant for such purpose, upon and subject to the terms and conditions of this Lease.
@P **D.** The Authority's Board of Commissioners, by Resolution No. 2025-047 duly adopted at a properly noticed special meeting held on June 5, 2025, has authorized the Executive Director of the Authority to negotiate, execute, and deliver this Lease on behalf of Landlord, and has found that the lease of the Premises to Tenant serves a valid public purpose and is consistent with Landlord's adopted waterfront revitalization plan.
@P **E.** The parties entered into that certain binding Term Sheet dated June 10, 2025 (the "**Term Sheet**") setting forth the principal business terms of this Lease. This Lease supersedes the Term Sheet in its entirety and, except as expressly provided in the Term Sheet with respect to exclusivity, the Term Sheet shall be of no further force or effect upon execution of this Lease.
@P **NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Landlord and Tenant agree as follows:

@H1 ARTICLE 2 — PREMISES; TERM; CONDITIONS PRECEDENT
@H2 2.1 Lease of the Premises
@P Landlord hereby leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and upon the terms and conditions set forth in this Lease, together with the appurtenant rights described in Section 2.6. The Premises are leased subject to the Permitted Exceptions and to Applicable Law. The legal description of the Premises set forth on **Exhibit A** has been derived from the ALTA/NSPS Land Title Survey prepared by Calloway Land Surveyors LLC dated April 20, 2025 (plat dated April 28, 2025), Survey Job No. CLS-2025-0417, and supersedes the metes and bounds description appearing in Exhibit A to Title Commitment No. TC-2025-07821.
@NOTE Section 2.1 is intended to resolve the conflict between the legal description in the Title Commitment and the description returned by the survey. See the accompanying Drafting Issues Memo at Items 14–17. The description in the Title Commitment as issued describes a parcel of approximately 279,535 square feet (6.42 acres) although it recites an area of 139,392 square feet (3.2 acres).
@H2 2.2 Term
@P The term of this Lease (the "**Term**") shall commence on the Commencement Date, which shall be the later of (a) October 1, 2025 and (b) the date on which all conditions precedent set forth in Section 2.7 have been satisfied or waived in writing by both parties, and shall expire at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on September 30, 2124 (the "**Expiration Date**"), unless sooner terminated in accordance with this Lease. The Term is ninety-nine (99) years. Tenant has no option or right to extend or renew the Term, and the Term constitutes the full and complete duration of this Lease.
@H2 2.3 Construction Period; No Base Rent
@P From the Commencement Date through the day immediately preceding the Rent Commencement Date (the "**Construction Period**"), Tenant shall have no obligation to pay Base Rent or Percentage Rent. During the Construction Period, Tenant shall be responsible for Taxes (subject to Section 6.2), insurance premiums, utilities, and other operating costs of the Premises as provided in this Lease.
@H2 2.4 Rent Commencement Date
@P The "**Rent Commencement Date**" shall be the earlier to occur of:
@C1 (a) the date that is thirty-six (36) months after the Commencement Date; and
@C1 (b) the first date on which certificates of occupancy (temporary or permanent) have been issued for both (i) not less than sixty percent (60%) of the Rentable Area of the Residential Tower and (ii) not less than fifty percent (50%) of the aggregate Rentable Area of the Project.
@P A certificate of occupancy or temporary certificate of occupancy issued for a leasing office, sales center, model unit, temporary structure, stand-alone parking facility, or any portion of the Improvements that is not part of the Residential Tower or the ground-floor retail space shall not cause the Rent Commencement Date to occur. If the Rent Commencement Date occurs pursuant to clause (b) above, Base Rent for the Rent Year in which the Rent Commencement Date occurs shall be prorated on a per diem basis from the Rent Commencement Date.
@NOTE Tenant's position. The Term Sheet (Section 3) would fix the Rent Commencement Date at the issuance of a certificate of occupancy "for any portion of the Project," which would accelerate the full $2,850,000 annual Base Rent upon the issuance of a certificate of occupancy for a single retail suite or temporary leasing office. See Drafting Issues Memo, Item 21.
@H2 2.5 Use of the Premises Before Rent Commencement
@P During the Construction Period, Tenant may occupy and use the Premises for pre-construction, construction, marketing, and leasing activities and for any Permitted Use, without payment of Base Rent or Percentage Rent.
@H2 2.6 Appurtenant Rights
@P Tenant's leasehold interest includes, without limitation: (a) the right to use all easements, rights-of-way, and appurtenances benefiting the Premises, including the public utility easement recorded at Volume 388, Page 201 and the public pedestrian access easement recorded at Volume 395, Page 112, of the Port Calloway Land Records; (b) the right to construct, maintain, repair, and replace the bulkhead and all shorefront improvements along the eastern boundary of the Premises, subject to all required permits; (c) the right of ingress and egress to and from Pier Street and Harbor Avenue; (d) the right to install, use, and maintain all utilities serving the Premises; and (e) all rights to air space above, and to the reasonable use of subsurface space below, the Premises, subject to the ELUR and to Section 3.9.
@H2 2.7 Conditions Precedent to Commencement
@P The obligation of each party to proceed to the Commencement Date is conditioned upon satisfaction (or written waiver by both parties) of each of the following on or before the Commencement Date:
@C1 (a) execution and delivery of this Lease by both parties;
@C1 (b) delivery by Landlord of a certified copy of Resolution No. 2025-047 of the Authority's Board of Commissioners, together with a certificate of the Executive Director and Secretary of the Authority certifying that such resolution has not been amended, modified, or rescinded and that the officer executing this Lease is duly authorized to do so;
@C1 (c) delivery by Landlord of evidence reasonably satisfactory to Tenant that the Mechanics' Lien described in Section 15.4 has been discharged of record or bonded off in accordance with Conn. Gen. Stat. § 49-37;
@C1 (d) delivery by Landlord of a recorded instrument, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Tenant and to the Leasehold Mortgagee, subordinating and amending the Declaration of Right of First Refusal recorded September 8, 2019, at Volume 405, Page 55 of the Port Calloway Land Records so as expressly to exclude from its operation any Leasehold Mortgage, any foreclosure, deed in lieu of foreclosure, or other exercise of remedies by a Leasehold Mortgagee, and any transfer by a Leasehold Mortgagee or its designee (Section 11.5);
@C1 (e) delivery by the City of an executed tax abatement agreement (or such other binding instrument as the City may lawfully adopt) implementing the Abatement Program in accordance with Section 6.2, together with evidence of all approvals required by the City Charter and the Connecticut Office of Policy and Management;
@C1 (f) delivery by Tenant of the letter of credit required by Article 7;
@C1 (g) delivery by the Title Company of a pro forma leasehold owner's title policy (or a title commitment marked to issue) insuring Tenant's leasehold estate, together with the ALTA 13-06 leasehold owner's endorsement, subject only to the Permitted Exceptions;
@C1 (h) delivery by Landlord and the Leasehold Mortgagee of the executed SNDA;
@C1 (i) delivery of an updated ALTA/NSPS Land Title Survey dated not more than ninety (90) days prior to the Commencement Date, certified to Tenant, Landlord, the Title Company, and the Leasehold Mortgagee;
@C1 (j) delivery of the written interpretation, no-action letter, or modification of the ELUR contemplated by Section 3.9, or, in the absence thereof, written confirmation from DEEP reasonably satisfactory to Tenant that the proposed ground-floor uses of the Residential Tower do not violate the ELUR;
@C1 (k) delivery by Landlord of the structural engineering assessment of the bulkhead contemplated by Section 8.9 and completion of the bulkhead work described in Section 8.9(c); and
@C1 (l) delivery by Tenant of the equity funding and loan proceeds required to be deposited at or before the closing of the Construction Loan, or evidence reasonably satisfactory to Landlord that such deposits will be made concurrently with the Commencement Date.
@P If any condition precedent in this Section 2.7 has not been satisfied (or waived) on or before December 31, 2025, then either party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Lease, whereupon neither party shall have any further obligation hereunder except for obligations that expressly survive termination. During any period of delay in satisfying the conditions in clauses (c), (d), (e), (i), (j), or (k), the Commencement Date, the Milestone Dates, and the Rent Commencement Date shall each be extended day for day, and no such delay shall constitute a Tenant default or a basis for the Reverter Right.
@H2 2.8 Memorandum of Lease; Recording
@P Concurrently with the execution of this Lease, the parties shall execute, acknowledge, and record a Memorandum of Lease in the form attached as **Exhibit F** in the Port Calloway Land Records. Tenant shall pay all recording fees and charges. Neither party shall record this Lease itself without the written consent of the other, provided that either party may record this Lease if the Memorandum of Lease is not of record. Upon the Expiration Date or earlier termination of this Lease, Tenant shall execute and deliver a recordable instrument terminating the Memorandum of Lease, provided that no such instrument shall be effective to terminate any New Lease or to impair the rights of any Leasehold Mortgagee.
@H2 2.9 No Merger
@P No merger of the leasehold estate created by this Lease with the fee estate in the Premises shall occur by reason of Landlord acquiring any interest of Tenant, or Tenant acquiring any interest of Landlord, unless and until the parties expressly so agree in a writing recorded in the Port Calloway Land Records. A Leasehold Mortgagee's acquisition of the leasehold estate, whether by foreclosure, deed in lieu of foreclosure, or otherwise, shall not effect a merger with any fee interest of Landlord.
