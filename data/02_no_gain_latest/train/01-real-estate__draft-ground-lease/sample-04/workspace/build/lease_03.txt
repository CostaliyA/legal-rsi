@H1 ARTICLE 7 — SECURITY DEPOSIT
@H2 7.1 Security Deposit
@P On or before the Commencement Date, Tenant shall deliver to Landlord, as security for the performance of Tenant's obligations under this Lease, an irrevocable standby letter of credit in the amount of Five Million Seven Hundred Thousand Dollars ($5,700,000), equal to twenty-four (24) monthly installments of the initial Base Rent (the "**Security Deposit**"). The Security Deposit shall be issued in substantially the form attached as **Exhibit C**, shall name Landlord as beneficiary, shall be drawable in one or more drafts at sight, and shall be governed by the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits, International Chamber of Commerce Publication No. 600, or the International Standby Practices (ISP98), as stated therein.
@H2 7.2 Issuer Requirements
@P The Security Deposit shall be issued by a commercial bank or trust company organized under the laws of the United States or any state thereof, having a minimum long-term unsecured credit rating of A- from S&P Global Ratings or A3 from Moody's Investors Service (or an equivalent rating from a nationally recognized rating agency) and having offices in the United States at which drawings may be presented. If, at any time during the Term, the issuing bank's rating falls below such threshold, or the issuing bank becomes subject to a receivership, conservatorship, or similar proceeding, Tenant shall, within sixty (60) days after written notice from Landlord, deliver a replacement Security Deposit meeting the requirements of this Article 7. At Tenant's election, and subject to Landlord's approval in accordance with the Landlord Consent Standards, Tenant may substitute for the Security Deposit: (a) a cash deposit held in an interest-bearing escrow account; (b) a letter of credit issued by a different financial institution acceptable to Landlord; or (c) a guaranty from a creditworthy Affiliate of Tenant. Nothing in this Section shall require that the issuing bank be "federally chartered."
@H2 7.3 Renewal; Non-Renewal
@P The Security Deposit shall have an expiration date not earlier than one (1) year after issuance (the issuer may provide for annual renewal by operation of law or by automatic extension). Tenant shall deliver a renewal or replacement at least thirty (30) days before the stated expiration. If Tenant fails to deliver a renewal or replacement within such thirty (30) day period and such failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after written notice from Landlord, Landlord may draw the Security Deposit and hold the proceeds as a cash security deposit. Landlord may not draw the Security Deposit solely by reason of the issuer's non-renewal if Tenant has delivered a compliant replacement before the expiration date.
@H2 7.4 Reduction of the Security Deposit
@C1 (a) **Reduction.** The Security Deposit shall be reduced to Two Million Eight Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($2,850,000), equal to twelve (12) monthly installments of the initial Base Rent, upon Tenant's satisfaction of the Reduction Conditions set forth in Section 7.4(b).
@C1 (b) **Reduction Conditions.** The Reduction Conditions are: (i) certificates of occupancy (temporary or permanent) have been issued for not less than ninety percent (90%) of the aggregate Rentable Area of the Project; and (ii) the Project has maintained an Occupancy Rate of not less than ninety percent (90%) for twenty-four (24) consecutive full calendar months, measured as of the first day of each such month.
@C1 (c) **Occupancy Rate.** "**Occupancy Rate**" means, as of any date of determination, the percentage obtained by dividing (i) the aggregate Rentable Area of the Project that is subject to a sublease, license, or occupancy agreement under which the subtenant is (A) in possession and open for business, and (B) not more than sixty (60) days in arrears in the payment of rent, by (ii) the aggregate Rentable Area of the Project that has been delivered for occupancy and is available for leasing, excluding (A) units taken offline for repair, restoration, renovation, or casualty restoration for not more than one hundred eighty (180) days, and (B) Affordable Housing Units that are vacant but are subject to an active marketing program. Occupancy shall be measured on a Rentable Area basis and not on a revenue basis. Affordable Housing Units and market-rate units shall be weighted equally.
@C1 (d) **Documentation and Timing.** Tenant may deliver a written request for reduction, together with a certification of the Occupancy Rate signed by an authorized officer of Tenant and a certified rent roll. Landlord shall reduce the Security Deposit (or endorse the letter of credit, or deliver a replacement letter of credit in the reduced amount) within thirty (30) days after receipt of such request, and shall execute and deliver all documents reasonably required by the issuing bank to effect the reduction. If Landlord fails to do so within such thirty (30) day period, Tenant may (i) deliver a replacement letter of credit in the reduced amount and demand return of the original, and (ii) offset against Base Rent any fees or costs incurred by Tenant as a result of such failure. Any dispute as to the Occupancy Rate shall be resolved by an independent certified public accountant selected as provided in Article 18.3, whose determination shall be conclusive.
@C1 (e) **Later Reductions.** The parties may, at Tenant's request, agree upon further reductions of the Security Deposit after the tenth (10th) Rent Year, taking into account the amortization of the Project and the reduced credit risk to Landlord.
@H2 7.5 Application of Security Deposit
@P Landlord may draw upon the Security Deposit only to cure or compensate Landlord for an actual monetary loss resulting from an uncured Event of Default by Tenant, and only after (a) delivery of the notices required by Section 16.1 and Section 10.2, and (b) expiration of the applicable Tenant cure period and Lender Cure Period without cure. The Security Deposit is security and not liquidated damages. The Security Deposit shall not be drawn or retained as a forfeiture or penalty upon the termination of this Lease, upon the exercise of the Reverter Right, or for any breach that does not result in a documented monetary loss to Landlord. Landlord shall apply amounts drawn to the specific obligations in respect of which the draw was made, shall provide Tenant with a reasonably detailed accounting within ten (10) Business Days after any draw, and shall pay to Tenant, promptly upon demand, any amount drawn in excess of Landlord's actual documented loss (with interest at the rate specified in Section 4.6). Tenant shall replenish the Security Deposit within thirty (30) days after any draw to the then-required amount; provided that Tenant shall not be required to replenish any amount drawn in excess of Landlord's actual loss, and provided further that if Tenant disputes a draw in good faith, the replenishment obligation shall be suspended (as to the disputed amount) during the pendency of the dispute.
@H2 7.6 Transfer of Security Deposit
@P Landlord shall transfer the Security Deposit to any successor fee owner of the Premises, and upon such transfer Landlord shall be released from all liability for the return of the Security Deposit, provided that the successor assumes the obligations of Landlord under this Lease in writing. Landlord shall cause any Leasehold Mortgagee to be made a beneficiary of the Security Deposit, or shall deliver a consent to the Leasehold Mortgagee's collateral assignment of the Security Deposit, upon request.
@H2 7.7 Return of Security Deposit
@P Landlord shall return the Security Deposit, or so much thereof as remains, to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the later of (a) the Expiration Date, (b) the date on which Tenant has surrendered the Premises in the condition required by Section 14.5, and (c) the date on which Tenant has paid all Rent and satisfied all other obligations. Landlord may retain only such amount as is reasonably necessary to cover documented amounts owed by Tenant.

@H1 ARTICLE 8 — ENVIRONMENTAL MATTERS; REMEDIATION
@H2 8.1 Acknowledgment of Environmental Conditions
@P The parties acknowledge the findings of the Phase I Environmental Site Assessment dated March 15, 2025 and the Phase II Environmental Site Assessment dated May 2, 2025, each prepared by Halcyon Environmental Consultants Inc. (collectively, the "**Environmental Reports**"), including the presence of petroleum hydrocarbon contamination in the Northwest Corner Area (approximately 4,200 square feet) and the Former Dry Dock Area (approximately 1,600 square feet) at total petroleum hydrocarbon concentrations up to 3,800 mg/kg and 1,200 mg/kg, respectively, both of which exceed the applicable RSR Residential Direct Exposure Criteria of 500 mg/kg. The parties further acknowledge that the contamination is attributable to the historical use of the Premises from approximately 1942 through 1987, that is, to a period before Tenant's interest in the Premises, and that Landlord has, since acquiring the Premises in 2012, conducted a partial remedial action and recorded the ELUR.
@H2 8.2 Remediation Plan
@P Tenant shall, in conjunction with the development of the Project, perform or cause to be performed the remediation described in the Environmental Reports, substantially in accordance with a Remedial Action Plan approved by DEEP and prepared under the oversight of an LEP engaged by Tenant. Tenant shall implement a DEEP-approved Soil Management Plan before any ground disturbance, shall conduct confirmation sampling, and shall cause the LEP to certify completion in a verification report filed with DEEP. Tenant shall obtain Landlord's prior written approval of the Remedial Action Plan and the Soil Management Plan, which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed and shall be deemed given if Landlord has not responded within fifteen (15) Business Days.
@H2 8.3 Remediation Costs — Allocation
@C1 (a) **Estimated Cost.** The parties acknowledge that the Environmental Reports estimate the total cost of remediation at Two Million Four Hundred Thousand Dollars ($2,400,000), comprising $1,850,000 for the Northwest Corner Area and $550,000 for the Former Dry Dock Area, subject to the assumptions and exclusions stated in the Environmental Reports (the "**Estimated Remediation Cost**").
@C1 (b) **Allocation.** The parties intend that, as among themselves, the remediation of the identified contamination be borne seventy-five percent (75%) by Landlord and twenty-five percent (25%) by Tenant. Accordingly, Tenant's share of Remediation Costs up to the Estimated Remediation Cost shall not exceed Six Hundred Thousand Dollars ($600,000) in the aggregate.
@C1 (c) **Excess Costs.** "**Remediation Costs**" means all costs reasonably incurred to investigate, remediate, and obtain regulatory closure of the contamination identified in the Environmental Reports, including excavation, transportation, disposal, backfill, confirmation sampling, LEP oversight, DEEP fees, preparation of the Remedial Action Plan, Soil Management Plan, and verification report, and the cost of any ELUR modification or DEEP approval. If Remediation Costs exceed the Estimated Remediation Cost, the excess shall be allocated seventy-five percent (75%) to Landlord and twenty-five percent (25%) to Tenant up to one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Estimated Remediation Cost, and one hundred percent (100%) to Landlord thereafter.
@C1 (d) **Pre-Existing Conditions.** Notwithstanding Sections 8.3(b) and 8.3(c), Landlord shall bear one hundred percent (100%) of all costs, liabilities, and claims arising out of: (i) contamination that migrates or has migrated off the Premises; (ii) contamination or conditions not identified in the Environmental Reports that are attributable to the period before the Commencement Date; (iii) any directive, order, or claim of any governmental authority or third party relating to conditions existing before the Commencement Date; (iv) the presence of any hazardous materials in the groundwater beneath the Premises; and (v) any naturally occurring or historical condition requiring remediation under the RSRs that is unrelated to Tenant's development of the Project.
@C1 (e) **Caps and Exclusions.** The Environmental Reports expressly exclude the cost of maintaining or modifying the ELUR, obtaining DEEP approvals and permits, preparing the Remedial Action Plan and verification report, legal and consulting fees, and bulkhead repair or replacement. The parties acknowledge that Remediation Costs will therefore exceed the Estimated Remediation Cost, and the allocation in Section 8.3(c) shall apply to such additional costs.
@H2 8.4 Remediation Credit
@C1 (a) **Amount.** Landlord shall provide to Tenant a credit in the aggregate amount of One Million Eight Hundred Thousand Dollars ($1,800,000) (the "**Remediation Credit**") against Base Rent, to be applied against the first monthly installments of Base Rent due after the Rent Commencement Date, as follows: (i) the monthly installments of Base Rent due for the first seven (7) full calendar months following the Rent Commencement Date shall each be reduced to zero (7 × $237,500 = $1,662,500); (ii) the monthly installment of Base Rent due for the eighth (8th) full calendar month shall be reduced by $137,500, with Tenant paying the remaining $100,000; and (iii) from the ninth (9th) full calendar month forward, the full monthly installment of Base Rent shall resume. The aggregate credits applied under this Section 8.4(a) shall equal $1,800,000.
@C1 (b) **Flexible Application.** Tenant may elect, by written notice to Landlord, to apply all or any portion of the unapplied Remediation Credit against Taxes, Percentage Rent, or any other amount payable by Tenant under this Lease, in lieu of Base Rent, and may elect to defer application of all or any portion of the Remediation Credit to any Rent Year. The Remediation Credit shall not expire during the Term.
@C1 (c) **Early Reconciliation.** If Tenant completes the remediation before the Rent Commencement Date and Tenant's Remediation Costs exceed $600,000 (or if Remediation Costs are less than the Estimated Remediation Cost), the parties shall reconcile the difference within sixty (60) days after the LEP certifies completion, and the Remediation Credit shall be adjusted so that Tenant bears not more than its allocated share under Section 8.3 and not less than such share. If the Remediation Credit as adjusted exceeds $1,800,000, Landlord shall not be obligated to fund the excess, but Tenant may offset the excess against Rent.
@C1 (d) **Survival; Assignment.** The Remediation Credit (i) is fully earned and non-refundable as of the Commencement Date; (ii) shall be available to any permitted assignee of Tenant's leasehold interest and to any Leasehold Mortgagee and any tenant under a New Lease; (iii) shall not be reduced, offset, or terminated by reason of any assignment, foreclosure, or New Lease; and (iv) shall be paid in cash by Landlord to Tenant to the extent not fully applied on or before the expiration or earlier termination of this Lease, other than a termination by reason of Tenant's uncured monetary default.
@C1 (e) **Dispute Avoidance.** Landlord shall not dispute the application of the Remediation Credit to any monthly installment of Base Rent. If Landlord disputes the application of the Remediation Credit to any amount, Tenant may continue to apply the Remediation Credit pending resolution, and Tenant shall not be in default for non-payment of any amount subject to the Remediation Credit.
@NOTE The Term Sheet (Section 7) provides a fixed $1,800,000 credit against Base Rent while making Tenant responsible for "any actual remediation costs in excess of the $2,400,000 estimate" — that is, a capped credit with uncapped overrun exposure. Section 8.3 converts the credit into the 75/25 cost share that the Term Sheet's own arithmetic implies, and Section 8.4(d) makes the credit survive assignment and foreclosure. See Drafting Issues Memo, Items 29–32.

@H2 8.9 Bulkhead

@C1 (d) **Rehabilitation.** Tenant shall be responsible for bulkhead rehabilitation or replacement required in connection with the development of the Project, other than the restoration described in Section 8.9(c) and other than any repair required by reason of a pre-existing structural deficiency unrelated to Tenant's work. The cost of bulkhead rehabilitation shall be a Project cost and shall not be included in Remediation Costs.
