@H1 ARTICLE 9 — DEVELOPMENT MILESTONES; REVERTER RIGHT
@H2 9.1 Milestones
@P Tenant shall achieve each of the following development milestones (each, a "**Milestone**") by the corresponding date (each, a "**Milestone Date**"), measured from the Commencement Date and subject to extension as provided in Section 9.3:
@TBL 0.7,2.4,2.0,1.0|No.~Milestone~Definition~Milestone Date
@ROW 1~Commencement of Construction~Physical pouring of footings for at least one building in the Project, following issuance of all necessary building permits~18 months (April 1, 2027)
@ROW 2~Completion of Structural Frame~Topping out of the structural frame of the Residential Tower~42 months (April 1, 2029)
@ROW 3~Substantial Completion of Phase 1~Certificate of occupancy for the Residential Tower and ground-floor retail, such that the Residential Tower is ready for occupancy and the retail space is available for tenant fit-out~60 months (October 1, 2030)
@ROW 4~Substantial Completion of Phase 2~Certificate of occupancy for the Office Building~84 months (October 1, 2032)

If the Commencement Date is other than October 1, 2025, the Milestone Dates shall be the corresponding number of months after the actual Commencement Date, and the parenthetical dates shall be adjusted accordingly without further amendment of this Lease.
@H2 9.2 Notice of Delay; Consultation
@P Tenant shall give Landlord written notice within thirty (30) days after Tenant becomes aware that any Milestone is reasonably likely to be achieved later than the applicable Milestone Date (as extended). Upon Landlord's request, the parties shall meet (in person or by videoconference) within fifteen (15) Business Days after such notice, and not less than quarterly thereafter while the delay continues, to review the status of the Project and Tenant's plan to achieve the Milestone. Landlord's participation in any such meeting, and any failure by Landlord to request a meeting, shall not waive, limit, or otherwise affect any of Tenant's rights or Landlord's obligations under this Article 9.
@H2 9.3 Force Majeure Events; Extensions
@C1 (a) **Definition.** "**Force Majeure Event**" means any event or circumstance beyond the reasonable control of Tenant and not caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant, including: acts of God; fire, flood, tidal flooding, storm surge, hurricane, nor'easter, and other severe weather events (recognizing that the Premises are located in FEMA Flood Zone AE and that a November 2024 nor'easter produced water levels of approximately 10.5 feet NAVD88 at the bulkhead); earthquake; war; terrorism; civil unrest; strikes, lockouts, and other labor disputes; epidemics, pandemics, and public health emergencies; governmental orders, moratoria, embargoes, and declarations of emergency; delays in the issuance of, or the failure or refusal of any governmental authority to issue, any building permit, zoning approval, wetlands permit, coastal permit, or environmental approval, or to conduct any required inspection or review; appeals, litigation, or administrative challenges brought by any third party (including any challenge to any entitlement or to the ELUR modification contemplated by Section 3.9); supply chain disruption, materials shortage, or unavailability of equipment; utility moratoria or delays; the discovery of hazardous materials, archaeological resources, buried debris, unmarked utilities, or other unknown or unforeseen conditions at the Premises (including contamination extending beyond the areas delineated in the Environmental Reports); the discovery of any human remains; any act, omission, or delay of Landlord, the City, or any of their respective officers, agents, or contractors (including any delay in satisfying a condition precedent under Section 2.7, in delivering any consent or approval required under this Lease, in delivering an estoppel, in performing any obligation under Section 8.9, or in obtaining or maintaining the Abatement Program); any delay in obtaining utility service or in obtaining access to the Premises; and any other event or circumstance of like kind beyond Tenant's reasonable control.
@C1 (b) **Extensions.** Each Milestone Date shall be extended on a day-for-day basis for each day of delay caused by a Force Majeure Event. The aggregate extension available under this Section 9.3(b) for all Milestones combined shall be eighteen (18) months, allocated as follows: twelve (12) months for Force Majeure Events generally and six (6) additional months for delays attributable to governmental permitting processes. Extensions shall be allocated among Milestones so as to preserve, to the maximum extent possible, the aggregate relief available, and an extension utilized for one Milestone shall not reduce the extension available for a Milestone that is affected by a different Force Majeure Event except to the extent of the eighteen (18) month aggregate cap.
@C1 (c) **No Cap on Certain Delays.** Notwithstanding Section 9.3(b), there shall be no cap on extensions of any Milestone Date arising from (i) any act, omission, or delay of Landlord or the City, (ii) any delay in the satisfaction of a condition precedent under Section 2.7, (iii) any DEEP review, interpretation, or modification of the ELUR under Section 3.9(c), (iv) the discovery of contamination extending beyond the areas delineated in the Environmental Reports, or (v) any Litigation or administrative challenge to the Project's entitlements brought by a party other than Tenant.
@C1 (d) **Procedure.** Tenant shall give Landlord written notice of a Force Majeure Event within thirty (30) days after Tenant becomes aware of it, describing the event and its anticipated effect, and shall give notice of the conclusion of the event. Failure to give timely notice shall not forfeit Tenant's right to an extension except to the extent Landlord is actually and materially prejudiced by the delay in notice.
@C1 (e) **No Default During Delay.** No delay properly the subject of an extension under this Section 9.3 shall constitute a Tenant default, an Event of Default, or a basis for the exercise of the Reverter Right.
@H2 9.4 Reverter Right — Conditions Precedent
@P Landlord may exercise the right described in this Article 9 (the "**Reverter Right**") only upon the occurrence of each of the following conditions precedent (each of which is a condition precedent to, and not merely a covenant qualifying, the exercise of the Reverter Right):
@C1 (a) Tenant has failed to achieve Milestone 1 or Milestone 3 by the applicable Milestone Date, as extended pursuant to Section 9.3, and such failure remains uncured after the notice and cure periods provided in Section 9.5;
@C1 (b) the failure is not attributable, in whole or in material part, to a Force Majeure Event, to any act, omission, or delay of Landlord or the City, to any delay in satisfying a condition precedent under Section 2.7, or to any of the matters described in Section 9.3(c);
@C1 (c) Tenant has not achieved Substantial Completion of the Milestone 1 or Milestone 3 work, as applicable, and has ceased construction of the applicable work for a continuous period of one hundred eighty (180) days (other than by reason of a Force Majeure Event). If, at the time of the asserted failure, the applicable work is ninety percent (90%) or more complete (measured by hard costs incurred), Landlord shall have no right to exercise the Reverter Right, and Landlord's sole remedies shall be specific performance and actual damages;
@C1 (d) Landlord has delivered the Reverter Notice required by Section 9.5 and all cure periods (including the Lender Cure Periods under Section 10.3) have expired without cure;
@C1 (e) no Leasehold Mortgage remains in effect, or, if a Leasehold Mortgage remains in effect, each Leasehold Mortgagee has received all notices required by Section 10.2, has had the full benefit of the Lender Cure Periods under Section 10.3 and Section 10.4, and has declined or failed to exercise its rights under Section 10.5 to obtain a New Lease or to designate a replacement developer or property manager; and
@C1 (f) Landlord has tendered, or is simultaneously tendering, the Purchase Price required by Section 9.6.
@H2 9.5 Notice; Cure Period
@C1 (a) **Reverter Notice.** If Landlord believes that Tenant has failed to achieve Milestone 1 or Milestone 3 by the applicable Milestone Date (as extended), Landlord shall deliver to Tenant, and simultaneously to each Leasehold Mortgagee, a written notice (a "**Reverter Notice**") specifying in reasonable detail the Milestone not achieved, the facts supporting the claimed failure, and Landlord's calculation of the applicable Milestone Date. A Reverter Notice that omits any of the foregoing shall be ineffective.
@C1 (b) **Cure Period.** Tenant shall have one hundred eighty (180) days after receipt of a Reverter Notice to cure the asserted failure (the "**Milestone Cure Period**"). If Tenant cures within the Milestone Cure Period, the Reverter Notice shall be deemed withdrawn and of no further effect, and Landlord shall reimburse Tenant for the reasonable costs incurred by Tenant in responding to the Reverter Notice.
@C1 (c) **Extended Cure.** If Tenant is diligently prosecuting cure and requires additional time, Tenant may extend the Milestone Cure Period by up to an additional one hundred eighty (180) days by delivering written notice to Landlord together with (i) a certificate of an independent construction consultant reasonably acceptable to both parties confirming that the applicable Milestone will be achieved within such additional period, and (ii) evidence that Tenant and each Leasehold Mortgagee are funding the remaining work.
@C1 (d) **Lender Cure.** In addition to, and not concurrent with, the Milestone Cure Period, each Leasehold Mortgagee shall have the Lender Cure Period described in Section 10.3(c) to cure the asserted failure, including by exercising its right to install a replacement developer, general contractor, or property manager. No termination, Reverter Right, or other remedy shall be effective, and this Lease shall not terminate, until all such periods have expired.
@H2 9.6 Purchase Price Upon Exercise of the Reverter Right
@C1 (a) **Tenant's Election.** If the Reverter Right is validly exercised and this Lease terminates, Tenant shall be entitled to receive, and Landlord shall pay to Tenant (or, at the election of any Leasehold Mortgagee, to such Leasehold Mortgagee), as a condition to the effectiveness of such termination, an amount (the "**Purchase Price**") equal to the greater of:
@C2 (i) the Fair Market Value of the Improvements as of the date of termination; and
@C2 (ii) Tenant's Unreimbursed Hard Costs,
in each case reduced by (A) any insurance proceeds received or receivable by Tenant on account of the Improvements, (B) the amount of Landlord's actual, documented damages resulting from Tenant's failure to achieve the applicable Milestone (determined net of any amounts Landlord has received from any other source on account of such failure, including any amounts drawn on the Security Deposit), and (C) any amounts recovered or recoverable by Tenant from contractors, sureties, or third parties on account of the Improvements.
@C1 (b) **Fair Market Value Election.** Tenant may, by written notice delivered with or before the date of termination, elect to have the Purchase Price determined solely by reference to clause (a)(i) above, in which event the reduction in clause (a)(B) shall not apply and Landlord shall have no claim against Tenant for damages.
@C1 (c) **Payment.** The Purchase Price shall be paid in immediately available funds on the date of termination, or, at Tenant's election, within ninety (90) days after the date of termination with interest at the rate specified in Section 4.6, provided that Landlord delivers to Tenant, at or before the date of termination, an irrevocable letter of credit or other security reasonably satisfactory to Tenant in the amount of the Purchase Price. If Landlord delivers an appraisal-based deposit, the Purchase Price shall be determined on an interim basis by the appraisal procedure in Section 9.7 and trued up upon final determination.
@C1 (d) **Consequence of Non-Payment.** If Landlord fails to pay the Purchase Price when due, then (i) the purported termination shall be void and of no force or effect, (ii) this Lease shall continue in full force and effect as if the Reverter Notice had never been delivered, (iii) the Milestone in question shall be deemed satisfied, and (iv) Landlord shall be liable to Tenant for all damages resulting from the attempted termination, including Tenant's costs, lost profits, and attorneys' fees. Landlord's obligation to pay the Purchase Price shall be secured by a lien on the Premises and the Improvements in favor of Tenant and any Leasehold Mortgagee.
@H2 9.7 Determination of Fair Market Value
@P If the parties cannot agree upon the Fair Market Value within thirty (30) days after the date of termination, each party shall, within ten (10) days thereafter, appoint a qualified MAI appraiser with at least ten (10) years' experience appraising mixed-use properties in Connecticut. The two appraisers shall each prepare an appraisal within forty-five (45) days. If the two appraisals differ by ten percent (10%) or less of the higher appraisal, the Fair Market Value shall be the average of the two. If they differ by more than ten percent (10%), the two appraisers shall jointly appoint a third MAI appraiser, whose appraisal shall be delivered within forty-five (45) days and whose determination shall be conclusive and binding. The Fair Market Value shall be determined on the basis of the highest and best use of the Improvements as improved, disregarding the termination of this Lease and any distress of Tenant, and shall not take into account the value of the land. The costs of the appraisers shall be borne by Landlord if the final determination exceeds Landlord's last written offer by more than ten percent (10%), and otherwise by Tenant.
@H2 9.8 No Forfeiture; No Automatic Termination
@C1 (a) **No Automatic Termination.** This Lease shall not terminate automatically upon the failure to achieve any Milestone or upon the expiration of any cure period. Termination may occur only upon Landlord's express written election, delivered after all applicable cure periods (including Lender Cure Periods) have expired, and conditioned upon payment of the Purchase Price as provided in Section 9.6. Time is not of the essence with respect to the Milestones.
@C1 (b) **No Forfeiture of Improvements.** Under no circumstance shall the Improvements, or any portion thereof, revert to, become the property of, or be retained by Landlord without payment to Tenant of the Purchase Price. Any provision of this Lease, the Term Sheet, or any other instrument that would purport to vest the Improvements in Landlord without compensation is hereby deemed modified to the extent necessary to require payment of the Purchase Price, and each party waives any right to assert otherwise.
@C1 (c) **Security Deposit Not Forfeited.** The Security Deposit shall not be forfeited, retained, or drawn upon by reason of the failure to achieve any Milestone or the exercise of the Reverter Right, except to the extent of Landlord's actual, documented damages as provided in Section 9.6(a)(B), and any excess shall be returned to Tenant.
@C1 (d) **Joint and Several Nature of Remedies.** The remedies available to Landlord for a failure to achieve a Milestone shall be exclusive, and Landlord shall not recover more than once for the same loss. Landlord shall have no right to both terminate this Lease and recover damages for the period after termination, to retain the Improvements without payment, and to retain the Security Deposit.
@H2 9.9 Subordination to Leasehold Mortgage; Lender Protections
@P The Reverter Right is and shall be subject and subordinate in all respects to each Leasehold Mortgage and to the rights of each Leasehold Mortgagee under Article 10. Landlord shall not exercise the Reverter Right, and no termination of this Lease shall be effective, unless and until Landlord has complied with Article 10, including delivery of all notices, the expiration of all Lender Cure Periods, and the exhaustion of each Leasehold Mortgagee's right to obtain a New Lease. Any exercise of the Reverter Right in violation of this Section 9.9 shall be void.
@H2 9.10 Savings Provision; Waiver of Penalty
@P The parties intend that the Reverter Right operate only as a reasonable and proportionate remedy for a material failure to develop the Premises, and not as a penalty or forfeiture. If any court or arbitrator determines that any provision of this Article 9 would operate as an unenforceable penalty or forfeiture, then such provision shall be reformed, and this Lease shall be enforced, so as to give effect to the parties' intent that Tenant receive the Purchase Price for the Improvements and that Landlord receive compensation only for its actual, documented damages. Tenant does not waive, and expressly reserves, any right or remedy it may have under Applicable Law arising from any forfeiture, penalty, unjust enrichment, or unconstitutional taking.

@H1 ARTICLE 10 — LEASEHOLD MORTGAGE; LENDER PROTECTIONS; NEW LEASE
@H2 10.1 Right to Encumber
@P Tenant may, at any time and from time to time, grant one or more Leasehold Mortgages encumbering all or any portion of Tenant's leasehold interest in the Premises and the Improvements, together with all rents, issues, and profits thereof, to one or more Leasehold Mortgagees, in each case without the consent, approval, joinder, or execution of any instrument by Landlord, without notice to Landlord (other than as provided in Section 10.2), and without the payment of any consent fee, administrative charge, or other compensation to Landlord. No approval, consent, or other condition of Landlord shall be required for the recording, enforcement, or foreclosure of any Leasehold Mortgage. Tenant may also collaterally assign to any Leasehold Mortgagee this Lease, all subleases, all construction contracts and plans, all permits, and all insurance and condemnation proceeds. Landlord shall, upon request, execute a commercially reasonable consent to any collateral assignment, and shall not unreasonably withhold, condition, or delay the same.
@H2 10.2 Notices to Leasehold Mortgagee
@P Simultaneously with the delivery to Tenant of any notice of default, notice of termination, Reverter Notice, notice of Landlord's default, or any other material notice under this Lease, Landlord shall deliver a copy of the same to each Leasehold Mortgagee at the address designated by such Leasehold Mortgagee in writing. No notice to Tenant shall be effective unless a copy is simultaneously delivered to each Leasehold Mortgagee in the manner provided in Article 18.1. Landlord shall also deliver to each Leasehold Mortgagee copies of any estoppel certificate, financial statement, or other material communication delivered to Tenant concerning this Lease, upon request.
@H2 10.3 Lender Cure Periods
@C1 (a) **Monetary Defaults.** Each Leasehold Mortgagee shall have sixty (60) days after its receipt of notice of any monetary default to cure such default on behalf of Tenant, including by paying all amounts due and performing any monetary obligation. Landlord shall accept such payment as if made by Tenant.
@C1 (b) **Non-Monetary Defaults.** Each Leasehold Mortgagee shall have ninety (90) days after its receipt of notice of any non-monetary default to cure such default on behalf of Tenant, or, if such cure cannot reasonably be completed within ninety (90) days, to commence cure within such period and thereafter diligently and continuously prosecute cure to completion.
@C1 (c) **Milestone Defaults.** Each Leasehold Mortgagee shall have one hundred eighty (180) days after its receipt of a Reverter Notice to cure the asserted failure, including by (i) advancing funds to Tenant to complete the applicable work, (ii) exercising its rights under any completion guaranty or construction contract, or (iii) designating a replacement developer, general contractor, or property manager reasonably acceptable to Landlord, and causing such designee to assume the obligations of Tenant under this Lease and to prosecute the applicable work to completion.
@C1 (d) **Runs with Tenant's Periods.** The Lender Cure Periods shall run in addition to, and not concurrent with, any cure period afforded to Tenant under this Lease. If more than one Leasehold Mortgagee exists, the cure periods shall run concurrently among Leasehold Mortgagees, and cure by any one shall be effective as to all.
@C1 (e) **No Termination Until Expiration.** No termination of this Lease, and no exercise of the Reverter Right, shall be effective unless and until the applicable Lender Cure Periods have expired without cure.
@H2 10.4 Landlord's Right to Perform; Reimbursement
@P If Tenant fails to perform any obligation under this Lease and a Leasehold Mortgagee elects to perform it, Landlord shall accept such performance, and the Leasehold Mortgagee shall be subrogated to Landlord's rights against Tenant to the extent of the amounts expended. A Leasehold Mortgagee shall not, solely by reason of such performance or by reason of taking possession of the Premises, be deemed to have assumed any of Tenant's obligations under this Lease or to have become a tenant under this Lease, except to the extent it expressly elects to do so.
@H2 10.5 New Lease
@C1 (a) **Right.** If this Lease is terminated for any reason whatsoever, including without limitation Tenant default, surrender, rejection in bankruptcy, operation of law, exercise of the Reverter Right, or otherwise, then each Leasehold Mortgagee (or its designee) shall have the right, upon written request delivered to Landlord within sixty (60) days after such Leasehold Mortgagee's receipt of notice of termination, to enter into a new ground lease of the Premises with Landlord (a "**New Lease**") on the same terms and conditions as this Lease for the remainder of the Term (measured from the original Commencement Date), subject only to the following:
@C2 (i) the Leasehold Mortgagee (or its designee) cures all monetary defaults then existing under this Lease within sixty (60) days after the effective date of the New Lease;
@C2 (ii) the Leasehold Mortgagee (or its designee) cures all non-monetary defaults that are reasonably susceptible of cure within ninety (90) days after the effective date of the New Lease, or, if such cure cannot reasonably be completed within such ninety (90) day period, commences cure within such ninety (90) day period and thereafter diligently and continuously prosecutes cure to completion within a reasonable time, but in no event later than one hundred eighty (180) days after the effective date of the New Lease;
@C2 (iii) the Leasehold Mortgagee (or its designee) assumes all obligations under the New Lease prospectively from the effective date of the New Lease; and
@C2 (iv) the New Lease shall have priority in all respects equal to the priority of this Lease, shall be subject only to the Permitted Exceptions, and shall recognize all subleases, licenses, and occupancy agreements entered into by Tenant or any subtenant before termination.
@C1 (b) **Execution and Effectiveness.** Landlord shall execute and deliver the New Lease within thirty (30) days after the Leasehold Mortgagee's written request, and the New Lease shall be effective as of the date of termination of this Lease. Landlord's failure to execute the New Lease within such period shall entitle the Leasehold Mortgagee to record the New Lease together with an affidavit of the Leasehold Mortgagee reciting such failure, which recording shall be effective to create the New Lease of record.
@C1 (c) **Preservation of Economic Terms.** The New Lease shall preserve the economic terms of this Lease as of the date of termination, including the Rent schedule, the remaining portion of the Remediation Credit, the Abatement Program, and the Purchase Price credit, and shall not impose any additional condition, fee, or requirement on the Leasehold Mortgagee or its designee. No Purchase Price shall be payable on a termination followed by a New Lease, and nothing in Article 9 shall require payment of the Purchase Price as against a Leasehold Mortgagee that exercises its rights under this Section 10.5.
@C1 (d) **Rejection in Bankruptcy.** The rights of each Leasehold Mortgagee under this Section 10.5 shall survive the rejection of this Lease in any proceeding under Title 11 of the United States Code or any similar state insolvency law, to the fullest extent permitted by Applicable Law, and Landlord shall recognize the Leasehold Mortgagee's right to a New Lease notwithstanding such rejection.
@H2 10.6 No Surrender or Amendment Without Mortgagee Consent
@P For so long as any Leasehold Mortgage remains in effect: (a) Landlord shall not accept a voluntary surrender, cancellation, or termination of this Lease from Tenant without the prior written consent of each Leasehold Mortgagee; (b) Landlord and Tenant shall not amend, modify, supplement, or terminate this Lease in any material respect without the prior written consent of each Leasehold Mortgagee; and (c) any purported surrender, amendment, modification, supplement, or termination effected without such consent shall be void and of no force or effect as against such Leasehold Mortgagee, and this Lease shall continue in full force and effect as if such action had not occurred. Landlord shall, upon request, execute any instrument reasonably requested by a Leasehold Mortgagee to confirm the foregoing.
@H2 10.7 Foreclosure and Assignment
@P Each Leasehold Mortgagee shall have the unconditional right to foreclose its Leasehold Mortgage (by judicial foreclosure, power of sale, or otherwise), to accept a deed in lieu of foreclosure, and to assign this Lease to a qualified transferee at or following any foreclosure sale or deed-in-lieu transfer, in each case without the prior consent of Landlord and without triggering any right of first refusal, right of first offer, or other restriction on transfer. Any such transferee shall meet financial and experience qualifications substantially similar to those required of Tenant as of the Commencement Date, as reasonably determined by Landlord. Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition, or delay recognition of any such transferee as Tenant under this Lease, shall execute any documents reasonably requested to effectuate or confirm such transfer, and shall not require any fee in connection therewith. Without limiting the foregoing, the exercise by any Leasehold Mortgagee of any right or remedy under its Leasehold Mortgage, and any transfer resulting therefrom, shall be a Permitted Transfer under Article 11 and shall be expressly excluded from the Right of First Refusal.
@H2 10.8 Bankruptcy
@P If Tenant becomes a debtor in any proceeding under Title 11 of the United States Code or any similar state insolvency law: (a) each Leasehold Mortgagee shall have the right to request that any trustee, debtor in possession, or other party in interest assume or reject this Lease in a timely manner consistent with 11 U.S.C. § 365; (b) each Leasehold Mortgagee shall have the right to cure any default and to provide adequate assurance of future performance on behalf of Tenant to the extent necessary to prevent rejection of this Lease; and (c) the New Lease provisions of Section 10.5 shall survive any rejection of this Lease. Nothing in this Lease shall be construed to restrict Tenant's or any Leasehold Mortgagee's rights under the Bankruptcy Code, including the right to assume, assign, or reject this Lease.
@H2 10.9 Refinancings; Estoppels; SNDAs
@P Tenant may refinance the Project from time to time throughout the Term. In connection with any financing, refinancing, or recapitalization, Landlord shall, within ten (10) Business Days after request: (a) execute and deliver an SNDA, recognition agreement, or consent in favor of the applicable Leasehold Mortgagee, substantially in the form of **Exhibit D**; (b) execute and deliver an estoppel certificate in form reasonably requested by the Leasehold Mortgagee or the Title Company; (c) deliver such consents, waivers, and instruments as the Title Company may require to insure the leasehold estate; and (d) confirm that it has no claim or offset against Tenant. Landlord shall not require any fee for the delivery of any such instrument. If Landlord fails to deliver any such instrument within such ten (10) Business Day period, Tenant may deliver the instrument on Landlord's behalf with a certification of such failure, and such instrument shall be binding on Landlord.
@H2 10.10 Leasehold Mortgagee Not Obligated
@P No Leasehold Mortgagee shall be obligated to cure any default, to perform any obligation of Tenant, or to pay any Rent by reason of holding a Leasehold Mortgage. Landlord shall look solely to Tenant (and to any Leasehold Mortgagee that has elected to exercise its rights under this Article 10) for the performance of Tenant's obligations.
