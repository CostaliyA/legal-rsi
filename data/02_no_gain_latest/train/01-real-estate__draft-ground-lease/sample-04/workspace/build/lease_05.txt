@H1 ARTICLE 11 — ASSIGNMENT, SUBLETTING, AND RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL
@H2 11.1 Permitted Transfers
@P Tenant may, without Landlord's consent, without notice (except as provided in Section 11.6), and without triggering the Right of First Refusal, assign this Lease or sublease all or any portion of the Premises, or transfer any direct or indirect interest in Tenant, to any of the following (each, a "**Permitted Transfer**"):
@C1 (a) any Affiliate of Tenant;
@C1 (b) any Leasehold Mortgagee, or any purchaser at a foreclosure sale, grantee under a deed in lieu of foreclosure, or other transferee pursuant to the exercise of any remedy under a Leasehold Mortgage, or any designee of any of the foregoing;
@C1 (c) any entity that acquires all or substantially all of Tenant's leasehold interest by merger, consolidation, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, provided that the acquiring entity's tangible net worth immediately after such transaction is not less than the tangible net worth of Tenant immediately before such transaction;
@C1 (d) any transfer resulting from the death, disability, or estate planning of Marcus Ellison, or from a transfer among Marcus Ellison, Coventry Capital Partners LP, and their respective Affiliates, family members, and family trusts; and
@C1 (e) any pledge, hypothecation, or grant of a security interest in any direct or indirect ownership interest in Tenant to a Leasehold Mortgagee or to any other lender providing financing to Tenant or its Affiliates, and any foreclosure, transfer in lieu of foreclosure, or other realization upon such interest, including the pledge of one hundred percent (100%) of the membership interests in Tenant required by the Construction Loan.
@H2 11.2 Transfers Requiring Consent
@P Any assignment of this Lease to a Person that is not a Permitted Transfer (a "**Transfer**") shall require Landlord's prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed, and shall be granted or denied within twenty (20) Business Days after Landlord's receipt of a complete written request. Landlord may deny consent only if: (a) the proposed transferee is not financially capable of performing Tenant's obligations, as demonstrated by financial statements or other evidence reasonably acceptable to Landlord; (b) the proposed transferee lacks experience reasonably comparable to Tenant's in the development, ownership, or operation of mixed-use or residential properties; (c) the proposed transferee is a governmental entity or an entity subject to an unresolved sanction or debarment; or (d) the proposed transfer would violate Applicable Law. Landlord shall not require any fee, increased rent, guaranty, or collateral as a condition of consent, and shall not condition consent on the resolution of the Right of First Refusal. If Landlord fails to respond within such twenty (20) Business Day period, consent shall be deemed given.
@H2 11.3 Subleases and Licenses
@P Tenant may sublease, license, or grant occupancy rights with respect to individual units or portions of the Improvements to residential, office, and retail tenants, and may enter into parking, telecommunications, signage, and other licenses, in each ordinary course of business, without Landlord's consent. Any sublease of the entire Premises, or of substantially all of the Premises, to a single subtenant that is not a Permitted Transfer shall require Landlord's consent in accordance with Section 11.2. No sublease shall release Tenant from its obligations under this Lease.
@H2 11.4 Right of First Refusal
@C1 (a) **Grant.** During the period commencing on the Commencement Date and ending on September 30, 2045 (the "**ROFR Period**"), Landlord shall have a right of first refusal (the "**Right of First Refusal**") to acquire Tenant's leasehold interest upon a proposed Transfer that is not a Permitted Transfer, on the terms and conditions set forth in this Section 11.4.
@C1 (b) **Notice.** Tenant shall deliver to Landlord a written notice (an "**Assignment Notice**") setting forth the identity of the proposed transferee, the material terms of the proposed Transfer (including the proposed purchase price and the form of consideration), and a copy of the executed letter of intent or proposed assignment agreement. Landlord shall have sixty (60) days after receipt of the Assignment Notice to elect to acquire the leasehold interest on the same material terms.
@C1 (c) **Exercise; Conditions.** If Landlord elects to exercise the Right of First Refusal, Landlord shall deliver written notice of exercise within such sixty (60) day period, and the closing shall occur on the same terms and within the same time period as the proposed Transfer, provided that Landlord shall not be entitled to any financing contingency, due diligence period, or other condition to which the proposed transferee is not subject. Landlord shall be entitled to a credit against the purchase price for the amount of any deposit or earnest money paid by the proposed transferee.
@C1 (d) **Lapse.** If Landlord does not exercise the Right of First Refusal within such sixty (60) day period, the Right of First Refusal shall be deemed waived with respect to the proposed Transfer, and Tenant may complete the Transfer to the identified transferee on terms not more favorable to the transferee than those disclosed in the Assignment Notice, within two hundred seventy (270) days after the expiration of such period (as extended by any delay caused by Landlord or by any governmental approval). If the Transfer is not completed within such period, the Right of First Refusal shall revive.
@C1 (e) **Exclusions.** The Right of First Refusal shall not apply to (i) any Permitted Transfer; (ii) any assignment, transfer, or other disposition in connection with a Leasehold Mortgage, or any foreclosure, deed in lieu of foreclosure, or exercise of any remedy thereunder, or any transfer by a Leasehold Mortgagee or its designee; (iii) any transfer by operation of law, including by merger, consolidation, or death; (iv) any transfer of a direct or indirect ownership interest in Tenant (as opposed to an assignment of this Lease); or (v) any sublease or license permitted under Section 11.3. The parties acknowledge that the Declaration of Right of First Refusal recorded September 8, 2019, at Volume 405, Page 55 of the Port Calloway Land Records does not contain such exclusions, and Landlord's obligation under Section 2.7(d) to record an amendment providing for them is a condition precedent to the Commencement Date.
@C1 (f) **Interaction with Consent.** Landlord's exercise or non-exercise of the Right of First Refusal shall not affect Landlord's obligation to grant or deny consent under Section 11.2, and Landlord's consent shall be deemed to have been given if Landlord exercises the Right of First Refusal.
@H2 11.5 Assignment by Landlord
@P Landlord may assign its interest under this Lease to any successor in title to the Premises, or to any entity succeeding to the functions of the Authority, provided that the assignee assumes in writing all obligations of Landlord under this Lease. Landlord shall give Tenant and each Leasehold Mortgagee thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any such assignment. No assignment shall release Landlord from liability for obligations accruing before the assignment.
@H2 11.6 Notice of Permitted Transfers
@P Tenant shall notify Landlord in writing within thirty (30) days after any Permitted Transfer described in Section 11.1(b) or 11.1(c).
@H2 11.7 Release of Tenant
@P Upon any assignment of this Lease that is consented to by Landlord or is a Permitted Transfer, and provided that the assignee assumes in writing all obligations of Tenant accruing from and after the date of assignment, the assigning Tenant shall be released from all obligations accruing after the date of assignment.

@H1 ARTICLE 12 — INSURANCE
@H2 12.1 Required Coverages
@P Throughout the Term, Tenant shall procure and maintain, at its sole cost and expense, with carriers having a financial strength rating of not less than A- VIII from A.M. Best Company (or an equivalent rating from a nationally recognized rating agency) and authorized to do business in Connecticut, the following insurance:
@C1 (a) **Commercial General Liability** on an occurrence basis, with limits of not less than $10,000,000 per occurrence and $20,000,000 in the annual aggregate, covering bodily injury, property damage, personal and advertising injury, products and completed operations, and contractual liability (including the insured contract coverage for the indemnities in this Lease);
@C1 (b) **Umbrella or Excess Liability** of not less than $25,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate, following form;
@C1 (c) **All-Risk Property Insurance** on a replacement cost basis (without deduction for depreciation), including coverage for fire, windstorm, flood, earthquake, terrorism, boiler and machinery, and such other risks as are customarily insured against by owners of comparable mixed-use properties, in an amount not less than the full replacement cost of the Improvements, with an agreed amount endorsement, ordinance or law coverage, and rental loss and business interruption coverage for not less than twenty-four (24) months;
@C1 (d) **Builder's Risk Insurance** during the construction period for each phase of the Project, on a completed value basis, covering the full value of all construction work in progress, materials, and supplies stored on or off the Premises, including flood and windstorm coverage;
@C1 (e) **Flood Insurance.** During construction and thereafter, flood insurance in an amount not less than the full replacement cost of the Improvements, written on a difference-in-conditions basis or pursuant to a private flood policy (or combination of National Flood Insurance Program and private coverage) sufficient to cover the full replacement cost. The parties acknowledge that coverage available under the National Flood Insurance Program is limited and is materially less than the replacement cost of the Improvements, and that Tenant's obligation is to obtain full replacement cost coverage and not merely the maximum NFIP limit;
@C1 (f) **Environmental Liability Insurance** with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence and $10,000,000 in the annual aggregate, covering pollution conditions on, at, under, or migrating from the Premises, including remediation costs, third-party bodily injury, and third-party property damage, maintained for the period required by Section 8.6 and any additional period reasonably required by the Leasehold Mortgagee;
@C1 (g) **Workers' Compensation** in accordance with the requirements of the State of Connecticut, and Employer's Liability with limits of not less than $1,000,000; and
@C1 (h) **such other coverage** as may be required by Applicable Law or by any Leasehold Mortgagee, consistent with institutional construction and permanent loan requirements.
@H2 12.2 Additional Insureds and Loss Payees
@P Tenant shall name Landlord, the City (solely with respect to the Public Open Space and the pedestrian easement area), and each Leasehold Mortgagee as additional insureds on the commercial general liability, umbrella, and excess liability policies, and shall name each Leasehold Mortgagee as first mortgagee and loss payee (together with Landlord as loss payee to the extent provided in Section 12.4) on the property, builder's risk, and flood policies. Landlord shall not be named as an insured or loss payee on the property insurance in a manner that would limit Tenant's or the Leasehold Mortgagee's recovery, and Tenant's obligation to name Landlord as a loss payee shall be subject to the rights of the Leasehold Mortgagee. The policies shall be primary with respect to Tenant's obligations, and shall contain waivers of subrogation in favor of Landlord and each Leasehold Mortgagee, together with cross-liability and severability of interests endorsements.
@H2 12.3 Certificates; Notice of Cancellation
@P Tenant shall deliver certificates of insurance to Landlord and each Leasehold Mortgagee before the Commencement Date, annually thereafter, and upon request, and shall give Landlord and each Leasehold Mortgagee not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any cancellation, non-renewal, or material modification. Tenant may satisfy any requirement with blanket, umbrella, or global policies (with appropriate endorsements), and may self-insure the deductible or self-insured retention portions, provided that the Leasehold Mortgagee has approved such self-insurance.
@H2 12.4 Application of Proceeds
@P All insurance proceeds on account of a Casualty shall be paid to a disbursing agent reasonably acceptable to the Leasehold Mortgagee and applied to the restoration of the Improvements in accordance with Article 13, subject to the rights of the Leasehold Mortgagee under its Leasehold Mortgage. Landlord shall have no right to receive or retain insurance proceeds except as expressly provided in Article 13 and except for any proceeds attributable to Landlord's fee interest or to a taking of the land. Any proceeds remaining after restoration and payment of all costs shall be paid to Tenant.
@H2 12.5 No Obligation to Insure
@P Landlord shall have no obligation to insure the Premises or the Improvements, and Landlord's failure to do so shall not limit Tenant's obligations under this Article 12.

@H1 ARTICLE 13 — CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION
@H2 13.1 Casualty — Repair and Restoration
@P If a Casualty occurs, Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord and each Leasehold Mortgagee and shall, subject to this Article 13, restore the Improvements to substantially their pre-Casualty condition (or to a condition of comparable utility and value) in accordance with Applicable Law, using available insurance proceeds. Tenant shall be entitled to all insurance proceeds for restoration, subject to the Leasehold Mortgagee's rights.
@H2 13.2 Casualty — Rent Abatement
@P If a Casualty renders all or any portion of the Improvements untenantable or unusable, Base Rent and Percentage Rent shall abate from the date of the Casualty in proportion to the portion of the Improvements rendered untenantable (measured by Rentable Area), until the earlier of (a) the date that the affected portion is restored to the point of being tenantable, and (b) one hundred eighty (180) days after the date of the Casualty.
@H2 13.3 Casualty — Termination Rights
@P If a Casualty occurs during the final ten (10) years of the Term (that is, after October 1, 2114), or if the Casualty occurs at any time and either (a) the cost of restoration exceeds fifty percent (50%) of the then-replacement cost of the Improvements, (b) restoration cannot reasonably be completed within three hundred sixty-five (365) days after the Casualty, or (c) the insurance proceeds available for restoration (together with any amounts Tenant elects to fund) are insufficient to complete restoration, then Tenant may terminate this Lease by written notice to Landlord delivered within one hundred twenty (120) days after the Casualty. Upon any such termination, Tenant shall be entitled to retain the insurance proceeds attributable to the Improvements (subject to the rights of the Leasehold Mortgagee) and shall not be obligated to restore. Landlord shall not have the right to terminate this Lease on account of a Casualty except as provided in this Section 13.3 or by reason of Tenant's failure to restore.
@H2 13.4 Condemnation — Allocation of Award
@P If a Condemnation occurs, the award, judgment, or settlement (the "**Award**") shall be allocated as follows: (a) Landlord shall receive the portion of the Award attributable to the value of the fee interest in the land; (b) Tenant shall receive the portion of the Award attributable to the value of Tenant's leasehold interest and the Improvements, including the value of the unexpired Term; (c) Tenant shall be entitled to recover, in addition, its relocation expenses, moving costs, business interruption losses, and other damages recoverable by a tenant under Applicable Law; and (d) each Leasehold Mortgagee shall be entitled to receive, and Landlord hereby assents to the Leasehold Mortgagee's right to receive, the portion of the Award attributable to the leasehold interest in the order of its priority. If the parties cannot agree upon the allocation, the allocation shall be determined by the appraisal procedure in Section 9.7, mutatis mutandis.
@H2 13.5 Condemnation — Restoration and Termination
@P If a Condemnation is temporary, this Lease shall continue, Tenant shall restore the Premises to the extent practicable, and Rent shall be equitably adjusted during the period of the taking. If a Condemnation is permanent and either (a) affects all of the Premises, or (b) leaves a remainder of the Premises that is unsuitable for the Permitted Uses or for the lawful and economic operation of the Improvements, then Tenant may terminate this Lease by written notice within ninety (90) days after the date of the taking. If a partial permanent Condemnation occurs and Tenant does not terminate, Base Rent shall be equitably reduced in proportion to the reduction in the income-producing capacity of the Improvements, Tenant shall restore the remaining Improvements to the extent practicable, and the parties shall cooperate in the prosecution and collection of the Award.
@H2 13.6 Tenant's Separate Claim
@P Nothing in this Article 13 shall limit Tenant's right to pursue a separate claim against the condemning authority, and Landlord shall not participate in or oppose such claim, provided that no such claim shall reduce the Award payable to Landlord for its fee interest.

@H1 ARTICLE 14 — MAINTENANCE, ALTERATIONS, AND SURRENDER
@H2 14.1 Tenant's Maintenance Obligations
@P Tenant shall, at its sole cost and expense, keep and maintain the Premises and the Improvements in good order, condition, and repair (reasonable wear and tear excepted), and in compliance with Applicable Law, including all structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, life safety, and building envelope systems, the engineered environmental barrier, the bulkhead, and the Public Open Space.
@H2 14.2 Alterations
@P Tenant may make non-structural alterations, renovations, and improvements to the Improvements without Landlord's consent. Tenant shall obtain Landlord's prior written consent, in accordance with the Landlord Consent Standards, before making any alteration that (a) involves a material structural change, (b) alters the exterior appearance or height of the Improvements in a manner visible from the public right-of-way or the waterfront, or (c) would materially increase Landlord's liabilities or materially impair the value of Landlord's reversion.
@H2 14.3 Alterations During Final Ten Years
@P During the final ten (10) years of the Term (after October 1, 2114), Tenant shall not make alterations or capital improvements to the Improvements exceeding $10,000,000 in the aggregate in any twelve (12) month period without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Landlord's consent shall not be required for (a) any alteration required by Applicable Law, (b) any alteration required to repair, restore, or protect the Improvements, (c) any alteration required by an insurer or a governmental authority, or (d) any leasehold improvement made for a subtenant in the ordinary course of business.
@H2 14.4 Landlord's Access
@P Landlord and its authorized representatives may enter the Premises upon not less than forty-eight (48) hours' prior notice (except in an emergency, when no notice shall be required) to inspect the Premises, to perform any obligation of Tenant that Tenant has failed to perform, or to comply with Applicable Law. Any such entry shall be conducted so as not to interfere unreasonably with Tenant's operations or with the rights of subtenants, and Landlord shall not enter any residential unit without the consent of the occupant or as required by Applicable Law.
@H2 14.5 Surrender
@P Upon the Expiration Date, the Improvements shall become the property of Landlord without payment or compensation to Tenant. Upon any earlier termination of this Lease:
@C1 (a) if the termination results from Tenant's uncured monetary Event of Default, from a Casualty or Condemnation, or from Tenant's exercise of a right to terminate expressly given in this Lease, the Improvements shall become the property of Landlord without payment or compensation to Tenant, subject in each case to the rights of each Leasehold Mortgagee under Article 10; and
@C1 (b) if the termination results from Landlord's default, from a Condemnation, or from the exercise of the Reverter Right, the Improvements shall not become the property of Landlord unless and until Landlord has paid the Purchase Price required by Section 9.6, or, in the case of a Condemnation, the Award has been allocated as provided in Section 13.4.
@P Upon surrender, Tenant shall deliver the Premises in good condition, reasonable wear and tear excepted, free of tenants and occupants claiming by, through, or under Tenant (other than subtenants whose subleases survive under Applicable Law or under a New Lease), and free of liens and encumbrances created by Tenant. Tenant shall have the right to remove its Tenant Personal Property and trade fixtures, provided that Tenant repairs any damage caused by the removal. Tenant shall not be required to remove any improvement or fixture if the removal would materially damage the Improvements.
@H2 14.6 No Waste
@P Neither party shall commit waste upon the Premises. Landlord shall have no obligation to make any improvement, alteration, or repair to the Premises, and shall have no obligation to rebuild after a Casualty or Condemnation.
@H2 14.7 Landlord's Cooperation on Surrender
@P Upon the expiration or earlier termination of this Lease, Landlord shall, upon Tenant's request, execute and deliver a quitclaim deed or other instrument reasonably necessary to confirm the vesting of the Improvements in Landlord, and shall promptly release the Security Deposit in accordance with Section 7.7. Landlord shall also execute such documents as may be reasonably required to permit Tenant's Leasehold Mortgagee to realize upon its collateral or to enter into a New Lease.
