@H1 ARTICLE 16 — DEFAULT AND REMEDIES
@H2 16.1 Tenant Events of Default
@P Each of the following shall constitute an Event of Default by Tenant:
@C1 (a) **Monetary Default.** Tenant fails to pay any installment of Base Rent, Percentage Rent, or other Rent within ten (10) Business Days after written notice from Landlord that such amount is past due; provided that Landlord shall not be required to give more than two (2) such notices in any twelve (12) month period, after which a failure to pay within five (5) Business Days after the due date shall constitute an Event of Default without notice;
@C1 (b) **Non-Monetary Default.** Tenant fails to perform or observe any material non-monetary obligation under this Lease, and such failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Landlord specifying the failure, or, if such failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant fails to commence cure within such period and thereafter diligently prosecute cure to completion;
@C1 (c) **Milestone Default.** Tenant fails to achieve Milestone 1 or Milestone 3 by the applicable Milestone Date, as extended, and such failure is not cured within the Milestone Cure Period and the Extended Cure period, and the Lender Cure Periods have expired, in each case as provided in Article 9;
@C1 (d) **Insolvency.** Tenant files a voluntary petition in bankruptcy, or an order for relief is entered against Tenant in an involuntary proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days, or Tenant makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or a receiver is appointed for a substantial part of Tenant's assets and not discharged within sixty (60) days; and
@C1 (e) **Abandonment.** Tenant abandons the Premises for a continuous period of one hundred eighty (180) days, other than during a period of Force Majeure Event, Casualty, Condemnation, or restoration; provided that vacating a building for the purpose of renovation, repositioning, or re-leasing shall not constitute abandonment.
@H2 16.2 Landlord's Remedies
@P Upon the occurrence of an uncured Event of Default by Tenant, Landlord may, subject to Article 10 and Article 9:
@C1 (a) terminate this Lease by written notice, whereupon Tenant shall surrender the Premises, subject to Article 14.5 and to the rights of each Leasehold Mortgagee;
@C1 (b) recover from Tenant all Rent accrued and unpaid, together with all damages proximately caused by the Event of Default, including the reasonable costs of re-letting and the amount by which the Rent for the balance of the Term exceeds the fair rental value of the Premises for the balance of the Term, discounted to present value at a rate equal to the yield on United States Treasury obligations of comparable maturity;
@C1 (c) draw upon the Security Deposit in accordance with Article 7, limited to the amount of Landlord's actual, documented loss;
@C1 (d) pursue specific performance and injunctive relief; and
@C1 (e) pursue any other remedy available at law or in equity.
@P Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages. Landlord's remedies are cumulative, except that Landlord shall not recover more than once for the same loss, shall not both terminate this Lease and recover Rent for a period after termination except as damages, and shall not retain the Security Deposit as a penalty. Landlord shall not have any right to accelerate Rent, to declare the entire balance of Rent for the Term immediately due and payable, or to forfeit the Improvements.
@H2 16.3 Landlord Events of Default
@P Each of the following shall constitute an Event of Default by Landlord:
@C1 (a) Landlord fails to perform or observe any material obligation under this Lease (including the obligation to deliver an SNDA, estoppel, consent, or other instrument, to discharge the Mechanics' Lien, to perform its obligations under Article 8 or Section 8.9, or to perform any obligation relating to the Abatement Program), and such failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant specifying the failure, or, if such failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Landlord fails to commence cure within such period and thereafter diligently prosecute cure to completion;
@C1 (b) Landlord breaches any representation or warranty in Section 15.1 or Section 15.6 in any material respect;
@C1 (c) Landlord wrongfully attempts to terminate this Lease, wrongfully exercises or threatens to exercise the Reverter Right, or wrongfully interferes with Tenant's quiet enjoyment of the Premises; or
@C1 (d) Landlord fails to pay any amount owing to Tenant under this Lease, including any amount owing under Section 8.4 or Section 8.7, within thirty (30) days after written demand.
@H2 16.4 Tenant's Remedies
@P Upon the occurrence of an uncured Event of Default by Landlord, Tenant may, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity:
@C1 (a) offset the amount of Tenant's damages against Rent next coming due, including amounts owing under Section 6.2(d), Section 7.4(d), Section 8.3, Section 8.4, Section 8.7, and Section 15.4;
@C1 (b) perform Landlord's obligation and offset the reasonable cost of such performance (together with an administrative fee equal to five percent (5%) of such cost) against Rent next coming due;
@C1 (c) seek specific performance, declaratory relief, and injunctive relief, and Landlord waives any defense based on the adequacy of a remedy at law;
@C1 (d) seek damages, including consequential damages to the extent proximately caused by Landlord's breach; and
@C1 (e) terminate this Lease, if the Event of Default is material and remains uncured for one hundred twenty (120) days after notice, in which case the Improvements shall be treated as provided in Section 14.5(b).
@P Tenant's exercise of any offset right under this Section 16.4 shall not constitute a default under this Lease, and Landlord shall, at Tenant's request, confirm in writing to each Leasehold Mortgagee that such offset is permitted under this Lease and does not constitute a default by Tenant.
@H2 16.5 Limitation on Landlord's Liability
@P Except with respect to Landlord's indemnity obligations under Section 8.7(b) and Section 3.7(c), and except for any liability arising from Landlord's gross negligence or willful misconduct, Landlord's liability under this Lease shall be limited to Landlord's interest in the Premises and the Improvements and the rents and proceeds thereof, and no commissioner, officer, employee, or agent of Landlord shall have any personal liability under this Lease. Nothing in this Section 16.5 shall limit Landlord's obligation to pay the Purchase Price under Section 9.6 or to make any payment required to be made from Landlord's general funds under Section 8.7(b).
@H2 16.6 Notice and Cure; No Waiver
@P No failure or delay by either party in exercising any right, power, or remedy shall operate as a waiver, and no single or partial exercise shall preclude any other or further exercise. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party. Landlord's acceptance of Rent with knowledge of a default shall not waive the default. Landlord shall give each Leasehold Mortgagee the notices required by Section 10.2 in respect of any Tenant default.
@H2 16.7 Consent Standards; Deemed Consents
@P Except as expressly provided otherwise in this Lease, any consent, approval, or waiver required of Landlord shall be governed by the Landlord Consent Standards. Landlord shall respond to any written request for consent within fifteen (15) Business Days (or such shorter period as this Lease may specify), and if Landlord fails to respond within such period, the consent shall be deemed granted. Landlord may condition any consent on reimbursement of Landlord's reasonable out-of-pocket third-party costs (not to exceed $5,000 per request), but shall not charge any consent fee.
@H2 16.8 Force Majeure
@P Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than the payment of money) caused by a Force Majeure Event, provided that the affected party gives notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effect of the event. This Section 16.8 is in addition to, and does not limit, Section 9.3.
@H2 16.9 Attorneys' Fees
@P If either party commences any action or proceeding to enforce or interpret this Lease, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees, expert fees, and costs from the non-prevailing party.
@H2 16.10 Survival of Tenant's Statutory Rights
@P Nothing in this Lease shall be construed to waive, limit, or impair any right or remedy of Tenant that cannot be waived under Applicable Law, including any right to relief from forfeiture, any statutory right of redemption, and any right under Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-1 *et seq.* to the extent applicable. Any provision of this Lease that is determined to be unenforceable shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by law.

@P Each party shall keep confidential the terms of this Lease and all financial statements, rent rolls, and other information furnished by the other party, and shall not disclose the same except (a) to its officers, commissioners, employees, attorneys, accountants, consultants, lenders, investors, and prospective transferees who have a need to know, (b) as required by Applicable Law, including the Freedom of Information Act, (c) in connection with any enforcement action, financing, or sale, and (d) to the extent the information is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Section.

@P Landlord acknowledges that it is a public agency subject to the Connecticut Freedom of Information Act, Conn. Gen. Stat. § 1-200 *et seq.* Landlord shall, before disclosing any information furnished by Tenant that Tenant has designated as confidential or proprietary, (a) give Tenant not less than five (5) Business Days' prior written notice (or such shorter period as may be required by law, but in no event less than twenty-four (24) hours), (b) assert in good faith all applicable exemptions, including Conn. @H2 17.6 Estoppel Certificates
@P Each party shall, within ten (10) Business Days after request, execute and deliver an estoppel certificate certifying such matters as the requesting party (or its lender, purchaser, or Title Company) may reasonably request, including that this Lease is in full force and effect, that no default exists, and the then-current Rent.
